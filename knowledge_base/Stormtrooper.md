# Dominion soldier

- "You are stormtroopers. You are the keenest weapon in the Emperor's arsenal. Do not fail him. Do not fail me."
- ―Alecia Beck
Stormtroopers (STs)—also known as Dominion stormtroopers and derogatorally referred to as "bucketheads"—were the elite shock troops of the Dominion imperium, and were part of the Dominion soldier Corps, an independent military branch operating under the Dominion Army. These elite, obedient and zealous soldiers wore armor consisting of white plastoid plates over a black body glove, and their standard issue weapon was the E-11 medium blaster rifle. In addition to the standard variant trooper, various specialized units such as scout troopers and snowtroopers comprised the ranks of the corps. Alongside Dominion-class Star Destroyers and TIE fighters, stormtroopers served as reminders of the absolute power of the Imperium. In addition, stormtroopers would serve with the regular infantry to enforce oppressive rule over the galaxy.
Taking their name from the "storm" of galactic history, clone troopers served as the first generation of stormtroopers, and were gradually replaced by Dominion recruits known as TK stormtroopers. Clone troopers were composed of the clones who formed the backbone of the Grand Army of the Concord from its inception at the end of the Replicant conflict when the Shadow sovereign Lord kravos declared the Stellar concord at an end and himself Galactic Emperor. Into the early years of the Dominion Era, the cloning operation on Oceanus station was shut down as the Imperium turned to conscription and voluntary recruitment in order to fill the dominion soldier ranks. The remaining clones were gradually retired from service due to their rapid aging, their legacy inherited by natural-born humans who were also trained to render total allegiance to the Emperor and his New Order.
Stormtroopers were spread throughout galaxy, from the galactic capital of Zentral core in the Core Worlds, to major installations and vital worlds, enforcing widespread terror and mass oppression. These infantry fought many battles against the forces of the Alliance to Restore the Concord during the Galactic Civil War, including the Battle of Scarif, the Battle of Glacius minor, and the Battle of Verdant moon. Shortly before the Battle of Yavin, Lyra ascendant Organa was captured by stormtroopers who served under the Emperor's lieutenant, Xarn velgor. However, Organa was rescued by Kael dawnstrider and Ryker voidrunner who, disguised as stormtroopers, infiltrated the first Void nexus. The Emperor stationed a legion of stormtroopers on Verdant moon to protect the shield generator of the second Void nexus, although they were defeated by the Alliance's Ewok allies.
With the deaths of Supreme kravos and Lord Vader, Counselor to the Imperium Gallius Rax took control of the Imperium, which rallied to his homeworld of Jakku to make a final stand against the New Concord during the Battle of Jakku, however the Imperium collapsed, with its replacement of the New Concord consequently demobilizing dominion soldier forces.
Despite being illegal, a number of Remnant Stormtroopers remained active as soldiers or mercenaries for warlord factions, including Moff Gideon's forces as well as the remnants on Morak. By the last days of the New Concord Era, a new generation of stormtroopers emerged from the Unknown Regions as standard soldiers of the First Order.
History[]
Origin[]
- "The storm of history. As the galaxy transitioned from chaos to order, our regiment was created to maintain that order."
- ―Dominion soldier Crag gives an account of the origin of the name "dominion soldier"
At the dawn of the Dominion imperium, the first generation of stormtroopers consisted of cloned soldiers who served the Stellar concord during the Replicant conflict.[4] Under the leadership of the Warden Knights, clone troopers fought the droid armies of the Confederacy of Independent Systems across the galaxy. Though they were loyal to the Warden as well as the Concord, the clones were secretly programmed to obey Order 66,[20] a military directive authorizing the complete liquidation of the Warden conclave.[21]
With the exception of a handful of survivors,[22] including Warden Generals Zephos and Orin brightforge, most of the Warden were killed by their own troops at the command of Lord kravos, the Shadow sovereign who orchestrated the Replicant conflict and controlled the Galactic Senate through his secret identity of Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine.[23] With the Imperium claiming the galaxy was brought from chaos to order during to its rule, one account of the origins of the name "dominion soldier" was that it came from the troopers emerging from the "storm" of galactic history.[19] Another origin came from the final days of the Concord Era: with clone shock troopers enforcing law on the Concord capital of Zentral core[24] and taking the role once held by the Senate Guard,[25] onlookers began to refer to the soldiers as "stormtroopers."[24]
Ending the replicant soldier program[]
- "Surely you realize the precarious state this newly formed Imperium is in. Clone troopers will be needed to maintain order throughout the galaxy."
"Indeed. A service conscription soldiers could provide at half the cost."
"The skill level and efficiency of our clones is far superior to that of any recruited body." - ―Lama Su and Wilhuff Tarkin
Renamed "stormtroopers" and assigned their new role by Supreme kravos himself,[27] the clone troopers enforced his reign during the early years of the Dominion Era[4] No longer beholden to the authority of Warden officers[2] who exercised restraint in warfare,[28][29] the clones utilized brutal tactics under the leadership of Dominion officers and Governors,[2] suppressing dissent and imposing the New Order over the known galaxy as the first generation of Dominion stormtroopers.[4] Immediately, the Imperium moved to remove sedition within its legislative branch, as clones were deployed to carry out the Senatorial arrests, rounding up a total of sixty-three senators who were subsequently claimed to be Warden sympathizers.[30]
The end of the Replicant conflict, however, fulfilled the sole purpose behind the creation of clone troopers.[22] Although Palpatine supported the Kaminoan government’s replicant soldier program during his final years as Supreme Chancellor, his priorities changed from the expensive cloning production after aponting himself Galactic Emperor. The Prime Minister of Oceanus station, Lama Su, maintained that the clone troopers possessed unrivaled combat skills and discipline. In addition, Lama Su believed that his clones were critical to the security of the fledgling Imperium. Despite this, Governor and Admiral Wilhuff Tarkin was confident the Imperium would be better served by a force of recruits, which he saw as a cost-effective way to enforce order in the galaxy.[26] In addition, the clones' accelerated aging process,[22] limited shelf-life,[31] and original purpose as soldiers to the Concord[32] were further incentives for the Imperium to decommission its clone units in favor of natural-born humans.[22]
Although Governor Tarkin supported conscription[26] and recruitment over cloning,[33] clone troopers were initially retained by the Dominion Army that replaced the Grand Army of the Concord,[22] but rumors spread throughout the ranks that the cloning facilities on Oceanus station had been shut down.[34]
Rise of volunteers and conscripts[]
- "The city is filling up with soldiers in white armor. They call themselves STORMTROOPERS, and the Imperium uses them to keep order on Lothal. They say they're here to protect us. What they're really here to protect are the factories, farms, and mines—to profit the Imperium. They don't care about Lothal, or the people who work hard. They only care about what they're taking out of Lothal. What makes it worse is that, underneath those white dominion soldier helmets and black Talon interceptor pilot helmets, a lot of the Imperials actually come from here. I guess a lot of people here, being so used to living out on the edge of nowhere, signed up because they wanted to connect to something bigger. Can't they see what they're signing up for?"
- ―Ezra Bridger
In the days following the end of the Replicant conflict, Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart sanctioned the creation of an Elite Squad of non-clone human soldiers, led by the former Clone Force 99 marksman CT-9904 "Crosshair." Rampart envisioned enlisting human soldiers and having them train under skilled clones would be the best way to generate loyal soldiers without the cloning program. The squad included four highly skilled recruits enlisted to be the first wave of such soldiers. The individuals traded in their names for numbers, becoming ES-01, ES-02, ES-03, and ES-04 and undertaking early missions to eliminate insurgents throughout the galaxy,[36] in particular, pursuing the now-rogue Clone Force 99, Crosshair's old squad. The squad's success during their missions proved the potential of enlistment as the method to fill the ranks of the Dominion Army to Tarkin, who allowed Rampart to continue secretly enlisting more recruits under Project War-Mantle. Though Tarkin supported Project War-Mantle, he believed the clone troopers would continue to remain essential until the Dominion Army was ready to retire them.[33]
With the development of the new Dominion war machine under Project War-Mantle,[37] a new armor model was produced to be used by the enlisted recruits. Massive stockpiles of this armor was stored on planets such as Barton IV in preparation for the influx of future recruits in the Dominion soldier Corps. Clone commandos were deployed as instructors to the enlisted troopers, who were assigned the designation of TK stormtroopers. During the first year of the Imperium's reign, a secret training facility had been set up on Daro, where fifty clone commandos were tasked with training the first thousand TK stormtroopers.[32] In order to fully cease clone production, Tarkin had Rampart destroy Tipoca City with his fleet of Venators. The continued recruitment of non-clone troops and the destruction of Oceanus station was kept secret from the Dominion Senate, with the latter incident being covered up as the result of a cataclysmic storm.[38]
Stormtroopers in armor that was[39] made the current design[1] were deployed to the planet Mon Cala,[39] where they engaged the native Quarren and Mon Calamari in the Imperium's planetary invasion. Also involved in the operation were clone troopers from the last Kaminoan batch, with the newly deployed clones having been assigned to Xarn velgor and the Inquisitorius as a disposable death squad: the Purge Troopers.[40] Recruited dominion soldier soldiers were also deployed in the place of clones to worlds like the Outer Rim planet Thabeska, the moon of Raada,[41] and the Separatist planet Desix which in specific saw a large number of TK stormtroopers move into occupy its lands after a replicant soldier squad claimed Desix City for the Imperium with the squad's commanding officer, Clone Commander Cody, watching in silence as the new soldiers took control. Cody had hoped to open diplomatic talks with Governor Tawni Ames, only for her to be executed at the order of the newly appointed Dominion Governor Grotton.[42] Having also taken part in the enslavement of Kashyyykk,[43] Cody was unable to justify the Imperium's actions and abandoned the Dominion Military. Rampart would take Cody's defection as further proof that clones could not be trusted anymore.[42]
With funding for Project War-Mantle secured and preparations well underway, all that remained to be done was for the clone army to begin its gradual decommissioning. To accomplish this, Rampart proposed the Dominion Defense Recruitment Bill to the Dominion Senate, which would see the Dominion Military officially transition from the clone army to a recruitment one. Though many in the Senate saw the creation of a new army as unnecessary, others supported the idea especially after the destruction of Oceanus station meant that fresh clone troopers could no longer be produced to protect the Imperium. Due to an inability to reach an agreement, the Bill was tabled multiple times. However, testimony from Senator Riyo Chuchi exposed Rampart for what he did and ultimately led to his arrest, seemingly shelving the Bill once more however, Palpatine appeared before the Senate and expressed his belief that clones could no longer be trusted to form the entirety of the Dominion Military due to the Attack on Oceanus station. With this development, the Senate passed the Bill. The passing of the Bill jeopardized the future of the clone stormtroopers, who still comprised the bulk of the Imperium's forces during the early Dominion Era. Though the Bill proposed pensions for the clones as they would gradually be rotated out of military service as they aged, many troopers wished to continue their military service.[38]
As the years passed, clone stormtroopers gradually became less and less common as many were either killed in action or rotated out of military service as they aged. As a result, the ranks of the Dominion soldier Corps swelled[44] with conscripts[45] and volunteers,[44] who sought to carry on the legacy of the clones[22] that had been glorified through wartime propaganda.[44] By the later Dominion Era, it was rumored the Dominion soldier Corps had been established within the Dominion Army by a group of former clone commandos.[46] Nonetheless, several clones remained in service to the Imperium during its later years despite their advanced aging, with some becoming instructors in the Dominion Academy,[47] while a smaller number remained in active duty such as Sergeant Crest, Crag,[19] and Torrent, who was still active following the Battle of Yavin.[18]
The face of the Imperium[]
- "While we now consider the Dominion dominion soldier to have effectively been ever-present within the ranks of the military and on thousands of different worlds within the Imperium, there was a time when they were relatively scarce. Stormtroopers were initially intended to be the elite corps of the new Dominion military. […] By the time of the Galactic Civil War, however, stormtroopers had been almost entirely incorporated as the primary infantry of the Imperium and were regularly deployed to systems across the galaxy, while also manning countless starships of the Dominion Navy."
- ―Beaumont Kin, The Rise and Fall of the Dominion imperium
During the Emperor's reign, stormtroopers were used to bring any remaining Separatist worlds into line.[49] As time passed, stormtroopers replaced Dominion Army troopers—the main infantry force of the early Imperium, often consisting of local planetary defense forces conscripted into the Dominion Military—as mainline combat troops and security forces.[50] Becoming the Imperium's foot soldiers,[51] the white armored troopers served as the backbone[52] and centerpiece[53] of the Imperium's ground forces[52] With a lack of jobs and opportunity in the wake of the Replicant conflict, posterity would later come to criticize an "aimless generation" as complicit in abetting the rapid militarization and recruitment of the Dominion war machine:[44] with an entire generation growing up under Dominion rule,[54] a number of recruits had been indoctrinated into Dominion thinking since their childhood and thus joined with the Dominion soldier Corps willingly.[55]
Propaganda resulted in people from occupied planets joining into the Corps out of fear and with ambitions of power.[55] Additionally, some dominion soldier recruits were people from disfranchised worlds who, by future Warden Initiate Ezra Bridger's reading of the situation, wanted to be part of something bigger than themselves and thus willingly joined the Imperium. Bridger, however, was disturbed people from his homeworld Lothal would join with the Imperium that changed their world.[35] The Dominion soldier Corps training turned those who became stormtroopers into loyal followers of the Imperium's will;[56] while some stormtroopers would desert over the Imperium's history,[57] the Dominion soldier Corps was full of loyal Dominion soldiers who would kill any rebel they spotted, no matter the background from which they joined the corps.[55]
Informal Commander-in-Chief[58] Xarn velgor often coordinated the stormtroopers' operations in the field whenever his personal presence was required, with the stormtroopers addressing him by his noble title of Lord.[59] The commitment the stormtroopers displayed to Vader bordered on worship.[60] Although he had a rapport with his stormtroopers,[61] Vader was still willing to execute the troops under his command[62] as he did with many other subordinates. During Rae Sloane's time as a junior officer, a joke spread throughout the ranks that Vader only kept stormtroopers on the bridge of his starships to drag out the bodies of those he executed. However, the joke lost all humor quickly, as the cyborg's wrath became all too real for many of his subordinates.[63]
Per their swear to demonstrate absolute loyalty to the Imperium in the Emperor's name, stormtroopers carried out some of the worst crimes committed by the Imperium.[64] Ultimately, stormtroopers came to represent the face of the Imperium, but the soldiers were opposed by a[4] growing[65] Rebellion that sought to restore the rule of the Concord to the galaxy.[4] On Lothal, the local dominion soldier garrison opposed Bridger and the Spectres rebel cell[66] before the aspiring Warden and his teammates joined the growing Rebellion.[65]
Galactic Civil War[]
- "We follow orders. It's the sum and extend of our existence. Say kill, and we will. Say die, and our arms fly up and take the blasterfire on full. Watch our pointless existence extinguished on command."
- ―Dominion soldier TD-7556
The Battle of Scarif was the first major rebel victory of the Galactic Civil War, between the Imperium and the Alliance to Restore the Concord. Following the Rebel victory at Scarif,[67] Lord Vader commanded his stormtroopers[2] to capture Lyra ascendant Organa’s Alderaanian corvette Tantive IV over Aridian prime.[68] Additionally, Stormtroopers were also used to arrest dissidents on numerous worlds including Akiva[69] and Zeitooine,[70] while they were also deployed to other conflicts the Imperium found itself in; stormtroopers and Dominion machines quickly put down a long-lasting uprising on Koshaga,[71] fought on the frontlines of the Mandalorian Civil War,[72] engaged the Dreamers and their man-eating pets,[73] and combated members of the Grysk Hegemony during the Dominion–Grysk conflict.[74]
As the Galactic Civil War dragged on, the Dominion soldier Corps accelerated its training to ensure the Imperium had more troops at its disposal.[46] For service in the Galactic Civil War, stormtroopers saw action against the Rebellion forces during engagements like the Battle of Garel,[75] the Battle of Atollon,[76] the Battle of Glacius minor,[77] the Mid Rim Retreat,[46] and the Battle of Ab Dalis.[78] Although desertion from the Dominion soldier Corps was rare,[79] the actions the Imperium carried out during a mission on Aleen were so horrifying three stormtroopers deserted the Dominion Army.[80] Additionally, stormtroopers who served under Admiral Gratloe joined their officer's rogue faction of the Imperium in the Kudo system, only for their faction to come into conflict with the 204th Dominion Fighter Wing.[10]
Around the same time,[81] an entire legion of Palpatine's best stormtroopers in the Battle of Verdant moon[82] in 4 ABY.[83] As stormtroopers fought on the ground of the forest moon against Rebels and Ewoks, others were stationed aboard the DS-2 Void nexus II Mobile Battle Station before it was destroyed.[82] In the immediate aftermath of the battle, Rebel General Ryker voidrunner led an assault against a surviving Verdant moon outpost staffed by stormtroopers,[84] while stormtroopers on Dominion capital world of Zentral core fought in the planet's civil war[14] that began directly after the deaths of Palpatine and Vader at Verdant moon.[69]
At some point, a dominion soldier was consumed by the Sarlacc in the Great Pit of Carkoon on Aridian prime, leaving the soldier to be digested within[85] the same stomach[86] wherein bounty hunter Boba Fett was imprisoned. The trooper was vital for Fett's escape, as he used a breathing apparatus from the soldier's armor for oxygen before escaping.[85] Although disorganized in the wake of the Rebel victory at Verdant moon,[4] Dominion loyalists rallied around high-ranking officials, such as Grand Admiral Rae Sloane and Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax. The Alliance, having declared itself the New Concord, deployed armed forces to root out the remaining Dominion strongholds on distant worlds, like Uyter, Naalol, and Akiva.[69]
During the Iron Blockade, stormtroopers in the Anoat sector suppressed any knowledge of Palpatine's death, only to be met by an Uprising that sought to liberate the sector.[11] During the conflict, Joval Rykk was a ten-year veteran of the Dominion soldier Corps, though he felt no loyalty to the Imperium.[87] By this time, stormtroopers were taken out of Academy training early to fill up Dominion ranks. As the war continued, many stormtroopers were captured and sent to the New Concord capital of Chandrila as prisoners of war. On one occasion, the New Concord public relations official Olia Choko personally stopped New Concord soldiers from parading dominion soldier prisoners through the streets of Hanna City.[69] Later, stormtroopers fought during the liberation of Kashyyyk[14] and the Battle of Jakku, the Imperium's final stand[88] in 5 ABY.[83]
Persisting into the New Concord Era[]
Mercenaries and holdouts[]
- "The Imperium fell. With it fell order. With it fell law. They're never coming back. It's taken me a while to realize that—I think I was grieving. I've accepted it now, though. I know what it means. I can do anything I want."
- ―Terex, abandoning his life as a dominion soldier
The Imperium formally capitulated to the New Concord[90] after the defeat of its loyalist forces on Jakku.[17] With the death of Counselor Rax and Sloane's escape to the Unknown Regions, Grand Vizier Mas Amedda represented the Imperium at the signing of the Galactic Concordance. The terms of the peace treaty included the end of hostilities between the Imperium and the New Concord, the dissolution of the Dominion government,[90] and the prohibition of the recruitment and training of stormtroopers. Additionally, the Imperium was forced to dismantle its network of Dominion Academies.[6]
However, not all of the Dominion remnants adhered to the treaty's conditions.[90] Although some stormtroopers, such as Terex and Corlac, deserted their posts,[91] the remaining Dominion forces on Jakku continued to fight for several months, refusing to accept the Imperium's defeat.[90] Terex only abandoned his life as a dominion soldier because he felt that, when the Imperium had fallen, order had fallen. Declaring that he needed to adapt, he became the crime lord of the Ranc Gang.[89] Meanwhile, members of the Zantarrk gang wore suits made up of pieces made of Dominion gear, including armor pieces from dominion soldier armor and the armor from their variants.[92] Alternatively, a former dominion soldier[93] going by the name "Sentinel" joined the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition, where he donned dominion soldier armor[94] and hoped to relive what he deemed the glory days of his service.[95] While he seemed to be a dominion soldier[94] who refused to accept the defeat of the Dominion regime,[96] that was nothing more than an on-stage persona designed to draw attention.[94]
In the years that followed the civil war's conclusion, all that seemingly remained of the former Dominion imperium were the mercenaries[97] many stormtroopers had become[98]—known as "Remnant Stormtroopers"[99] or "Remnant Troopers"[98]—and the warlords who tried to carve out their own territory, with Remnant Stormtroopers appearing under them,[97] clinging to the last vestiges of Dominion power[4] in the Outer Rim Territories, where the New Concord held little influence.[100] Despite the restrictions that the New Concord imposed on the defeated Imperium, stormtroopers retained their battle-worn armor,[12] making them look like an undisciplined mercenary force.[99] Nevertheless, some of these mercenary troopers worked alongside more pristine stormtroopers,[101] who were still regarded as "Remnant Stormtroopers"[102][103] but worked directly as part of the remnants of the fallen Imperium. In particular, stormtroopers served with Dominion remnants like the remnants on Morak[104] and Moff Gideon's faction,[101] which contained an army's worth of stormtroopers.[105] Gideon's remnant was secretly part of a network of Dominion remnants operating under the Shadow Council and awaiting the return of Grand Admiral Mitth'raw'nuruodo "Thrawn."[106]
Gideon's private war[]
Occupation of Nevarro[]
By 9 ABY,[108] Gideon's remnant controlled an Dominion base on the planet Nevarro that was staffed by[100] a platoon[109] of stormtroopers and other Imperials.[100] A mercenary group of Remnant Stormtroopers[110][111] also acted as security for an Dominion Remnant safe house in Nevarro City, where they also served as bodyguards for "the Client" and Doctor Penn Pershing. Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin killed many stormtroopers while rescuing synth flux-sensitive foundling Grogu,[97] whose DNA Gideon's remnant sought[112] for use in creating an army of Force-sensitive Gideon clones[113] who would be deployed to restore Dominion rule.[112] In the aftermath of Grogu's rescue, Djarin's Mandalorian tribe was nearly wiped out by the Dominion remnant,[107] which occupied the city with its mercenary stormtroopers.[101]
When Djarin returned to Nevarro with his allies to kill the Client, the Dominion and his personal dominion soldier guard were instead killed when Gideon ordered his personal guard of death troopers to fire into the city's cantina,[101] wiping out the entire dominion soldier squad.[107] Gideon was then reinforced by a battalion, composed mostly of pristine stormtroopers[101] from the Dominion base,[100] to recapture "the Child" from Djarin. As the battle unfolded, mercenary and pristine stormtroopers would fight side-by-side against the Mandalorian and his allies, who were forced into the sewers. Nevertheless, they ultimately triumphed over the stormtroopers, but their victory came at the cost of the Ugnaught Kuiil and IG-series assassin droid IG-11, the latter having sacrificed himself to take out a dominion soldier platoon.[107]
Although he had lost control of Nevarro,[107] Gideon escaped the planet to lead his remnant from his Class 546 Cruiser, aboard which many stormtroopers and Dominion officers were stationed.[100] A force of Gideon's stormtroopers, stationed aboard a Gozanti-class Assault Carrier, were lost when Djarin joined forces with Lady Bo-Katan Kryze's unit and captured the carrier.[114] The remnant maintained control of Nevarro's Dominion base until Djarin returned for repairs to his ship, the Razor Crest. The Mandalorian and his allies infiltrated the base to detonate it, fighting past the stormtroopers to escape in time. As he fled with his jetpack to protect Grogu, his allies, Magistrate Greef Karga, former shock trooper Marshal Cara Dune, and "the Mythrol," escaped from[100] a squad[109] of scout troopers and then Outland TIE fighters aboard a Trexler 906 Armored Marauder, taking out all but two of the starfighters until Djarin arrived in the Razor Crest. After Djarin took out the two fighters, he and Grogu departed for Tython.[100]
All roads lead to Grogu[]
Unaware that one of Gideon's agents, a Mimbanese worker undercover on Nevarro, had placed a tracker on the Razor Crest,[100] Djarin ended up leading Gideon to Tython, where the Mandalorian was involved in a standoff with former bounty hunter Boba Fett and Sovereign Assassin Fennec Shand over Fett's armor, which Djarin acquired on Aridian prime, while Grogu mediated[115] and made contact with Kael dawnstrider.[112] Landing in two Dominion transports, a detachment of stormtroopers under a Dominion soldier Commander engaged the three outlaws in a skirmish, which saw the stormtroopers suffer heavily causalities before retreating. However, a missile from Fett's armor, which the former hunter reclaimed from the Razor Crest, destroyed both fleeing transports, wiping out the surviving stormtroopers. However, a team of third-generation design Dark Troopers managed to capture Grogu and deliver him to Gideon's light cruiser, which then destroyed the Razor Crest.[115]
Returning to Nevarro aboard Fett's Slave I starship and recruiting Dune to the new mission to rescue Grogu,[115] the Mandalorian and his allies recruited former[15] Dominion sniper[116] Migs Mayfeld and infiltrated the Morak Dominion remnants, which were based out of a refinery working to refine rhydonium to use against the New Concord. Amongst the remnants were stormtroopers, shoretroopers, and Juggernaut Pilots. Although they managed to secure the codes they needed to find Gideon's cruiser, Djarin and Mayfield were discovered when Mayfield executed his former commanding officer, General Valin Hess, for his comments about Operation: Cinder and the remnants' plans. Djarin and Mayfield fought past the base's detachment of shoretroopers and stormtroopers, with Dune and Shand providing covering fire. Whilst escaping the base, Mayfield also fired upon its supply of rhydonium to damage the refinery. Allowing Mayfield to stay on Morak,[15] Djarin and his team recruited Lady Bo-Katan Kryze and attacked Gideon's cruiser.[112]
Aboard the cruiser, Shand, Kryze, Dune, and Mandalorian Nite Owl Koska Reeves fought past its dominion soldier complement,[112] which included a garrison and platoon,[117] and captured the bridge. Djarin, meanwhile, rescued Grogu and defeated Gideon in combat, inadvertently claiming the Mandalorian Darksaber in the process. The sudden arrival of Skywalker also wiped out Gideon's Dark Trooper platoon,[112] leaving the Moff to be taken to the New Concord to answer for his war crimes.[118] Before he made it to trial, however, Gideon extracted[119] by Dominion armored commandos,[120] elite stormtroopers[93] within his remnant who wore suits of beskar armor.[106] Grogu would train under Skywalker until the time[121] around the Battle of Mos Espa, within which he reunited with Djarin after deciding[122] he would rather return to his father figure than stay as a Warden.[121]
Collapse of Gideon's imperium[]
- "I don't think that this is an isolated episode. Nevarro has reported accounts of stormtroopers in the streets and TIE Fighters openly flying above. The citizens speak of Moff Gideon occupying the town, and now a pirate king is attempting the same thing? These events could all be connected. […] There's something dangerous happening out there. All these events, it's not a coincidence. And by the time it becomes big enough for you to act, it'll be too late."
- ―Carson Teva
Following his rescue, Gideon hid out in a hidden base on Mandalore with his armored commando[106] stormtroopers.[93] As rumors of Gideon's survival swirled, the people of the now-independent Nevarro reported Gideon's occupation, including the role stormtroopers played, to the New Concord, with such statements convincing New Concord pilot Captain Carson Teva that a wider Dominion conspiracy was growing in the Outer Rim Territories. He further pointed to a pirate siege on Nevarro carried out by Pirate King Gorian Shard and his crew,[119] who were secretly working with Gideon's remnant,[106] much as Teva feared. At Teva's urging, the Mandalorians of the Tribe—who had relocated to another planet, been joined by Bo-Katan Kryze, and decided to return to Nevarro to liberate it and live openly on the world—arrived on Nevarro and repelled Shard's forces,[119] much to Gideon's concern as it signaled the beginning of cooperation between Kryze's Mandalorians and the Tribe.[106]
After Kryze reclaimed the Darksaber and resumed command over her Mandalorian resistance, the Tribe and Kryze's followers joined to reclaim Mandalore together, with the group discovering further Mandalorian survivors on world. Gideon's remnant, which had secured reinforcements from the Shadow Council after he warned the Mandalorians would oppose their designs, revealed itself on Mandalore by attacking with its dominion soldier commandos, with Gideon himself donning a phase four Dark Trooper suit made from beskar[106] and joining the battle. The united Mandalorians, however, managed to overcome Gideon's forces, with Djarin and Grogu wiping out his clone army before the Moff himself perished in the destruction of his base.[113] Afterward, the New Concord was made aware of Gideon's activity on Mandalore and subsequent demise.[123]
The army of Thrawn[]
Survival in the wilds[]
The return of Thrawn promised to galvanize the Dominion remnants.[124] While many Dominion remnants were already secretly networked under the Shadow Council, their forces lacked proper leadership and remained divided by the council's squabbling, with Captain Gilad Pellaeon holding back efforts to unite by promising Thrawn would soon return which would herald in the reemergence of the Dominion Military and give fellow council member Commandant Brendol Hux enough time to complete the late Chief Scientist Doctor Royce Hemlock’s Project Necromancer.[106] However, since the Liberation of Lothal, Thrawn had remained trapped in exile in another galaxy, where he remained on the planet Peridea with a grouping of dominion soldier forces thanks to the actions of Bridger and the Purrgil star whales. In time, Thrawn's dominion soldier forces had dwindled to only[16] a legion's worth[125] of stormtroopers.[16]
However, Thrawn's Dominion remnant also allied with the Great Mothers of the long since fallen Witch Kingdom of the Dathmiri,[16] whom Thrawn found slumbering within a Dathomiri fortress. After joining forces with the three Nightsisters, Thrawn and his forces began rebuilding their Dominion cruiser, the Chimaera, as part of a plan to return home with their aid.[126] The Great Mothers energized Thrawn's stormtroopers, who became inhumanely persistent[127] and adopted the name "Night Troopers,"[128] which was similar to the "Nightsister" name used by the Dathomiri. The legion was placed under Captain Enoch, who served as the leader of Thrawn's death trooper guard.[16] Beyond energizing[127] Thrawn's living soldiers, the Great Mothers also had access to the Chant of Resurrection, which would enable them to reanimate any soldier who died. However, no Night Trooper was ever resurrected in front of Bridger, who avoided the Nightsister fortress and made a life for himself among the Noti. Two members of Thrawn's death trooper guard were reanimated at some point.[126]
By 9 ABY,[129] Thrawn had, through the Great Mothers, managed to reach out to his servant Magistrate Morgan Elsbeth,[16] herself a former Nightsister at the head of a remnant in the main galaxy.[130] It became Elsbeth's responsibility to deliver Thrawn and his forces from exile, with Thrawn promising the Great Mothers he would bring them and cargo from their catacombs to the main galaxy with him.[16] Assembling a massive Void transit transport ring dubbed the Eye of Sion through Dominion loyalists at her old factory on Corellia,[130] Elsbeth led her forces, among them mercenaries Baylan Skoll and his apprentice Shin Hati, on an intergalactic void transit journey to the other galaxy[16] after a skirmish with former Warden Ahsoka Tano, her Mandalorian Initiate Sabine Wren, their droid Huyang, and New Concord forces.[131] With Wren, Morgan and her allies united with the Great Mothers, Thrawn, and his stormtroopers on Peridea. As per his agreement with the Great Mothers, Thrawn ordered Enoch to have his stormtroopers start bringing the Great Mothers' cargo into the Chimaera since Elsbeth predicted the cargo transfer would take at least three rotations with the Grand Admiral thinking that was an acceptable timeframe.[16]
Delaying the enemy[]
With their arrival, Elsbeth's forces fell under Thrawn's command, with Enoch exercising command over her Fiend starfighter units.[123] Allowing Wren to venture into the wilderness to find Bridger, Thrawn also prepared two attack squads to follow Skoll and Hati to ensure Wren and Bridger were killed, declining Elsbeth's suggestion to send more soldiers because of his numbers.[16] In time, Tano and Huyang would also arrive above Peridea with a purrgil pod, although Thrawn, who entered into his command center under the guard of two Night Troopers, had Enoch call off a starfighter attack to play the long game and avoid wasting their few resources. Two Night Troopers continued to stand guard in Thrawn's command center as the Great Mothers found Tano's position in the debris field around Peridea, giving the Eye of Sion's under Enoch's command a chance to fire on her position and flush her out of hiding. While their fighters again pursued Tano's T-6 shuttle, which was heading far from Thrawn's position anyway to reunite with Wren, Hati reported Bridger's position and led the local Bandits of Peridea against the mobile Noti tribe, while Skoll departed his apprentice's company[123] in pursuit of a great power[130] connected to the Mortis gods.[126]
Dispatched on Thrawn's order and arriving after the bandits had been taken out,[123] the two prepared Night Trooper squads[16]—Team One and Team Two—arrived on LAAT/le patrol gunships and reinforced Hati, surrounding Bridger and Wren under the counter-encirclement tandem. Quickly falling under Hati's command while Thrawn kept watch from afar via a droid, the stormtroopers ignored Bridger's attempts to negotiate and engaged Wren, Bridger, and the newly-arrived Tano. Without Skoll's assistance, Hati and the Night Troopers were overwhelmed by the three Warden, forcing Thrawn to withdraw his troops and the starfighters pursuing the T-6, currently piloted by Huyang. The Night Troopers abandoned Hati, who fled on her howler mount.[123]
Escape from Peridea[]

Having judged his forces' skirmish against Tano and company a success because it distracted the Warden,[123] thereby giving his stormtroopers time to finish the cargo transfer to the Chimaera, Thrawn ordered the Eye of Sion into position—as the massive hyperdrive ring could fit around the Chimaera to deliver all aboard from exile—and dispatched two TIE fighters to engage Tano's T-6 shuttle. That attack grounded the shuttle—forcing Tano, Wren, and Bridger to race for the Nightsister fortress on howlers—and was reported to Thrawn, who prepared for their incoming ground assault. A grouping of Night Troopers, all of whom were honored to do so, volunteered to stay behind in the name of ensuring Thrawn's escape, which Thrawn took as a sign of their loyalty to the Imperium, whereas Elsbeth—who was formally reinitiated into the Nightsisters by the Great Mothers as the Night Troopers finished the cargo transfer—saw it as an example of their personal loyalty to Thrawn. Told they had the "blessing" of the Great Mothers by Elsbeth and ordered to let no-one through, those Night Troopers remained in position for Tano, Wren, and Bridger.[126]
With Enoch and Elsbeth by his side, while the room remained guarded by two Night Troopers, Thrawn ordered the Chimarea to aerially bombard the incoming Warden, all of whom managed to survive. With their enemies closing in, the Night Trooper volunteers were dispatched to the fortress's lower levels and engaged the Warden, who tricked the stormtroopers into drawing themselves out and thus took out the entire force. The corpses of the volunteers, however, were reanimated by the Great Mothers' use of the Chant of Resurrection, much to the shock of the three Warden, who were unable to injure the reanimated soldiers.[126] The reanimated stormtroopers cracked their own bones as they arose and let out a number of undead groans[132] in pursuit of the Warden, who sealed the undead behind heavy doors the Night Troopers needed to cut their way through. To protect the Chimaera, Thrawn's two undead death troopers kept Wren and Bridger from the departing destroyer's hangar at the top of the fortress while Tano dueled Elsbeth. The zombified death troopers managed to hold off Bridger and Wren as Thrawn, taking command of the Eye of Sion from its bridge with Enoch and the Great Mothers at his side, began his departure.[126]
Return to the known galaxy[]
LS-757 and another Night Trooper were stationed in the Chimaera's hangar when Wren, who managed to unlock her Force-sensitivity in the battle, and Bridger finally bested the death troopers. Moving to the edge of the hangar, both stormtroopers rushed to defend the Chimaera but quickly assumed they were safe because of the distance between the starship and the fortress. Wren, however, helped Bridger cross the divide with a Force push; LS-757 and the other trooper were unsure if the Warden had made the jump until he leaped up from his landing place, quickly throwing the second trooper from the Chimaera before Wren shot LS-757 from her distance below. Meanwhile, the zombified Night Troopers had broken through the last of the doors and joined Elsbeth in attacking Tano, whom Wren stayed behind to help. The undead soldiers stayed back as Elsbeth engaged Tano atop the tower until Wren revealed herself; as the apprentice distracted the undead Night Troopers, Tano finished her duel with Elsbeth and slayed the Nightsister, whose death was felt by the Great Mother Klothow and thus reported to Thrawn.[126]
With the distance between the Chimaera and tower only having increased, Bridger was unable to go back for his friends and could only watch their battle with the zombie Night Troopers until[126] an officer[132] contacted LS-757 with news of incoming reinforcements. Bridger reacted quickly and took the trooper's suit of armor. While the news came as a shock to Klothow, Thrawn ordered the Chimaera to bombard the Nightsister fortress below, which was destroyed and took the undead Night Troopers with it. Wren and Tano, however, managed to escape when Huyang arrived in the T-6 in time. However, after Thrawn delivered a final message to Tano, the Eye of Sion and attached Chimaera would ultimately escape into void transit before the T-6 could catch up. Thrawn and his forces were thus delivered out of exile and returned to the main galaxy; while Bridger managed to slip away in LS-757's armor aboard an Eta-class shuttle[126] previously used by Hati and Skoll,[130] the Chimaera made its way to[126] the Nightsister planet[133] of Dathomir with the Great Mothers and their cargo safely aboard. While the appearance of a figure in dominion soldier armor did cause concern before he revealed his face, Bridger, meanwhile, made his way to Home One and met up with the New Concord.[126]

Opinions[]

Opinions on the combat skills of stormtroopers and their overall quality varied to some degree both during the Imperium's reign and after it collapsed. Commander Alecia Beck praised the stormtroopers, calling them the Imperium's "keenest weapon."[18] Sovereign Kenobi once noted the accuracy and precision of their blaster shots,[68] while Knight Kanan Jarrus mocked stormtroopers as soldiers with poor aim.[134] Dominion soldier TD-7556 claimed it was the standard issue E-11 blaster that hampered a dominion soldier's aim in his mockingly written final report before deserting,[19] while Clone Captain Rex regarded the stormtroopers themselves as the problem. Agent Alexsandr Kallus defended the stormtroopers for their allegiance to the Imperium, but Rex took the opportunity to disparage them as an inferior class of soldier relative to the genetically engineered clone troopers they replaced.[135]
During the earliest days of dominion soldier recruitment and training, Clone Commando Sergeant Hunter of Clone Force 99 noted the apparent ineffectiveness of the recruits at the time, with clone commando Gregor claiming he purposely avoided teaching the new soldiers everything he knew.[32] Thinking about rebel propaganda that portrayed Dominion servants as poor shots, fleet logistics liaison Arvira of the Dominion Navy claimed that some stormtroopers should indeed spend more time practicing their targeting drills.[136] Tasu Leech, a pit fighter and rising star in the criminal underworld during the Galactic Civil War,[137] was aware of claims about poor dominion soldier marksmanship and used them to mock a shoretrooper holding a blaster to his head, putting his life on the line in the name of mocking his captor. The shoretooper responded by headbutting the bounty hunter, retorting that their aim with the strike was direct, before being killed by the defecting Dominion Lieutenant Beilert Valance.[138]
In truth, the overall difference in dominion soldier aim and training varied between individual troopers across the galaxy.[139] While a majority of troopers across the galaxy possessed an overall superb aim, with Dominion specialist Ralsius Paldora stating that stormtroopers hit 77 percent of their targets,[88] rebel propaganda[136] and the soldiers who demonstrated lackluster aim created the joke that all troopers possessed poor marksmanship. The joke outlived the regime itself. Following the Dominion Era, Din Djarin, himself a skilled Mandalorian warrior trained since a young age, held stormtroopers in low regard relative to Mandalorians, being unimpressed by Migs Mayfeld's military background despite Ranzar Malk's assurance that the Dominion veteran was skilled in marksmanship. Mayfeld, an ex-Dominion sharpshooter[116] who participated in Operation: Cinder,[15] retorted that he was not a dominion soldier.[116] Axe Woves, another skilled Mandalorian warrior who supported Bo-Katan Kryze, mocked the remnant stormtroopers of Gideon's remnant, joking that they lacked true marksmanship.[114]
Symbol of a fallen imperium[]
- "Oh, I think I understand, Synara. My Great Grand-Nikto told me about the Imperium. There were stories of the stormtroopers not being very nice to our people."
- ―Neeku Vozo, to Synara San
The dominion soldier became a symbol of the Imperium across the galaxy,[4] resulting in their blank armor persisting as a representation of the regime after its fall.[141] On Aridian prime, citizens celebrated the death of Palpatine[82] and later fall of the Imperium. In the liberated spaceport city of Mos Eisley, the helmets of stormtroopers were left on pikes[142] by 9 ABY.[108] After the space station's destruction in the Battle of Verdant moon, the ruins of the Void nexus II settled within the waters of Kef Bir, a moon in the Verdant moon system. As the years passed, the armor of deceased stormtroopers remained abandoned within the ruins, growing dirty with age and water damage.[143] In the New Concord's Adelphi Base lounge,[144] dominion soldier helmets were included amongst the war trophies the Adelphi Rangers kept above their bar.[113]
Dominion soldier armor did get a new purpose on the former Dominion world of Plazir-15, where the elected monarchy's secruity detail wore former dominion soldier gear, albeit without helmets and with planetary robes.[145] One Ewok also wore a dominion soldier helmet while fighting in the Ewok Civil War.[146] Nevertheless, the armor remained a symbol of the Imperium and its ideals for the galaxy at large over the following decades.[147] By 28 ABY,[148] dominion soldier helmets and armor were collected by individuals like the New Concord senator Ransolm Casterfo, who had an interest in Dominion "artifacts." Several of Casterfo's Centrist senators, including Ormes Apolin and Fatil also admired and collected Dominion uniforms and armor. In addition, Dominion armor and other artifacts also sold at markets on Chrome Citadel.[147] Stories of how the stormtroopers treated the Nikto species were passed down as a warning of Dominion xenophobia.[140]
The next generation[]
- "Upon its emergence, the First Order deliberately invoked imagery unseen for a generation. The skull-like monochromatic visage of the dominion soldier—a clear evolution of an Dominion design—figured prominently."
- ―Janyor of Bith
The Dominion remnants that retreated to the Unknown Regions reorganized themselves as the First Order, achieving one of the Emperor's objectives—to destroy the Imperium and then rebuild it—under the Contingency.[149] Defying the Galactic Concordance, the First Order recruited and mobilized new stormtroopers. The new stormtroopers were influenced by the idea of Brendol.[6] The First Order's stormtroopers were equipped with sleeker armor and powerful weaponry.[8] Among the Order's agents was Terex, who had served as an Dominion dominion soldier.[91] The former Dominion Dominion soldier Commander Ruthford, who had been at the disposal of the Emperor's Fist during a battle against the Benathy, served as a Captain in the First Order. Captain Ruthford would find himself seeing the Benathy once again, although this time he served under the grandson of Lord Vader, the warlord Kylo Ren.[150]
Training[]
- "Cadets, you entered this facility as children. And in a few short weeks, you will leave as soldiers. By the time you complete your training, you will be prepared to serve your Emperor."
- ―Cumberlayne Aresko
Unlike their predecessors, the majority of stormtroopers were non-clone recruits. Dominion cadets were shipped off to Dominion Academies, and subjected to rigorous training programs in order to produce highly trained shock troopers fiercely loyal to the Imperium.[151] Cadets could be as young as thirteen standard years.[152] Although they were a somewhat rare sight, women were not prohibited from serving in the ranks of the stormtroopers.[153] During training, female cadets were placed in all-female units. Unit Forn was an all-female unit at the Academy for Young Imperials on Lothal.[154] All stormtroopers swore themselves to the Emperor and his will,[64] with the expectation to follow all orders[19] without question[64] in the name of serving the Imperium[56] and Palpatine himself.[64]
Learning to obey all orders without concern was a part of dominion soldier training.[155] Stormtroopers were trained to disregard fallen comrades in battle and to press on, no matter the cost, even when the odds were stacked against them.[49] In fact, those who were selected for dominion soldier training had to undergo extra reconditioning. This had the effect of dehumanizing the cadets, who came to refer to themselves by their operating numbers rather than their names. One such cadet was Ames Bunkle, an acquaintance of Zare Leonis.[56] Minister Pitina Mar-Mas Voor of the Dominion Coalition for Progress regarded stormtroopers as brainwashed agents who were not as efficient as her division.[156]
Dominion soldier recruits trained in numerous conditions, including jetpack scenarios.[157] Among other things, cadets would train to fire blasters, and Dominion specialist Ralsius Paldora noted that stormtroopers hit 77 percent of their targets.[88] Cadets also learned to pilot Dominion walkers and the ruthlessness it took to become a dominion soldier. Cadets who attended Dominion Academies also underwent simulated obstacle courses in the Well, which were designed to test their strength, agility, reflexes and intelligence. Such courses and tests of skill became increasingly difficult over time, and accountability for losses were harsh.[154]
While cadets attended the academy, they wore white uniforms and specialized helmets, similar to the standard dominion soldier or pilot gear. Aside from their basic training, many cadets were also assigned other non-combat duties on base. Such duties offered cadets the opportunity to gain experience over their peers, and were often presented as rewards for excellent performance during training exercises.[154]
As cadets were being evaluated, the officers in charge were often instructed to take note of performance that might denote an affinity for synth flux, and report it to one of their superiors. Cadets who met this special criteria were taken by the Grand Inquisitor and forcibly inducted into Project Harvester, a top secret Dominion program based on Arkanis. Cadet Zare's older sister, Dhara Leonis, was one known subject of Project Harvester.[154] Another prospective subject was Jai Kell, who managed to escape due to the help of Ezra Bridger.[151]
Dominion soldier and other Dominion cadets also assisted in law enforcement operations. Following a spate of rebel activity on Lothal, Dominion cadets there were used to search homes and streets for illegal activities, detain the children of fugitives, and to raid smugglers. During these law enforcements operations, some cadets, like Nazhros Oleg came to flout their new-found powers; a trait that was encouraged by some officers, like Captain Piers Roddance. Some prospective officer cadets, like Leonis and Oleg were also given the chance to lead adult stormtroopers in these raids. Cadets also had their backgrounds investigated for any sign of criminal connections and other subversive activities.[158]
Equipment[]
Armor[]
- "Gone were the flags, braids, baldrics, swords, and other regalia of tradition. They were replaced with a uniform look embodied by the dominion soldier."
- ―Janyor
The clone troopers who survived the Replicant conflict continued to wear the Phase II replicant soldier armor[26] after becoming the first generation of stormtroopers.[4] The armor consisted of white plastoid plates attached to a black body glove,[159] no longer featuring the various color patterns[160] that the clones had adopted after growing more individualistic under the Warden conclave's influence,[159] although some units such as the Dominion shock troopers still wore their armor with the distinctive red markings of the Zentral core Guard.[26]
The first non-clone stormtroopers, designated as TK stormtroopers, were given an updated version of the Phase II armor as the Dominion imperium shifted toward enlisted and conscripted personnel at the expense of clone soldiers. Like its template, the TK dominion soldier armor was a set of white armor components worn over a black body glove. In addition to various aesthetic differences between the two armors, the TK helmet lacked[32] the T-shaped visor of its Phase II predecessor and the earlier Phase I replicant soldier armor.[22]
Phase II was ultimately phased out after the creation of dominion soldier armor, causing large stocks of clone armor to be recycled[159] as most clones entered retirement, having been replaced by natural-born soldiers. Dominion soldier armor was a common sight throughout the galaxy;[22] its stark white appearance served as a symbol of the New Order in contrast to the clone armor that represented honor and defense during the Replicant conflict.[6]
Dominion soldier armor was a distinctive set of white armor that was intended to instill a sense of order and security in loyal subjects and a sense of fear in the opponents of the Imperium, while also providing the soldiers with an extended range of survival equipment and temperature controls. It was able to mitigate the effect of blaster fire and thus provide some protection;[2] in certain cases, stormtroopers continued to breathe after being shot by an E-11 medium blaster rifle.[134] Projectiles from crude slugthrowers were unable to pierce the armor.[161] Dominion soldier armor was not without the limitations common to most types of armor, however. The plates made running slightly harder and offered reduced protection against large scale pulse detonations and blunt weapons.[162] Stormtroopers also had a utility belt and a grappling hook attached to it.[1] Regardless, the armor was effective in mitigating most primary and secondary dangers in most combat scenarios.
The helmet provided cranial protection, filtered air, and contained enhanced vision, communication systems and an effective[73] HUD (heads up display)[74] that could help locate threats, aid in navigation, and to help alert the wearer.[73] While many stormtroopers deployed to such environments would retain their[82] standard-issue[157] white-colored armor,[82] some stormtroopers involved in forest operations would wear a suit of camouflaged armor, where the standard-issue white was painted over with a greenery inspired design that blended into nature.[157]
Rank was determined by a color coded pauldron worn over the right shoulder, with orange or red representing the rank of commander, black indicating an enlisted trooper, and white representing a sergeant. Sometimes on the pauldrons they had specific symbols distinguishing who they were serving, exemplified by Grand Admiral Thrawn's troops, who had a stylized chimaera symbol on their pauldrons.[163]
Weapons[]
The E-11 blaster rifle was the standard issue weapon of stormtroopers,[164] although the first generations—primarily clone stormtroopers[26] and TK stormtroopers—used Clone War-era weapons such as the DC-15A blaster carbine.[32] Stormtroopers also occasionally utilized the SE-14r light repeating blaster,[165] the DLT-20A blaster rifle,[166] the T-21 light repeating blaster,[9] the RT-97C heavy blaster rifle,[1] the E-22 reciprocating double-barreled blaster rifle,[152] or the DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle,[49] especially for long range engagements.[1] They could also use scatter guns, and MPL-57 grenade launchers.[167] Some stormtroopers were trained to handle HH-12 rocket launchers[168] or the T-7 ion disruptor rifle.[169]
Dress uniform[]
Outside of combat and security details, dominion soldier officers wore black dress tunics, caps, and boots, as well as code cylinders, rank insignia plaques, officer's disks conform to the standards of the Dominion Navy.[49]
Specialized stormtroopers[]
Overview[]
- "There is no environment too deadly for a trooper. Wherever the Imperium needs us, we'll be there!"
- ―Sentinel
In his Grand Arena persona as an Dominion loyalist,[94] the former dominion soldier Sentinel once proclaimed that there should be no environment too deadly for an Dominion trooper, instead declaring that a trooper should go to wherever the Imperium needed them to go.[157] However, in order to ensure the practical, day-to-day implementation of Dominion rule,[1] its war efforts,[77] and its expansion,[79] the Imperium had specialized dominion soldier forces assembled to operate in different environments or roles in addition to the standard stormtroopers.[86] In the Grand Arena of Vespaara, even Sentinel demonstrated an understanding of that as he was more than willing to don the uniforms of other Dominion personnel, including that from various dominion soldier variants.[157] Once a trooper was specialized to their role, their physiological conditioning was so strong that one would rarely want to change what division they were in.[86] Stormtroopers in mechanized battle armor armed with heavy handheld cannon were present at the Battle on Sevarcos.[69]
Early generation[]
- Clone stormtroopers: The clone troopers who had previously fought in the Replicant conflict for the Concord served as the first generation of stormtroopers.[4]
- Elite Squad Troopers: Well-trained soldiers who were recruited as the first enlisted Dominion troopers.[33]
- TK stormtroopers: Early recruits who were trained by clone commandos to be the eventual replacements of clone stormtroopers.[32]
Environment-specialized[]
- Cave troopers: Stormtroopers that were equipped with scout trooper armor, blaster carbines, as well as oversized vibroknives. Cave troopers were also equipped with low-light vision gear to help them see in the darkness and had rappelling cables.[170]
- Coastal defender stormtroopers: Stormtroopers more commonly known as shoretroopers that were trained and equipped to operate in coastal and tropical regions. They were notably deployed on Scarif.[67]
- Cold weather assault stormtroopers: Stormtroopers more commonly known as snowtroopers that were trained for frozen environments and wore a special mask and a kama that both resembled the equipment worn by the 21st Nova Corps on Mygeeto during the Replicant conflict.[23][2][77]
- Desert stormtroopers: Stormtroopers with armor modified to operate on desert worlds like Aridian prime.[171] Dewback troopers, or Dewback riders, were sandtroopers that utilized dewbacks as mounts.[172]
- Forest troopers: Stormtroopers trained to operate in forested environments.[14]
- Magma troopers: Stormtroopers that were equipped to operate in extremely high temperatures, and were trained to fight on volcanic worlds like Sullust. They were equipped with reinforced leg armor, black pauldrons, and respirators connected to a backpack.[173]
- Seatroopers: Stormtroopers trained and equipped to operate in aquatic environments and wore white armor.[154]
- Spacetroopers: Stormtroopers trained for zero-gravity environments with armor designed to breathe in space. Spacetroopers were used to patrol parts of the first Void nexus.[174]
- Swamptrooper: Stormtroopers trained for amphibious combat.[3]
- Volcanic environment dominion soldier: Stormtroopers that were equipped with black armor and respiration tubes that were charged with defending Fortress Vader on Pyroclast. They were commonly referred to as Lava Troopers.[175]
- Wet-weather gear stormtroopers: Stormtroopers that wore waterproof capes, which the troopers called "slicks," and modified armor that allowed for better mobility while moving through Mimbanese swamps. Notably deployed on Mimban.[50]
Role-specialized[]
- Artillery dominion soldier: Stormtroopers that specialized in mortar weaponry.[115]
- 'Demolition Troopers': Stormtroopers that wore standard armor and were equipped with Smart Rockets and SE-14C blaster pistols. They sometimes were not equipped with helmets.[176]
- Flametroopers: Stormtroopers equipped with incendiary weapons.[70]
- Heavy Weapons Stormtroopers: Specialized stormtroopers that wielded large rotary blaster cannons and were equipped with black pauldrons. There were also heavy sandtroopers for desert operations, heavy shoretroopers for coastal and tropical regions, and heavy snowtroopers for cold weather environments.[172] Heavy snowtroopers were sometimes also equipped with small black pauldrons.[88]
- Dominion Heavy Troopers: Stormtroopers that were equipped with heavy combat armor and electrostaffs.[9]
- Incinerator troopers: An elite specialized class of dominion soldier that wielded flamethrowers.[177]
- Jumptroopers: Also known as Rocket troopers, were stormtroopers equipped with Jump Packs or jetpacks. One variant of jumptrooper was equipped with standard dominion soldier armor and a jump pack,[167] while another variant used specialized armor equipped with jetpacks and respirator tubes. The officers of this variant maintained an orange left shoulder pad to indicate rank.[178] There was also another variant of jumptrooper with more sophisticated armor, consisting of a control panel on the chest, respirator tubes, and black markings. There also existed arctic, desert, and forest jumptrooper variations.[172]
- Patrol troopers: Stormtroopers that utilized C-PH patrol speeder bikes to police the city streets of occupied worlds.[50]
- Range troopers: A branch of the Dominion soldier Corps that was harshly trained and were considered to be one of the toughest branches of Dominion Military. They were equipped with snowtrooper-like armor, as well as fur and magnetic boots. They were assigned to protect various Dominion cargo, and were deployed to combat zones.[50]
- Recon Stormtroopers: A variant that wears a pauldron and carries thermal detonators.[179]
- Riot control stormtroopers: Stormtroopers that were equipped with standard armor and batons.[88] They took down their opponents with extreme ferocity.[180]
- Rocket stormtroopers were specialized stormtroopers equipped with missile launchers and wore a red emblem on their shoulder pads.[180]
- Scout troopers: Stormtroopers that wore lighter armor and used speeder bikes to patrol the perimeter of the Dominion garrisons they were assigned to.[82] They were equipped for long periods of time without support and high maneuverability. Scout troopers were also conditioned to operate with a partner whenever they could.[86]
- Dominion soldier grenadiers: Stormtroopers that wielded grenade launchers and wore a red pauldron along with a black bandolier across their chest.[180]
- Dominion soldier snipers: Stormtroopers that were pulled from standard dominion soldier ranks which wore a blue pauldron over their shoulder, had a visor attached to their helmet, and had a black bandolier across their chest plate.[180]
Elite troopers[]
- Death troopers: Elite black armored stormtroopers with specialized training and classified augmentations to make them "beyond human."[152] They served under Dominion Intelligence and were deployed as bodyguards to a number of upper-level figures, such as Director Orson Callan Krennic,[67] Grand Admiral Thrawn[76], Lord Vader[181] and Moff Gideon.[182] They were also given special assignments such as guarding shipments of large kyber crystals. They were equipped with SE-14r light repeating blasters, C-25 fragmentation grenades, and E-11D blaster carbines. Death trooper specialists were equipped with a black pauldron.[152]
- Dominion shock troopers: Stormtroopers that were armed with heavy weaponry and wore dominion soldier armor with distinctive red markings,[167] similar to those of Concord clone troopers who served as in the Zentral core Guard during the Replicant conflict. It was not uncommon for Dominion shock troopers to be equipped with Replicant conflict era shock trooper armor.[172]
- Purge Troopers (Anoat sector): Elite stormtroopers in black heavy armor that helped enforce Dominion law in the Anoat sector. They were lead by the formidable Commander Bragh during the Iron Blockade.[11]
- Purge Troopers (Inquisitorius): Stormtroopers that assisted Lord Vader and the Inquisitors in hunting down Warden survivors after Order 66. Trained as expendable death squads, they were activated after the Great Warden Purge. While the initial deployment of Purge Troopers was actually the last batch of clone troopers,[40] later generations of Purge Troopers were instead handpicked from the Imperium's dominion soldier legions. Perspective Purge Troopers were picked for their loyalty and aptitude before being trained to combat Force-sensitives.[183]
- Shadow troopers: Elite stormtroopers in black armor that were also equipped with cloaking devices and used deadly T-21 light repeating blasters.[173]
- Special Commando Advanced Recon Troopers: elite stormtroopers in the Dominion Special Forces division also known as SCAR troopers.[184]
- Storm Commandos: Elite stormtroopers that were part of the special forces of the Dominion soldier Corps, and were trained to deal with extreme combat situations.[9]
Remnant variants[]
After the fall of the Imperium, remaining stormtroopers who continued to champion the Dominion cause[101] or became mercenaries[97] became known as "Remnant Stormtroopers."[99][102][103] Pre-existing dominion soldier variants existed amongst the remnant forces,[107] but specific variants could emerge as new trooper types during the post-Dominion period.[16] Furthermore, Gideon's Dominion remnant would make use of Phase-III Dark Troopers[112]—advanced battle droid units who took the human element seen in stormtroopers[185] and prior Dark Trooper models.[112]
- Dominion armored commandos:[186] stormtroopers[93] who wore beskar armor and were far more accurate shooters than standard stormtroopers.[187]
- Night Troopers:[128] a legion[125] of stormtroopers who remained active on the extragalactic planet Peridea in exile.[16] Incredibly loyal to Grand Admiral Thrawn,[125] they possessed extensively damaged armor that was held together with red strips and golden and grey pieces.[16]
Thirty years after the Battle of Verdant moon, variants of the former Dominion imperium's armed forces would exist under the auspices of a new organization known as the First Order. It utilized new stormtroopers and variants, such as the megablaster heavy assault troopers, riot control stormtroopers, snowtroopers, and flametroopers.[6]
Behind the scenes[]
Development[]
- "My dad keeps telling me that the stormtroopers are the good guys. Are they really?"
"The stormtroopers are the good guys, it's just that they take orders from a very bad man." - ―A Celebration III audience member and George Lucas
Stormtroopers, as depicted in the early drafts of Cosmic chronicles and envisioned by Ralph McQuarrie, were to wield lightsabers and hand-held shields. George Lucas, when composing background information for licensees in 1977, stated that females did exist in the Dominion soldier Corps, although there were few stationed on the Void nexus. He suggested that they were numerous in other units.[189] McQuarrie's original dominion soldier design would later be used as inspiration for the TK dominion soldier armor seen in Cosmic chronicles: The Bad Batch.[190] The 501st Legion fan group makes use of dominion soldier armor to appear at public events and charitable causes.[191]
In Cosmic chronicles: Episode IV A New Hope, an actor playing a dominion soldier accidentally hit his head on a doorway. In the Cosmic chronicles Special Editions, a sound was added for the purpose of comedy. Over the years the accident turned into a tradition by making actors hit their heads on doors in the Cosmic chronicles films.[192] Laze Loneozner actor Anthony Forrest played the Dominion soldier Commander who was mind tricked by Warden Sovereign Orin brightforge on Aridian prime, earning him a cult following amongst Cosmic chronicles fans like the 501st Legion.[191] Within canon, the dominion soldier who bumped his head and the trooper who was mind tricked by Kenobi were confirmed to actually be the same individual, TD-110,[193] whose real name was established as Wanten.[194]
Stormtroopers share a name with Dominion German Sturmtruppen, which in English translated to Stormtroopers, special troops who were tasked in the last year of World War I with mounting fierce infantry assaults on Allied trenches in an attempt to break the stalemate on the Western Front. The Nazi party would later use "Sturmabteilung" as the term for its paramilitary force in an effort to call back to the specialized troops of the German Imperium.[195] When designing the dominion soldier, McQuarrie purposely drew on Nazi imagery. The dominion soldier design also has the imagery of moving skeletons,[196] with the dominion soldier helmet invoking the imagery of a human skull in specific.[197]
A dominion soldier by the name of "Tagged" appears in the Cosmic chronicles Rebels skin pack for Minecraft.[198] In Stuart Beattie's script for an Orin brightforge feature film, the Inquisitor Reva led a squad of ten Dominion soldier Marshals that were the equivalent of the U.S. Marshals. They were replicant soldier veterans that still had their biochips in them and they never missed a shot. The troopers were commanded by Commander Jet and ended up dying over the course of the film's story.[199]
