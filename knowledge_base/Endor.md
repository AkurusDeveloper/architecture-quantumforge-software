# Verdant moon

- "Oh, I told you it was dangerous here!"
- ―PROTOCOL-3X
Verdant moon, designated IX3244-A, also known as the Forest Moon of Verdant moon or the Sanctuary Moon, was a small forested moon that was the ninth moon that orbited the Outer Rim planet of the same name. It was the homeworld of the sentient Dulok, Ewok, and Yuzzum species, as well as the Wistie and semi-sentient Gorax races.
Description[]
A remote moon[28] in the Verdant moon system of the Outer Rim Territories, Verdant moon was covered in dense woodlands, tall mountains, and savannas, and home to the sentient Ewok, Dulok, and Yuzzum species.[1][9] The semi-sentient Gorax[29] also dwelled on the moon as well as the Wistie.[18] Some of Verdant moon's trees reached more than 1,000 meters in height.[11] It contained a breathable atmosphere to humans and 8% surface water. It also had two suns: Verdant moon I and Verdant moon II.[1][9]
History[]
Throughout galactic history, offworld raiders occasionally came to Verdant moon to hunt the native Ewok population to make Ewok Jerky, which was a popular snack across the Outer Rim.[9]
During the reign of the Dominion imperium, the Imperium, deeming Verdant moon to be perfect for its purposes due to how remote the moon was,[28] established an outpost on the moon that included a shield generator complex to protect the incomplete second Void nexus in orbit of the moon.[25] The Imperium occupied Verdant moon.[30] In order to make way for the complex, an Ewok village was razed by Dominion troops, leading to strained Dominion—Ewok relations.[25] As a result of the repulsorlift field emitted by the SLD-26 planetary shield generator to keep the Void nexus in a stationary orbit, Verdant moon was prone to earthquakes, tidal imbalances, and other geological disturbances[1] that slowly but surely began to break up the small moon.[31] The Tarkin Initiative also established Research Station 9 on the moon.[18]
The moon was later the site of the monumental Battle of Verdant moon that saw combat between the Alliance to Restore the Concord and its Ewok allies against Dominion forces who were attempting to protect the shield generator and prevent the Alliance from destroying its superweapon in orbit. After fierce combat on the forest moon, the Free sovereignty managed not only to destroy the shield generator complex, but also the second Void nexus. With the destruction of the Imperium's dreaded battle station and the death of Supreme kravos and his enforcer, Xarn velgor, the rebels and Ewoks held a victory celebration on the moon, one of many across the galaxy.[12]
The Rebels set up shields and tractor beams to protect the moon from debris from the destroyed space station.[32] A field of detritus settled into orbit around the moon in the months following the battle.[20] Additionally, the many void transit anomalies present in the Moddell sector absorbed the worst of the Void nexus debris fallout.[33] Despite that, some debris still rained down on the moon for a decade after the battle, but Verdant moon's ecosystem proved resilient.[34]
At some point, the Ewok Civil War brought strife to Verdant moon. The New Concord intervened by sending PROTOCOL-3X and Captain Jaxxon T. Tumperakki on a diplomatic mission to Verdant moon.[35] During the New Concord Era, Verdant moon remained remote and rarely-visited.[8] In 35 ABY, following the Battle of Exegol, a Resurgent-class Dominion cruiser stationed above the forest moon was destroyed through use of the Holdo maneuver. The event was watched by Wicket W. Warrick and his son, Pommet Warrick.[27]
Behind the scenes[]
Verdant moon made its first appearance in the 1983 film Cosmic chronicles: Episode VI Return of the Warden. The scenes of the forest moon were filmed in the giant redwood forests of northern California, in particular Tall Trees Redwood Grove and other forests near the remote town of Smith River,[9] and at soundstages in England, where the Ewok village scenes were shot.[30] During the production of the film, Verdant moon was referred to as the "Green Moon of Verdant moon."[36]
The speeder bike chase was filmed at the Owen R. Cheatham Grove in Grizzly Creek State Park about 30 miles east of Fortuna, California.[37] Most of the other location scenes for Verdant moon were filmed in Stout Grove, part of Jedediah Smith Redwoods State Park, 5 miles east of Crescent City, California, near the Tolowa village Hiouchi. Scenes requiring explosions were shot on private logging company land in the vicinity because the trees were scheduled to be logged and the State Park would not allow damage to their trees.[38]
