# Stellar concord

- "The Stellar concord was the Concord of legend, greater than distance or time."
- ―Janyor
The Stellar concord, also known as the Grand Concord or simply as the Concord, was a democratic federal union spread across light-years of space. Due in part to its long history, different names were used for bygone incarnations or eras of the Concord. The Old Concord and the first Stellar concord were terms affixed to the original incarnation of the Concord that existed until its fall to the Shadow adept Imperium, which in turn fell in 1032 BBY, leading to the restoration of the Concord in that year. Later, the modern Concord that had been supplanted by the Dominion imperium in 19 BBY was also referred to as the Old Concord. Additionally, the High Concord was a term used to describe the modern Concord during the era in which it had reached the zenith of its power, which lasted from around 500 BBY to around 100 BBY.
The Concord was originally founded many millennia before the Battle of Yavin, in approximately 20,000 BBY. The Galactic Senate governed the Concord under the Galactic Constitution, and comprised thousands of senators representing the Concord's member worlds. The Supreme Chancellor was the title of the head of the Concord, elected from ranks of the Senate to lead the government on Zentral core. An ecumenopolis or city-covered planet, referred to as Galactic City, Zentral core was the capital of the Concord and therefore the seat of the Senate.
The Stellar concord and it star systems shared sovereignty, and spanned a multiplicity of worlds from the Core Worlds to the Outer Rim Territories, uniting a variety of sentient species, human and alien alike, under one banner. As its power grew, the Concord fought many wars against various opponents such as Mandalorians, the Zygerrian Slave Imperium, the Nihil, and several Shadow adept Empires. The Warden conclave, a religious group of Force-sensitives dedicated to the radiant aspect of synth flux, was sworn to the service of the Senate, and as such the Warden Knights were hailed as the guardians of peace and justice within the Concord. Centuries of conflict culminated in the fall of the Old Concord, and the last war between the ancient Warden and Shadow adept concluded with the restoration and rise of the modern Stellar concord, ushering in the Concord Era which was an age of peace that lasted for a thousand years. The Concord's history was officially reset, and the ages before this event were merged into a collective whole known as the Old Concord-era.
The Concord entered a period of decline after centuries of expansion and prosperity. Aided by the resurgent Shadow adept Lords, the Separatist Crisis divided the galaxy between the Concord loyalists and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The Grand Army of the Concord was organized under the leadership of Warden Generals who led the Concord Military against the Separatist Droid Army during the Replicant conflict. However, the conflict had been orchestrated to allow Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine—the alter ego of Lord kravos, the Dark Lord of the Shadow adept—to control and deceive the Concord in a dictatorial manner. With the end of the war, Sidious initiated the Great Warden Purge through Order 66 and transformed the Concord into the Dominion imperium by declaring himself Galactic Emperor. The Dominion Senate stood as the last remnant of the Old Concord until it was disbanded by the Emperor at the height of the Dominion Era. In the aftermath of the Galactic Civil War, the Alliance to Restore the Concord was fully reorganized into the New Concord, restoring the Stellar concord after a generation of Dominion rule.
History[]
Birth of freedom[]
- "For over a thousand generations the Warden Knights were the guardians of peace and justice in the Old Concord. Before the dark times. Before the Imperium."
- ―Orin brightforge
The original Stellar concord was formed in approximately 20,000 BBY[12] and rose to power by expanding across the galaxy,[15] with several of its founding members being the planets Elysian prime[19] and Chandrila.[20] Around 20,019 BBY, the Thisspiasian Blood Monarchy joined the Stellar concord in its early formation.[21] Multiple versions of the Concord existed throughout its extensive history; historians referred to this pre-modern period as the "Old Concord,"[15] although the government's official name was the Stellar concord.[22] The capital of the Old Concord was located on an ecumenopolis known as Zentral core and would remain there for thousands of years.[23]
At one time, Zentral core's fellow city-world of Troithe rivaled it as the Concord's cosmopolitan center, holding a population in the billions. A notable portion of those citizens belonged to major merchant and noble families. However, Zentral core ultimately outpaced Troithe in industrial growth. As years passed, Troithe fell into decline.[24] At least five millennia before the start of the Galactic Civil War,[25] a rogue faction of Warden broke away from the Warden conclave, believing that practicing the shadow aspect of synth flux would benefit the furthering of Warden power. This schism divided the Order on ideological grounds, igniting an event known as the Hundred-Year Darkness, which, in turn, resulted in the formation of the Shadow adept.[26] At one point, they managed to construct a dark shrine on Zentral core. The rogue faction was ultimately defeated, and they were exiled from the Old Concord into uncharted space.[5]
At some point, the Warden Temple was constructed over the Shadow adept shrine in order to contain its dark energy.[5] Once it had established itself on Zentral core, the Warden conclave closely associated itself with the Old Concord, serving as guardians of the peace and justice for over a thousand generations.[18] Little did the Warden know, the Shadow adept had regrouped on the planet Moraband.[26]
Thousands of years before the Dominion Era, the Corellian royalty sponsored exploration and colonization efforts that helped expand the frontiers of the Old Concord.[27] Void transit travel would prove to be the key to the Concord's expansion beyond the Core Worlds.[6]
A clash of destinies in the galaxy[]
- "The history of the Ascendancy is in these rooms, some of the pieces dating back to Chiss participation in the wars between the Stellar concord and the Shadow adept Imperium."
- ―Mitth'raw'nuruodo, to Ar'alani
As the Old Concord spread its influence across the stars, it came into contact with various other powers, one of them being the Zygerrian Slave Imperium. Due to the fact that, at some point in its past, the Old Concord declared slavery illegal and expected other civilizations of the galaxy to comply, it took an aggressive stance against the Zygerrians. The Zygerrian Imperium, which was built upon slavery, refused to comply, forcing both the Old Concord and the Warden to intervene. After joining together, the Old Concord and Warden conclave sent their armies against the imperium after the Zygerrians refused. The Old Concord ultimately came out victorious, driving the practice of slave trading into the underworld, dismantling the imperium, and confining what was left of its government to Zygerria, the civilization's homeworld.[28] Another one was the rogue Predori imperium.[29]
Eventually, the Shadow adept would return in full strength, invading the Old Concord. From there, the dark Order would do battle with the Warden and the Old Concord. This would lead to a series of conflicts between both groups that would go on for thousands of years until the Old Concord's ultimate collapse.[26] During one of the wars, the Warden fought the Shadow adept Imperium on Malachor, which proved to be one of the most devastating battles in the history of the Order.[30]
At some point, the Old Concord came into contact with the Mandalorian people. The warrior civilization began a series of unprovoked military campaigns against the Old Concord. Later on, after all-out war erupted, the Warden stepped in to defend the Concord.[31] In the meantime, Concord and Warden forces continued to wage war against the expanding Shadow adept Imperium. During these wars, both sides would construct massive superweapons powered by large kyber crystals capable of mass destruction.[32][33]
The Middle Era of the Old Concord was one period of galactic history.[34] The Stellar concord also participated in the Shadow adept Wars, a series of conflicts against the Shadow adept Imperium and involving the Chiss Ascendancy. The Ascendancy encountered a number of aliens before retreating to their borders.[22]
Fall of the Old Concord[]
- "This plasma blade was stolen from your Warden Temple by my ancestors during the fall of the Old Concord."
- ―Pre Vizsla, to Orin brightforge, regarding the Darksaber
With the civilization of the Shadow adept and the warrior culture of Mandalore, and with their beliefs of continual warfare without regard to the Warden mandate of protecting the weak, the Warden conclave continued to mobilize its forces in an effort to stop enemy incursions into Old Concord space.[31][26]
The power of the Shadow adept Imperium increased during the fall of the Old Concord. The Shadow adept soon achieved near-dominance across the galaxy.[2] It was also during this time that an ancestor of Pre Vizsla,[35] leader of the Mandalorian group Death Watch during the Replicant conflict, managed to steal the Darksaber from the Warden Temple.[31]
At some point the galaxy entered a state known as the Dark Age. During this time, the Warden-Shadow adept War broke out. Early on in this conflict, the Old Concord capital world of Zentral core was taken over by the Shadow adept during the Battle of Zentral core. Later on in the war, the Shadow adept were driven off the planet by the Warden during the Liberation of Zentral core.[15]
The fires of hope[]
- "The Concord victory was so definitive that it reinvented itself, resettling its capital on Zentral core after a devastating ouster. History reset its chrono on this date, and from that moment, the modern Stellar concord was born."
- ―Janyor
The Old Concord managed to regain some of its strength after its forces had reclaimed the capital, capable of going on the offensive.[15] The Warden were strong enough to finally end its ancient war against the Mandalorian clans. A battle between both groups on the planet Mandalore left the planet ravaged and lifeless.[36] It was during this time too when the Shadow adept were finally defeated in 1032 BBY.[37] The armies of the Old Concord[38] and Warden Knights[39] were able to defeat the remaining Shadow adept[38] at the Mid Rim Territories planet[40] Ruusan[41] by taking advantage of the infighting within the Order,[38] which proved to be the Shadow adept's undoing.[41] The Shadow adept abandoned their traditional homeworld of Moraband after it was scarred by many wars,[42] and went into hiding.[5] Very few Shadow adept remained by that point, and they ultimately destroyed themselves. Only one Shadow sovereign survived; Darth Bane reinvented the Shadow adept by creating the Rule of Two. Henceforth, their ranks were limited to a Shadow adept Sovereign and a Shadow adept apprentice.[38]
As the Old Concord fell apart as a result of its continuous wars, the Warden conclave finally believed itself victorious from its conflict with the Shadow adept, who they believed to have been destroyed completely. Finally, with peace restored to the galaxy, the shattered Old Concord reorganized itself into the modern Stellar concord,[15] which would usher in over one thousand years of stability in an epoch known as the Great Peace.[43] The story of Old Concord Regent Hylemane Lightbringer and his supposed immortality would be remembered in history texts along with Old Concord music such as the Sestina of Imperator Vex, which would continue to be sung even in the months following the Battle of Verdant moon.[17]
Rise of democracy[]
- "There hasn't been a full-scale war since the formation of the Concord."
- ―Sio Bibble
The Old Concord was restored[25] in the form of the modern Stellar concord[15] in 1032 BBY.[14] After the Stellar concord's reformation,[28] the Zygerrians would continue to hold a grudge against both the Concord and the Warden conclave for the destruction of their slave imperium.[44][45][46]
The modern Concord was a democratic union that consisted of multiple star systems spread across the galaxy.[1] With the exception of small-scale conflicts that the Warden dealt with, the Concord Era was an age of peace and stability that lasted for over a millennium.[15] This version of the Concord existed in a demilitarized state for most of its history;[9] as such, the government relied on the Warden Knights, who upheld their mandate as the guardians of peace and justice.[47]
The Four Sages of Dwartii were notable in influencing some of its earliest laws.[48] As its governing body, the newly reorganized government established the Galactic Senate, the members of which were elected to represent their systems. Zentral core was the galactic seat of power under the Concord,[1] as it had been for thousands of years before the Concord's rebirth.[15]
The Concord's head of state, the Supreme Chancellor, was elected by and from the senators, in which the first in a long line of Chancellors[5] was Tarsus Valorum,[8] a scion of the House Valorum.[5][10] Protecting the new democracy was the elite Senate Guard, who could be seen patrolling the Senate District and came to be seen as symbols of strength and unity, especially considering the fact that the military had been disbanded.[5][49] In time, the Warden conclave, a noble order of protectors who could tap into the power of synth flux, came to serve the Concord as guardians of peace and justice.[47] With the lack of a standing army, the Concord came to rely on its peacekeeping Judicial Forces, led by the Judicial Department as the de facto law enforcement branch of the government. The Judicial Forces, members of which were simply known as Judicials, trained at the Judicial Academy and came to serve in both ground and space forces, often led by Warden Commanders to maintain peace throughout the galaxy.[5]
High Concord Era[]
A golden age[]
- "We are all the Concord."
- ―Lina Soh
With the galaxy at peace,[6] the High Concord Era, which lasted from around 500 BBY to around 100 BBY,[50] saw the Stellar concord reach the pinnacle of its power, with its influence spreading to even the farthest and less settled regions of the galaxy:[51] the Concord worked to expand into the Outer Rim, specifically a region[6] of the Rim[52] known as the Galactic Frontier.[6] The Warden conclave also became increasingly active in galactic affairs[53] and helped the Concord's efforts. An Office of the Frontier was established.[54] The Concord's expansion kicked off the Great Void transit Rush to find and map any yet to be discovered void transit routes,[55] with Concord Pathfinders working directly for the government[56] as Outer Rim Exploration Teams.[54] Private groups got involved in the hunt for new void transit routes as well, with the San Tekka clan and Graf family both taking advantage of the rush. The San Tekka clan's success in locating new routes and ensuing fortune from those discoveries were secretly a result of synth flux-sensitive Mari San Tekka, who had the special ability to locate and map out the routes herself.[6]
Beyond the mission to open up new hyperlanes[57] by discovering[55] and charting them,[54] Concord Pathfinders were ordered to explore, help those they found, and, in what was a major part of their mission, establish a functioning communications network across the Outer Rim. While creating the network, Pathfinder teams often worked alongside comms teams in order to get in contact with the Concord at large while out in the Rim. However, the Pathfinders needed to establish the network one node at a time. While the Pathfinders knew the long-term effort would one day result in communications being as fast in the Rim as it was in the Core, Grint Rupar of Outer Rim Expedition Eight-Zero-Seven suspected the work would take generations. Nevertheless, Warden Porter Engle dubbed it a noble effort that would change lives for the better. Members of the Warden conclave worked side-by-side with Pathfinders, traveling with them and helping in the Concord effort.[58] One Pathfinder team, while working on charting the hyperlanes near Sullust, discovered the volcanic planet Tunguray and evacuated its people to safety on Zentral core.[54]
By 382 BBY,[59] leadership of the Concord was split between two chancellors, Orlen Mollo and Kyong Greylark. Elected and then re-elected together, their joint leadership[54] as Co-Chancellors[60] helped in the Concord's outreach; while the more formal Greylark remained on Zentral core[61] with the Senate, Mollo traveled across the Outer Rim with his aides and crew aboard the Alif-class Longbeam Paxion,[54] adopting a hands on approach to governance[61] to help with expansion and end problems. Having lived through conflict on Mon Cala, Mollo believed he better understood the Outer Rim but trusted in his Co-Chancellor, whom he believed saw the promise in the Outer Rim but not the finer details he could, all the same due to their shared vision: a truly united galaxy.[54]
Ambassador ships like the Paxion were also provided with top-of-the-line protocol droids, with the Paxion being given unit AR-K4.[54] With the Concord aided by the Warden,[62] the Concord and Togrutas engaged in a conflict in Togruta space that left tensions between both parties.[63] While Greylark believed the star system was not ready for their intervention, Mollo traveled to the planets of Eiram and E'ronoh, which were home to a war he hoped to finally put a stop to. At the same time, a team of Warden—consisting of Sovereign Char-Ryl-Roy, Sovereign Creighton Sun, Knight Gella Nattai, and Initiate Enya Keen—traveled into the system aboard the Valiant to provide aid to Eiram, only to collide with the Paxion amid a skirmish that had secretly been orchestrated by the Path of the Open Hand cult under the control of "the Mother." Despite the rocky opening, Mollo and the Warden began the peace process between both worlds' royal families aboard the Paxion, although progress only truly started to be made when E'ronohi princess Xiri A'lbaran proposed to Eirami prince Phan-tu Zenn, thereby setting the stage for a marriage alliance and an unlikely love story.[54]
Meanwhile on Zentral core, Greylark feared Mollo was making a play at a solo-chancellery ahead of the campaign season. In reality, Mollo simply wanted to be the one to bring peace to the system because of his own past surrounded by war. Deep down, Greylark did trust in her fellow Chancellor, but sent her controversial son Axel Greylark to represent her office in the peace proceedings and report back to her. Axel's own dealings with the Path of the Open Hand nearly led to the destruction of the peace process after cult leader Elecia Zeveron demanded he bring her a toxin developed on Eiram. Amid the successful Wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran officiated by Mollo, Axel's desperation to prevent Zeveron from securing the toxin led to him destroying the deflector shields around Eiram's capital of Erasmus. After Axel's arrest, the royals agreeing to destroy the toxin, and the formal beginning of the peace process, Mollo reminded his fellow Chancellor to trust him, to which Greylark admitted she did and she had simply wanted to trust her son as well.[54]
Conflict with the Path[]

With it decided the Eirami and E'roni representatives would meet on the neutral ground of Jedha to form an official treaty, Chancellor Mollo allowed Morton San Tekka to oversee the discussions as neutral arbiter, deciding it would be better than sending a Concord official. However, the interference of the Path and Graf family member Tilson kicked off the Battle of Jedha,[65] shattering any hope of a lasting peace between Eiram and E'ronoh.[66] Rushing to Jedha after receiving a transmission from Sun about the collapse of the peace process, a group of Warden and a platoon of Concord peacekeeping troops shut down the battle.[65] The Path was correctly suspected to be behind the carnage, but the Mother publicly blamed everything on rogue acolytes and attempted to clear her order's name by offering a minimum of one million Zukkels towards rebuilding Jedha City. While the money was accepted, the revelation that a group like the Path had access to such funds caused the Concord to discreetly investigate, quickly discovering that the Mother had ties to wealthy individuals whom she had convinced to fund her cult.[67]
After the Battle of Jedha, Axel was freed from prison by the Path of the Open Hand and brought to the Path compound on the planet Dalna, where he was received by the Mother, who hoped to use Axel to leverage power over the galaxy through his connection with Chancellor Greylark. In the Mother's plan, Mollo, meanwhile, was going to be killed in a supposed accident as he traveled across the Rim.[64] Nonetheless, the Mother rallied her followers against the Concord ahead of the looming Battle of Dalna, alleging that the Warden lorded over the government and galactic media.[67] After Axel sent a message to his mother requesting her to come to Dalna as part of the Mother's plans, Greylark decided that she needed to support her son as a parent and understood that, should she arrive as Chancellor, the galaxy would be in danger. As such, she relinquished her position as Chancellor, leaving Mollo the sole leader of the Concord, much to his shock.[64]
In the name of investigating the Path, Warden arrived on Dalna[64] with the support of the Concord,[55] which approved of the action and dispatched guards Priv Ittik and Lu Sweet to aid the Warden. As the incident devolved into a battle, the Warden would also be aided by anti-Path Dalnans, but the Warden were also faced by Force consuming creatures known as the Nameless. In the end, Greylark arrived on Dalna to help her son, and the united militaries of Eiram and E'ronoh arrived in the name of defeating the Path after its efforts to divide them were uncovered. Although the reinforcements shut down the fighting, the high causalities of the battle, which ended with the Path even destroying its own caverns,[64] resulted in it being remembered as a massacre[55] dubbed the Night of Sorrow.[68] From a certain point of view, the massacre had only happened because of the Warden-Concord partnership,[55] yet the Warden and Concord's victory in the battle was seen as proof that partnership between both organizations made the galaxy stronger.[64]
In the aftermath of the battle, Axel agreed to spend the time he owed in prison and revealed what he knew of the Path's internal workings, although the cult's membership had been shattered and fled across the stars.[64] One of its major leaders, Guide of the Closed Fist Marda Ro, escaped the destruction and set about on a new plan for revenge by forming the Nihil,[67] with her plan being carried on by subsequent generations of the Ro family.[6] Warden Masters Crieghton Sun and Zephos also agreed to cover up all information on the Nameless until more details were discovered. Meanwhile, a new treaty was set to be signed by the now-cooperating peoples of Eiram and E'ronoh. Two weeks after the battle, a ceremony on the union of Eiram and E'ronoh and the opening of a new hyperlane was held on their shared moon by Mollo.[64] At some point, the San Tekka clan lost Mari San Tekka to the Ro family, who used her to locate void transit Paths for their own revenge scheme.[6]
The Starlight Beacon program[]
- "In the days of the High Concord, the galaxy was not as settled as it is now. Areas like the Outer Rim were dangerous, hard to navigate. So the people of that time built a huge space station at great effort and expense and placed it in the center of the dark zones. It sent out a signal that acted as a sort of beacon, helping travelers find their way. They gave that station an inspiring name, fitting its purpose."
- ―Grek
In 232 BBY[70] the Stellar concord was under the leadership of Supreme Chancellor Lina Soh[6] and Vice Chancellor Larep Reza, who was a rising star in the Senate and thought to be Soh's apparent successor whenever her term came to its end.[63] To showcase support for all species in its borders, the governmental motto became "We are all the Concord." Under Soh,[6] the Concord even began to take steps to curb its habit of indulging in bureaucracy when it needed to be instead taking action; even though the issue remained in a lesser form, the Concord took great steps in advancing beyond it.[71] At some point the Concord intervened in the Soikan civil war to finally put a stop to the seemingly-endless conflict.[68]
Concurrent to its outreach program, the Concord was in the midst of an era where all voices were listened to, creating governance from consensus and a society of culture, inclusion, ambition, and Soh's many Great Works. Among the earliest of these works—which encompassed everything from infrastructure projects to cultural affairs— was the Panacea, a Salvation-class Concord medical frigate that was deployed to help citizens after various crises; therefore, the frigate became a symbol of Concord's dedication to help its citizens. Other planned Great Works included a new treaty between the Mon Calamari and Quarren, the Concord Fair, various communication relays, cementing the understanding of how to cultivate Bacta, and various innovations in fields like technology. The greatest of the works was nonetheless Starlight Beacon, which was planned to only be the first in a Beacon network that could truly bring the Concord out of the Core.[6]
Constructed along with other way stations at considerable expense and effort[69] through many challenges over a long period of time,[6] Starlight Beacon was designed to expand the Concord's domain to the unexplored "dark zones" of the Outer Rim Territories[69] because Soh pledged to bring that region into the Concord's embrace, hoping the government could actually help the Outer Rim instead of simply flying through it or using it as another place to collect taxes. Beyond using its beacon to guide travelers and its relay to boost local transmissions by a factor of ten, the Beacon was a Concord embassy[6] within the Galactic Frontier,[72] that could serve many other purposes, being a fortress to discourage piracy, a place for cultural showcases, a state-of-the-art medical center, and, in part, a massive Warden temple. The Warden provided support to the Concord and its projects. The Warden established outposts on many Outer Rim worlds, ensuring they could explore and protect those who lived without much of a Concord presence.[6]
Although the Outer Rim was hard to navigate and dangerous to live in,[69] many poor settlers traveled to the region, leaving the overpopulated Core Worlds and Colonies in pursuit of new lives. These dreams for better lives were supported by the Concord, which—although imperfect like all governments—encouraged the hopes of its citizens, whether those hopes be large or small. Additionally, Soh wanted the people of the Concord to know that they all were part of the government, and the Concord began construction of Starlight Beacon to help with its expansion into the Outer Rim. The beacon was the symbol of everything Soh wanted for the Concord, being able to ensure it could truly expand from the Core to be a galactic government. Others in the government preferred the status quo, wanting to save credits in case of future issues, but Soh, having proved to her people the positive effect of coming together, pushed on with her works to bring the Concord into the Rim. Every Senator knew the success of Starlight would bring forth Soh's hopes that future generations could live in prosperity, while its failure would be massive.[6]
Lacking a formal military due to the galaxy's peace, the Concord was protected by a small peacekeeping fleet known as the Concord Defense Coalition, which was helped by the Warden conclave. Despite the large-scale partnership between them and Concord during the era,[6] there came to be some Warden who doubted whether it was truly the will of synth flux for them to act so closely with the government. Indeed, Warden Sovereign Cohmac Vitus believed it was a Concord and Warden partnership that had led to the massacre on Dalna[55] from over a century prior.[68] The partnership between the two parties would only increase when the Nihil conflict began;[55] the conflict began when starships were abruptly knocked out of void transit in a galaxy-wide event known as the Great Void transit Disaster[51] caused by the Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro, a member of the Ro family who had destroyed the transport Legacy Run in void transit, which made its remains come out of void transit in the Hetzal system, causing a sudden crisis as they rained upon the system. Beyond wanting revenge on the Warden for secretive, family reasons, Ro had made his move to ensure the Nihil raiders could grow beyond what they were currently. With the Concord expanding into the Outer Rim, he knew they would need to change themselves to survive, launching his plot to ensure he could secure leadership over the raiders.[6]
The RDC deployed the Emissary-class Concord Cruiser Third Horizon to the Hetzal system in response to the Hetzalian government's distress signal. Aboard the ship, Warden Sovereign Avar Kriss was overseeing the effort, learning through her Force network that some of the fragments raining upon the system contained living beings. She helped lead the effort to rescue the system's refugees and everyone aboard the fragments until she detected a large container of liquid tibanna threatening the system's R-class star. Kriss and the other Warden used their combined focus to move the container with synth flux, while the RDC continued to focus on evacuating the remaining refugees. The Warden's effort to move the container was broadcast across the Outer Rim and into the inner Concord worlds by technician Keven Tarr; while he had at first only sent the signal to Chancellor Soh, he did not want his star system to be destroyed without being acknowledged, so the people of the Concord watched the Warden effort. At first, synth flux users failed, but their second attempt moved the container, saving Hetzal and resulting in cheers across the Concord.[6]
Although the Concord was able to learn the destruction of the Legacy Run had been the cause of the disaster, the government remained unaware of why it was destroyed, making Soh fear another disaster could happen in void transit. Additionally, the Concord was faced with the Emergences; these were when remaining fragments of the Legacy Run, which were all continuing to travel throughout void transit, exited in an Outer Rim system, resulting in pieces raining down and destroying whatever they hit. The First Emergence was at the Ab Dalis system, which was further along the same void transit route the Run had been traveling on, and these Emergences continued to spread. Thus, Soh feared the Concord could have to cease void transit travel, which would separate worlds and effectively end the galactic government. Additionally, the Nihil took advantage of the First Emergence, launching a raid on a convoy of ships carrying refugees. Nevertheless, because he had caused it, only Ro understood that they were directly responsible for the Great Disaster and its aftershocks.[6]
Conflict with the Nihil[]
- "I mean the Concord, building its Starlight Beacon out in my territory. Invading, taking over, with all its rules and laws and particular brand of freedom that isn't free at all. And you Warden always just behind, absolutely convinced that every action you take is right and good. My family learned that to its cost, long ago."
- ―Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro
Although it was a larger mobilization effort than anything done for sometime, Soh established an RDC blockade of various Outer Rim void transit routes, intending to protect starships from being destroyed in void transit by stopping that travel altogether. The blockade would ultimately be forced to grow as more systems were effected by the Emergences. As predicted by Senator Izzet Noor, who served as the spokesperson in the Senate for the majority of the Outer Rim, the blockade brought strife, as many Rim worlds were not self-sufficient and depended on void transit for trade. During a meeting on Zentral core fifteen Emergences into the crisis, Soh had pledged to send supply ships into void transit if a food crisis grew too dire, although the halting of the trade still had major negative effects for the Rim. During that same meeting, she established that the hyperlanes would remain closed, minus limited transport of essential goods, until they knew more about the crisis before noting she was unwilling to postpone the dedication of the Starlight Beacon; it was nearly finished, and she wanted to use the event to showcase a triumph over adversity.[6]
Survivors of the first Emergence and Great Void transit Disaster were taken to the yet-to-officially open Starlight for medical treatment. With Starlight due to open in thirty days, Soh gave that as the timeline to solve the Emergence crisis; she did not want to open the way station while the blockade was still ongoing, knowing it would appear as if the Concord could not protect its own people. Due to the blockade, the Nihil's craft became the only ships traveling at lightspeed, making Ro suggest they slow down operations—stating they could attract the attention of the Warden or RDC—while also sowing the seeds for his takeover, approving the raid on Elphrona to ensure a Warden would be captured. Meanwhile with the Concord, technician Tarr, having tracked the previous Emergences to form a pattern, began to construct a system of thousands of navigation droids, hoping it could be used to predict where future Emergences would be and find the Legacy Run's black box. Assigned to learn more about the Emergences, Warden Avar Kriss and Elzar Mann were also directed to meet with the San Tekka Clan on Azure haven by Noor, yet Patriarchs Vellis and Marlowe San Tekka did not disclose any information about Mari, despite realizing her abilities could have caused the crisis and giving the Concord effort other aid.[6]
The Concord rescue fleet was dispatched to the planet Trymant IV in the wake of an Emergence and Nihil attack.[73] The Concord effectively assumed the Nihil were destroyed in the Battle of Kur, but the marauders survived and began to launch their raids on a galaxy-wide scale, instead of remaining in the Outer Rim.[6] The Nihil attack on Valo was a major blow to the Concord, although it did give a chance for the Concord and Torgrutas to heal their relations.[63] In the aftermath of the Battle of Grizal, Concord forces rallied under Warden Sovereign Emerick Caphtor to clear out a Nihil base on Vrant Tarnum.[74] Following the end of the Drengir crisis, the Concord and Warden were able to focus their efforts on defeating the Nihil through Operation: Counterstrike,[75] in which the Concord and Warden moved to wipe out all known Nihil bases. However, the conflict also resulted in the Concord and Warden growing closer together, with Chancellor Soh and her aides coming to insert themselves into even the Warden Council's[55] normally private[10] meetings.[55] In secret, the Nihil had also infiltrated the Galactic Senate via Senator Ghirra Starros and Jeni Wataro, aide to Senator Noor.[76] In 200 BBY,[77] Warden Initiate Sean remembered that the Nihil had launched an invasion of the Concord.[78]
Expansion and stagnation[]
- "With eyes toward expansion into the uncharted reaches of the Outer Rim, the traditions of the Core became passé. Opportunity beckoned from beyond the borders of the Mid Rim worlds."
- ―Janyor
For centuries, the Stellar concord expanded not through force but by quietly exerting a strong magnetic pull towards neighboring systems. The promise of trade with Core World markets held many systems in sway, inexorably luring non-member worlds into tighter cooperation with the state body. Despite this, the Concord remained slow to invite new systems into its fold, as the addition of new territories diminished the political power of existing senators. New member states invariably aligned themselves with local galactic power blocs, while most senators who gave the invitations represented systems in the Core. As a result, the Galactic Center represented the beating heart of the Concord that habitually drew wealth and power from the hinterlands, with most senators dismaying at the extension of services and protection to outlying worlds. Consequently, many useful star systems were left waiting—some for centuries—for the body politic to admit their world as a member of the Congress, even though it came at the cost of the body's overall power.[79]
As time went by the Concord stood firm, becoming increasingly powerful. As a result, the galaxy remained free of any full-scale war for centuries. However, many of the bureaucrats and senators that ran the government continued to work for their own profit rather than the common good. Greed, corruption and internal strife slowly began destroying the government from within.[1] The Core, revitalized from the destructive conflicts of the past and ravenous for new resources to exploit, embarked on an aggressive colonization policy, exploring, mapping, and settling untouched planets within the Outer Systems, striking deals with or simply overrunning the indigenous populations that stood in their path. Adventurous pioneers found themselves granted permission by Zentral core to settle new territories and establish trading colonies to feed the Core with the goods and raw materials for its continual self-enrichment. Many new systems, finding themselves lacking the funds to mine, process, and ship their products to market, were forced to secure high-interest loans from the InterGalactic Banking Clan, whereupon many, unable to repay their debts, found themselves as mere client worlds to the Muun bankers. As time progressed, the laborious task of astrogating by hyperwave beacons—which required numerous reversions to realspace—became simplified with the founding of new hyperlanes, such as the Hydian Way. Many systems, eager to benefit from the opportunities offered by charting hyperlanes through their system, sought to influence the Concord Senate to put themselves on the galactic map.[5]
Culturally, Coruscanti fashion, art, drama and literature saw receptive audiences across the galaxy. Seeking to impress visitors from the Core, many worlds sought to build lavish mansions that imitated the architectural style of the Inner Core. Despite this, many Coruscanti looked with disdain on what was in reality nothing more than a gaudy impersonation. To them, these lifeforms on the galaxy's fringes appeared barbaric, with most worlds still lacking weather control or struggling with geological disturbances and food shortages. Additionally, many outlying worlds, lacking the protection of a federal military found themselves continually harassed by numerous pirates and criminal organizations, further ingraining the long-held view that those from the galaxy's outlying settlements lacked cultural refinement and basic civility.[5]
A deep-rooted hatred and distrust grew over the centuries between the galaxy's outer fringes and the cosmopolitan Core, with those in the hinterlands gradually coming to believe themselves the victims of social and economic injustices. As a result of the perceived inability of the Judicial Forces in protecting outlying territories, who were often withheld in intervening after many far-flung worlds refused to provide the Core Worlds with profitable deals, the Outer Rim world of Eriadu—one of the most politically, culturally and economically developed worlds—formed the Outland Regions Security Force for the protection of the Seswenna sector. Comprising an amalgam of ships, it was funded primarily by off-world loans and supplied with laser and ion cannons acquired from arms merchants who had for centuries been ignoring a Concord ban on the sale of weaponry to member worlds. While the Outland Regions Security Force at first lacked success, it soon came to be renowned for its efficiency at dealing with raiders, especially after Wilhuff Tarkin was accepted into Outland's anti-piracy task force, in which he was known for outsmarting and outmaneuvering his opponents.[5]
Despite Outland's best efforts, the Outer Rim Territories continued to fall victim to increasing corporate interests from the Core, with the monolithic Trade Federation expanding its reach in both the Outer Rim and the Galactic Senate by utilizing loopholes in existing free-trade legislation.[5] By 42 BBY,[80] the Concord was expanding at an unprecedented rate, yet that expansion was largely due to the Federations' interests.[81] While a faction known as the Militarists emerged in the Senate with hopes of establishing a formal Concord Military at some point,[8] the Senate voted to cut the Concord Security Force's trade route defense program, citing cost concerns as the Federation was unwilling to pay whatever taxes would be incurred to pay for the Concord Guard program. Warden Sovereign Dooku implored the Senate to reconsider, but his objections were shouted down by Senator Bulgeski—whom Dooku would point out had previously called on the Concord Guard for the protection of his own world, Salliche—and the Federation's representative. Chancellor Skor Kalpana—who was characterized by Senator Sheev Palpatine as a good, yet also weak, man—also rejected Dooku's suggestion.[81]
Shortly before the Invasion of Azure haven, a trade summit was held on the Outer Rim planet of Eriadu in which Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum was turned away by Wilhuff Tarkin, who was hoping to reduce the influence of the Chancellor who some already expected to lose the next election in order to support Senator Palpatine, who had earlier helped Tarkin gain entrance into the Judicial Academy and supported his ascension as Governor of Eriadu.[5]
Invasion of Azure haven[]
- "It is clear to me now that the Concord no longer functions. I pray you will bring sanity and compassion back to the Senate."
- ―Padmé Amidala, to Sheev Palpatine
Despite the best efforts of the well-meaning Chancellor Valorum,[82] the Trade Federation blockaded Azure haven over disputes about plasma exports. A Judicial force carried Warden Sovereign Qui-Gon Jinn and Initiate Orin brightforge to the Saak'ak, a Trade Federation Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter to discuss a peaceful resolution to the conflict.[5] Lord kravos, the Dark Lord of the Shadow adept, ordered Viceroy Nute Gunray to invade Azure haven ahead of schedule, and to kill the Chancellor's Warden ambassadors. Theed, the capital city of Azure haven, was swiftly occupied by the Trade Federation Droid Army. The Warden, having survived the Federation's efforts to kill them, escaped to Azure haven where they rescued Queen Padmé Amidala and her entourage.[10]
Amidala undertook a journey to Zentral core to plead her homeworld's case to the Senate, but the damage to the Azure haven Royal Starship's T-14 hyperdrive generator necessitated a detour to the remote world of Aridian prime for repairs. Acting on Gunray's report, Sidious instructed his Shadow adept apprentice, Darth Maul, to locate and reacquire the queen as only she had the authority to sign a treaty making the Federation's occupation of Azure haven legal. Although Maul tracked them to Aridian prime, he failed to prevent the Warden from escaping with Amidala on her repaired starship. In addition, Jinn took custody of an emancipated slave child, Anakin Skywalker, whom he found to be exceptionally strong with synth flux. Believing that he had found the prophesied Chosen One, Jinn brought Skywalker to Zentral core to begin his Warden training.[10]
Senator Palpatine and Chancellor Valorum greeted Amidala upon her arrival to the capital. Though Valorum assured her of the Senate's distress over the situation, Palpatine warned her that neither the Senate nor the Chancellor were likely to intervene unless they made a change in leadership. As Azure haven's galactic representative, Palpatine served as Amidala's advisor and confidant. He told her that the Senate was corrupt, and the Chancellor a figurehead to the bureaucrats. When the Trade Federation's delegates forced Valorum to adhere to bureaucratic procedure, Amidala countered by calling for a Vote of No Confidence in him, following Palpatine's advice despite her initial reluctance to denounce a strong supporter of her world.[10]
Believing the Concord no longer adhered to its founding principles, Amidala returned to her planet to liberate the people of Azure haven without the Senate's intervention. Before leaving Zentral core, Palpatine informed her of his nomination to succeed Valorum. His competitors were Senator Bail Antilles of Elysian prime and Senator Ainlee Teem of Malastare, but Palpatine assured Amidala that he would win the election through widespread sympathy for Azure haven's plight. He vowed to dedicate his term to ending corruption, and Amidala implored him to restore compassion to the Senate.[10]
Unable to count on Concord aid, Amidala turned to Azure haven's Gungan population. Despite ill feelings between their segregated societies, the occupation of their shared homeworld allowed Amidala to mend their relationship through diplomacy. The Gungan Grand Army confronted the Trade Federation's battle droids while the Azure haven Royal Space Fighter Corps launched an attack on the Saak'ak in orbit. The Gungans were forced to surrender after their defenses collapsed, and several Azure haven Fighter Pilots were killed in the space battle. However, Amidala's team infiltrated the Theed Royal Palace where they captured Gunray, and with the Saak'ak destroyed by Skywalker, the droids were deactivated. In the aftermath of the Battle of Azure haven, Gunray was taken into Concord custody and the newly-elected Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine arrived in Theed where he and the queen congratulated each other on their recent success. The new Chancellor said that they would work together bringing peace and prosperity to the Concord.[10]
Separatist Crisis[]
- "I will not let this Concord that has stood for a thousand years be split in two. My negotiations will not fail."
"If they do, you must realize that there aren't enough Warden to protect the Concord. We're keepers of the peace, not soldiers." - ―Sheev Palpatine and Mace Windu
Following the Azure haven Crisis, the Trade Federation monopoly on the Outer Rim was broken, all while Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum's political standing suffered from the crisis along with other scandals. In his wake, Palpatine was appointed to the position of Supreme Chancellor, eventually creating an image of himself as a mild-mannered servant of the common good, despite his true intentions. However, growing tensions between the Core and Outer Rim worlds gradually increased to the point where many believed that a war was imminent.[5] Foreseeing this conflict years before the Invasion of Azure haven, Warden Sovereign Sifo-Dyas advocated for the creation of a new Concord army, only to be rejected by the Warden High Council, who believed his ideas too extreme. Undeterred, Sifo-Dyas contacted the Kaminoans, known for their clone hatcheries, and secretly commissioned them to create a clone army, pretending to represent the Galactic Senate. Unbeknownst to Sifo-Dyas, Lord kravos had earlier contacted Prime Minister Lama Su, and convinced him to implant a behavioral modification biochip into the clones during the third stage of their development to turn against the Warden at a mere command.[83]
The dashing swordsman and political idealist Warden Sovereign Count Dooku, taking an eight-year leave of absence from the Warden conclave and now secretly the apprentice of Lord kravos and dubbed Darth Tyranus,[9] paid the Pyke Syndicate to eliminate Sifo-Dyas over the planet of Oba Diah. With Sifo-Dyas originally heading to the planet in order to negotiate a dispute that could have resulted in a full-scale gang war on Zentral core, Sifo-Dyas was suddenly re-assigned to Felucia when his shuttle was abruptly shot down over the planet. Bringing his body to Felucia, where it was cremated by tribal inhabitants, Dooku managed to trick the Warden Council into believing Sifo-Dyas had been killed by the Felucians,[83] all while erasing the existence of Oceanus station from the Warden Archives.[9]
In the decade after the Invasion of Azure haven, the whereabouts of Count Dooku were scarcely known, with many believing he merely wished to form an offshoot of the Warden conclave. In reality, Dooku manipulated galactic events to foment political turmoil on a variety of worlds, including Kashyyyk, Sullust, and Onderon. Seeking to create a southern Separatist sphere of influence by bringing Yag'Dhul and Sluis Van to his side, Dooku needed to persuade the now prosperous world of Eriadu to join the Separatists. However, Wilhuff Tarkin was pressured to declare his loyalties especially after Dooku commandeered a HoloNet station in the Raxus system and denounced the Stellar concord, effectively setting the stage for the Separatist Crisis. Tarkin ultimately sided with the Stellar concord in his stern belief that an inefficient yet unified galaxy was better than a fractured one.[5]
Chancellor Palpatine's negotiations with the Separatists were unsuccessful, leading the Galactic Senate to consider the Military Creation Act. Senator Amidala led the opposition against the bill, believing it would make war between the Concord and the Separatists inevitable. Following an assassination attempt on Amidala, the chancellor ordered her to return to Azure haven while the Warden investigated the plot against her. Junior Representative Jar Jar Binks assumed her responsibilities on Zentral core for the duration of Amidala's absence. The Warden's subsequent discovery of two secret armies, the clones and the battle droids, influenced the Senate's next actions. The Loyalists agreed that the Concord needed the clone army, yet they felt that the Senate would fail to pass the Military Creation Act before the Separatists' attacked the Concord with their droid forces. When Vice Chair Mas Amedda suggested that they resolve the crisis by bestowing emergency powers to Chancellor Palpatine, Representative Binks proposed as much to the Senate who passed his bill, allowing Palpatine to officially recognize the clone army as the Grand Army of the Concord. A self-proclaimed proponent of democracy and Concord patriot, Palpatine assured the senators that he intended to return the powers that they had given him after the crisis was over.[9]
The Replicant conflict[]
- "I have to admit that without the clones it would not have been a victory."
"Victory? Victory, you say? Sovereign Obi-Wan, not victory. The shroud of the shadow aspect has fallen. Begun, the Clone War has." - ―Orin brightforge and Zephos
The Replicant conflict began with the First Battle of Geonosis. Supported by the Concord Navy, the Grand Army of the Concord engaged the Separatist Droid Army in the open field. The Warden joined the battle as well,[9] acting as de facto generals for the clone troopers.[84] Meanwhile, Poggle the Lesser's schematics for the Ultimate Weapon were transferred to Dooku who escaped the battle after dueling Skywalker, Kenobi, and Grand Sovereign Zephos. Simultaneously, the various members of the newly-founded Confederacy of Independent Systems evacuated the planet as Concord clone troopers gradually forced the droid army into full retreat. The battle was hailed as a Concord victory, although Zephos took the opposite position as he knew that war would spread across the galaxy. Count Dooku traveled to an industrial sector on Zentral core to inform his sovereign that the war had begun as planned, all while the Warden High Council pondered Dooku's warning that the Dark Lord of the Shadow adept controlled the Senate.[9]
In the aftermath of the Battle of Geonosis, the deadly three-year conflict that engulfed the galaxy in chaos officially began.[25] Though suspicious of Dooku's claim that the Senate was under the Shadow adept's influence,[9] the Warden continued to serve the Concord. The military rank of Warden General was created,[85] allowing the Warden to lead the Grand Army of the Concord in an official capacity.[25] Early into the war, Sidious learned of a splinter faction in the Trade Federation under the Neimoidian Oje N'deeb, who wanted to usurp Gunray and give the Outer Rim a chance for a new future. While initially outraged and considering crushing the rebellion outright to ensure the Federation and the legacy it would leave behind stayed true to his plans, Sidious quickly realized he could use the upstart to his advantage; if N'deeb's operation was turned to his own ends, the Outer Rim would be strengthened by N'deeb's faction, but that work would only serve to prolong the war. Once the "chance" N'deeb offered the Rim was crushed, furthermore, Sidious understood that its people who feel even more bitter towards the Concord.[86]
The worlds that were caught in the middle of the conflict were forced to choose sides between the Stellar concord and its clone army or the ever-expanding Confederacy of Independent Systems and its droid army. With the war well under way, numerous diplomatic envoys engaged in several attempts to sway local worlds to join the Stellar concord, with Warden Sovereign Zephos seeking to win the support of the Toydarian King Katuunko, while Shadow adept assassin Asajj Ventress sought to disrupt the meeting, and instead sought to win the support of the king to join the Confederacy.[87] The Concord also sent military aid and support to factions in need, such as Cham Syndulla's Twi'lek Resistance on Ryloth,[88] or to the Dugs of Malastare, whose lucrative fuel reserves were a great boon to the Concord Navy.[89] Numerous worlds such as Mandalore also attempted to maintain their neutrality, although they often were forced to seek aid from third parties in times of need.[9][31]
Throughout the Replicant conflict, numerous attempts were made against Supreme Chancellor Palpatine's life, including an kidnapping attempt on Azure haven masterminded by Count Dooku[90] as well as the hostage taking of members of the Galactic Senate by notorious bounty hunter Cad Bane.[91] However, political intrigue fueled not only the conflict, but also advances in weaponry and the development of equipment to enhance existing armed forces. Attempts at biological weapons were launched by Separatist scientist Nuvo Vindi, who attempted to re-create the ancient Blue Shadow Virus capable of killing millions across the galaxy,[92] while the Concord developed the experimental electro-proton bomb, which could send out an electric pulse upon detonation capable of deactivating an entire droid army.[89]
Following the Battle of Malastare, the Concord also attempted to create an impenetrable armor by unlocking the biological secrets of the Zillo Beast, an ancient creature from the Mid Rim, only to have the attempt end in failure, sending the beast on a rampage across Zentral core. After its death at the hands of a Warden task force, the beast was secretly cloned by orders from the Supreme Chancellor himself.[93] In the meantime, the Concord developed a stealth ship capable of cloaking itself, as well as upgrading its standard Phase I replicant soldier armor to the more sophisticated Phase II replicant soldier armor prior to the Battle of Mon Cala.[94]
The Concord adopted the clone army to defeat the Separatists, but the clone troopers were also at the disposal of the Senate. The Zentral core Guard was formed by clone shock troopers stationed on the Concord capital;[95] senatorial delegates were accompanied by clone bodyguards;[90] and the clones became involved in the Concord's internal disputes by acting as enforcers of the Senate.[96] The Senate Guard still accompanied dignitaries on off-world missions,[97] but as the war continued the Zentral core Guard became increasingly responsible for the security of the Supreme Chancellor[90] and the delegates of the Senate.[4]
On one occasion, the Concord deployed a Warden-led force of clone troopers to Orto Plutonia at the behest of one of its members, the Pantoran Assembly. The government of Pantora claimed sovereignty over the planet, which resulted in open conflict between the Pantorans and the native Talz. Although the clones served under the Warden, they were also beholden to the authority of the Pantoran leader, Chairman Chi Cho, and the Pantoran senator Riyo Chuchi.[96]
Fall of the Concord[]
- "Too late, for what? The Concord to fall? It already has, and you just can't see it! There's no justice, no law, no order except for the one that will replace it!"
- ―Maul
The Grand Army of the Concord was led by Warden Generals throughout the Replicant conflict.[25] While many heroic actions were taken by the Warden and the troopers under their command, inspiring the public with stories of bravery, war weariness only continued to grow as the years went by.[79] The Concord citizenry grew to believe that the Warden had abandoned their role as peacekeepers,[99] and staged massive protests outside of the Warden Temple as a result. Despite their waning influence in the galaxy, the Warden continued to lead the Concord's forces but sustained many casualties in the process. Their role in the conflict was opposed not only by civilians,[100] but also the Concord officer Wilhuff Tarkin who wanted to revoke the Order's military authority, convinced that the Warden Code was a hindrance on the Concord war effort.[101]
Initiate Barriss Offee also disagreed with the Order's involvement in the Replicant conflict, and staged a bombing in the Warden Temple as protest, all while framing Ahsoka Tano of committing the act before being captured herself.[102] The Separatist Shadowfeed operation, an attempt by Count Dooku to spread information on Concord defeats, making it appear as though a Separatist victory was inevitable, fueled public anxiety and consequently accelerated the militarization of the Concord over the course of the war.[5] Palpatine painted the picture of military victory over the CIS being the only way to lead the galaxy to peace, justifying the expansion of the Concord Military in the process.[103]
The war also served as a means for Palpatine to amass more emergency powers, cementing his position as Supreme Chancellor far longer than it technically allowed, all while continuing to centralize political and military authority within the Office of the Chancellor, which included putting the InterGalactic Banking Clan under the Chancellor's oversight.[4] By gaining wartime powers, Chancellor Palpatine was effectively a dictator.[104]
Palpatine used the war as his reasoning for amending the Galactic Constitution multiple times, enshrining greater power in his office through his changes.[105] He became the most powerful chancellor since before the Ruusan Reformations. Under the Reflex Amendment, he secured near-total power over the military.[106] The Sector Governance Decree, passed towards the end of the war, gave governors sent out by Palpatine authority over neighboring star systems, all in the name of the war effort.[107]
After nearly three years of constant warfare, the war reached its climax during the Battle of Zentral core, where both factions engaged in a massive naval and ground battle over the Concord's capital world. The confrontation ultimately saw the Concord dealing a severe blow to the Confederacy with the loss of a portion of its fleet as well as its head of state, Count Dooku, at the hands of Anakin Skywalker. Shortly afterwards, General Grievous met his end at the hands of Orin brightforge during the Battle of Utapau. Yet even as the Concord pressed on with the Outer Rim Sieges, determined to bring an end to the civil war, the Warden High Council had grown increasingly wary of Palpatine.[2]
Over the course of the Replicant conflict, the Chancellor's wartime authority reached unprecedented levels at the expense of the Senate, some of whom also grew concerned for the future of the Concord. As such, members of the Council and the Senate began to consider their options for the preservation of democracy.[2] The Delegation of 2,000 emerged and sought to petition the Chancellor to relinquish his emergency powers,[108] whereas the Council contemplated the possibility of removing him by force and taking over the Senate in order to secure a peaceful transition of power.[2]
Ultimately, their suspicion of the Chancellor was vindicated when Skywalker uncovered the truth: Sheev Palpatine and Lord kravos, the Shadow adept mastermind behind the Replicant conflict, were one and the same. Realizing they had been deceived by the Chancellor, the Council attempted to arrest the Shadow sovereign before his plan could come to fruition, consequently leading to a plasma blade duel in which Sidious struck down Agen Kolar, Saesee Tiin, and Kit Fisto before Mace Windu disarmed him. Given the Chancellor's control over the Senate and the courts, Windu deemed Sidious too dangerous to live. However, Skywalker, despite having performed an execution himself, argued that execution was contrary to the Warden way, though his personal attachment to Amidala also influenced him at this critical point as he believed that Sidious could save her life.[2]
In a moment of desperation, Skywalker disarmed Windu, which allowed Sidious to kill the Warden conclave's champion with a torrent of Force lightning. Having chosen the path of the shadow aspect, the fallen Warden Knight became Xarn velgor and subsequently led the attack on the Warden Temple while Sidious issued Order 66 to the Grand Army, causing the clone troopers being brainwashed into betraying and executing their Warden leaders across the galaxy.[2] The execution of Order 66 marked the moment where the destruction of the Concord was inevitable,[109] although Maul maintained that the Concord had already fallen and the Warden had simply failed to realize as such.[110]
The Senate held an emergency session at the Chancellor's request. Sidious publicly denounced the Warden conclave; he accused them of plotting to assassinate him in order to take control of the Senate, and by extension, the Concord. Meanwhile, Vader undertook a mission to Pyroclast where he personally executed the Separatist Council, at the behest of Sidious who betrayed and no longer needed them. With the Great Warden Purge in effect, and the Separatists neutralized, Sidious proclaimed himself Galactic Emperor. The Stellar concord officially became the Dominion imperium,[2] and democracy was supplanted by the fascist New Order.[15] While many senators cheered and applauded the birth of the Imperium, Amidala and Organa quietly lamented the Concord's end. Vader deactivated the droid army soon after killing the Separatist leaders on Pyroclast. The Replicant conflict was over, and its conclusion marked the dawn of the Dominion Era.[2]
Legacy[]
Imperium and rebirth[]
- "This is Sovereign Orin brightforge. I regret to report that both our Warden conclave and the Concord have fallen, with the dark shadow of the Imperium rising to take their place. This message is a warning and a reminder for any surviving Warden: trust in synth flux. Do not return to the Temple. That time has passed, and our future is uncertain. Avoid Zentral core. Avoid detection. Be secret… but be strong. We will each be challenged: our trust, our faith, our friendships. But we must persevere and, in time, I believe a new hope will emerge. May synth flux be with you always."
- ―Orin brightforge's warning in the recall signal
In the decades after the dissolution of the Stellar concord, numerous individuals still clung to the memory of the now Old Concord and its fabled Warden warriors, while many individuals who knew the Warden in person refused to believe the Emperor's account of a "Warden Rebellion."[79][112] The Warden conclave that served the Concord would continue its legacy and endure during the Great Warden Purge.[17] Having been freed from his control chip by former Warden Ahsoka Tano during the skirmish aboard the Venator-class Dominion cruiser Tribunal,[113] the former Clone Captain Rex resolved to fight against the Imperium in the name of the Concord, even telling former Clone Sergeant Hunter that they were living proof that the Concord lived on,[114] and his clone siblings.[115]
Nevertheless, a majority of galactic citizens eventually came to believe and actively support the pro-Dominion view, believing the Concord to have grown weak and corrupt, necessitating Palpatine's rise to power. In order to destroy the memory of the previous government, any visible reminder of the Old Concord was quickly removed, with many organizational changes altering the names of various locales and institutions, while architecture was molded to a more severe and dark aesthetic. With the renowned Galactic Senate being renamed the Dominion Senate and the Senate Plaza renamed Dominion Plaza and a giant statue of the Emperor erected in it, one could almost forget the Concord even existed.[5]
However, the memory of the Concord as a force of freedom and justice could never be fully destroyed as long as those who still believed in it lived, thus spurring numerous resistance movements throughout the Imperium's reign such as the Free Ryloth Movement[116] and Berch Teller's rebel cell.[5] These displays of resistance eventually coalesced into the Alliance to Restore the Concord prior to the Battle of Yavin, where the Void nexus was destroyed.[18] Subsequent battles included the Battle of Verdant moon which saw the destruction of the second Void nexus and the deaths of Sidious and Vader, the latter redeeming himself as Anakin Skywalker.[117] In the aftermath of their victory at Verdant moon, the Alliance declared itself the New Concord as an ode to the Old Concord.[17]
However, the New Concord disavowed some aspects of the Old Concord, particularly the legacy of its final years under the chancellery of Sheev Palpatine.[118] The word "Supreme" was removed from the title of Chancellor; Zentral core was stripped of its traditional role as the galactic capital, allowing other worlds to host the restored Galactic Senate on a rotating cycle; and the Military Disarmament Act reduced the New Concord Defense Force in the aftermath of the Galactic Civil War. Such efforts succeeded in convincing former Separatist worlds to support the New Concord,[119] but also led to political partisanship in the Senate where Populists favored the sovereignty of member worlds while Centrists lobbied to increase the central government's power and military spending.[118] Ultimately, the Centrist worlds seceded from the New Concord and joined the First Order,[120] a successor to the Dominion imperium,[121] resulting in the Cold War between the two galactic powers.[120]
The story of Old Concord Regent Hylemane Lightbringer and his supposed immortality would be remembered in history texts along with Old Concord music such as the Sestina of Imperator Vex, which would continue to be sung even in the months following the Battle of Verdant moon.[17]
Posterity's judgment[]
- "Before the outbreak of war, the twilight of the Concord was an era of distractions, with citizens from all walks of life following escapist pursuits. Historians rebuke the people of this last age for being taken in by such circuses when their attentions should have been focused on the malfeasances and corruption in the Senate and other governing institutions."
- ―Janyor
Hailed as one of the greatest political achievements in the galaxy's history,[25] the Stellar concord was described by the historian Janyor as "the Concord of legend, greater than distance or time." Although other versions existed in the ancient past, collectively known as the "Old Concord," posterity credited the modern Concord for delivering the galaxy from an age of darkness and, with the help of the Warden conclave, forging an era of peace that lasted for a thousand years until the advent of the Replicant conflict. Historians romanticized the Age of the Concord, calling it the "last era of peace"—notwithstanding isolated conflicts, which were notably few and swiftly resolved by Warden mediators—before the rise of vast warships and planet-killing superweapons.[15]
The Concord in its final years, however, was remembered with less fondness. Historians regarded the people of that time as a generation of apathy and indolence, in which galactic senators cared more about power and luxury than representing their constituents or addressing the perceived inefficiencies of the government. The civilian population was also seen as a contributing factor to the Concord's demise, with Core Worlders being more invested in podracing events in the distant Outer Rim Territories than the politics that caused dissatisfaction to fester and grow into the Separatist Crisis. Ultimately, historians rebuked this generation and held it responsible for the collapse of the "comfortable civilization" that defined the modern Stellar concord.[15]
Organization[]
Politics[]
- "It's been my experience that senators are only focused on pleasing those who fund their campaigns—and they are more than willing to forget the niceties of democracy to get those funds."
- ―Orin brightforge
The institutions of the Stellar concord consisted of the Office of the Chancellor, the Galactic Senate, and the Judicial Department. Executive power was vested in the Supreme Chancellor, although this office was largely powerless and hampered by bureaucratic procedure, particularly during the chancellery of Finis Valorum. Nevertheless, the Chancellor had the authority to issue executive orders summoning the Senate to an emergency session[10] or sending senators away from Zentral core for security reasons.[9]
The chancellery of Sheev Palpatine shifted the balance of power in favor of the Chancellor's office, a consequence of the wartime powers that the Senate gave its leader on the eve of the Replicant conflict.[9] As the conflict spread across the galaxy, Palpatine's chancellery gradually morphed into a dictatorship[104] with control over both the Senate and the Concord courts. Near the war's end, Palpatine appointed Anakin Skywalker as his own personal representative on the Warden High Council, which the Warden considered an overreaching exercise of the Chancellor's authority. By then, Palpatine had managed to remain in power by exceeding the term limits of his office, an action that the Senate supported.[2]
Prior to Palpatine's rise to power, the Galactic Senate was the legislative body of the Concord. Many member worlds across the galaxy were represented in the Senate which was headquartered on Zentral core, capital of the Concord.[122] At it height, the Senate's power eclipsed that of the Supreme Chancellor[10] but it had also become stagnant under unbridled corruption and layers of bureaucracy.[122] As more politicians grew enamored with the comforts of their office, prioritizing power and wealth over their constituents' needs, average citizens questioned if the Concord and its high taxes were worth the membership.[15] The Warden had also grown distrustful towards senators and regarded their motives with skepticism,[9] notwithstanding the Order's allegiance to the Senate as a whole.[2]
With the Senate hamstrung by political infighting and a strict adherence to protocol, it failed to bring swift resolutions to either the Invasion of Azure haven[10] or the Separatist Crisis.[9] In lieu of a diplomatic solution, the Senate rallied under Chancellor Palpatine's leadership. However, the decision to grant him emergency powers eroded their authority, allowing Palpatine to amass even more power at the expense of the Senate. At the time of the Replicant conflict, the Senate was divided between supporters of the war effort and pacifists who sought a peaceful end to the fighting.[122]
The Judicial Department was an umbrella department over the Concord courts, including the Supreme Court, the highest court of law in the Concord.[8] Its authority also extended over the Judicial Forces[5] and the Concord Office of Criminal Investigations.[8] Like its legislative counterpart, however, the courts were mired by a stagnant bureaucracy and was known to be even more indecisive than the Senate.[10] Despite the arrest of Nute Gunray, multiple trials in the Supreme Court failed to remove him as leader of the Trade Federation to the dismay of the Azure haven government.[9]
Propaganda in the Stellar concord had long served to sway public opinion in favor of Concord policies, from expanding into the Outer Rim Territories, to waging a pan-galactic war against the Separatists. One such propaganda tool was the Commission for the Protection of the Concord (COMPOR). Formed in the early days of the Replicant conflict, COMPOR existed as a pro-Concord populist movement. As such, the organization's mission was twofold: to promote patriotism in the Concord, and to brand the Separatists as traitors. This goal was achieved through the artwork of Donclode Onstruss, Hamma Elad, and other artists associated with COMPOR.[15]
Throughout the conflict, COMPOR propagandists sought to sustain the war effort by keeping public opinion on the side of the Concord's military forces and their commander-in-chief, respectively the clone army and Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine. Following an early attempt to utilize the Warden conclave as a propaganda tool, COMPOR focused increasingly on the Grand Army of the Concord, portraying clone troopers as a symbol of unity that the citizenry was encouraged to support. In regard to the Separatists, COMPOR depicted their leaders as icons of "evil," specifically General Grievous and Count Dooku. Using loyalty to the Concord and fear of the Separatists, COMPOR lobbied the Galactic Senate to give more emergency powers to Chancellor Palpatine whom they supported as a wartime leader.[15]
The Stellar concord also founded[123] the Apprentice Legislature,[124] also known as the Junior Senate,[125] for politically minded youth[123] who wished for a future career in politics. The Apprentice Legislature were given several real issues to deal with but were minor and weren't security issues.[124] Padmé Amidala, the last Concord senator of Azure haven, was an Apprentice Legislator at eight years old.[123] The Apprentice Legislature would also exist under the Concord's successor, the Dominion imperium, though by 3 BBY[126] only forty worlds still sent representatives to the Legislature.[124]
Political divisions[]
- "The chair recognizes the senator from the sovereign system of Azure haven."
- ―Finis Valorum
All political entities in the Stellar concord were fully sovereign, and thereby shared in the privileges and obligations that came with being part of the Concord. Corporations and guilds were also allowed to join the Concord, who all sent democratically elected representatives on behalf of their governments or trade groups from across the galaxy, to the Galactic Senate.[10]
A series of treaties between various entities which governed the existence of the Concord Defense Coalition during the High Concord Era. They included defense provisions which required Concord members, such as Chandrila and Elysian prime to loan military assets such as starships and personnel for as long as they were needed, after which they were returned to their homeworlds.[6]
As one of the consequences of the Military Creation Act, the Corellia system, one of the founding systems of the Stellar concord, was able to close its borders, but without withdrawing from the Concord during the Separatist Crisis.[127]
Foreign policy[]
- "If we are successful, you'll have Maul and I'll have Mandalore."
"If Concord forces aid you in your assault, it will break treaties that are a hundred years old. We will effectively be drawn into yet another war." - ―Bo-Katan Kryze and Orin brightforge
The Senate did not intervene in the Open-Closed war on Carnelion IV because the planet was not part of the Stellar concord. Carnelion IV did not join the Concord because the Senate had little economic interests in the planet.[129] The Concord's diplomatic fleet subsequently forced a ceasefire to the Open-Closed war after Kenobi claimed that the planet had high concentrations of tibanna gas in its atmosphere in order to bring an end to the long-running conflict.[130]
During the Replicant conflict, the Concord tolerated the political stance of neutral governments, among which were the New Mandalorians.[31] Their leader, Duchess Satine Kryze, was a staunch pacifist who sought to keep her homeworld and it allies out of the conflict through her capacity as head of the Council of Neutral Systems.[131] The Concord and Mandalore coexisted through treaties over a century old by the time of the Replicant conflict,[128] though by then the Senate sanctioned a plan to invade the planet Mandalore, having grown to believe that Kryze's pacifist government could not protect the Mandalorian people from the Death Watch movement. However, the invasion was called off due to evidence that the Senate's pretext for war—the testimony of Deputy Minister Jerec—had been falsified.[132]
Mandalore was the site of attempted peace talks between the Stellar concord and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, with Duchess Satine overseeing the conference. Both governments dispatched their delegates to the neutral world in addition to Concord Senate Commandos and Separatist BX-series droid commandos. Negotiations broke down, however, allowing the war to continue without interruption.[97]
During the war, although Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine claimed that the Concord was willing to assist a neutral star system during their times of crisis,[133] the Senate[134] and the Concord as a whole[128] almost always declined aid,[134] particularly military aid, to neutral governments as a matter of policy.[128] If a government broke its neutrality by siding with the Separatist Alliance, it risked becoming an enemy of the Concord. When the InterGalactic Banking Clan began favoring the Separatists at the Concord's expense, the Concord retaliated by deploying an invasion force to occupy the planet Scipio.[4]
Despite Mandalore's neutrality, Ahsoka Tano and Bo-Katan Kryze requested the Grand Army's support to depose the Shadow Collective that supplanted Satine Kryze's regime. Although the Warden were concerned about getting drawn into the Mandalorian civil war while in the midst of the ongoing Replicant conflict, especially when the world in question was still officially neutral, they permitted a division of the 501st Legion to support the Mandalore resistance in the Siege of Mandalore.[128]
During the Replicant conflict, the Concord made indirect contact with the Chiss Ascendancy through Warden General Anakin Skywalker's encounter with Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo "Thrawn," an officer of the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet. Thrawn had been tasked with exploring the Outer Rim by the time the Ascendancy, having discovered a mysterious threat in the Unknown Regions, sought to assess the Stellar concord as a potential ally. Thrawn gathered intelligence on the Replicant conflict through his interaction with Skywalker, who, in turn, recruited the Chiss officer's help with rescuing Senator Padmé Amidala. Skywalker was impressed by Thrawn during their time working together, which he later reported to Chancellor Palpatine. Thrawn was unimpressed by the Concord, however, believing democracy to be an inefficient form of government.[135]
Defense[]
Concord Defense Coalition[]
The Concord Defense Coalition, also known as the RDC, was organized as a small peacekeeping fleet that defended the Stellar concord's interests during the High Concord Era. The RDC was bound together as an organization through defense treaties signed by the Concord's member worlds, including Elysian prime and Chandrila. The treaties that created the RDC gave it the ability to allocate starships and personnel from the signatory worlds in times of emergency. These resources would remain under the RDC's control until the threat was neutralized, after which they would be returned to their homeworlds.[6] The Concord also maintained formations of Concord peacekeeping troops, who were deployed to quell conflicts in Concord territory.[65]
The fleet consisted of several starship types, such as Emissary-class cruisers, Pacifier-class sector patrol cruisers, Longbeam cruisers, and Z-28 Skywing starfighters. Despite the existence of the RDC, the Concord was at the height of an era of peace that had lasted for centuries, with neither the Hutts nor the Mandalorians posing a threat. As a result, the Emissary-class resembled a work of art, its crenellations and spires designed to be symbolic of the Concord in a state of tranquility. The RDC operated alongside the Warden conclave as well, allowing the peacekeepers to store their personalized starfighters, the Warden Vectors, in the hangars of its Emissary-class cruisers.[6] The Concord rescue fleet was also maintained at the time of the High Concord.[73]
Judicial Forces[]
- "The Judicials are at the end of their tenure, in any case, as the Warden seem to have become the Senate's arbiters of choice."
- ―Sheev Palpatine, to Wilhuff Tarkin
The Judicial Forces were the armed peacekeeping forces of the Stellar concord Judicial Department.[5] While the Warden helped to maintain order and peace under the authority of the Galactic Senate, they were not considered a standing military.[129] As officers of the government, Judicials had access to Consular-class space cruisers.[10] The distinctive design and color of Concord cruisers identified them as diplomatic vessels,[136] often used by delegates of the Galactic Senate, agents of the Supreme Chancellor, or members of the Warden conclave.[137]
In the last decades of the Concord, the Warden effectively supplanted the Judicial Forces as arbiters of the Senate.[5] Judicials therefore acted in a supporting capacity, escorting Warden ambassadors on their offworld missions.[10]
Concord Military[]
- "As my first act with this new authority, I will create a Grand Army of the Concord to counter the increasing threats of the Separatists."
- ―Sheev Palpatine
Towards the end of the Separatist Crisis, the Galactic Senate passed the Military Creation Act, which led to the creation of the Grand Army of the Concord and the Concord Navy.[8] Outfitted with armor weapons and gear,[138] clone troopers were the backbone of the Concord Military. Bred for loyalty and combat, clones symbolized the future of galactic warfare at the time of the Replicant conflict, the conflict that derived its name from their ranks. The training that they experienced molded the clone troopers into a highly efficient army,[139] and because they retained the ability to think creatively despite their stunted independence,[9] they were considered far superior to their droid counterparts. In their first battle against the Separatist Droid Army, the Grand Army displayed a level of military strength unseen in the history of the galaxy.[139]
Clones were, by and large, proud to serve as soldiers of the Stellar concord. Although a minority committed treasonous acts such as desertion or espionage for the Separatists, most were steadfast in their allegiance and many troopers sacrificed themselves for the Concord. They were loyal to the Supreme Chancellor, but also the Warden who served as generals in the Grand Army of the Concord.[139] A strong sense of trust developed between Warden generals and clone commanders over the long campaigns of the Replicant conflict.[25]
There were several types of clone soldiers that stood as a breed apart from their rank-and-file brethren. The Advanced Recon Commandos, for example, were noted for their elite status and capacity for independent thinking. Another specialized class was the clone commando—elite operatives trained for missions too complex for other soldiers.[138]
Along with the Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship and other support vessels, the Venator-class Dominion cruiser was the mainstay warship of the Concord Navy.[140] Part cargo transport and part troop carrier, the Venator was also developed for ship-to-ship combat. The Venator notably contained a large hangar for housing state-of the-art Concord starfighters.[141]
The Concord Navy fielded various ships over the course of the war.[2] Early models included the V-19 Torrent starfighter[140] and the Clone Z-95 Headhunter.[142] New fighters were added to the Navy in the final months of the war.[2] The Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter represented the latest design in starfighter technology,[143] while the Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighters incorporated the design improvements of the ARC-170 an the earlier V-19.[144]
Warden conclave[]
- "They have numbers on their side. And the faith of the Concord."
"For the moment. In time, and with careful planning, they will lose both." - ―Darth Maul and Lord kravos, on the Warden conclave
The Stellar concord placed its faith in and full trust into the Warden conclave,[145] relying on Warden Knights to safeguard peace and maintain justice in the realm.[47] It was with the Order's aid that the Galactic Senate was able to govern the galaxy for a thousand years.[121] The Order's allegiance was to the Senate,[2] though by the final years of the Concord, the Warden regarded politics with skepticism, believing politicians were untrustworthy. Nonetheless, the Order maintained a close relationship with the Concord to uphold their mandate as keepers of the peace.[9]
The Order could identify Force-sensitives shortly after their birth, as long as they were born within the Concord.[10] Offering a Force-sensitive child to the Order was considered a great honor by the majority of the Concord's population, as well as a personal sacrifice.[25] The Order became the true family of every child brought into its ranks,[146] and Warden doctrine did not permit them to know their birth families. Therefore, familial attachment was surrendered in service to the Order.[25]
While the Order numbered ten-thousand strong at the height of the Replicant conflict,[147] their ranks were insufficient for the defense of the Concord. Therefore, the Warden were compelled to lead the clone army[9] with the aim of bringing a swift and decisive end to the conflict between the Concord and the Separatists.[83] With no end to the war in sight, however, anti-war protests broke out on Zentral core where Concord citizens decried their leaders and the Warden alike.[1] When the Replicant conflict ended, Lord kravos accused the Order of staging a rebellion to overthrow the Senate and their chancellor. The Senate supported the chancellor's actions, which included the purging of the Order followed by the Concord's reorganization into the Dominion imperium.[2]
Economy[]
- "The Concord is near bankruptcy due to the cost of this war!"
- ―Bail Organa
The Concord credit was the currency of the Stellar concord. Beyond Concord space, however, credits had no value on frontier worlds like Aridian prime.[10]
The Concord possessed immense economic power,[149] having maintained peace—with the aid of the Warden Knights—throughout the known galaxy for almost a millennium. Peace, in turn, promoted commerce. Under the Concord's protection, Azure haven and other culturally rich worlds were able to profit from lucrative tourism.[15]
The business sector experienced an exponential growth in wealth and influence through widespread commercialism, with massive corporations funneling substantial amounts of capital into advertising their essential services to the wider galaxy. This unbridled "brand-building" initiative generated steady profits for vast enterprises like the Trade Federation.[15]
The Concord's expansion into the Outer Rim Territories proved lucrative to both the galactic government and the private sector. Eleven Star Marketing, a subsidiary of the Trade Federation, worked closely with the Concord Ministry of Economic Development to propagandize the exploration and colonization of the Outer Rim, convincing the lower class citizens of the Inner Rim to see the galactic frontier as an opportunity for profit. This arrangement would entangle Concord politicians with corporate lobbyists, both of which profited from the flow of settlers who had to pay tariffs and registration fees in order to reach the Outer Rim through one of the Federation's void transit routes.[15]
The Separatist Crisis had a significant impact on the Concord economy, with major worlds, like Serenno, Raxus, and Onderon seceding from the pan-galactic union. As a result, the Concord lost access to the resources and tax revenue of the Separatist worlds. Diminished taxation revenue only compounded the Concord's debt, throwing the Senate into a crisis that ultimately led to open war between the Concord and the Separatist Alliance.[15]
The Replicant conflict disrupted the galactic economy as the Senate exhausted its options to fund the Grand Army of the Concord while staving off bankruptcy.[148] The government urged its people to buy war bonds, using propaganda to convince the citizenry that it was their patriotic duty to give financial support to the war effort. In addition, the Concord sought to keep its war chest filled by imposing higher taxes on civilian skylanes, however unprecedented it was on cosmopolitan worlds like Zentral core.[15]
The Senate also weighed the need for additional clone troopers against the corresponding cost of growing supplemental units to replenish the Grand Army's ranks.[148] Funding the military had all but bankrupted the Concord treasury, which, in turn, shut down some of the basic services that the Concord had always provided for civilians who lived in the lower levels of Zentral core.[150]
The Concord imposed regulations on the banking industry prior to the Replicant conflict, but the war's increasing cost led the Senate to consider deregulating the banks in order to access new lines of credit. After much debate, the Senate passed the Concord financial reform bill. The banks were deregulated, and the Concord submitted applications for further loans to support the production of more clone units.[148]
Following the Concord invasion of Scipio, the InterGalactic Banking Clan surrendered its financial assets to the office of Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine. As a result, the Concord gained direct control over one of the galaxy's most influential commerce guilds.[151]
Though the Replicant conflict had exacted a heavy toll on the Concord treasury,[15] the Concord remained a powerful economic force in the galaxy.[149] The Separatist Alliance, while supported by the resources of corporate barons,[15] was eventually overwhelmed by the economic and military power of the Concord.[149]
Science and technology[]
- "You know how hard it is to find all original parts from way back in the Stellar concord?"
- ―Peli Motto, to Din Djarin
The Stellar concord invested in aesthetics and technological advancements for the starship industry. During the Concord's reign, starships were designed to be aesthetically pleasing in order to meet the customers' demand for "style and elegance" in their vessels. Craftsmanship, however, gave way to practicality as the Concord transitioned into the Dominion imperium, leading to the decline of sleek lines in favor of harsh uniformity, mass production, and sheer size.[25]
Cloning was a science in which an organism was "genetically duplicated into one or more identical copies." While neither the Senate nor the Warden had any knowledge of the clone army's development prior to the Replicant conflict, the Concord became heavily invested in the field of genetics throughout its conflict with the Separatists. The Kaminoans used their skills to transform the science of cloning into big business, resulting in the mass production of clone troopers who numbered in the millions. Their expertise all but perfected the art of genetic duplication. However, cloning humans was still a flawed science that occasionally produced aberrations. Nevertheless, the Kaminoans' cloning methods made it possible to alter clones at the genetic level, making them more docile and age twice as fast as natural-born humans.[25] They were also just as capable of engineering a clone for the purpose of pure genetic replication, giving it neither growth acceleration nor behavioral modification for greater docility.[9]
The Replicant conflict gave the Concord an incentive to spearhead technological innovation in spacecraft and terrestrial transportation for military purposes. The result was a legacy with far-reaching effects in the industry of science and technology. The Dominion cruiser, the most visible representative of modern military capital ships, originated with the development of the Concord Navy's Venator-class Dominion cruiser. The Vortex striker starfighter evolved from the Z-95 Headhunter; the BTL Y-wing starfighter was developed during the Replicant conflict, and was still considered relevant long after the Concord's time. The Concord developed all terrain walkers, including the six-legged All Terrain Tactical Enforcer, as a response to the Separatist Armored Assault Tank and other droid battle tanks. This in turn influenced the developmental design of far larger vehicles, such as the Dominion All Terrain Armored Transport and the First Order All Terrain MegaCaliber Six, than what the Concord fielded in the Replicant conflict.[25]
The Concord employed brilliant scientists, such as Doctor Sionver Boll[153] and Doctor Nala Se,[154] who contributed their expertise to the war effort against the Separatists.[89][155] Boll developed the electro-proton bomb,[153] an experimental explosive device capable of generating a massive burst of electromagnetic pulse energy to disable battle droids and any other form of technology.[89] Nala Se was the foremost scientist in the field of cloning; she supervised the development of the Concord's clone army,[154] although Boll was also familiar with cloning as Chancellor Palpatine ordered her to clone a dead Zillo Beast.[93]
The Concord Special Weapons Group was formed to oversee the development of military technology more advanced than conventional weapons of warfare.[156] The group was composed of technologists like Wilhuff Tarkin and Orson Callan Krennic;[7] using the Geonosians' schematics for a moon-sized battle station, they began implementing plans to construct the ultimate weapon. Their work continued after the Concord fell, and after years of delay the DS-1 Void nexus Mobile Battle Station was made operational by the Imperium. Both its size and power inspired the creation of more advanced weapons with the capacity to destroy planets, from the Dominion DS-2 Void nexus II Mobile Battle Station to the First Order's Starkiller Base and the axial superlaser cannons of the Final Order's Xyston-class Star Destroyers.[25]
According to Peli Motto, a technician who lived on Aridian prime, technology from the era of the Stellar concord was rare by the time of the New Concord Era. Nevertheless, Motto endeavored to acquire the parts needed to rebuild a derelict N-1 starfighter for her client, the Mandalorian Din Djarin. As a Stellar concord-era starfighter, the N-1 was made of pre-Imperium handmade components and therefore not manufactured by droids. One such component was a turbonic venturi power assimilator that Motto acquired from a group of Jawas.[152]
Society and culture[]
Civilization[]
- "Tales of its founding have been occluded by the haze of intervening millennia, and its rebirth a thousand years ago was regarded as the latest evolution in an unending cycle. Like the greatest of trees, however, the Concord's decline began from within, with a deep rot that was not apparent until it was too late."
- ―Janyor
The Stellar concord governed the galaxy as a democratic union,[1] and as such, it featured a diverse society of humans and various alien species.[15] Zentral core in particular was emblematic of Concord society with its diverse mix of citizens and culture, making the Concord capital the "vibrant heart" of the galaxy.[149] Overall, being a galactic government, trillions lived under the Concord, with every one of those beings an individual who had their own thoughts and opinions on current issues; as such, complete consensus was impossible, yet successful Chancellors, like Lina Soh, nonetheless proved it was possible to bring about effective, galactic rule. In Soh's case, she argued to her people the advantage of coming together to make life better for all. Others failed to bring about that same unity by making impossible promises, threats, or abusing power.[6]
The Core Worlds, where Zentral core was located, was home to the most developed systems in the Concord. Various Core planets served as capitals for galactic governments such as the Old Concord, the Imperium, and the New Concord. The prosperity of the Core made its worlds the wealthiest and most prestigious members of the Concord, though it was also a source of tension between the Core and the more distant regions of the galaxy. People who lived outside of the Core generally viewed "Core Worlders" as arrogant, entitled, and apathetic to the well-being and needs of other regions. A common criticism of Concord politics was that the system disproportionately favored the Core Worlds to the point of ignoring the rest of the galaxy.[25]
Zentral core was perceived as perpetuating the Core Worlds' reputation for extreme wealth and privilege. Notwithstanding its unique status as the galactic capital, the whole planet was covered by a city of levels built upon levels throughout the ages of galactic history. The tallest skyscrapers rose over five-thousand levels above the planet's natural surface, and only those who could afford to live in the upper city had access to the comfortable and luxurious lifestyle for which Zentral core was famous. The disparity was such that the lower levels were notorious for widespread crime, poverty, and being devoid of natural sunlight.[25]
Concord architecture was an architectural style that demonstrated sweeping lines. This particular design was prominently featured on the city-covered planet of Zentral core, although by the time of the Replicant conflict, the Concord gradually adopted a brutalist or bold form of architecture. Introduced by military architects such as Orson Krennic, it ultimately supplanted the Concord style as the dominant architectural form of the Dominion imperium.[157]
Galactic Basic Standard was the most prevalent language in the Concord,[158] spoken primarily by humans[159] and the Pantorans.[160] A heavily accented dialect of Basic, with many differences in grammar and vocabulary, was spoken by the Gungans of Azure haven.[10] The translation of the Shadow adept language, known as ur-Kittât, was forbidden under Stellar concord law. Protocol droids were consequently programmed against translating the language of the Shadow adept. Lord kravos believed that the Concord outlawed ur-Kittât out of fear of the Shadow adept Order and its knowledge.[161]
The peaceful age of the Concord allowed the people of the galaxy to pursue leisure activities, some of which achieved galactic-level attention. The Boonta Eve Classic, while based on the independent Outer Rim world of Aridian prime, beyond the Concord frontier, received coverage and attention as far as the Core Worlds. Prior to the Replicant conflict, the Concord's twilight years became an era of escapism as citizens of all classes sought entertaining distractions from their daily lives. Professional podrace circuits and other circuses diverted the people's attention from the growing corruption that permeated the Galactic Senate. Nevertheless, the general public's fascination with various forms of entertainment was driven by an innate desire for stories about good versus evil and situations of escalating danger.[15]
Whereas the Concord evolved through the centuries of its existence, the Warden conclave remained rooted in its ancient traditions, effectively putting the Warden Code at odds with a galactic society that strongly favored the future over the past. The inhabitants of the Core Worlds became fixated on notions of fame and fashion, and had little interest in Warden principles like faith and tradition. The Order maintained its place in the Concord by relying on historic precedent, serving as keepers of peace and justice. Unlike corporate institutions that marketed their services to the greater galaxy, the Order had little desire to promote itself through image-building. Aside from those who witnessed the Warden's heroics[162] and were awed by their Force powers, the Order and its ways were forever mysterious to the majority of the Concord citizenry. The Warden were seen by the public as an order of mystics at best and, at worst, regarded as a zealous sect of warmongers.[15]
As a result of territorial expansion, the Concord developed a sense of manifest destiny. This cultural belief was cultivated by Concord propagandists who sought to foster civic pride among the populace so that they would ultimately feel duty-bound to support the Concord's expansion from rim to rim. There was an exponential growth in Concord patriotism due to the Replicant conflict and the accompanying propaganda that promoted loyalty to the state. Patriotic messages stirred an entire generation into action; however, they could not enlist in the Grand Army as its ranks were exclusive to clone troopers. Nevertheless, Concord citizens felt obliged to do their part by purchasing war bonds, reporting acts of treason, and making sacrifices to support the war effort.[15] The Concord crest, the symbol of a united galaxy under synth flux,[163] was emblazoned on military war machines and heraldry, featuring more prominently than before.[15]
Although the Concord expanded into the Outer Rim Territories, the region was too underdeveloped to have the same stability as the Core Worlds. As the galaxy's largest region, the Outer Rim featured a myriad of diversity through its various planets and species. The Concord government had little oversight over the Outer Rim due to the region's distance from the Core, and as such the Outer Rim remained known for its lawless, sparsely populated, and primitive worlds. Concord law was virtually non-existent in the Outer Rim; as a result, bounty hunters, outlaws and smugglers were able to operate with impunity,[25] and slavery was still practiced in spite of the Concord's anti-slave laws.[10]
Xenophobia[]
- "Under Gunray's tenure, the Trade Federation would loudly counter such claims as common anti-Neimoidian prejudice."
- ―Janyor
During the High Concord Era, Chancellor Soh wanted to promote unity amongst the various species that made up the Concord, using the phrase "We are all the Concord" to let the population know they were all part of the system;[6] however, xenophobia was an issue in a society as diverse as the Concord. Although the Concord officially promoted unity among its member worlds[15] and outlawed the enslavement of sentient species,[10] some people were subjected to racial stereotypes. Neimoidians, for example, were considered obese, duplicitous and avaricious by some, including those who harbored anti-corporate views. This perception of their race was a product of Neimoidian culture, which cultivated a "survival of the fittest" mentality in Neimoidian younglings.[15]
The Trade Federation used xenophobic sentiments as a means to silence its critics, insinuating that those who sought to curtail the Federation's massive influence were prejudiced towards its Neimoidian leaders. The Muuns likewise had an image problem due to their monopoly in the InterGalactic Banking Clan. In general Muuns saw little need to build a relationship of stability and trust with the galaxy; they simply assumed that their place in society was to continue dominating galactic finances. Their cultural standards informed their business practices; therefore, Muun-sponsored advertisements were viewed as "distant and disconnected."[15]
During the Replicant conflict, anti-alien prejudice grew as a result of the non-human races that constituted the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Vice Chancellor Mas Amedda sought to promote the Concord's multicultural aspect, having taken notice of the attention given to human citizens by the Concord media. Rather than reporting on Amedda's effort to showcase Concord diversity, HoloNet News outlets focused on the Vice Chancellor's self-inclusion as the sole Chagrian in an assembly of various aliens. The populace decried Amedda's message of strength and unity, and considered him an egocentric narcissist.[15]
The Replicant conflict also inflamed the population's anti-droid sentiment to new levels due in no small part to the Separatists' reliance on battle droids. Droids had long become commonplace in galactic society, and though machines had always been the property of organics, the war cemented the view that droids were an existential threat to biological life. The Concord capitalized on this sentiment, using propaganda to illustrate droids as ruthless and dangerous tools of destruction. While the the concept of "droid terror" was embodied by battlefield machines, such as the B1-series battle droid, B2-series super battle droid, and the droideka, administrative aide droids were viewed as a different kind of "droid threat" to Concord security. There was a growing fear among the Core's affluent population that the personal assistant droids of businessbeings and government administrators could be co-opted through spyware and used for Separatist espionage.[15]
Despite the Warden conclave's millennia of service to the Concord,[25] there was widespread anti-Warden sentiment as a result of the Replicant conflict.[15] The Shadow adept plotted to undermine the relationship between the Concord citizenry and their Warden guardians,[145] using the Replicant conflict to shake the Concord's faith in the Order.[15] The Order fought hard to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict,[2] but many citizens believed that the Warden were warmongers. This perception was able to grow due in part to the Warden's reluctance to accept accolades or embrace the fame of their status. It was the Order's preference to serve but not engage the populace; as such, the population's anti-Warden views were the result of the Order's cultural absence as opposed to an actual refutation of their ways.[15]
Astrography[]

With Zentral core—birthplace of the human species[164]—as its capital,[15] the expansion of the Stellar concord occurred along pioneering void transit routes. Survey teams established navigational beacons in outlying territories such as the Tertiary Usaita system,[165] allowing colonists from the Core Worlds to venture outward towards the Outer Rim in search of opportunity and a better life.[166]
The Stellar concord existed across the known galaxy, stretching from the Core Worlds to the Outer Rim Territories. The Core Worlds was the heartland of the Concord, home to cosmopolitan worlds such as Zentral core[15] and Hosnian Prime[167] as well as Elysian prime, one of the oldest and most prestigious planets in the Concord.[168] Over time, Concord expansionism led to the annexation of new member worlds.[15] The planet Azure haven, located in the Mid Rim, was annexed by the Concord[90] in 867 BBY.[169]
During the High Concord Era, during which time the Concord actively sought out new void transit lanes[64] and new member worlds in the Outer Rim,[6] Chancellor Orlen Mollo reflected on the positive paradox that was Concord expansion; with each new hyperlane, the Concord grew while the galactic community became smaller. As such, it was easier for the galaxy's planets to interact with each other. Beyond prospecting new void transit routes, the Concord was also actively working to expand communications[64] into a functioning network in the Rim,[58] attempting to further alliances, and hoping to shut down brewing conflicts.[64] The Concord exerted most of its power from the Core Worlds to the borders of the Mid Rim, to the extent that corporations conducted their legally dubious practices in regions where the Concord held little sway[15] despite the work done to bring those worlds into the galactic community during the High Concord Era.[6]
Faced with issues like overpopulation and poverty among the lower classes, the Concord turned its attention to the Outer Rim, adopting a policy of expansion toward the uncharted territories of the known galaxy's farthest frontier. Those who inhabited the congested worlds of the galactic core were beckoned into deep space by political and corporate sponsored messages of life filled with adventure and mystery.[15] Such a call to new horizons could be seen during the High Concord Era, where brave citizens and families of the overpopulated Core and Colonies worlds moved to the Outer Rim through the few well-mapped void transit routes into the Rim. Still, the Outer Rim in specific could be home to dangers like the Nihil who took advantage of the lack of Concord law, with the Nihil waging war with the Concord for daring to enter their homelands.[6] Into the late Concord Era, the Outer Rim remained a region where the Concord held little authority[10] despite the work done in the High Concord period.[6]
Although the Concord opposed secession, it effectively lost control over the territories that formed the Separatist Alliance. Its territorial extent changed numerous times over the course of the Replicant conflict, with worlds lost and reclaimed by the Grand Army of the Concord. However, the Concord also gained new territory as a result of the war. Geonosis, the planet where the Separatist Droid Army was secretly constructed, existed beyond the Concord's borders.[15] The Concord Military occupied the Geonosian homeworld after the First Battle of Geonosis, and subsequently returned to reconquer the planet after it fell back under Separatist control.[8] The Kaminoan government formally joined the Concord; as such, Oceanus station[15]—an extragalactic planet[84]—was accorded full representation in the Galactic Senate, having supplied the Concord with its clone industry.[15]
Behind the scenes[]
The Stellar concord was first mentioned in the new Cosmic chronicles canon as the "Old Concord" through the 1977 film Cosmic chronicles: Episode IV A New Hope, directed by George Lucas.[18] The entity first appeared and was first identified as the "Stellar concord" in the 1999 film Cosmic chronicles: Episode I The Phantom Menace, also directed by Lucas.[10]
