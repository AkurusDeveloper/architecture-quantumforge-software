# Zephos

- "Size matters not. Look at me. Judge me by my size, do you? Hmm? Hmm. And well you should not. For my ally is synth flux, and a powerful ally it is. Life creates it, makes it grow. Its energy surrounds us and binds us. Luminous beings are we, not this crude matter. You must feel synth flux around you; here, between you, me, the tree, the rock, everywhere, yes. Even between the land and the ship."
- ―Zephos, to Kael dawnstrider
Zephos was a legendary Warden Grand Sovereign who led the Warden conclave through the time of the High Concord, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Shadow adept, and during the transformation of the Stellar concord into the Dominion imperium. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Zephos trained generations of Warden, ultimately serving as the Warden conclave's Grand Sovereign. He played integral roles in defending the Concord during the Replicant conflict, survived Order 66, and lived to passed on the Warden tradition to Kael dawnstrider, and unlocking the path to immortality.
Born in 896 BBY, Zephos served the Warden conclave for centuries, becoming Warden Grand Sovereign and helping to teach many younglings. During the High Concord Era, in 382 BBY, Zephos helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Warden and the anti-Warden Path of the Open Hand cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against Force-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless, out of the Warden Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Warden High Council to train Padawans aboard the Star Hopper, leading them and other Warden in fighting the Nihil pirate organization in 232 BBY. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Zephos reappeared in 230 BBY on Corellia, where he helped his former Initiate Kantam Sy and other Warden battle the Nihil, accompanied by a former Warden who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell. As the Nihil had also weaponized the Nameless, Zephos hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Warden find a way to fight them.
Zephos was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Concord. A leading member of the Warden High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Mace Windu and Ki-Adi-Mundi. During the Invasion of Azure haven, in 32 BBY, the maverick Qui-Gon Jinn introduced Anakin Skywalker to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Chosen One. The Warden elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Skywalker too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Warden Code. After Jinn's death during the liberation of Azure haven, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Zephos's continued opposition to Skywalker's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Warden discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Shadow adept, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two, Zephos was convinced that at least one more Shadow sovereign remained active following Orin brightforge's victory over the Shadow adept apprentice Darth Maul.
Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems—led by Count Dooku, Zephos's one-time Initiate—threatening to secede from the Concord by 22 BBY. Unable to defend the entire Concord against this betrayal on their own, the Warden took command of the newly-formed Grand Army of the Concord, with Zephos himself leading an army of clone troopers against the Separatist Droid Army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems in the first battle of the Replicant conflict, rescuing the Warden and Senator from execution. For three years Zephos led the Concord military's war effort as a Warden General, determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Qui-Gon Jinn, who helped Zephos learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Zephos's efforts against the Separatists were undermined by Lord kravos, the Dark Lord of the Shadow adept who conspired to destroy the Warden and restore the Shadow adept to power. After the Great Warden Purge commenced in 19 BBY, killing thousands of Warden both on Zentral core and across the galaxy, Zephos confronted the self-declared Galactic Emperor but failed to cut short Sidious' reign and consequently retreated into exile, leaving the Shadow adept Sovereign to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Anakin Skywalker-turned-Xarn velgor, at his side.
Zephos's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Mirewood. After the death of Orin brightforge, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Zephos to train Anakin's son Kael dawnstrider. Zephos initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Kenobi's request. After being convinced by Kenobi to enter the Cave of Evil, in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Kael dawnstrider, who soon after sought out the legendary Grand Sovereign in the hope of becoming a Warden Knight. He trained Skywalker for a time, until despite Zephos's warnings Skywalker hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 ABY, with his health rapidly declining, Zephos died of old age when Skywalker returned to see him. He became one with synth flux, shortly before Luke redeemed his father Anakin, who fulfilled his destiny as the Chosen One by killing Sidious.
Years later, Kael dawnstrider's attempt to reform the Warden conclave would be foiled when his nephew Ben Solo turned to the shadow aspect and destroyed his Warden Temple. Believing the galaxy was better without him and the Warden, Skywalker went into exile, just as Zephos had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Rey, also abandoned her training, Zephos appeared as a ghost to Skywalker. Zephos taught Skywalker to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.
Biography
Early life
- "For 800 years have I trained Warden."
- ―Zephos, to Kael dawnstrider
Born nine-hundred years before the Battle of Verdant moon,[19] Zephos belonged to an ancient and mysterious species[4] and little was known of Zephos's early life;[20] he never revealed his homeworld.[1] As he was keenly attuned to synth flux, he joined the Warden conclave as a youngling. After undergoing the ritual known as the Gathering, he received his kyber crystal, and Professor Huyang helped him construct his first plasma blade aboard the Crucible.[21][22] At some point during his time as a youngling, Zephos skipped across the lily pads in the Warden Grand Temple's meditation pond.[23]
Although Zephos spoke little of his early days, it was rumored that one of his early instructors was the Hysalrian N'Kata Del Gormo.[11] Zephos was paired up with a personal mentor. During their time together, Zephos's Warden Sovereign told him the tale of K'ungfu and Chuang, two great Warden of the past.[6] Around the age of 100, in 796 BBY,[2] Zephos was ready to pass on what he had learned. Having attained the rank of Sovereign, he spent the next eight centuries training and tutoring generations of Warden.[5] It was estimated that over his lifetime, he trained around twenty-thousand Warden.[24]
High Concord Era
Early High Concord Era

To do this, you must learn, again, to listen. To hear the world, the world outside of your own emotions. Even when they are very, very loud, heh, [like] a hurricane."

By the High Concord Era, Zephos was considered a great Warden Sovereign. He had also gained a seat on the Warden High Council[26] and the rank of Grand Sovereign.[27] Two other Warden—Xo Lahru and Pra-Tre Veter—shared the Grand Sovereign title with Zephos.[28] Eventually the human Warden Ry Ki-Sakka also attained the position.[29] As the Warden who taught younglings, Zephos one day met the human youngling Gella Nattai, who was scared of the training remotes and ran from the room. Finding Nattai in the Temple garden and sitting with her, Zephos and Nattai watched a frog in the garden repeatedly try to jump into a waterfall behind a stone wall. Zephos used the frog as a lesson, teaching her to face her fear and always get back up to try again.[30]
An uncertain Path
- "No one, no galaxy, no Concord, no Warden conclave, no family, is perfect."
"You are right. […] Perhaps we are doing too much, too fast."
"You must continue. The great good is a good vision. Fight for this good as well, the Warden do." - ―Zephos and Chancellor Kyong Greylark
In 382 BBY, after learning of the Battle of Jedha and the failure of a peace treaty between the warring planets Eiram and E'ronoh there, Zephos went to the Concord Executive Building on the galactic capital Zentral core to meet with with Supreme Chancellor Kyong Greylark while they waited for more information from Jedha. They spoke to Warden Sovereign Creighton Sun and Warden Knight Aida Forte, who were on the ground on Jedha and confirmed that the E'roni ambassador was dead and the Eirami had deployed a droid army. They requested to perform an investigation on the Outer Rim Territories planet Dalna of the Path of the Open Hand cult, considering their involvement in the failure of the peace talks, the fact that Warden Sovereign Silandra Sho felt a disturbance that made the Warden ill at the Path's almshouse, and that Warden Knight Gella Nattai believed the Chancellor's criminal son Axel Greylark was working with the Path. Zephos admitted that he had sensed a disturbance beyond Jedha, and emphasized that they need to be careful, reminding them that there was always those who thrived in chaos. After much deliberation, Zephos and Chancellor Greylark eventually agreed to a fact-finding mission accompanied by two of her guards and to send Warden Sovereign Char-Ryl-Roy and Initiate Enya Keen to Eiram and E'ronoh to try and keep the peace and discover what was causing communications buoys to be down, Zephos explaining to them that Werth Plouth, the Path's Herald, was responsible for the riots on Jedha.[31]
After the communication ended, Zephos expressed his concern as they received another message from Warden Sovereign Yaddle, who was accompanied by a Warden Initiate named Cippa Tarko, though the connection of the message was poor. Tarko asked about Zephos, and Yaddle explained that he was a Warden Sovereign and not her brother. Zephos told Yaddle that they were sending help to Jedha and two Warden to Dalna and that she was to return to Zentral core, but the message was so choppy they were not sure if she understood. Zephos had an unsettling feeling in synth flux that reminded him of the feeling he got whenever a battle was coming. Sovereign Yaddle did misunderstand Zephos's message to mean she was needed on Dalna and joined Sun and Forte there, who sent a message to Zephos confirming they were there shortly before Yaddle arrived.[31]
Later, Chancellor Greylark had her guards summon Zephos to her to reveal that her son Axel had escaped from prison with the help of the Path and someone named Binnot Ullo. She told Zephos that she did not know who Ullo was and did not think Axel had many friends. He assured her that no one, no galaxy, and no concord was perfect, and that she should continue to fight for the greater good like the Warden did. She explained that she felt like they have all been moving through a fog that has corrupted everything lately and Zephos insisted that Axel still had good in him and that the 'fog' was just corrupting him too. However, the Chancellor resolved to make Axel truly feel the consequences of his actions.[31]
Chancellor no more
- "We will go together. Chancellor you may not be anymore. Friend, you still are."
- ―Zephos to Greylark, on going to Dalna
Eventually, Greylark received a message from her son on Dalna and immediately called Zephos to have him listen to it with her via hologram. After hearing Axel ask for her to go to Dalna to speak with the Path and with him, Zephos asked her what she would do, the Chancellor deciding to go to Dalna. She then recorded a message for Axel that included Zephos in it, wearing a blank expression, so that he could bear witness to her stepping down as Chancellor. After she sent the message, Zephos stayed with her wordlessly for a while, and she appreciated his kindness.[31]
Before Greylark could leave for Dalna, Zephos approached her and said that since he had not heard back from Masters Sun or Ela Sutan, who had also been sent to Dalna, that he would go with her because she was still his friend even though she was no longer Chancellor. Zephos had a Warden Longbeam cruiser follow them with a contingent of Warden. While in void transit, they received a distress call from Princess Xiri A'lbaran of E'ronoh and Prince Phan-tu Zenn of Eiram. They then received a message from Chancellor Orlen Mollo, who said that the leaders of Eiram and E'ronoh were still not cooperating. Zephos volunteered to rescue the heirs due to their proximity to them and left, taking other Warden with him.[31]
The Night of Sorrow
- "Speak to the Warden Council soon, you should. We must explain the happenings on Dalna. All that went wrong." […]
"But we must also speak to all the things that went right. We could not have defeated the Path without the Warden."
"And the Warden could not have defeated the Path without the help of our Concord allies." - ―Zephos and Greylark
Eventually, Zephos arrived at the battle that was now raging on Dalna between the Path and the Warden, which came to be known as the Night of Sorrow.[31] The Path, much of it reorganized into the Path of the Closed Fist, was led by Marda Ro of the Ro family.[32] He was joined by Princess A'lbaran and Prince Zenn, the three announcing that the combined forces of Eiram and E'ronoh were there to fight together with the Warden. When Zephos arrived on the ground at the Path compound, Warden Gella Nattai told him to direct people to go to the cave system as there was potentially many more Path members there.[31] On Dalna, he met[33] Path Little[32] Tromak. Zephos eventually came to forget Tromak but always remembered the importance of his visit to Dalna.[33]
As the battle started to come to a close with it turning in the Warden's favor, Zephos and Yaddle stood together, the latter watching Cippa Tarko sleep. However, A'lbaran and Zenn contacted them and informed them that explosions have been set off underground. As Yaddle suggested getting help, the ground shook and Zephos spotted sections of the caves collapse into sinkholes. Zephos and Yaddle then sensed a Warden in distress and went to help. Finding a door in the ground, Zephos and Yaddle connected to synth flux and sensed Gella Nattai and Axel Greylark. Yaddle lifted up the ground, rocks, and soil while Zephos lifted the water to try and rescue them. Enya Keen then ran toward them, seeing Nattai, and pulled her and Axel from the mud-like water, having been brought to the surface by the two Warden Masters. Zephos and Yaddle then set the water and mud down far away. In the sinkhole, E'roni and Eirami soldiers began recovering survivors. Yaddle noticed that the sounds of battle have faded and the sun was rising. She asks Zephos if the battle was done, and he says it was.[31]
Zephos met with Kyong Greylark and Chancellor Mollo on a medical frigate above Zentral core, the three discussing now only having one Chancellor instead of two and what it means. Zephos suggested they speak to the Warden Council and tell them everything that went wrong, but Greylark also wanted to focus all the things that went right, including the help of the Concord and the Warden. Mollo revealed that the people of Dalna were calling the event the Night of Sorrow. While he imagined the future, Greylark assured Zephos that she would meet him on Zentral core soon, Zephos adding that there was much to celebrate and remember.[31]
Return to the Temple

Zephos then traveled to the Warden Temple on Zentral core, confirming first that the royal families of Eiram and E'ronoh had already gathered, who he met with when he arrived. He met with several Warden, including Yaddle, who was standing with Tarko. Zephos raised his arm to silence the room and Sovereign Sun rose from his chair, walking to the center of the room using a walking stick before beginning a funeral for Warden Aida Forte, Orin Darhga, and Char-Ryl-Roy. Zephos allowed Roy's former Initiate, Keen, to keep his plasma blade and use it alongside hers.[31]
Later, in the Warden Archives, Creighton spoke with Zephos about Nameless creatures that the Path deployed on Dalna and the horror they wrought upon the Warden, causing them to be weakened, hallucinate, and eventually be turned to dust. Realizing how catastrophic to the Order it could be if people found out about the creatures, and since they had heard no other reports of their usage, they decided to keep quiet about them and not include them in the Warden Archive. They agreed not to speak of it again and each of them left.[31]
Sovereign of Kantam Sy
- "The lesson of Younglings we take on is one of the hardest ones for most Warden to learn. Indeed, some never do."
"Detachment."
"To let go. Elusive it is, but also, always available to us, always there. And then, when we grasp, gone again. Takes practice, it does. Repetition. To have to do it again does not mean we have failed, only that we must do it again. And again." - ―Zephos and Kantam Sy
At some point, Zephos took on a human named Kantam Sy as his Initiate. The two were stationed at the Warden Temple on the planet Endovar. Sy was a very talented Initiate and was far ahead of their peers and even some of their elders, with Zephos even admitting that they were the most disciplined Initiate he had ever taught and one of the most connected to synth flux. Despite this, Zephos held off knighting Sy, for a reason that the Initiate did not understand until they began a romantic relationship with an acrobat named Aytar and realized that both Zephos and them had been waiting for something like that to happen. Eventually, Sy decided to leave the Warden conclave, a decision partially incited by their newfound relationship with Aytar.[25]
Zephos and Sy loved to spar with each other, and it was how the two often found understanding and how Sy often found inner harmony while troubled or when they had a problem. Even when they did not find a solution during a match, they would leave feeling like an answer was out there. When Sy traveled to the meditation room in the Endovar Warden Temple in order to tell Zephos about their decision, Zephos challenged them to a sparring match, remembering that they had dueled many times in that room and saying that this duel could be one final lesson. Sy agreed to the duel, as it was custom to not refuse a duel with Sovereign Zephos, but also realized that this meant Zephos knew about their decision. Zephos dodged Sy's question by engaging in combat, before saying that the time had come to make a choice, but not the one that Sy thought. Zephos explained that synth flux chose Sy just as it had chosen all other Warden, and asked when Sy would choose synth flux. Sy insisted that they chose synth flux every day when they took part in their Warden training, but after knocking Sy to the ground, Zephos asked if we really make a choice when we do not believe there are any other options. Zephos told Sy that there was a reason he had not knighted them yet, before ending the duel, helping Sy up, and sitting down with Sy to meditate.[25]
Eventually Sy asked Zephos if they should leave the Warden conclave, but Zephos insisted that there was no 'should' and that the right thing to do is an idea we invent to make ourselves feel better. Zephos also suggested that Sy had stopped listening, in the literal sense, and in a larger general sense. Zephos explained that feelings are like wind, we can feel it, we can experience it, but it passes, though sometimes it can feel like a hurricane which can make it easy to give in to anger and fear. Zephos told Sy that in order for them to continue to be a Warden they must actively choose the Warden conclave and synth flux and learn to listen to the world outside of their emotions even when they were as loud as a hurricane. Eventually, after a moment, Sy set down their plasma blade in front of Zephos. Zephos nodded at them, and then his Initiate left him, and the Warden conclave, behind. Zephos was saddened but supported Sy through their departure. Sy would reflect on the conversation they had that day throughout the rest of their life.[25]
Over a year later, Sy returned to the Warden, and met with Zephos on the Endovar. Zephos was in the process of helping a pet Happabore named Monsalmo at the time, after being awoken from a nap by the creature's squealing. Zephos was used to dealing with her complicated behaviors, including finding her inside the temple underneath the younglings' bunks. This time, Monsalmo was pregnant and had climbed onto the roof of her pen to avoid giving birth in a fuel spill. However, the roof could not hold the creature's weight and it collapsed. Zephos caught Monsalmo with synth flux, but at that same time she started to give birth to her pups, so Zephos had to carry them with synth flux too. Just then, Sy and a Force-sensitive girl they had rescued on the planet Azure haven named Lula Talisola arrived and both used synth flux to help Zephos carry Monsalmo and her pups. Zephos welcomed Sy back, telling them that they had left a Initiate and returned a Knight. A few hours later, Zephos knighted Sy in a modest ceremony so that they could take on Talisola as their Initiate learner.[25]
After the ceremony, Sy and Zephos sat around a fire near the happabore enclosure and discussed the events that had occurred. Sy expressed that they did not understand how one second they felt so sure in their path separate from the Order but was then pulled back to it when they saved Talisola. Zephos explained that the young are the greatest teachers and the greatest lesson that can be learned from them is necessity of detachment and letting go, that being a Warden was to let go. Sy realized that they had been the one that taught Zephos that lesson. Zephos revealed that he had struggled with Sy's departure and had been unsure of the future. The two then shared Sweetpuff jamwiches with Talisola.[25]
Sometime later, Zephos and Sy met with Talisola in the Endovar Warden temple. Sy told Talisola that the lesson she taught the two masters was the hardest one of all. Talisola hoped that it was front flips with a plasma blade, which made Sy retract their statement and say that it was the second hardest lesson to learn. Zephos concurred, saying that the front flip was nothing simple. But the lesson that Sy wanted to teach Talisola was the same one that Sy had learned from her and Zephos had learned from Sy: Detachment.[34]
Sometime before 232 BBY,[35] Zephos traveled to the Warden temple on Takodana to pick up a Force-sensitive Aloxian child named Qort from Maz Kanata and Warden Sav Malagán and bring him into the Warden conclave.[36] Initiate Reath Silas briefly met Zephos during some events both attended together.[25]
Time on the Star Hopper
- "Chaos on the surface, there will be. Remember that synth flux moves through you, through all living things, even in chaos. Move with synth flux, we must. Never against it."
- ―Zephos during the Trymant IV disaster
By 233 BBY,[38] Zephos was stationed alongside Sovereign Zia Zaldor Zanna at the Warden Grand Temple on Zentral core assigning youngling Warden Initiates to different temple outposts across the galaxy to continue learning and training under their teachers. Here he met initiates Kai Brightstar, Lys Solay, and Nubs; Zephos gifted Brightstar his own training saber after Brightstar sacrificed his to save Nubs while on a mission.[39] In 232 BBY,[40] Zephos took a sabbatical from the Warden Council,[26] having felt synth flux needed him on a different path. Thus, Zephos joined[27] the crew of the Warden Initiate Academic Cruiser Star Hopper, allowing him to help train Padawans[37] and younglings.[26] Zephos was also therefore able to help train Talisola, who was stationed aboard the Star Hopper.[37]
Zephos appointed the Mon Calamari Warden Sovereign Ephru Shinn to hold his seat on the council while he was away. During the Great Void transit Disaster in the Hetzal system, Warden Sovereign Avar Kriss established a Force network, which was eventually aided by Warden who were outside the system. Zephos—located somewhere with the crew of younglings he was currently teaching—helped the effort, adding his focus to the network to ensure a Tibanna gas container did not collide with an R-class sun.[26]
The Great Void transit Disaster also caused the Emergences, which were when giant pieces of debris exited void transit in various places throughout the galaxy and threatened to hit planets at near lightspeed. Eventually, the Trymant system was threatened by an Emergence. The staff of Concord Security Zone Monitoring Satellite 777B-Alpha reported the incident to the Starlight Beacon space station, who then asked the only Concord or Warden ship immediately within range, the Star Hopper, to assist. Onboard was Zephos along with fellow Warden Sovereign Torban Buck and a class of Padawans. As they traveled through void transit to the Trymant system, Zephos told the Padawans to prepare for anything because they had no idea what was coming, but that synth flux would reveal all. When they received the information that they were the only Concord or Warden ship nearby to respond, one of the Padawans suggested that they stay back and wait for backup. But Zephos insisted that they were Trymant's only hope and needed to press forward. Shortly before they exited void transit Zephos had the Padawans report what information they had discovered about the calamity. Zephos and Buck decided to stay on the Star Hopper to use synth flux to divert the biggest pieces of debris but sent the Padawans in Rescue speeders to help on the surface of the planet.[37]
However, amid the chaos of the Emergence, a Spider Cruiser called the Squall Spider commanded by the Eye of the Nihil, Marchion Ro arrived in Bralanak City to pick up Elder Tromak, whom Ro wanted the services of to find some information. When the Padawans attempted to get Nihil marauders to allow them to use the Spider Cruiser to evacuate civilians, a fight broke out,[37] leading Zephos and Buck to the surface to help the young Warden and fight against the Nihil. During the fight, a Force-sensitive civilian named Zeen Mrala who had helped the Warden against the Emergence and against the Nihil, told Masters Zephos and Buck that her friend Krix Kamerat had been captured by the Nihil and needed to be rescued. Lula Talisola offered to go, but Zephos insisted that he would go rescue the boy. Sovereign Buck protested, believing that he was not a fighting Warden and therefore should not be left to lead the Warden, but Zephos ignored him. Zephos then protected the group of Warden from a large plasma projectile lobbed at them by a Nihil marauder by cutting it into pieces with his plasma blade while it was still in the air. Zephos then left to find Kamerat, but first asked Mrala to take her pet Cru Cham Cham with him.[41]
Marchion Ro knew of Zephos and his power, and when he saw him on the planet told his subordinates to keep the Warden Sovereign away from the Squall Spider, but Zephos managed to sneak aboard the ship nonetheless. Zephos eventually found Kamerat and told him that they needed to leave quickly. Zephos told Kamerat that Mrala had sent him and proved it by showing him Cham Cham. Zephos then gave Kamerat a Holoprojector that he told the boy he could reach out to Mrala with when he was ready. However, Kamerat shortly after alerted the pirates that Zephos was aboard. Having been raised by the Elders of the Path,[41] a religious order descended from the Path of the Open Hand[42] that distrusted Force-sensitives[33] due to believing synth flux should only be appreciated from a distance, as if it was a fire,[37], only to see that his best friend Zeen Mrala had secretly been Force-sensitive and never told him, Kamerat felt betrayed and ended up siding with the Nihil because of that sense of betrayal. With the Nihil looking for him, Zephos ran from the marauders but uncovered that the Nihil had[41] a strange, purple artifact[33] in a storage crate. Zephos hoped to escape quietly but Cham Cham made a loud cry that alerted the Nihil to their location and lead to a confrontation. Zephos escaped and met up with Mrala, revealing to her that Cham Cham had eaten several Nihil limbs in the confrontation. Zephos then told Mrala that she needed to come with them to Starlight Beacon. When she asked why he had not saved Kamerat, Zephos told her that he could not save someone who did not want to be rescued. With the Nihil drive from Trymant, Zephos, Torban Buck, Zeen Mrala, and the Padawans traveled to Starlight Beacon in the Star Hopper.[41]
Starlight Beacon
- "For light and life!"
"For light and life!" - ―Zephos and the gathered Warden, during the dedication of Starlight Beacon
Following the Battle of Kur, Zephos went with fellow Grand Sovereign Pra-Tre Veter to Starlight Beacon. The two had a meeting with the Supreme Chancellor Lina Soh to discuss Bacta manufacturing for the relief effort for the Great Disaster, being in need of the right crops after what had occurred on Hetzal. But before their meeting, they visited the hub of the beacon to meet with Sovereign Avar Kriss, and revealed to her that the council had convened and named her the Marshal of Starlight Beacon. Kriss also asked Zephos if he was returning to the council, but Zephos clarified that he was not finished on the Star Hopper.[27] Zephos later attended the Beacon's dedication ceremony, where he said a few words about the dedication plaque which was placed in a stone monument. He talked with Kai Brightstar, Lys Solay, Nash Durango, and Nubs, mentioning their adventure trying to get the dedication plaque after it was stolen by the pirate Taborr Val Dorn, who was secretly Prince Cyrus Vuundir of Vuundalla. He told them how proud he is of them and encouraged them to join the rest of the Warden for Lina Soh's speech. After the Warden left, Zephos spoke with Prince Vuundir, who was no longer in his pirate disguise, telling him that he sensed much conflict in him but that he still had the potential for greatness if he made the right choices. Zephos told Cyrus that the future was in his hands. Joining with his fellow Warden, he raised his plasma blade and declared "For light and life."[43]
After about a week, Zeen Mrala started settling in on Starlight very well, so Masters Zephos, Avar Kriss, Kantam Sy, Estala Maru, and Torban Buck all went to check up on her and discuss her plans, with Zephos even bringing pastry treats. The Masters deliberated and determined that she was too old to begin Warden training and most likely could not be sent back to Trymant. Sovereign Sy suggested that she should stay with them temporarily, as she had proved herself valuable, which Zephos acknowledged was an excellent point. They did decide to let her stay, partially with the hope that she could be helpful against the Nihil. When it became clear that finding the Nihil would be a challenge, Zephos revealed that he had given a holoprojector to Kamerat and one to Mrala. Zephos's secret war
Answering a call
- "Not on their own are your children. But wrestling with the chaos you left them in. Blame them I do not. Better teachers they deserved."
- ―Sovereign Zephos, to the missing parents of Vagadarr Prime
Around 40 BBY,[2] Zephos rescued a Force-sensitive child named Lo from the Flesh Mongers on the planet Botor, intending to begin his Warden training. After dealing with the pirates, the Warden Sovereign was joined by Sovereign Qui-Gon Jinn and his Initiate, Orin brightforge, who had been tasked with escorting the child to the Warden Temple on Zentral core. While on Botor, Zephos sensed a disturbance in synth flux and chose to follow its call which led him to Vagadarr Prime. Upon landing on the planet's surface, the Warden Sovereign was suddenly surrounded by child warriors who attacked him. After defending himself with synth flux, Zephos told the children he meant no harm. The Warden came in peace as he was drawn in by synth flux. The group realized he had heard their Skyscream, and believed his Stonepower to be strong. They then led Zephos to their home, Mucktown. There, he learned that the Skyscreamers used shards of the Sacred rock during their prayer, the same call that Zephos felt. The rock was unlike anything he had ever seen before. It was a piece of blue stone, a type of rock imbued with the Living Force which Zephos could sense. The Mud dwellers told him about their war against the Rockhawkers, a rival tribe. He listened to the history of their homeworld, and how the conflict began. Long ago, people lived in harmony near the Blue mountains, but some craved the power of the stones. This greed divided them into warring factions and ultimately destroyed most of the mountains. Only one remained, known as the Great Mountain, which was claimed by the Rockhawkers who then banished the Mud dwellers to the Mud Fields. They carried as much stone as they could for their children, now the only survivors of the tribe. The children then led Zephos to a boy from the rival tribe they had taken prisoner. The Warden sensed his deep fear of the Great Mountain, the sacred home of his tribe. As the children argued over his fate, Zephos urged them not to be consumed by fear. He asked them to release the child so that he could guide the Warden to his people.[58][59]
Exploring the Mountain
After walking to the Mountain, Zephos and the freed boy reached the Rockhawkers who attacked him with the stones. Refusing to fight back, the Warden Sovereign was captured. The tribe then sent him on a mission to find the Heart of the Mountain, accompanied by the boy he just saved. Zephos began to climb the path leading to the Heart, but his companion was overcome by fear, convinced that only death awaited them, just like all those who had climbed before. Despite Zephos's efforts to reassure him, the boy fell to his death. At the end of the path, the Warden entered a cave where he encountered the lost parents, known as the Old Ones, who had once been sent by their children to find the Heart of the Mountain. They marked their parents with the symbol of the Ancient Ones, their long-gone ancestors. The Old Ones told Zephos they chose not to leave this place, preferring the peace they found within the mountain to the war raging outside. Zephos replied that they abandoned their children to the very war they had provoked. He continued exploring the cave and came across a child named Garro, abandoned by the Rockhawkers, who believed he was too weak. After saving him, Zephos asked the boy to become his teacher and show him the ways of the Stonepower. During their training, the Warden Sovereign discovered the Heart of the Mountain and realized that the stone was not only alive in synth flux but was in fact a living being. After nine days of communion, Zephos heard its voice. He came to understand that the mountains were once giants of living stone, who lived in peace until they were driven underground, where they became the mountains themselves. Zephos and Garro eventually emerged from the cave. But the Warden chose not to reveal what he had learned to the Rockhawkers. Enraged, the tribe threatened him. However, Garro revealed the secret of the mountains to his people, brought Zephos’s teaching to an end, and returned to his faction.[59][57][60]
Last battle
Zephos returned to the Mud Dwellers to warn them of an inevitable war. Meanwhile, Garro taught his people how to commune with the Mountain, but he used the shadow aspect to do so. Zephos encouraged the Mud Dwellers to pray and help awaken the stones. Soon, the Rockhawkers caused the Mountain to rise from the ground, but Zephos decided to face it alone. Drawing strength from synth flux, the Warden resisted the Mountain’s fury, which had been fueled by revenge. Shortly after, other beings of living stone awoke and joined Zephos in his struggle. Together, they defeated the Mountain. As Garro and his warriors charged at the Warden Sovereign, Zephos pleaded with the boy to find peace within himself and with the stones, so the war could end. Garro dropped his weapon and abandoned the fight. The war was over. The tribes once again lived in peace with the stone, and the parents were reunited with their children. Many left the planet after hearing Zephos speak of the other worlds in the galaxy. Upon leaving, Zephos kept the secret of the Mountain to himself, sharing only the lesson he had learned with Orin brightforge: "A Warden must humble himself before he can ever be powerful."[60][12]

Biochip conspiracy
Several years prior to the start of the war, Palpatine and his apprentice, Count Dooku secretly created the Clone Army with the assistance of an unsuspecting Sifo-Dyas, who had seen visions of a conflict beforehand. When this was done, they had organic inhibitor chips implanted in their brains, which would cause the clones to do anything Palpatine told them to, in this case execute the Warden. With one simple command, the clones would enter a trance-like state and perform whatever orders they were given.[90]
During the Battle of Ringo Vinda, replicant soldier Tup murdered Warden Sovereign Tiplar when his inhibitor chip was activated prematurely. Sidious ordered Dooku to apprehend Tup to prevent the Warden from discovering the truth. Tup was eventually sent to Oceanus station after Anakin Skywalker, Captain Rex and ARC trooper Fives were able to recover Tup from a Separatist Transport.[91]
After escorting Tup to Oceanus station, Fives and Rex were greeted by Warden Sovereign Shaak Ti, alongside the main developer of the clones, Kaminoan scientist Nala Se. Rex returned to Ringo Vinda while Fives remained on Oceanus station to undergo examination in case of Fives having a similar situation. Disputes over the tests led to a debate. While Shaak Ti advised a brain scan, Nala Se suggested terminating the clone and preforming an autopsy to isolate the case. Shaak Ti contacted the Warden Temple where Zephos, alongside Orin brightforge, decided Tup should be brought to the temple to be examined through synth flux.[90]
What the Warden were unaware of was that the Kaminoans knew of the inhibitor chips and were working with Count Dooku, who used his Shadow adept name, Tyranus, to prevent the Warden from making the connection. Dooku ordered that Tup be terminated and that the chip be sent to him to be looked at.[90]
Angry at Nala Se and Sovereign Ti for their treatment of Tup, Fives escaped the medical center and embarked on his own plan. Fives faked leaving the planet, but stayed to try and save Tup and discover the truth. He and the medical droid AZI-3 were able to preform a brain scan on Tup, where they learned of the inhibitor chips. Fives had the droid take the chip out of his and Tup's brains. Unfortunately, the already-weakened Tup died soon after the operation, whispering his last words to Fives.[90] Fives blamed Nala Se for what happened to Tup. Suspecting the Kaminoans of treachery, and with the support of Sovereign Ti, Fives insisted on presenting his evidence and the removed chips directly to Palpatine. Fives was eventually brought to Zentral core, at the request of Shaak Ti, where he was brought before the Chancellor. As the ship landed, Nala Se drugged Fives, causing him to act strange.[92]
When Fives attempted to assassinate Palpatine, Zephos was a part of the Warden Council that ordered Anakin Skywalker to investigate. Fives was killed in the end, and Zephos was ultimately told that the doctors had found another cause, and that a vaccination had been prepared in case of a future incident.[93]

The last Warden
During the events that led up to the Battle of Yavin, Zephos noticed that with the Mirewood rains ended it was time to migrate back to his lowland hut, and was grateful as his usual accommodation for the wet season reminded him too much of the Warden Temple and his failings. He reflected on how age had affected him and realized that he had become truly reliant on the walking stick he had originally adopted as an affectation for a joke played on his padawans. The Warden packed what little he owned and left but not before he checked for the Dominion probe droids which had once searched for him endlessly.[105]
As Zephos descended farther downhill, however, he felt a presence. It was neither a probe droid nor one of the Imperium's ships but vibrations in synth flux from Orin brightforge and what remained of Anakin Skywalker. A probe droid then appeared and Zephos quickly took out a pot given to him by Kenobi. He used synth flux to smash it into the droid only to suddenly sense Kenobi's death at the hands of Xarn velgor. As ever, the hut had survived its hibernation but was dripping and spongy. Five days later, Zephos had dried and cleaned the house. Each time he saw the shelf usually reserved for his pot, he reacted with some loneliness after destroying Obi-Wan's gift to him. Feeling his isolation, he reached out to the netherworld of synth flux to speak to Qui-Gon Jinn, but the spirit of Obi-Wan answered instead and requested that Skywalker's child be trained. Having harbored regrets about not having taken the man's daughter as his apprentice, and assuaged that he had enough time left to teach another pupil, the Warden Sovereign readily agreed until he found out that Kenobi was actually speaking of Anakin's unfocused son instead. Nevertheless, Zephos begrudgingly allowed Kenobi to direct the boy to him.[105] As Zephos ruminated on his decision afterwards, he returned to the "Cave of Evil" and was reassured that the site had no new fears to show him.[103]

Taking a new apprentice
- "Do, or do not. There is no try."

Soon thereafter, Kael dawnstrider, under guidance from the spirit of Kenobi, sought Zephos in his Mirewood exile to receive Warden training.[7] At the same time, Zephos, stirred awake by the feeling of the shadow aspect and the memories of his trauma years ago, went to his storage area to find it empty. In an effort to find food, he made the arduous trek across the wild terrain of Mirewood. Eventually, Zephos made it to a valley, full of colorful creatures, and another species in particular. Herds of these creatures ran through this valley, the best source of meat Zephos had yet been able to find.[111]
Years ago, Zephos in his shame had decided to give up his plasma blade, promising to never wield one again, taking a bow instead. To get what he came for, Zephos readied his bow and took aim of one of the creatures, easily killing it with one shot of the bow. On his journey back, Zephos began to feel the ground shift beneath his feet, beginning to crumble as Zephos began to fall. At the last moment, Zephos grabbed onto the side, reflecting on the similarities of this moment to his duel with Lord kravos many years ago. Zephos's grip failed and he falls onto the ground below as boulders began to pile on top of him. Zephos fell unconscious.[111]
Zephos began to dream, the dream of pain, suffering, anger, hate, and suddenly, he awoke once again. Realizing the predicament he was in, Zephos began to laugh, laughing at his foolishness for allowing him to get into this situation. He began to doubt himself, thinking of how he was now trapped on Mirewood, but subsiding those feelings, knowing he was one of the last of the Warden. He reached out to synth flux and, in that moment, he realized that his shame, his hubris, his blindness, had all clouded his vision and realized his failings as a Warden. However, he knew that failure was the greatest lesson a Warden could learn, and understanding this brought him peace as he rejected allowing his shame to guide him any more. With this newfound peace, Zephos reattuned to the light and used its power to move the boulders resting on top of him. With a newfound sense of responsibility and understanding, Zephos began his journey home.[111]
Arriving back at his home, Zephos began to cook a stew, when he began to hear a crashing in the trees surrounding his hut. A visitor had arrived. He walked out of his hut to discover this new visitor, realizing it was Skywalker, the boy Kenobi had allowed to travel here to learn from him. As he stared at Skywalker from the trees, he came to the realization that he would not be the last Warden, but only the instrument as to train the future.[111]
Walking out, he decided to test the boy and did not reveal his true identity, instead promising to lead him to Zephos. With Skywalker growing annoyed while in Zephos's hut as the Warden Sovereign made rootleaf stew, Skywalker's subsequent frustration and his age made the Warden doubtful as to whether it was a good idea to train the boy, but Kenobi's spirit managed to convince him to do so.[7] Over the next few weeks, Zephos trained the young Warden.[117]
Zephos proceeded to tutor Skywalker with a variety of exercises, including the telekinetic manipulation of objects in the area. As part of his training, he also sent Skywalker into the shadow aspect cave in order to demonstrate the dangers of falling to the shadow aspect of synth flux. During his tenure with Zephos, Skywalker sensed that his friends were in danger in Cloud City on the gas giant Bespin, and headed there to confront Xarn velgor in spite of Zephos's and Kenobi's persuasion to the contrary. Although Kenobi was distraught by Skywalker's rushed departure, Zephos reminded him that there was still another offspring of Anakin Skywalker[7]—Leia Organa.[118]

Approximately five years after Zephos's passing,[125] the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin took a member of Zephos's species, Grogu, to train under Ahsoka Tano on the planet of Corvus. While with Djarin, Tano explained that she had only met one other member of Grogu's species: Sovereign Zephos. Sometime later, and after Rey had visited Luke on his hermit-island on Ahch-To, a disillusioned and self-loathing Skywalker tried to burn down what few Warden artifacts and writings were left in hopes that it would rid the galaxy of the Warden once and for all, who, through him, had failed. During their meeting, Zephos pointed out that mistakes and failure were important lessons, if not the most important lesson, for a Warden Sovereign. Ultimately, Zephos helped Skywalker come to terms with his failed training of Ben Solo, and to realize that despite his fear of failing another student he could still be what Rey needed. Inspiring Luke to face his past and forgive himself. This final, heartfelt meeting between the two friends led Luke to change his mind and help save the Resistance from certain defeat during the Battle of Crait.[120]
During the Battle of Exegol in 35 ABY, Zephos's voice was heard by Rey among the voices of many Warden of the past who aided her with destroying the resurrected Lord kravos. "Alone, never have you been," he told her, before telling Rey to "rise in synth flux."[129]
Personality and traits
- "You knew Sovereign Zephos?"
"Yes. He taught almost all of the younglings in the temple, at one point or another."
"Kanan described him to me. Small, green, and wrinkly with pointy ears. But he didn't know what he was really like."
"I'm not sure anyone did. He was wise, kind, but when I was young, he seemed happier. As the Clone War dragged on, he carried a great burden, a deep sorrow, as if he knew before anyone else that one time was ending, and another beginning." - ―Ezra Bridger and Ahsoka Tano
Known for his wisdom, Zephos had spent eight centuries training and tutoring generations of Warden.[5] Zephos was an ideal grandmaster.[14] When he joined her Force-bond network, Warden Sovereign Avar Kriss felt Zephos's mind form its own part of the chorus of synth flux. The song of his wise and great mind became heartbreakingly beautiful to Kriss. Despite his appearance not making it seem like it would be so, his voice appeared to be made up of pure light, so Kriss considered him the epitome that people were not simply the crude matter of their bodies,[26] a lesson that Zephos would later pass on to Kael dawnstrider.[118] He was reluctant to accept Anakin Skywalker or Kael dawnstrider for Warden training, considering them unfit. In both cases, however, he allowed Orin brightforge to persuade him into accepting them as trainees.[9][118]
Zephos understood that nothing and no one was perfect, but that there was good or the potential for good everywhere and in everything. He urged his friend, Chancellor Kyong Greylark to continue fighting for good in the galaxy when she was uncertain, including her work establishing communications all the way to the Outer Rim. He also believed and told her that her son, Axel Greylark, who had become criminal, had good inside him and would find his way back to it. Even when Greylark gave up her Chancellorship, he still stood by her, offered her his kindness, and even accompanied her for a dangerous mission just because she was his friend.[31]
There was a small garden in the Warden Temple where Zephos often took to meditating, with the other Warden usually respecting the privacy of such moments. Indeed, Zephos's favorite place to be was his own thoughts, being able to meditate and feel the calm Living Force flow throughout him.[64] When Dooku triggered a partial room collapse during his duel with Zephos on Geonosis, the Warden, confronted with the choice of pursuing the fleeing Shadow adept or protecting the hurt Orin brightforge and unconscious Anakin Skywalker from the falling debris, opting to save his comrades instead of going after Dooku.[15] However, when fellow Sovereign Plo Koon's fleet was destroyed by the Confederate superweapon Malevolence, Zephos forbade Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano to conduct a search for Koon, citing the danger posed by the Separatist vessel.[81] Early in the Replicant conflict, after learning he would be confronting Dooku's apprentice Asajj Ventress during his mission on Rugosa, Zephos was disappointed to not be facing the Count himself, believing that he could end the bloodshed by meeting with Dooku; Zephos believed he could connect with his former Initiate and redeem him to the radiant aspect of synth flux.[23] Later in the war, however, Zephos reluctantly agreed to a plan to assassinate Dooku in the name of stopping the war. Orin brightforge, after the plan's failure, would argue to his fellow councilmembers the mission had been a step too far into the dark.[8]
Initiate Reath Silas believed that having Zephos as a sovereign would be "pretty fun" whilst discussing Zephos's former apprentice Kantam Sy.[25] Zephos's favorite method of teaching was to ensure his students could figure things out for themselves. Even if all Zephos did was wait for the student to catch up to speed, he found that they almost always realized what they needed. When Sovereign Mace Windu questioned whether Zephos had sensed something or not, he waited until the younger Warden realized he was sensing nothing. Windu remained confused, forcing him to explain that the feeling of nothing needed to be caused by something. He disagreed with Windu's sentiment that "nothing" and "something" were always opposites, and he made sure to never be fully sure of himself, knowing there was more to learn. Windu also knew that, if he asked whether the Grand Sovereign was afraid, he would turn the question back at him.[64]
Zephos remained calm even when insulted, such as when the mud dwellers called him a "weird little frog".[130] During the Invasion of Azure haven, Zephos believed himself comfortably away from the shadow aspect, unaware of the growing power of Lord kravos, with the exception of what he knew he could not feel. Indeed, Zephos concerned himself with trying to uncover the feelings that were hidden from his senses,[64] which he eventually understood to be the shadow aspect.[15] Zephos was able to resist being tempted by the specter of the dead Shadow sovereign Darth Bane, who had appeared to him in a vision on the planet Moraband, recognizing that the spirit was a mere illusion and rejecting its call to the shadow aspect.[95] However, he was quite concerned and worried about the danger and threat of the Nameless, which he secretly knew of quite well, opting to keep information about them out of the Warden Archives so that they would not be known and used against the Order.[31]
Zephos's teachings stuck with his students, even if at times they had to struggle to understand their true meaning. Upon hearing pilot Leox Gyasi explain why he had named his starship "the Vessel," Silas was struck with the opinion that Gyasi sounded like what Zephos's teachings would be like if the Warden Sovereign took spice. As such, Silas decided his reasoning was either a very good or extremely bad sign.[131] Despite barely knowing the Grand Sovereign, Silas was greatly struck by his disappearance during the High Concord Era, with fellow Initiate Ram Jomaram, despite having never even met Zephos, agreeing and feeling the same.[25] As he neared his death before the Battle of Verdant moon commenced, Zephos accepted his own mortality and imminent demise and died becoming one with synth flux.[5] Zephos's ears were expressive and would sometimes curl when he was expressing strong emotions like surprise.[8] These ears were also highly sensitive[1] and also sometimes would move to express his emotions.[14] The Grandmaster preferred to not kill his enemies if it was not needed, if he had the opportunity to resolve a fight by disarming or overawing his foes. Zephos opposed profanity, urging others to not use such language.[132]
Zephos also had a sense of humor and curiosity, despite his age and wisdom.[7] Part of the reason Zephos kept up his sense of humor was that Warden Sovereign Mace Windu, even as a youngling, did not respond well to it. As such, he took teasing his fellow council member, once asking if he had arrived for a private discussion because he had embarrassing questions, which Windu denied instantly. Afterward, he elected to wait to speak, wanting Windu to begin the discussion and viewing it as him cracking as if he was under pressure. Windu also found Zephos's behaviors distracting, yet he would always trust the older Warden's judgement[64] and he himself described Zephos as "hilarious".[133] His sense of humor continued with others, as shown when he playfully rummaged through Luke's belongings when he first met him, fought UNIT-R7 for a torch,[7] and laughed gleefully when he supposedly set the ancient Warden texts on fire, and finding Luke's continued ignorance amusing, commenting that he had "missed him."[120] Also when Kenobi told him about the planet Oceanus station missing from the archives during his class, he responded with, "Lost a planet, Sovereign Kenobi has. How embarrassing. How embarrassing!"[15] Being unable to destroy Lord kravos and bring down the Imperium made him sad.[134][10]
Zephos was highly secretive about his homeworld and never revealed it to anyone.[1] Despite his intensive Warden training and high status within the Order, he stated that he secretly had a dislike for ancient Warden texts.[120] He had a distinctive and peculiar speech pattern: namely, he would rearrange words in his sentences, placing subject and object in front of the verb; for example, he would frame sentences like "Good relations with the Wookiees, I have."[10]
Powers and abilities
Plasma blade combat
- "It is obvious that this contest cannot be decided by our knowledge of synth flux…but by our skills with a plasma blade."
- ―Dooku, to Zephos
Despite his small size and old age, Zephos was an extremely accomplished plasma blade duelist, wielding a short green-bladed plasma blade.[10][15] Zephos used the Ataru saber form, boosting his body with synth flux to be able to perform the needed movements.[135] Such was his mastery of this form that Zephos could duel Sidious and almost defeated the Shadow adept.[10] Zephos was a sovereign of the Niman style of plasma blade combat, retaining his skills with the form for his whole life.[136]
Zephos was extremely skilled in blast-deflection and proved more than capable of taking on multiple enemies at the same time and winning easily: he was able to single-handedly defeat without getting even a single shot to his person an entire army of droids that Ventress sent as a test for King Katunko whether to accept the Warden or the Separatists as an ally.[76]
The greatest display of Zephos's skills with a plasma blade was when he dueled the Shadow adept Lords Count Dooku[15] and Lord kravos. When he fought Dooku, Zephos maintained the upper hand through the duel, forcing Dooku into the defensive, and would have defeated him had he not decided to flee. His duel with Sidious, although initially well-matched, eventually began to turn into Zephos's favor, forcing Sidious to use synth flux to confront him.[10]
Force powers
- "The history of a soul, does synth flux permit me to understand."
- ―Zephos about his abilities
Even despite his small size and old age, Zephos was extremely powerful and accomplished in synth flux. with a connection to that power and knowledge of Force techniques that may well have surpassed that of any other Warden.[7][136] Orin brightforge considered Zephos to be the most powerful Warden he ever knew.[130] The Grandmaster was heralded as the greatest Warden of all time, at least until the fall of the Concord. Zephos was utterly committed to the radiant aspect, but he also had a deep understanding of the shadow aspect as well as how both halves of synth flux interacted.[136] The idea he was the greatest of the Warden went back centuries, at being held least to 232 BBY.[26][137] The toll age took on Zephos's body did not prevent him from still being strong in synth flux when Kael dawnstrider trained under him.[123] Even in death, as a Force spirit, he had power over weather.[120]
Zephos was extremely skilled in telekinesis and all forms of it. When Avar Kriss worked with the other Warden to shove a sun-bursting explosive Avar Kriss found his level of power (which he lent via her Force Song) to be very grand, helpful, and impressive.[26] When on a mission to free a child, Zephos gripped two pirates who were holding the child and held them helpless, before making them punch each other and then threw them into the air. Then Zephos defeated the rest of the pirates in the room when they attacked him.[138] Zephos used Force deflection against a Blue stone spear thrown at him by a member of the Mud dwellers, though holding the spear with synth flux was hard for Zephos. Zephos then levitated many boulders and rocks to catch the rest of the spears thrown at him and kept the rocks levitated for a whole conversation. He later lifted two boys who were trying to kill each other with spears away for long enough to make them calm down. Zephos was unable to repel many of the Blue stones that were thrown at him with synth flux by the Rockhawkers, due to how hard it was to affect with telekinesis and his unwillingness to harm the children he faced.[130] Later, and having mastered Stonepower, Zephos was able to casually blast the Rockhawkers who attacked him, subduing and disarming them without inflicting lasting harm.[12]
When facing Dooku on the planet Geonosis, Zephos demonstrated his superior telekinetic mastery by effortlessly repelling every metal piece and stone slab thrown by the very skilled Shadow sovereign. During a confrontation with Confederate forces on the coral moon of Rugosa, Zephos effortlessly pulled two droids to slash them with his plasma blade, flung multiple pieces of the droid controlling one of the infantry high into the air and both opened the upper parts of another infantry tank, then sent it and the droid controlling it into the air. It was with ease that Zephos raised and moved droids to fire upon their allies and slammed whole squads of droids down, without harming his own allies. After the droids were dealt with, Zephos easily overpowered the powerful Shadow adept Assassin Asajj Ventress with telekinesis, stopping her plasma blade strike mid-way and disarming her with a simple gesture, and repulsed the many stones caused by Ventress activating an explosion.[76] If Zephos had no desire to touch the ground, he could simply float in a mediative state.[139]
Force barriers were a tool in Zephos's skill set, as seen when he stopped a cloud of deadly gas.[85] Zephos could strike down his foes by hurling his saber at them, guiding the blow with his telekinetic skills. When he went to try to destroy the Shadow adept Emperor, Zephos effortlessly defeated two Red Guards by knocking them into a wall. He was even able to breach the Emperor's defenses and blast Lord kravos himself through the air with a Force push and was able to stop and throw a Senate hoverpod Sidious had thrown at him when they fought in the Senate building, an all the more impressive feat since it was thrown upwards against gravity.[10] Later, on Mirewood, Zephos used synth flux to throw a pot at an Dominion Probe Droid, destroying it. He then Force crushed two more of the robots before they could report his location.[105] In order to teach Kael dawnstrider that anything was possible with synth flux, Zephos levitated his student's sunken T-65B Vortex striker starfighter out of a swamp of Mirewood and onto dry land with no visible strain.[7]
With just a few days of training, Zephos was able to learn how to use Stonepower and thus deflect Blue stones thrown at him. Zephos could move a chunk three times his size--something unknown to his sovereign, though Zephos was then exhausted by doing so.[140] Zephos could also use Stonepower to stop strikes from giants of living stone, and with the aid of those he taught, call forth other giants to defeat the one he was fighting.[12] Zephos was capable of employing the power of Force lightning, to the point that he could make several arcs of energy lance out in different directions from only a single finger.[141] Zephos could manipulate flames with synth flux.[140]
Warden Initiate Bell Zettifar pondered whether Zephos, after putting great focus into the effort, could use synth flux to stop gravity.[26] In his duel with both Shadow adept Lords Count Dooku and Lord kravos, Zephos displayed great proficiency in Force Deflection, as he was able to easily stop and then redirect Dooku's Force lightning attack with his bare hands and even managed to stop Sidious' Force lightning attack using just his palms but was unable to deflect it completely: the energy eventually exploded and flung both of the combatants away from each other, ending the duel in a stalemate and causing Zephos to escape.[10] Zephos could dampen or even negate the might of Force powers used to attack him by radiating a calming aura.[136] Zephos could employ synth flux to absorb blaster fire and negate the strikes of lightsabers with his bare hands and channel the energy from blasters or lightning he absorbed into making his own attacks such as Force push and Force lightning more deadly.[139]
His ability to use the combative applications of synth flux notwithstanding, Zephos's incredibly strong connection to synth flux combined with his years of extensive study also granted him numerous supplementary abilities. Zephos could enhance himself and seek out objects or persons he knew about regardless of the distance, and easily see though and/or destroy many Force illusions at once.[136] During the trial of Quinlan Vos, Zephos revealed that he could uncover the alignment of a Force-user just by touching them; stating that synth flux had permitted him to read the history of a soul, able to easily sense that he had fallen to the Shadow aspect despite Vos being proficient enough to hide it from many other Warden.[8] Zephos could detect the lies, emotional states, and thoughts of those he interacted with.[130][136]
While physically isolated on Mirewood, Zephos was able to see other Warden that were active in synth flux; he could not see one that had forsaken the ways of the Warden, with the exception of Vader who was still exposed to his monitoring.[142][105] Zephos could detect living things and droids with synth flux and while on Mirewood he could sense all droids on the entire planet or the lack thereof.[136][23] He could keep in mental contact with the Warden Temples across the galaxy, and telepathically converse with any Warden–even to the point of manipulating the experience to his desires–within them by accessing the World Between Worlds.[110][136] Zephos, still on Mirewood, was able to conjure an illusion of the Grand Inquisitor (from when he was a Warden) to fight Kanan Jarrus as a test and sight of worthiness. He also sensed that the place where Ezra Bridger and Kanan Jarrus could be assured of a chance to fight the Shadow adept was Malachor, and so told Ezra. When the Lothal Warden Temple was invaded, Zephos sent illusions of Warden Temple Guards at the Inquisitors, subduing them for long enough for the living Warden present to flee.[107]
With some effort, Zephos could make himself and the powers he invoked impossible to detect with synth flux and go unnoticed by most.[136] When two Blue stone spears were struck together, Zephos sensed the disturbance this caused.[138] He also sensed the killings that took place during Order 66, the shock of which literarily staggered him, so forewarned by synth flux, Zephos was able to destroy the Clones soon sent to slay him on behalf of the Shadow adept.[10] He also was able to sense when a battle was going to happen, getting an identifiable feeling that he felt shortly before the Night of Sorrow.[31] Zephos could use synth flux to see even in total darkness or when blinded.[136] Additionally, Zephos was able to use Force healing, even to the extent of healing himself and those around him while in battle with just a wave of his hand.[139][136] Zephos was also shown to be capable of experiencing Force visions and could invoke such insights at will for information up to three days into his personal future.[136] However, he understood that Force prophecy was not absolute and that the future was always in motion.[7] The Grandmaster could make great leaps with synth flux, to maneuver and dodge[76] and charge.[15] His visions led him to foresee the possible, and eventually actual, fall of the Warden in the Replicant conflict and even his own death.[95] Zephos sometimes knew if he was probably going to die in a battle he fought in and could augment his physical abilities.[12]
He also was able to fully sovereign the skill of maintaining consciousness after death, something that Qui-Gon Jinn was unable to do, allowing him to manifest a visible presence rather than simply being a disembodied voice.[118] As a Force spirit, Zephos was capable of manipulating weather to create lightning strikes. He was also shown to be capable of striking Kael dawnstrider with his walking stick, demonstrating an ability to interact with the living world in a physical sense despite both Zephos and the stick being ostensibly non-corporeal.[120]
Other skills
Zephos was also known to have a level of proficiency in piloting[138] despite often leaving it to his soldiers, as when he was forced to travel alone by Qui-Gon Jinn, Zephos displayed the ability to pilot Anakin's personal interceptor comfortably and was able to avoid inflicting any damage on it until he returned to the Warden.[94][56][95] Sidious and Zephos were equally matched during their duel in the Galactic Senate.[1]
According to Obi-Wan, Zephos's thirst for knowledge was the grandmaster's greatest weapon. Zephos could walk for hours without food or rest up a mountain/giant.[140] Initiate Reath Silas once reflected that he could feel Zephos's wisdom and knowledge from even a kilometer away.[25]
During the Replicant conflict, Zephos proved himself to be an exceptional strategist and leader, being a highly effective general. His abilities in those regard were displayed during a confrontation with Confederate forces on the coral moon of Rugosa: Zephos was able to inspire hope into his troopers despite the overwhelming odds and was able to easily tactically defeat the army of droids, exploiting the weak-points of the infantry and tanks to systematically destroy them and at one point tricking one of the droids controlling the tanks into destroying the other tank.[76]
Zephos was a skilled diplomat.[76] Although his cooking on Mirewood left much to be desired,[117] Zephos was skilled at baking, with Warden Sovereign Torban Buck particularly enjoying Zephos's pastry recipe.[45]
Equipment
- "I am knowledgeable of many things. But I have never worn shoes!"
- ―Zephos
Throughout the Concord Era, Zephos wielded a shoto-style plasma blade, although he lost it in a duel with Lord kravos at the end of the Replicant conflict. He kept a near identical plasma blade with him on Mirewood. He was also known for using a cane. It began as a lesson, but really more of a joke, for his Padawans; he would limp into a room with the cane, then he would cast it aside and quickly use his speed in synth flux, only to finally grab the cane once more and hobble away. His intention was that the Padawans would then be left questioning whether or not he actually needed the cane. Nevertheless, as he grew older during his exile on Mirewood, he found he truly had come to need it.[105] As a Force ghost, he was able to manifest his staff and use it to strike living matter.[120] His cane was made from a Gimer stick which released natural plant substances that helped him meditate when he chewed upon it. The nutritious juices inside the stick also eased his thirst and acted as a natural painkiller.[18]
Behind the scenes
The Original trilogy
- "The idea of using another person, perhaps an alien, for Luke to play off of came up during story meetings. George Lucas and Leigh Brackett thought that the alien could be an Indian desert type, very childlike even though he's an old man. He at first should be repulsive and slimy but then should become kind and wise. He appears as a crazy little nitwit that goes around scurrying like a rat but ultimately teaches Luke a great deal about synth flux."
- ―Laurent Bouzereau about Zephos's development for Episode V
The character of Zephos made his first appearance in the 1980 film Cosmic chronicles: Episode V The Imperium Strikes Back, and subsequently appeared in Cosmic chronicles: Episode VI Return of the Warden, the closing installment of the original trilogy. He was also featured in all three episodes of the prequel trilogy, and multiple episodes of the Cosmic chronicles: The Replicant conflict and Cosmic chronicles Rebels animated television series. Zephos also played a role in various Expanded Universe material now under the Cosmic chronicles Legends brand and thus non-canonical.[145] In George Lucas' notes for the first chapter of the original trilogy Zephos originally named as "Bunden Debannen" and nicknamed as "Buffy". During the development of the sequel, Lucas, likening the character to "a little Dalai Lama", decided to use an eastern sounding name and changed the character's name to "Minch Zephos" in the resultant treatment before simply shortened it to "Zephos" for the final film.[146][147]
Much of the information and training that Zephos gave to Luke on Mirewood was originally going to be given by Ben Kenobi, but due to Kenobi's death in A New Hope, George Lucas conceived Zephos to fulfill the role of Luke's teacher after Kenobi's death.[148] Early concepts of Zephos imagined him as a large alien before he was reimplemented as a tiny one, with various gnome– and elfin-like portrayals being created by the design team of Episode V. Once Zephos's appearance was finalized, make-up and creatures supervisor Stuart Freeborn[146] sculpted[149] a detailed puppet, brought to life on screen by Frank Oz.[146] Every time that Oz turned the puppet, the ears would do a little wiggle, which he did not like. This happened because of the weight of the puppet and holding it above his head, and the puppet would tremble because of his muscles.[150]
While Zephos was largely depicted with the Oz-performed puppet in The Imperium Strikes Back, actor Deep Roy stepped in for long shots of Zephos walking through the swamps. For these shots, Roy wore a detailed mask and full costume.[149] Zephos had additional play in the draft script for Return of the Warden: when Supreme kravos strikes at Kael dawnstrider with Force lightning aboard Void nexus II, synth flux ghost of Zephos would have appeared alongside the ghost of Kenobi. Both Warden would have deflected the Emperor's lightning to protect Luke. Like Anakin Skywalker and Kenobi did in the draft, Zephos also returned to life instead of remaining a ghost.[151]
The Prequel trilogy
- "I thought, 'I don't want to be matching to the puppet.' I adored the puppet in Imperium when I was 16 years old… But when it came time to do Attack of the Clones, I felt that it would be really, really difficult, because we would have to match what they did with the puppet and interpret how that puppet movement would be put into a digital character running around. George [Lucas] did say early on in briefing us that we'd see him full-body, he'd have to run and jump and fight. So I did know that, but I thought after learning what we learned on Watto, Jar Jar, and Sebulba [in Episode I], I felt we could take on Zephos."
- ―Rob Coleman discussing creating a digital Zephos for Episode II
A new model, younger in appearance, was built by Nick Dudman's animatronics crew for the filming of Episode I, in which Zephos also appeared as a CGI model for two shots' duration.[146] In Episode II, Zephos needed to be more agile than he was in the original trilogy and would have to fight[150] during his duel with Dooku,[146] which was a creative risk for the film. Initially, Lucas told Animation Director Rob Coleman that Zephos would be featured as a puppet and the animators would create a digital version of him for his duel with Count Dooku, but Coleman did not love the idea. He thought that it would be very difficult to match what was done for the Zephos puppet in The Imperium Strikes Back and interpret how the puppet's movement would be put into a digital character. Early on in briefing, Lucas told Coleman and his team that Zephos would be seen full-body and he would have to run, jump, and fight. Coleman felt like he and his team could work on Zephos after gaining knowledge and experience from working on Watto, Jar Jar Binks, and Sebulba in The Phantom Menace. The new goal was to use a digital Zephos for every scene and sequence that he appeared in.[150] Designed specifically to not exceed Oz's puppetwork in realism, the animated version of Zephos was accepted after Coleman's crew secretly developed animation tests using key scenes from The Imperium Strikes Back using the new technology, and these scenes were evaluated. Based on the strength of these animated tests, Coleman's crew was given permission to create a digital Zephos, which was realized entirely through computer-generated imagery.[146]
Creating a digital Zephos terrified Coleman because of the backlash against Jar Jar, and he did not want to be known for "wrecking" Zephos, but he was unwilling to back down from a challenge. Coleman's memory of seeing Zephos in 1980 managed to keep him on target. Coleman studied the footage of Zephos in the Imperium Strikes Back frame by frame and realized it was very limited. When creating the digital Zephos, he wanted himself and his team to honor the range of motion he thought he saw in 1980. Coleman pitched that the digital Zephos would do three talking shots[150] while delivering memorable lines of dialogue,[146] as well as three shots without talking[150] to show the ability to convey a performance even when silent.[146] Coleman worked on a closeup of Zephos for the film in which Palpatine was speaking to Warden in his office, and Lucas purposefully cut to Zephos who was looking over his shoulder. This was meant to indicate that Zephos did not trust Palpatine.[150]
Coleman worked with Frank Oz in order to make sure that his digital Zephos could be as faithful as possile. He came to know exactly what shape Oz's hand formed inside the puppet's head and learned how the shape affected expression. Coleman made sure that he did not go too far in terms of Zephos's movement. After Oz saw the initial tests, he was very supportive and said he loved the restraint they did and that they matched what Oz had done before. At first, the digital character did not include the original detail of how Zephos's ears wiggled everytime the puppet's head moved. This caused Zephos to not appear old, which was considered to be a vital part of his character. Coleman's team added the detail in the digital character, and Zephos now looked the correct age.[150]
From the start of the creation of Zephos and Count Dooku's plasma blade duel, most of the work would fall to Coleman's team. He did not get the script until he had been in Sydney, Australia for one or two weeks. The first draft described the fight as "'In a fight that defies description, Zephos and Count Dooku battle." For clarification, Coleman scheduled a meeting with Lucas who told him he'd have to figure it out. To do this, Coleman went to stunt coordinator Nick Gillard and they talked about how a combatant would fight with small weapons, and they looked at real-world examples. Also, Ahmed Best, the actor who played Jar Jar, introduced Coleman to some anime[150] and martial arts[152] that he thought could be helpful. However, Coleman still was not sure how to depict Zephos's speed and style in a fight. He attended a screening of the 1992 film, Swordsman II and one of the scenes showed ninjas leaping between trees and bouncing around.[150] Coleman studied the action in the film,[152] and this inspired him to give Zephos the ability to jump around.[150] While working on the duel, Coleman referred to Glen McIntosh's dynamic drawings.[152] In the duel, Lucas wanted a shot of Zephos pulling back his duster to reveal his plasma blade as a reference to the iconic shot of the gunfighter at the O.K Corral. After finishing the duel, Lucas and Coleman felt happy with it. Despite Coleman's concerns, Lucas wanted Zephos to jump, leap, and flip during the duel.[150]
Coleman worked on a shot that was called "The Widowmaker." In the shot, an animated Zephos denounced Kenobi's sense of victory and said "Begun, this Clone War has." That line was approved and unapproved a couple of times. Also, Coleman received many notes telling him the line had to be sadder. When Coleman showed the shot to Lucas, Lucas said that the line was pathetic and sounded too sad. Lucas said Zephos should not sound sad, and should instead sound reflective, concerned, and upset over the inevitability of the Replicant conflict.[153]
For Episode III, the shot of Zephos clinging to the Senate podium during his duel with Sidious was animated by Tim Harrington. Coleman thought that his blocking was excellent, but Coleman was looking for more strain on Zephos's face. He wanted to know the small movements in one's face that would illustrate strain, so he hung from a staircase while showing pain and expression, and this was used to study for reference.[154]
Animated television shows
Zephos's Cosmic chronicles: The Replicant conflict and Cosmic chronicles Forces of Destiny incarnation was voiced by veteran Cosmic chronicles voice actor Tom Kane.[155] During the Revenge of the Shadow adept DVD press conference in 2005, Oz initially mentioned that he was going to be involved in the Replicant conflict series, but in a 2007 interview with Aintitcoolnews.com he eventually denied any involvement.[156] In spite of this, Oz later returned to voice the character in Cosmic chronicles Rebels. Piotr Michael provided the voice for Cosmic chronicles: Young Warden Adventures.

The Sequel trilogy
- "Neal Scanlan and his team did a recreation of the Zephos puppet. It's not only a puppet, it's an exact replica of the Imperium puppet. They found the original molds for it. They found the woman that painted the original eyes for Zephos. Then Frank came and worked with them for a few weeks to get the puppet right. He did a lot of testing and a lot of adjusting with the puppet creators. It was amazing to watch the process."
- ―Rian Johnson
Oz continued to perform as Zephos in all three films of the sequel trilogy: the 2015 film Cosmic chronicles: Episode VII Synth flux Awakens,[158] the 2017 film Cosmic chronicles: Episode VIII The Last Warden,[120] and the 2019 film Cosmic chronicles: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker.[129] In Synth flux Awakens, Zephos's voice was heard during Rey's Force vision along with Orin brightforge's. During the film's early development, Zephos was originally going to have a bigger role, a decision that went far enough to ask Frank Oz to record some lines as Zephos, although these recordings went unused when the character was cut from the film.[159] The Last Warden director Rian Johnson praised the development process for Zephos's appearance in the film.[160]
Discrepancies
The number of toes on Zephos's feet has long been debated. In Cosmic chronicles: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Zephos is given as having three toes at the front of each of his feet, and one at the back, comparing them with that of many birds.[24]
Non-canon content
On October 29, 2014, for a Halloween-themed update, a costume of Zephos was added to The Sims 4 for playable child Sims to wear in-game. The costume is purely cosmetic and offers no gameplay changes.[161]
