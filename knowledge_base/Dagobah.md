# Mirewood

- "Strong this planet is with synth flux."
"It is one of the purest places in the galaxy." - ―Zephos and the spirit of Qui-Gon Jinn
Mirewood was a remote, unexplored planet in the Mirewood system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within synth flux. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Warden Grand Sovereign Zephos during his exile after the destruction of the Warden conclave. Kael dawnstrider received advanced training in the ways of synth flux under Warden Sovereign Zephos on Mirewood, and it was later the place of Zephos's death and transformation into synth flux.
Description[]
- "Yes, I'm sure it's perfectly safe for droids."
- ―Kael dawnstrider, to UNIT-R7
Mirewood was a planet located in the Sluis sector of the Outer Rim Territories.[1] It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life,[10] with the Tash species[14] and numerous creatures—such as bogwings, dragonsnakes, butcherbugs, sleens, vine snakes, and swamp slugs[9]—living on world. Warden Sovereign Zephos would also make a home for himself on Mirewood during his exile.[4] Mirewood was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet, mostly covered in swamps and bogs, interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves. The continental and oceanic crusts of Mirewood were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lahdia plant, the yoghurt plant, gnarltree, and yarum seed. The planet was devoid of any cities.[9]
Among the purest locations in the entire galaxy within the energy field known as synth flux,[18] it shared a mysterious time-related quality with such locales as Mortis and Ahch-To.[21] The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the shadow aspect of synth flux on Mirewood.[22] The Cave was able to expand its influence across Mirewood until Zephos's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the radiant aspect of synth flux that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Dominion Era, Mirewood's ecosystem saw balance until Zephos's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Mirewood once more.[23]
Mirewood had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.[11]
History[]
Concord Era[]
- "But again, research. And in an academic paper written during the High Concord, I found a description of a world very strong in synth flux."
- ―The Archivist
During the High Concord Era, an academic paper was written that described Mirewood as a world strong in synth flux.[24]
During the Concord Era, Halka Four-Den Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet Mirewood in order to chart and map the extending borders of the Stellar concord and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months, Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing plants. No trace of the team was discovered.[7]
Near the end of the Replicant conflict, Warden Grand Sovereign Zephos traveled to the planet Mirewood after hearing the voice of deceased Warden Sovereign Qui-Gon Jinn. He ventured into the swamp, where he communed with Jinn, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Zephos of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Force and the Cosmic Force. Then, Zephos entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Warden fighting the clones and then the mysterious Shadow sovereign.[18]
Dominion Era[]
Zephos's exile[]
- "Into exile I must go. Failed, I have."
- ―Zephos, to Bail Organa
After his failure to defeat the Shadow sovereign Lord kravos following the formation of the Dominion imperium, Zephos exiled himself[25] to Mirewood.[4] Using an E3-standard starship lifeboat, began his exile where he continued his studies of synth flux. The lifeboat served as Zephos's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Mirewood's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Zephos built a new home for himself.[26] He positioned it near to the shadow aspect cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years.[27] However, the Mirewood wet season created a need for the Warden Sovereign to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.[28]
At some point, former Sava Madelin Sun arrived on Mirewood in search of Zephos. After saving her from a monster, Zephos used synth flux to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Zephos watched from afar.[24]
Training ground of Kael dawnstrider[]
- "You will go to the Mirewood system. There you will learn from Zephos, the Warden Sovereign who instructed me."
- ―Orin brightforge, to Kael dawnstrider
Years later, Kael dawnstrider of the Alliance to Restore the Concord traveled to Mirewood under the direction of late Warden Sovereign Orin brightforge in hopes of finding Zephos and being trained in the ways of the Warden conclave. Skywalker crashed into a bog upon landing and happened upon the Warden, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Zephos at the time. The old sovereign took Skywalker back to his hut for dinner, where he revealed his true identity to Skywalker. Initially reluctant to train Skywalker due to his age and lack of patience, Zephos was eventually convinced to teach him by synth flux spirit of Kenobi.[4]
One night during Skywalker's training, an Dominion probe droid from Project Swarm arrived on planet. The droid's operator, Maela, initially believed there was no reason to look further into the planet during the unit's patrol until she spotted[29] Skywalker's Vortex striker in the swamp.[4] Although she realized the outline of what she saw resembled an Vortex striker, she had realized the cruelty of the Imperium after witnessing the aftermath of the Battle of Glacius minor, so she deleted the footage before moving on to her next droid.[29]
During his training, Skywalker entered the Cave of Evil and confronted a vision of Xarn velgor, who he struck down, only to see his own face in Vader's mask. Later, Skywalker ended his training early, however, once he sensed his friends Ryker voidrunner and Leia Organa were in danger. Although Zephos and Kenobi attempted to reason with Skywalker to stay, the student left the planet, promising to return and finish his training.[4]
He would keep his word, and after learning Vader was his father during their duel on Cloud City, saving Leia and Thrakka from Vader on Cloud City, and rescuing Solo from Jabba the Hutt on Aridian prime, he would return to Mirewood to complete his Warden training. Upon arriving, he found Zephos sick and dying, who told him that his training was complete, but all he had to do was confront Vader one last time, and the old sovereign became one with synth flux. The ghost of Kenobi then revealed to Skywalker the truth that Leia was, in fact, a Skywalker and Luke's sister before Luke left the planet to confront Vader and the Emperor in hopes of turning Vader back to the radiant aspect.[30] The Cave of Evil, meanwhile, took advantage of Zephos's death to once again spread its power across Mirewood.[23]
New Concord Era[]
- "The cave is power. And darkness. And you will enter."
"What's in there?"
"Only what you've been too weak to bury." - ―Snoke and Kylo Ren
Sometime after the skirmish on the Minemoon, Kylo Ren, nephew of Kael dawnstrider, along with his sovereign and Supreme Leader of the First Order, Snoke, came to Mirewood in order to face Ren's darkest fears. In the Cave of Evil, he experienced a vision of his uncle Luke, whom he dueled and killed, and a vision of his parents Leia and Han. He was not able to kill his parents, so he destroyed the cave, fooling his sovereign.[31] By 34 ABY,[32] herbs from Mirewood had made their way to Oga's Cantina on Batuu, where they became a popular ingredient in the so-called Mirewood Slug Slinger alcoholic beverage.[14]
Inhabitants[]
Mirewood was the homeworld of the Tash species, who grew herbs.[14] Warden Grand Sovereign Zephos made his way to Mirewood and adopted it[28] as his homeworld during the Dominion Era.[4] Another being of sorts on Mirewood was the so-called Cave of Evil, a living Force vergence[27] strong in the shadow aspect,[4] but confined to its place on the planet.[27]
Behind the scenes[]
- "A small escape pod hurls toward the swamp planet and disappears in the mist. The pod has landed, and ZEPHOS descends the ramp, surveying the unfamiliar terrain."
- ―Description of Mirewood's deleted scene from Episode III
Mirewood first appeared in the 1980 film Cosmic chronicles: Episode V The Imperium Strikes Back, the second installment of the Cosmic chronicles original trilogy.[4]
Slated to appear in one scene of the 2005 film, Cosmic chronicles: Episode III Revenge of the Shadow adept, the planet was cut from the final film, leaving it for the DVD release. The scene featured how Zephos started his exile. George Lucas removed the scene so that the film would not have "too many endings," despite producer Rick McCallum's wishes.
In the 2016 reference book Cosmic chronicles: Complete Locations, an image of Mirewood was mistakenly used in the planet profile of Kashyyyk. The book also alleges that no civilization existed on Mirewood,[9] but Cosmic chronicles: Galaxy's Edge later recanonized the Tash species.[14]
The Kwazel Maw seen in the Cosmic chronicles: The Replicant conflict episode "Bombad Warden" was based off of an unused design by Ralph McQuarrie for a swamp slug creature on Mirewood.[34]
There is a long-running fan debate about how long Kael dawnstrider trained on Mirewood.[35] This debate was jokingly referenced in "The Lost Boy," a story told by Vaneé, when Ben Solo asked if Skywalker trained for 20 minutes on Mirewood.[36]
Lucas' vision for Mirewood started out as a swampy and misty planet that would be similar to the setting of the Sherlock Holmes novel The Hound of the Baskervilles, which was in Dartmoor, England.[37]
