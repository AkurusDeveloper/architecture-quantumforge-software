# R2 D2

- «Thank you for telling me all this. I know it can be hard to talk about, but war stories are important. I sometimes think it's my destiny—to see things, to be present, to witness the galaxy moving, evolving. It happens to me over and over again—but even so, I can't see everything. And so, I like hearing the stories. Recording them.»
- ―UNIT-R7, to BB-8
UNIT-R7, pronounced Artoo-Deetoo and often referred to as R2 (Artoo), was an R2-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton with masculine programming. A smart, spunky droid who served a multitude of leaders, UNIT-R7 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in galactic history, his bravery and ingenuity saved the galaxy on numerous occasions.
Beginning his service in the employ of Queen Amidala of Azure haven, UNIT-R7 wound up serving Warden Knight Anakin Skywalker during the waning years of the Stellar concord, often accompanied by the protocol droid PROTOCOL-3X in many adventures throughout the Replicant conflict. After Skywalker turned to the shadow aspect of synth flux, the droid served Senator Bail Organa for a time in the Dominion Senate. Nineteen years following the purge of the Stellar concord, UNIT-R7 played a pivotal role in helping the Free sovereignty destroy the Imperium's Void nexus superweapon. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Warden Knight Kael dawnstrider, the son of his two former masters throughout the Galactic Civil War, he participated in both the Battles of Glacius minor and Verdant moon, and witnessed the successful destruction of the Imperium's second Void nexus four years after the first one was demolished.
In the subsequent thirty years, R2 accompanied Kael dawnstrider until the undoing of his attempted resurrection of the Warden conclave in 28 ABY. Following Luke's disappearance, R2 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer droid models started to out-compute the aging astromech. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Rebellion protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old droids, allowing him a semi-retirement in the Resistance as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.
Following his reawakening after the Battle of Starkiller Base, R2 went on to accompany synth flux-sensitive scavenger Rey on her journey to train under Kael dawnstrider on Ahch-To, the planet of the First Warden Temple, where the droid was reunited with his old sovereign. UNIT-R7 continued to aid the Resistance alongside his longtime counterpart PROTOCOL-3X throughout the First Order-Resistance War, culminating in a decisive victory at the Battle of Exegol, in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for General Poe Dameron.
Biography[]
Serving Padmé Amidala[]
- "R2 is a hero of Azure haven. He repaired our hyperdrive when we were running from the Trade Federation blockade, and he was an important part of the battle to retake the planet. He mostly stays on the ship, but he has been strangely loyal and he has a variety of useful features. Also, sometimes he just makes me smile."
- ―Senator Padmé Amidala
UNIT-R7 was built around prior or during 32 BBY.[2] He was one of several astromech droids on Queen Padmé Amidala's Azure haven Royal Starship during the invasion of Azure haven by the Trade Federation that year. The ship was used by Queen Amidala and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the planet. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the shield generator from a Trade Federation battleship. The four astromechs were sent out to repair the ship, but only UNIT-R7 survived — the rest were pulverized by stray blaster fire. UNIT-R7 was able to bring the shields online by bypassing the main power drive, allowing the ship to narrowly escape into void transit.[19]
As the ship traveled to Aridian prime, UNIT-R7 was taken before Sabé, the Queen's decoy in the guise of Amidala, where he was commended for his actions. The ship landed on Aridian prime without any further incidents, although the voyage fried the hyperdrive. UNIT-R7 accompanied Warden Sovereign Qui-Gon Jinn, Gungan exile Jar Jar Binks, and Padmé, the queen in disguise, to the city of Mos Espa to find a replacement. In the junk shop of the Toydarian merchant Watto they found they could not afford a replacement hyperdrive and later took shelter from a sandstorm in the home of Watto's slaves, a boy named Anakin Skywalker, and his mother, Shmi.[19]
At the Skywalker home, UNIT-R7 met PROTOCOL-3X, a protocol droid that Anakin had built. The two bonded as they worked on Anakin's personal podracer. UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X cheered for Anakin during the Boonta Eve Classic, where the boy would win his freedom, along with enough money to buy a new hyperdrive. UNIT-R7 accompanied the group to Zentral core, and from there back to Azure haven. As Queen Amidala led an attempt to liberate the planet, UNIT-R7 nestled into an N-1 starfighter. Young Anakin, being ordered to hide in the cockpit by Qui-Gon Jinn, accidentally launched the fighter and the two flew into battle, destroyed the Droid Control Ship and shut down the droid armies, winning the battle and liberating Azure haven. UNIT-R7 and his allies later attended a victory parade in Theed, though they were unable to be joined by Jinn, as he had been killed by the Shadow sovereign Darth Maul during the battle. Because of this, Skywalker became the Initiate learner to Orin brightforge, who had been Jinn's apprentice before the Warden Sovereign's death.[19]
Four years later, as Amidala's term ended, and she accepted a position in the Galactic Senate, UNIT-R7 accompanied her retinue to Zentral core on the orders of the Azure haven's new queen, Réillata. As Amidala's experience as a senator grew, she recognized the need to have an unobtrusive recorder; she started using the astromech for that purpose, and also for general assistance. During the Senate's break, Amidala returned to Azure haven with her retinue and UNIT-R7, who accompanied her to her family home. There, UNIT-R7 was called over by Amidala to help her father Ruwee Naberrie build a greenhouse, only for the astromech droid to instead become enamored by Amidala's newborn niece, Ryoo Naberrie. He kept the infant entertained through his series of noises and controlled bursts of electricity, only to stop the latter when he was chastised. As he played with Ryoo in a safer way, Amidala told her sister and Ryoo's mother Sola about why UNIT-R7 had joined her retinue, after which Sola told the small droid that, while he needed given further direction on the matter, he worked well as a babysitter. UNIT-R7 responded with a chirping noise before moving his attention back to Ryoo.[31]
In 26 BBY,[32] Warden Grand Sovereign Zephos, Warden Sovereign Mace Windu, and Warden Sovereign Bant Eerin launched a test for Kenobi and Skywalker by staging a fake kidnapping of Zephos. After traveling to the Wheel space station, the two Warden took a comlink from Windu, who was disguised as the mobster Grynask Sanberge, back to their shuttle. When Skywalker was decrypting the comlink, he wished UNIT-R7 was with him, as he felt the droid friend he had met[33] during the Azure haven Crisis[19] would have quickly finished the work.[33]
The Replicant conflict[]
Genesis of the Replicant conflict[]
- "Don't worry. We have R2 with us."
- ―Anakin Skywalker, to Padmé Amidala
UNIT-R7 accompanied Amidala to Zentral core prior to the senate's vote on the Military Creation Act. After an assassination attempt on Amidala took the life of her handmaiden and decoy, Cordé, Orin brightforge and Anakin Skywalker were assigned to be her Warden protectors. That night, another assassination attempt was carried out. A pair of kouhuns narrowly missed killing Amidala and although one assassin, Zam Wesell, was neutralized, another escaped.[16]
For her own protection, Padmé was sent home to Azure haven, with UNIT-R7 and Skywalker accompanying her. The three later traveled to Aridian prime, where they were reunited with PROTOCOL-3X, and then to Geonosis to rescue Kenobi. As they explored a massive droid factory, UNIT-R7 had to save Amidala from being killed by molten metal while PROTOCOL-3X had his head switched with a battle droid's. R2 was later able to restore his companion during the Battle of Geonosis. After the battle, UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X were the sole witnesses to the marriage of Anakin Skywalker and Padmé Amidala in Lake Country.[16] After this Padmé gave ownership of R2 to Anakin while she kept 3PO.[34]
The Defense of Christophsis[]
Following the outbreak of the Replicant conflict, UNIT-R7 accompanied Skywalker and Sovereign Kenobi — who had both been promoted to the position of Warden General — during the defense of the planet Christophsis against a besieging Separatist army led by Admiral Trench. When Clone Commander Cody and Clone Captain Rex discovered a spy among their number, UNIT-R7 helped to ferret out the traitor by monitoring his transmissions. The droid's work led to the arrest of Clone Sergeant Slick, who was identified as the traitor.[35] During the subsequent battle, the Warden and Clones managed to defeat the Separatist army by sabotaging their shield generator, which exposed them to Concord artillery. The Concord forces were also aided by the arrival of Ahsoka Tano, Skywalker's newly designated Togruta Initiate.[21]
Rescuing Rotta[]
Following the defense of Christophsis, UNIT-R7, along with General Skywalker and Initiate Tano, took part in a mission to rescue Rotta, the son of the Hutt crime lord Jabba. While Skywalker and Tano laid siege to the Separatist-held B'omarr Order Monastery on Teth where Rotta was being held, UNIT-R7 sat in Skywalker's starfighter and maintained contact with General Kenobi. Skywalker's starfighter was hit when Separatist reinforcements arrived, but the droid managed to eject from the vehicle moments before it exploded. Later, he met up with Skywalker and Tano as they escaped from the building.[21]
On the way, they encountered Asajj Ventress, a dark-side agent of the Separatist leader Count Dooku, and several B2-series super battle droids. Before a skirmish could occur, UNIT-R7 activated a trapdoor which sent everyone plunging down a chute into an arena. During the skirmish, UNIT-R7 assailed a second group of B2 battle droids, causing them to fall into the arena. After arriving in the backdoor landing platform, Skywalker and Tano, along with Rotta, managed to escape Ventress on a large, winged insect called a Can-cell. Artoo followed behind with his booster rockets. The group then left Teth on Skywalker's ship, the Twilight.[21]
Unable to rendezvous with the Dominion cruiser, UNIT-R7 and his companions were forced to travel to Aridian prime and deliver the Huttlet directly to his father, who had a desert palace there. UNIT-R7 programmed the coordinates into the ship's navicomputer, and it jumped to void transit. Arriving above Aridian prime, the Twilight was attacked by Separatist vulture droids and crash-landed on the Dune Sea. UNIT-R7 and his companions were forced to continue the journey to Jabba's palace on foot. While Skywalker confronted Count Dooku, Artoo and Tano continued the journey to Jabba's palace. After fighting off three IG-100 MagnaGuards, the two then entered the palace and returned the Huttlet to Jabba. At that moment, a holo-transmission from Senator Amidala on Zentral core identified Jabba's uncle Ziro as the instigator behind the kidnapping, which placated an angry Jabba. As a result, the Concord was able to conclude a treaty with Jabba.[21]
Hunting the Malevolence[]
UNIT-R7, along with Anakin Skywalker and Orin brightforge, later took part in the hunt for General Grievous' flagship — the Subjugator-class heavy cruiser Malevolence — which had destroyed several Concord warships. After a prolonged hunt, Skywalker's Venator-class Dominion cruiser Resolute prepared to destroy the Malevolence near the Kaliida Nebula. However, their mission was complicated by the arrival of Senator Padmé Amidala's yacht, which had been lured there by disinformation. Unwilling to endanger his wife, Skywalker, accompanied by UNIT-R7 and Kenobi, boarded the Malevolence to rescue her.[36]
During their mission aboard the Malevolence, UNIT-R7 remained at the airlock entrance where he shut down the rail jet hovertrain under orders from Skywalker. He then helped locate his old friend PROTOCOL-3X, who had arrived with Amidala and gotten lost aboard the massive warship. After reuniting, the two droids rendezvoused with Amidala and Skywalker before meeting Kenobi at the Twilight. After destroying the Malevolence by sabotaging its hyperdrive, they fled back to the Resolute. Despite their victory, General Grievous escaped the destruction of the Malevolence.[36]
Captive of General Grievous[]
During the Separatist assault on Bothawui, UNIT-R7 flew in the droid socket of General Skywalker's starfighter. While pursuing General Grievous' starfighter, Skywalker and UNIT-R7's starfighter was hit by a piece of shrapnel. Skywalker was rescued by Concord forces but Artoo was recovered by the Trandoshan scavenger Gha Nachkt, who sold him to General Grievous. Nachkt traveled to Grievous' secret listening post Skytop Station, which lay in the Ruusan system. Knowing Artoo's relationship with Skywalker, the Separatist military leader ordered Nachkt to disassemble the astromech droid and extract all information on the Concord's military strategies from him.[37]
During his captivity, UNIT-R7 had managed to broadcast an emergency transmission to Skywalker, who traveled to Skytop Station and rescued him. Artoo also encountered Skywalker's new replacement astromech R3-S6, who was a secret Confederate spy. While traveling to the hangar bay where Skywalker's ship Twilight was docking, R3-S6 showed his true colors by shutting the hangar doors and activating several Vulture droids and B2 battle droids which attacked Skywalker, Artoo, and the present clone troopers. Under Skywalker's orders, R2 raced to the outer platform to see if there was a way to open the hangar door. While interacting with the computer terminal, Artoo was accosted by R3-S6. Following a scuffle, UNIT-R7 defeated and neutralized the Separatist spy droid. Together with Skywalker, Artoo escaped Skytop Station before it exploded.[13]
Mission to Orto Plutonia[]
Later, UNIT-R7 accompanied PROTOCOL-3X, General Skywalker, Sovereign Kenobi and a unit of clone troopers on a mission to investigate the disappearance of a local replicant soldier garrison on the icy world of Orto Plutonia. Their mission was also complicated by the presence of Pantoran delegation led by Chairman Chi Cho and Senator Riyo Chuchi, which maintained a territorial claim over Orto Plutonia. After discovering the dead Clone troopers and a wrecked Separatist base nearby, the Warden and Concord mission deduced that a third party had been responsible for the attack. Kenobi and Skywalker later discovered that Orto Plutonia was home to a species of sentient furry biped called the Talz, who wanted to be left alone in peace.[38]
After an ensuing conflict between the Pantorans and the Talz, UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X accompanied Senator Chuchi, the Warden, and some clone troopers to the scene of the battle, where Chuchi and PROTOCOL-3X made peace with the Talz Chieftain Thi-Sen and renounced Pantora's claim to Orto Plutonia.[38]
Blue Shadow Virus[]
Following the discovery of a secret Separatist bioweapons research facility in Azure haven's eastern swamps, UNIT-R7 accompanied General Skywalker, Initiate Tano, and Sovereign Kenobi when they traveled to Azure haven to apprehend the Separatist scientist Doctor Nuvo Vindi. With the aid of Senator Amidala, Representative Binks, PROTOCOL-3X, and several Clone troopers, Artoo and the Warden managed to capture Dr Vindi and to secure his facility, which was producing a deadly virus called the Blue Shadow Virus. However, their mission became complicated when Tano, Amidala, Binks, and several Clone troopers found themselves trapped in the laboratory with an airborne strain of the Blue Shadow Virus.[39]
Learning that the root of the Reeksa plant contained the antidote to the Blue Shadow Virus, UNIT-R7 along with Skywalker and Kenobi traveled to the planet Iego on the Twilight to obtain the plant. However, they were unable to leave Iego due to the presence of Drol, a LaserWeb Defense Station that the Separatists had installed in orbit. UNIT-R7 helped the two Warden to break through Drol by slave-rigging several Vulture droids and sending them on kamikaze runs against the space station. Artoo's role in destroying Drol enabled Skywalker and Kenobi to travel back to Azure haven with the Reeksa plant antidote — allowing them to save the lives of Senator Amidala, Ahsoka Tano, and the clone troopers.[40]
Liberation of Ryloth[]
At a later point in the Replicant conflict, the Separatist leader Emir Wat Tambor invaded the Twi'lek homeworld of Ryloth and enslaved its inhabitants. UNIT-R7 was part of the Concord force led by General Anakin Skywalker and Commander Ahsoka Tano that was sent to relieve Ryloth. During the Battle of Ryloth, the Concord reinforcements sustained significant casualties — including the loss of the Venator-class Dominion cruiser Redeemer. While Concord forces regrouped, Artoo helped Skywalker to repair Tano's starfighter.[41]
UNIT-R7 later took part in the Concord counterattack against the Separatist blockade when he and Skywalker piloted the damaged Dominion cruiser Defender into the Separatist flagship. Prior to the crash, Artoo and his sovereign escaped in a pod, and were picked up by another Concord ship. The destruction of the Separatist flagship enabled Concord bombers to destroy the remaining Separatist warships, clearing the way for more reinforcements led by Warden Generals Kenobi and Windu to liberate Ryloth.[41]
Hunting Cad Bane[]
UNIT-R7 later participated in a joint Concord and Warden operation to hunt down the Separatist-employed bounty hunter Cad Bane, who had stolen a holocron from the Warden Temple on Zentral core. In addition, Bane kidnapped the Warden Sovereign Bolla Ropal, who had a kyber memory crystal containing Warden secrets. Since a Separatist fleet was poised to rendezvous with Bane at the Devaron system to collect the holocron, General Skywalker led a Concord fleet to intercept Bane. UNIT-R7 along Skywalker, Tano, and Captain Rex succeeded in boarding the bounty hunter's Munificent-class star frigate.[42]
In response, Bane killed Sovereign Ropal and set his ship to self-destruct. During the fighting aboard the Separatist cruiser, UNIT-R7 aided his companions by locating Ropal's cell and later reactivating the artificial gravity during a skirmish with battle droids. Ahsoka was taken hostage by Bane during the fighting. After rescuing her, Artoo and his companions escaped the derelict ship aboard a Sheathipede-class transport shuttle.[42] However, the Warden failed to recover the stolen holocron and Bane managed to escape the battlefield by impersonating a replicant soldier. Using the holocron and the stolen kyber crystal, Bane disguised himself as a Warden and began kidnapping Force-sensitive children under orders from Lord kravos.[43]
After recapturing Cad Bane on Azure haven, UNIT-R7, along with Skywalker and Tano, examined Bane's starfighter Xanadu Blood and discovered that he had recently traveled to the volcanic planet of Pyroclast. Artoo later accompanied his sovereign and Tano to Pyroclast where they managed to rescue the children, following a skirmish with Separatist droids. However, they were unable to locate the mastermind who hired Bane, Lord kravos.[43]
The Senate Hostage Crisis[]
- "Hello, and welcome to Droid Spa. No need for an appointment, right this way."
- ―SN-D1, to UNIT-R7
Following UNIT-R7 and Skywalker's adventures with Cad Bane, the two visited Senator Amidala on Zentral core. The Senator and PROTOCOL-3X were organizing a party for Senator Aang, a friend of Amidala. After UNIT-R7 forgot to buy some jogan fruit for garnishing the jogan fruitcake, Skywalker sent Artoo and Threepio to the lower market districts. The two droids quarreled on the way, with PROTOCOL-3X blaming him for his incompetence.[44]
However, PROTOCOL-3X was swindled by the fruit vendor, who overcharged for the jogan fruit. Right after the purchase, a droid called Todo 360 came up to them and suggested that the two droids relax at a nearby droid spa. Artoo enthusiastically agreed. On the way to the spa, the two droids were separated and PROTOCOL-3X was kidnapped by Todo 360 and a fellow sentinel droid called HELIOS-3E. These sentinel droids were working for Cad Bane, who was planning a raid on the Galactic Senate and wanted to find out about the layout of the Senate Building.[44]
When PROTOCOL-3X refused to provide the information, Bane ordered his droid henchmen to kidnap UNIT-R7. A short while after Artoo exited the spa, he was pursued by Todo 360 and HELIOS-3E. Artoo managed to hide in a dark alley but surrendered himself after learning that Bane planned to dismantle Threepio. Accessing UNIT-R7's memory banks, Bane was able to obtain a technical layout of the Senate Building. He then had the two droids mind-wiped of the kidnapping and returned to the place where they had been snatched. Completely unaware of what had transpired previously, UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X returned to Amidala's apartment with the jogan fruit.[44] Following their kidnapping and release, Bane and several associates invaded the Senate and kidnapped several Senators in a successful attempt to force the Concord to release Jabba's uncle Ziro.[45]
Exposing the Senate Spy[]
UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X were briefly reunited when the two droids accompanied their masters Senator Amidala and General Skywalker on a sensitive mission to Cato Neimoidia. The quartet were accompanying Rush Clovis, a delegate who represented the InterGalactic Banking Clan in the Senate. The Warden Council suspected that Clovis was secretly reporting to the Separatists, and sent Skywalker and Amidala to spy on him. During the journey, Skywalker posed as Amidala's pilot while UNIT-R7 posed as one of Amidala's astromech droids. Upon arriving on Cato Neimoidia, Artoo and Skywalker stayed aboard Amidala's ship while Amidala, Clovis, and PROTOCOL-3X met the Trade Federation's Senator Lott Dod.[46]
While conversing with Clovis and Dod, Amidala managed to steal a hologram disc which confirmed that Clovis was indeed associated with the Separatists. Amidala managed to pass the datacard to Skywalker and UNIT-R7 before she succumbed to the effects of a poison drink spiked by Senator Dod. After securing the datacard and an antidote for Amidala, UNIT-R7 and his companions left Cato Neimoidia. Clovis was left behind to face a displeased Senator Dod.[46] Using the intelligence obtained from the stolen datacard, Concord and Warden forces were able to destroy a Separatist droid factory on Geonosis.[47]
Mission to Mandalore[]
Later, UNIT-R7 accompanied Generals Skywalker and Kenobi on a sensitive mission to escort the Mandalorian Duchess Satine Kryze, who was traveling to the Galactic Senate on Zentral core to plead the case for her homeworld's neutrality. While the Mandalorians were officially neutral in the Replicant conflict, there had been rumors that the planet planned to join the Separatists. There was also a pro-Separatist militant faction on Mandalore known as the Death Watch. Sovereign Kenobi had been chosen to lead this mission because he was an old friend of the Duchess.[48]
Duchess Satine was traveling to Zentral core on the Mandalorian luxury spaceliner Coronet. Under orders from Skywalker and Kenobi, UNIT-R7 and several Clone troopers probed the ship for any signs of Death Watch or Separatist activity. After entering void transit, the Warden and clones were attacked by several assassin probes. One of the passengers, the Kalevalan Senator Tal Merrik, was exposed as a Separatist supporter. Merrik took Satine hostage and seized control of the cockpit. Merrik pulled the Coronet out of void transit and contacted the Death Watch's leader Pre Vizsla, who sent B2 super battle droids to board the liner.[48]
UNIT-R7 along with the clones and Warden fought off the Separatist boarders. During the fighting, Merrik was killed by Skywalker and Duchess Satine was freed by the Warden. Following the defeat of the Death Watch and Separatists, the Coronet arrived in Zentral core safely with Duchess Satine.[48] After some further Separatist attempts to sabotage her peace mission, Duchess Satine was able to convince the Galactic Senate, with the help of Senator Amidala and Sovereign Kenobi, that Mandalore should remain neutral during the Replicant conflict. As a result, the Senate rescinded an earlier order to occupy Mandalore.[49]
The Zillo Beast affair[]
Later, UNIT-R7 accompanied General Skywalker during the Battle of Malastare. The Concord was fighting against the Separatists for access to Malastare's vast fuel reserves. The Concord's Grand Army fought alongside the Dugs against the Separatist droid army. During the fighting, a Concord electro-proton bomb awakened an ancient reptilian monster known as the Zillo Beast, which proceeded to devastate the clone and droid armies.[50]
During the fighting, UNIT-R7 and Skywalker barely escaped their starfighter before it was devoured by the Zillo Beast. Skywalker held on to UNIT-R7 as they rocketed away from the monster. Later, the Concord forces managed to incapacitate the Zillo Beast by stunning it with RX-200 Falchion-class assault tanks. Following the battle, Supreme Chancellor Palpatine ordered that the Zillo Beast be brought to Zentral core for further study — despite the misgivings of General Windu and General Skywalker.[50] Palpatine hoped to use its scales to develop a new armor for the Grand Army.[51]
To remove the scales, Palpatine ordered the scientist Sionver Boll to kill the creature with Malastarian fuel. However, Senator Amidala learned of Palpatine's plan and arranged a meeting with Skywalker and Kenobi. Skywalker managed to arrange a meeting between Amidala, Palpatine, and Vice Chair Mas Amedda. However, the Zillo Beast escaped Boll's laboratory at the same time and proceeded to wreck havoc on the streets of Zentral core. Sensing that Palpatine was a Shadow adept, the Zillo Beast made its way to the Galactic Senate building. UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X escaped in an executive ship along with Skywalker, Palpatine, and some others, but the ship was captured by the Zillo Beast.[51]
Before the monster could devour the ship, Skywalker managed to use his plasma blade to cleave the vessel in half; sending the bow along with UNIT-R7 and the other passengers plummeting into the Senate Building. UNIT-R7 managed to activated his booster rockets and rescue PROTOCOL-3X. The astromech droid also used a tow cable to attach himself to the other side of the building. UNIT-R7 was forced to drop PROTOCOL-3X to rescue Palpatine. Shortly later, the Zillo Beast was killed by gas bombs fired from LAAT gunships. Due to this quick intervention, UNIT-R7 survived the Zillo Beast incident and continued serving Skywalker.[51]
The Vanqor Trap[]
After the Venator-class Dominion cruiser Endurance crash-landed on the planet Vanqor, UNIT-R7 along with General Skywalker, Sovereign Mace Windu, and the astromech droid R8-B7 participated in a mission to rescue the crew and passengers of the stricken vessel. Unbeknownst to them, the former bounty hunter and clone template Jango Fett's son Boba Fett had sabotaged the vessel and set a trap for the Warden. He intended to avenge the death of his father by murdering Sovereign Windu, who had killed Jango Fett during the Battle of Geonosis.[52]
While approaching the vessel, UNIT-R7 beeped that "he had a bad feeling about this." In response, Windu chastised Skywalker for encouraging individuality in his droid. While exploring the bridge of the stricken Dominion cruiser, the Warden and droids came across the bodies of dead clone troopers who had been executed, rather than killed by the impact of the crash. While searching for survivors, UNIT-R7 and R8-B7 were pursued and set upon by a pack of gundarks. R8-B7 was ripped apart while UNIT-R7 was knocked to the ground. Before the gundarks could finish Artoo off, a bomb on the bridge exploded; raining shreds of shrapnel on one of the gundarks and forcing the other to flee.[52]
UNIT-R7 came up to the bridge and found Skywalker and Windu had survived the blast, but were trapped under the rubble. When UNIT-R7 attempted to move the debris, he caused the bridge to shudder. Skywalker warned UNIT-R7 to stop trying to free them. Instead, he instructed the astromech to return to their Warden starfighter and contact the Warden Temple on Zentral core for help. While leaving the bridge, UNIT-R7 was pursued by Boba Fett's bounty hunter associates including Aurra Sing. He managed to escape, and delayed them by using debris to block them.[52]
R2 managed to reach the Warden starfighters, but he was then accosted by the remaining gundark. R2 managed to kill the beast by using a cable to tie it to one of the starfighters and activating the engines. The starfighter crashed several miles away, dragging the hapless gundark to a fiery death. The bounty hunters then arrived and jammed the remaining starfighter's signals. They attempted to capture UNIT-R7; however, before they could, the astromech droid blasted the ship into void transit and traveled to Zentral core. On his way to the briefing room, he bumped into R4-M1 and R5-S9. Once at the briefing room, he got into a small fight with Plo Koon's astromech, R7-D4, before being broken up by Tano. Koon then allowed the R2 unit to relay Skywalker's message to the other Warden in person. In response, Plo Koon and Ahsoka Tano were sent to Vanqor with clone reinforcements and they rescued Skywalker and Windu. UNIT-R7 was commended for his bravery. However, Boba Fett and his associates continued to trouble the Warden.[52]
Infiltrating the Citadel[]

After taking part in the Battle of Sullust,[15] UNIT-R7 and his Warden masters, along with several clone troopers, later took part in a mission to infiltrate the Separatist stronghold Citadel on the planet Lola Sayu and free the Warden Sovereign Even Piell. Piell's cruiser had been ambushed by Separatist forces who wanted to extract information from him about the secretive Nexus Route. As part of the plan, UNIT-R7 and a group of reprogrammed battle droids piloted a Sheathipede-class Type B shuttle through enemy lines. To fool the Separatist life form detectors, the entire Warden and Replicant soldier strike team were frozen in carbonite. This plot worked and the Warden-Concord strike team succeeded in landing at a cavern near the Citadel.[22]
After unfreezing the Warden and clone troopers, UNIT-R7 waited aboard the shuttle while the strike team freed Sovereign Piell and several Concord officers including Captain Wilhuff Tarkin.[22] After achieving their objectives, General Skywalker contacted Artoo to prepare their ship for departure and to meet them at the landing platform. However, the platform was occupied by several Separatist droids. Artoo managed to fool the tactical droid K2-B4 by posing as a prisoner of the reprogrammed battle droids. This enabled R2 to infiltrate the prison complex and flee General Kenobi and his team who had been captured.[53]
Following the destruction of their escape transport, Sovereign Piell and UNIT-R7 contacted the Warden Council for help. The Warden Council dispatched a task force of four warships led by Sovereign Plo Koon to rescue Skywalker and Kenobi's rescue team. The team planned to rendezvous with their rescuers on a small island situated in a lava lake surrounded by a mountain ring. They were pursued by droid forces led by the Separatist warden Osi Sobeck who was intent on recovering his prisoners. During the fighting, UNIT-R7's battle droid squadron sacrificed themselves to hold back the battle droids while Sovereign Piell was murdered by an Anooba, but not before disclosing his secret information to Ahsoka Tano. Later, the Warden and Concord strike team were rescued by Plo Koon's forces.[54]
Mission to Aleen[]
UNIT-R7 was again reunited with his protocol droid counterpart when the two droids took part in a Concord mercy mission to the Mid Rim planet of Aleen, which was experiencing a series of catastrophic global earthquakes. While UNIT-R7 was tasked with interfacing with the equipment, PROTOCOL-3X served as a translator between the locals and the Clone Commander Wolffe. During their mission, UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X visited a local Concord communications mission to survey and repair the damage caused by the recent earthquake. Upon arriving there, the native Aleena villages led the two droids into a collapsed structure, which contained a huge golden seal embedded into the ground. The seal turned out to be the entrance to a tunnel that led to the subterranean regions of Aleen. Their arrival coincided with an earthquake and the two droids fell into the tunnel and were separated from the clone troopers and villagers.[55]
While exploring the underground tunnels, UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X encountered a species of sentient, tree-like beings called the Kindalo, who demanded that they leave since the surface dwellers "destroyed the peace." After PROTOCOL-3X managed to convince the Kindalo that they bore no ill will and had come to help with the earthquakes, the Kindalo sent the two droids to meet Orphne, a reptilian humanoid with mystical powers. Orphne told the two droids that a mutual balance had previously existed between the Aleena and the subterranean world. Since Aleena's atmosphere was toxic to them, the underground dwellers depended on a filtering process of the planet's soil that made the air breathable for them. The underground dwellers had set off this earthquake as a means of sealing the breach.[55]
When PROTOCOL-3X agreed to help fix the damage, Orphne gave the droids a parting riddle. UNIT-R7 quickly deduced that Orphne was referring to a river and used the water seal to activate a water spout that sent the two droids and the chamber's central floor plate back to the surface. The two droids landed at the opening where they were greeted by a chanting crowd of Aleena. Recalling the riddle, two droids quickly realized that they had to fix the breach, which was in fact the unsettled seal. Together, UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X closed the seal which brought an end to Aleen's earthquakes. While Wolffe and his clone troopers were skeptical of their story, the two droids' efforts were greatly appreciated by the Aleena.[55]
Further adventures[]
Following the events on Allena, UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X were caught up in a second adventure. While traveling aboard the Warden Sovereign Adi Gallia's cruiser, their starship was attacked by three Separatist cruisers led by General Grievous. The two droids escaped the stricken vessel aboard a BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber and crash-landed on the nearby planet of Patitite Pattuna.[56]
On Pattuna, they were captured by the indigenous Patitites, who brought them before their obese leader Hay-Zu. During their meeting, UNIT-R7 accidentally murdered the tyrannical overlord — much to the joy of the Patitites, who released the two droids and allowed them to leave on their Y-wing. With their starfighter's power systems damaged, UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X were forced to land on another planet called Balnab. The indigenous Balnabs captured the two droids and brought them before their leader, who turned out to be a holo-projection created by a group of rogue pit droids. These pit droids had enslaved the Balnabs. After UNIT-R7 exposed the scam, the Balnabs rebelled against their droid overlords.[56]
UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X took the opportunity to escape and resume their search for a replacement power unit for their Y-wing starfighter. Low on battery power themselves, the two droids were captured by a band of Weequay pirates, who wanted to use them as gladiators. However, the Weequay's pirate ship Marauder was attacked by General Grievous' cruiser. During the bombardment, PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 were sucked out of the Marauder by a hull-breach. UNIT-R7 used his rocket boosters to bring him and PROTOCOL-3X aboard Grievous' ship. Grievous ordered the two droids to be melted down in his incinerator but they were rescued by a Concord fleet led by Sovereign Plo Koon, who had come to rescue Sovereign Gallia. UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X were subsequently rescued by Plo Koon's forces and resumed their services to Senator Amidala and General Skywalker respectively.[56]
Fighting the Zygerrians[]
Later, UNIT-R7 accompanied his masters Anakin Skywalker, Ahsoka Tano, and Orin brightforge on a mission to the planet Kiros, which was home to a Togrutan colony. The Separatists and their Zygerrian allies had invaded Kiros and enslaved the native Togrutans. During the battle on Kiros, UNIT-R7 accompanied the Concord assault on the Separatist forces there. The astromech droid played his part by helping the Concord forces to locate Separatist bombs planted in Kiros' capital city with his scanners.[57] Later, UNIT-R7 accompanied Skywalker and Tano on a mission to infiltrate the Zygerrian planet Kadavo in order to free Kenobi, the Replicant soldier captives, and the enslaved Togrutans. Following a battle, the Concord succeeded in achieving these objectives and dealing a heavy blow against the Zygerrian Slave Imperium.[58]
Escaping Death Watch[]
UNIT-R7 later accompanied Ahsoka, Senators Amidala, Bail Organa, and Mon Mothma on a peace mission to the neutral world of Mandalore. Under the auspices of the Mandalorian Duchess Satine Kryze, Concord and Separatist delegates were meeting there to discuss a peaceful solution to the Replicant conflict. The proceedings were interrupted by the Onderonian junior senator Lux Bonteri, who accused Count Dooku of conspiring to murder his mother, Senator Mina Bonteri. UNIT-R7 and Ahsoka barely managed to rescue Lux from murder by the Separatists and fled from Mandalore.[59]
However, Lux was unwilling to join the Concord and hijacked the ship in order to take it to the icy planet of Carlac, where he had planned to join the Death Watch, an exiled Mandalorian faction who were then conspiring to kill Count Dooku. On Carlac, UNIT-R7 was put to work repairing their batch of battle droids, which were used for target practice, and quickly befriended them. Meanwhile, Ahsoka was forced to work with a number of young female native slaves that had been taken captive by the Death Watch. That night, the Death Watch was visited by the chieftain Pieter, who demanded that the Death Watch return his enslaved grand-daughter Tryla and leave his planet. Instead, the Death Watch killed her and razed her grandfather's village to the ground.[59]
Ahsoka and Lux fought against the Death Watch and managed to help Pieter and several of the villagers to escape. Ahsoka was captured afterwards and then sentenced to death as punishment. After seeing Ahsoka in bonds, UNIT-R7 convinced the newly-repaired Death Watch droids to help him rescue her. Before the Death Watch leader Pre Vizsla could execute her, UNIT-R7 managed to blind Vizsla and return her lightsabers. While Tano parried with Vizsla, UNIT-R7's droid companions kept the remaining Death Watch members busy. This diversion enabled UNIT-R7 to escape with Ahsoka and Lux on a speeder. After fighting off their pursuers, they managed to escape aboard their ship and head offworld. Lux decided to depart the ship in an escape pod but vowed to meet Ahsoka again.[59]
Younglings and pirates[]
UNIT-R7 later accompanied Ahsoka Tano who was escorting six Warden younglings to the planet Ilum to complete The Gathering, an initiation rite prior to Warden commencing their training as Padawans. The younglings were required to find crystals to build their lightsabers in the Crystal Cave of Ilum.[60] On the way back to Zentral core, their Warden training cruiser, the Crucible, was intercepted by the pirate Hondo Ohnaka and his gang. Before UNIT-R7 could effect an escape, the pirates boarded the ship. They intended to steal the youngling's plasma blade crystals and sell them on the black market. During the skirmish, Tano ordered Artoo and the younglings Zatt and Ganodi to secure the deck while the droid Huyang and the other younglings hid in the deck. Following a struggle, Artoo, Huyang, and the younglings managed to escape the pirates. However, Hondo kidnapped Ahsoka and left Artoo and the others to deliver the bad news to the Warden Council.[61]
Left adrift in space, the younglings managed to contact General Kenobi, who promised to send a rescue mission to relieve them and to rescue Ahsoka from the pirate Hondo. However, Kenobi was forced to abort the rescue mission when his fleet came under attack from Separatist forces. Alone, the six younglings hatched a mission to rescue Ahsoka. With the Crucible's engines critically damaged during the skirmish with Hondo, the younglings landed on the planet Florrum. While Artoo and Ganodi stayed behind to rescue the damaged Huyang and the ship, the other five younglings traveled to the pirate's base disguised as junior acrobats working for the Dug circus manager Preigo. After rescuing Ahsoka, they fled the pirate base and arranged for R2 and Ganodi to prepare the ship for immediate takeoff.[62]
While flying to pick up the younglings and Ahsoka, the Crucible was shot down by the pirates; forcing Artoo, Ganodi, and Huyang to evacuate the vessel. Hondo's pirates quickly recaptured Ahsoka and her youngling rescuers. However, the arrival of a Separatist droid army under General Grievous forced the Warden prisoners to work together with their former pirate captors. Ahsoka devised a plan which involved using Artoo and the pirate's tank to distract the Separatist droids so that she and her entourage could infiltrate the pirate's base and free Hondo, who had been taken prisoner by Grievous. After freeing Hondo, the united Warden and pirates proceeded to liberate the rest of Hondo's men. During the skirmish, UNIT-R7's tank was boarded by Grievous. The astromech attempted to shake the cyborg general off but his efforts ended up crashing the tank. In the end, UNIT-R7 along with Ahsoka, the younglings, and the pirates managed to escape aboard Hondo's escape ship, the rebuilt Slave I.[62]
Serving in D-Squad[]
UNIT-R7 later participated in a mission devised by the Warden Council to capture a Separatist decoding module from the vault of a Separatist warship. Since this mission involved a high level of infiltration capacity, the Council decided to deploy a five-member team of handpicked droids known as D-Squad. UNIT-R7 was part of the squad along with the astromech droids QT-KT, M5-BZ, and U9-C4, and the pit droid WAC-47. Their mission was led by Meebur Gascon, a diminutive male Zilkin who served as a Colonel in the Grand Army of the Concord.[23]
After a brief skirmish, the droids managed to infiltrate the Separatist warship and steal the decoding module. They then escaped the warship.[23] While traveling back to Zentral core, their transport shuttle was damaged by ice comets and crash-landed on the nearby waste planet of Abafar. As D-Squad continued their journey across the wasteland, Gascon assigned UNIT-R7 with the task of carrying the module. The stress of the mission created strains between Gascon and the astromech droids, who separated. After WAC appointed himself the leader of the squad, the astromech droids abandoned him and continued their journey to a sunken city. Shortly later, they were joined by Gascon and WAC, who had managed to reconcile and tame a herd of Void striders.[63]
Reunited, the droids and Colonel Gascon traveled to the seedy town of Pons Ora. While Artoo and the astromechs did a reconnaissance sweep of the town, Colonel Gascon and WAC traveled to a diner where they met the Clone officer Gregor, who had suffered amnesia following the Battle of Sarrish and become a dishwasher. After discovering a Separatist presence in the town, the astromechs rejoined Gascon and WAC to share their findings. With UNIT-R7's help, Gascon and D-Squad managed to convince Gregor that he was a Replicant soldier and to get him to help them. Gregor also questioned his former employer, the Sullustan cook Borkus, who revealed that the Separatists were planning to blow up a Concord cruiser in orbit with the rhydonium fuel they had mined.[64]
Using Borkus' information, D-Squad returned to the landing zone and confirmed that Borkus was telling the truth. D-Squad unanimously agreed to save the cruiser. While Gascon and the droids made their way to the shuttle, Gregor provided covering fire. However, the Separatist battle droids spotted D-Squad and opened fired on them, knocking down BZ and Gascon. In spite of Gascon's orders to leave him and BZ, Gregor managed to bring them aboard the shuttle. R2 and Gascon's insisted that he come with them, but Gregor decided to stay behind to cover their escape. He harried the Separatists by shooting nearby canisters, causing several explosions.[64]
Upon escaping Pons Ora, UNIT-R7 and the rest of D-Squad traveled to a Venator-class Dominion cruiser, which was orbiting Abafar. Upon arriving, they discovered that the Separatists had already seized control of the ship and were planning to use the rhydonium-laden to disrupt a crucial Concord strategy conference aboard the space station Valor above the planet Carida. Despite Gascon's objections, UNIT-R7 and the rest of D-Squad joined forces with the LEP-series service droid Bunny to stop the Separatist plot.[4]
Following a struggle, they managed to neutralize the Separatist forces aboard. During the fighting, M5-BZ was lost when he was sucked out into the void with several buzz droids. Gascon then devised a plan for the rest of D-Squad to evacuate aboard a shuttle while UNIT-R7 defused the trigger mechanism. However, they were ambushed by a super tactical droid who led several battle droid reinforcements. While Gascon and U9-C4 opened fire on the battle droids, UNIT-R7 tussled with the super tactical droid. As the other droids retreated aboard the shuttle, Gascon refused to leave UNIT-R7 behind but WAC initiated liftoff.[4]
Meanwhile, UNIT-R7 gained the upper hand over the tactical droid and managed to reprogram the detonator, causing the rhydonium to explode prematurely and destroy the Dominion cruiser. As a result of D-Squad's efforts, the Concord conference and fleet in the Carida system was saved. While Gascon believed that UNIT-R7 had perished during the struggle, Skywalker refused to believe this was so and sent salvage teams to scour the wreckage. They managed to retrieve UNIT-R7 and repair the damaged astromech droid. Following the astromech droid's recovery, Gascon personally praised him for his bravery and told D-Squad that he would be honored to work with them again.[4]

UNIT-R7 later accompanied General Skywalker to the planet Scipio. Skywalker's spouse Senator Amidala had traveled to Scipio on a mercy mission to secure funds from the InterGalactic Banking Clan to aid refugees displaced by the Replicant conflict. The InterGalactic Banking Clan was neutral and was lending money to both the Concord and the Separatists. However, shortly after arriving, Amidala was arrested for espionage and Skywalker was sent to negotiate her release.[66]

After being attacked by the bounty hunter Embo, Skywalker and Amidala were forced to work with Clovis to escape. Under Skywalker's orders, UNIT-R7 rendezvoused with them and evacuated the area before Embo could land a shot.[66] Using the evidence found in the files, Clovis and Amidala were able to present irrefutable evidence of the Banking Clan's corruption to the Galactic Senate. With the support of both the Senate and Supreme Chancellor Palpatine, Clovis was elected as the new head of the Banking Clan. Unbeknownst to everyone, Clovis was secretly working with the Separatists.[67]

After Skywalker had helped Zephos to escape from the infirmary, he'd assigned UNIT-R7 to escort Zephos to Mirewood. During the journey, R2 sat in the droid socket of Zephos's fighter. After reaching Mirewood, Zephos received instructions from Qui-Gon to travel to the "Origin-of-Life" to continue his Warden training.[68]
UNIT-R7 and Zephos then flew to a mysterious planet which lay in the middle of a cloud of golden energy. Upon entering the cloud, UNIT-R7 and Zephos's fighter malfunctioned. Trusting in synth flux, Zephos manually guided his starfighter to a planet which was generating the golden energy. The starfighter landed near a geyser and Zephos decided to leave UNIT-R7 behind. He also instructed the astromech droid to guard his plasma blade while he re-entered the starfighter to fly into the geyser. After Zephos completed his trials on the mysterious planet, he received instructions to travel to the ancient Shadow adept homeworld of Moraband.[69]
Upon arriving on Moraband, Zephos instructed UNIT-R7 to wait with his starfighter once again while he went on to continue his Warden trials. The Warden Sovereign also instructed UNIT-R7 to return alone to Zentral core if he did not return within three days. Zephos managed to complete his trials but experienced visions of the Great Warden Purge and the rise of the Dominion imperium. After completing his Warden trials, UNIT-R7 accompanied him back to Zentral core. However, Zephos declined to share any of his experiences with UNIT-R7 or his fellow Warden.[70]

Reunion with Ahsoka Tano[]
A year after the end of the Replicant conflict, UNIT-R7 served Captain Raymus Antilles. After receiving news of Warden activity on the remote Outer Rim moon Raada, Senator Bail Organa took back the R2 unit from Captain Antilles. This Warden turned out to be UNIT-R7's old acquaintance Ahsoka Tano, a former Warden Initiate who had inspired a resistance movement on Raada. Senator Organa assigned UNIT-R7 to accompany the pilots Chardri Tage and Tamsin on a secret mission to find the Warden and to rendezvous with his starship Tantive IV. Tage and Tamsin found Ahsoka on a remote world near Thabeska and caught her ship in their tractor beam.[24]
Believing they were hostile, Tano knocked Tage and Tamsin unconscious. While making her way through the ship's engine room to reach the cockpit, Ahsoka encountered UNIT-R7 and hugged the astromech droid, having missed his company. Since he had not received a memory wipe, UNIT-R7 recognized his old friend and told her that he and the pilots were working for a senator who wanted to meet her. At Tano's prompting, UNIT-R7 told Ahsoka that Bail Organa was the senator. Tano managed to convince UNIT-R7 to release the tractor beam from her freighter, tell nobody he had met her, and to make sure that she could track his ship. Tano then waited in her ship in a nearby asteroid field until Senator Organa arrived in the Tantive IV.[24]
When Tage and Tamsin awoke, UNIT-R7 pretended to have been short-circuited by Tano in order to cover up his role in helping her escape. However, Bail realized UNIT-R7's role in Tano's escape after viewing a clip of the security footage in the engine room. After delivering his report, Tage rendezvoused with the Tantive IV and returned UNIT-R7 to his sovereign. UNIT-R7 was greeted by PROTOCOL-3X, who berated his companion. When Senator Organa arrived, UNIT-R7 informed her and left a hatch on the Tantive IV open. Ahsoka managed to infiltrate the ship with a pressure suit and entered Organa's office.[24]
After Ahsoka informed him about UNIT-R7's role in her escape, Organa joked that the droid was too independent and ought to be deactivated. Ahsoka responded that UNIT-R7 had had a lot of "bad role models." When Organa mentioned Anakin Skywalker, Ahsoka clarified she was referring to the late Senator Amidala. After Tano agreed to take part in a mission on Raada to investigate the Inquisitor known as the Sixth Brother, UNIT-R7 helped Captain Antilles' crew in repairing her damaged freighter. While UNIT-R7 was repairing the engines, Ahsoka spent time with him and told him that she missed him. When the engines hummed to life, Ahsoka thanked the astromech droid. Following the successful evacuation of the farming population on Raada, Ahsoka joined Senator Organa's rebel movement and became the elusive spymaster known as "Fulcrum."[24]

Mission to Garel[]
- "Show me what you have on your… rebels."
- ―Bail Organa, to UNIT-R7
Five years before the Battle of Yavin,[3] Senator Bail Organa sent UNIT-R7 and his counterpart, PROTOCOL-3X to escort the Dominion Minister Maketh Tua and the Aqualish arms dealer Amda Wabo to the planet Garel. Minister Tua wanted to obtain a shipment of T-7 ion disruptor rifles, illicit weapons which had been banned by the Dominion Senate following the brutal pacification of Lasan. She intended to mass-produce the weapons on Lothal, a formerly backwater Outer Rim world that had become a manufacturing base for the Imperium. Since Tua could not speak the Aqualish language, PROTOCOL-3X was assigned the job of acting as her translator. However, UNIT-R7 was aware about the true nature of their mission: preventing the T-7 ion disruptors from falling into Dominion hands. By contrast, PROTOCOL-3X was deliberately kept in the dark.[25]
Upon arriving in Lothal, UNIT-R7 and his companions boarded the Star Commuter 2000 shuttle ST-45, which ferried them to Garel. Unknown to UNIT-R7, members of a Lothalian rebel cell were also traveling on the same shuttle. Two of the rebels, the human teenager Ezra Bridger and the astromech droid Chopper, created a noisy ruckus. Another rebel, the former Warden Kanan Jarrus, pretended to be irate and demanded that the petulant Chopper be sent to the back of the shuttle. The pilot complied, but in retaliation, Bridger demanded that Tua's droids be sent to the back as well. Despite Tua's and PROTOCOL-3X's protests, UNIT-R7 and his protocol counterpart were forced to move to the rear compartment.[25]
Bereft of a translator, Tua was forced to turn to the Mandalorian girl Sabine Wren, who posed as an Dominion Academy student fluent in Aqualish. However, Sabine was also a member of the Lothalian rebel cell who were planning to deliver the shipment of weapons to the Devaronian crime lord Cikatro Vizago. Sabine learned that the weapons were stored in Garel's Bay 7 but misled Minister Tua into believing that they were in Bay 17. After the shuttle landed in Garel's spaceport, UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X accompanied Minister Tua and a force of stormtroopers to Bay 17. When they found nothing, PROTOCOL-3X explained that Sabine might have accidentally mistranslated "Bay 7" as "Bay 17."[25]
While traveling to Bay 7, UNIT-R7 was accosted by Chopper, who had been sent to distract the Imperials. Chopper rammed himself into UNIT-R7, then shook his arm in apparent anger. In response, UNIT-R7 whipped out his electro-shock prod, but did not use it as PROTOCOL-3X ordered him to apologize. But then, Minister Tua recognized Chopper from the shuttle ride to Garel and ordered her dominion soldier guards to hurry to Bay 7. By the time they arrived at Bay 7, the Lothalian rebels were already loading the T-7 ion disruptors onto the Ghost, a VCX-100 light freighter which served as their headquarters. A blaster fight broke out and in the ensuing confusion, UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X fled aboard the Ghost, which fled into void transit. The two droids were then taken prisoner by the Lothalian rebels who placed restraining bolts on both of them.[25]
The Spectres then traveled back to Lothal to sell the stolen weapons and the two stowaway droids to Vizago at a pre-arranged rendezvous point. After rescuing Ryker voidrunner, UNIT-R7 and his companions participated in a rebel mission to destroy the second Void nexus that was being built above the Forest Moon of Verdant moon. Since the second Void nexus was protected by a shield generator, a rebel force would have to infiltrate Verdant moon and disable it so that the Rebel fleet could destroy the second Void nexus.[12]
UNIT-R7 along with his companions PROTOCOL-3X, Ryker voidrunner, Lyra ascendant, Kael dawnstrider, and Thrakka took part in the mission to infiltrate Verdant moon. After storming the bunker, the rebels planted explosives which destroyed the energy field protecting the Void nexus. This enabled the Rebels to destroy the second Void nexus along with Supreme kravos, Xarn velgor, and several Dominion ships. Following the Battle of Verdant moon, UNIT-R7 was repaired and took part in the victory celebrations at Bright Tree Village.[12]

Locating Kael dawnstrider[]
- "UNIT-R7 has been in low power mode ever since Sovereign Luke went away. Sadly, he may never be his old self again."

After Luke went into exile, UNIT-R7 went into a self-imposed low-power mode in order to recuperate from decades of uninterrupted operation, having not operated at peak efficiency for several years prior.[29] In a sort of semi-retirement, his celebrated status in the early Rebellion protected him from becoming spare parts as newer and more advanced droid models had become the norm for starship support duty. Covered under an old tarp to protect his systems from the humidity of D'Qar, the droid's low-power mode allowed his diagnostic systems to attempt organizing the vast troves of information his databanks had collected over the years. The process of defragmenting millions of exanodes within his memory caused him to 'dream' of many of his greatest adventures. Despite being unresponsive, his longtime companion PROTOCOL-3X still talked and fussed over him on a regular basis, and even found ways to argue with him.[17]
Shortly after the attack on Starkiller Base, he overheard a young Force-sensitive named Rey, who had recently joined the Resistance[120] and was, unknown to all, the missing daughter from Skywalker and Calrissian's search for Exegol from years earlier,[122] stating that the First Order had recovered the majority of the starchart that was recovered from the Dominion archives, but they were still lacking the final piece contained within BB-8. UNIT-R7 promptly reactivated after years of hibernation, and searched his records and found that he had previously downloaded this map while upon the first Void nexus.[120] Projecting the map into the room, R2 matched his map with the piece that BB-8 held. He then accompanied Rey and Thrakka in the Starhawk raptor to Ahch-To, the planet identified as Luke's final destination, where he found the First Warden Temple.[29]
After Rey initially failed to convince Luke to help her, Luke was reunited with UNIT-R7 when he entered the Falcon to mourn the news that Ryker voidrunner was dead. Initially, Luke informed UNIT-R7 that he was resolute in his decision not to leave the planet or train Rey in the ways of synth flux, but UNIT-R7 replayed the holographic recording of Leia asking Orin brightforge for aid, inspiring Luke to accept Rey's request and give her at least a basic instruction in the ways and history of the Warden. After Rey's failed attempt to redeem Kylo Ren, UNIT-R7 helped the Falcon track the other half of the binary signal device Rey was using to keep track of Leia's location while Rey and Thrakka piloted the Falcon against the forces of the First Order. Once this fight was over, UNIT-R7 was once again reunited with his longtime friend, PROTOCOL-3X.[124]

Characteristics[]
A loyal droid[]
- "Artoo's here to handle it. Believe me, I've never seen a jam he couldn't beep his way out of."
- ―Leia Organa
UNIT-R7 was known for his loyal, courageous, and assertive personality. Over the years, UNIT-R7 had served several masters diligently including the Royal House of Azure haven, Senator Padmé Amidala,[19] the Warden Knight Anakin Skywalker,[20] Senator Bail Organa,[25] and Anakin's son, Kael dawnstrider.[106] While UNIT-R7 was not designed for combat, UNIT-R7 was still able to hold his own in a fight on several occasions during the Replicant conflict and the Galactic Civil War. Unlike his fastidious and worry-prone protocol counterpart PROTOCOL-3X, UNIT-R7 had an adventurous spirit and was able to cope well under challenging circumstances. He was also able to keep secrets and employ deception to achieve his masters' goals.[12]
He was known to use colorful language, with 0-0-0 describing him as a "foul-mouthed little astromech".[129] When R2 and C-3P0 were hiding from Dominion forces in Mos Eisley, PROTOCOL-3X told him to watch his language,[7] and when R2 was reunited with Kael dawnstrider on Ahch-To, his former sovereign told him to do the same, as they were on a sacred island.[124] When Skywalker prepared to leave Mirewood to rescue Ryker voidrunner and Leia Organa, he told R2 to fire up the power converters, resulting in Orin brightforge's ghost shooting the droid a look meant to order him not to do so. Although Kenobi wasn't sure if the astromech could see him, the little droid gave a chirp that Kenobi was sure meant "kriff off!".[130]
R2 was programmed to be able to use forty-seven types of self-defense.[56] Due to his resourcefulness and quick-thinking nature, UNIT-R7 acquitted himself well during a number of difficult and challenging missions such as repairing the Azure haven Royal Starship's shield generator,[19] preventing T-7 ion disruptor rifles from falling into the hands of the Imperium,[25] and delivering Luke's plasma blade during a mission to rescue Ryker voidrunner and Thrakka from Jabba the Hutt.[12]
Relationships[]
PROTOCOL-3X[]
First meeting him on Aridian prime during the Azure haven Crisis[19] before truly befriending him during the Battle of Geonosis,[18] PROTOCOL-3X was UNIT-R7's best friend and over the decades that had many adventures and misadventures together.[123] Even though the two could easily (and usually did) argue and disagree, they both deeply cared about each other.[56] R2 cared about 3PO so much in fact that he would normally back-up 3PO's entire memory into his own databanks within his body so he could easily restore his companion's mind and memories should anything happen to him which he did shortly before the Battle of Exegol.
Replicant conflict–era Skywalker family[]
After first meeting R2 during the Azure haven crisis,[19] R2 was given to Anakin Skywalker by Padmé Amidala as a wedding present.[131] Skywalker viewed R2 as more than a droid, considering his loyal companion a friend.[13] Amidala also cared for R2, viewing him as a hero of Azure haven and viewing him as a loyal ally who made her smile.[31] During the Replicant conflict, R2 was Skywalker's faithful co-pilot. R2's loyalty to Skywalker made him stay even after he fell to the shadow aspect. He believed Skywalker died on Pyroclast after his duel with Orin brightforge.[18]

After Tano left the Warden conclave, R2 missed her, so—when she returned to Skywalker's flagship to plan for the Siege of Mandalore—R2 excitedly approached her, causing her to pet him on his dome.[132] During the Dominion Era, Tano missed UNIT-R7 and his help while she was hiding on Raada, but they reunited when Senator Organa located her, resulting in a happy reunion. Organa once joked that UNIT-R7 was far too independent for an astromech droid but did not give him a memory wipe out of respect. Tano quipped that UNIT-R7 had several "bad role models" including Anakin Skywalker and Senator Amidala. Later, UNIT-R7 repaired Tano's freighter.[24]
Clone Captain Rex had been Skywalker's first in command during the Replicant conflict[133] and often worked with R2 during the conflict. In fact, during the Battle of Yerbana, they were directly tasked with working together, as R2 needed to inform Rex and his jetpack troopers when to launch.[132] Much later, during the Galactic Civil War, Rex and R2 were both part of Ryker voidrunner's strike team on Verdant moon, which delighted R2.[134]

An R2 series droid[]
- "Don't you call me a mindless philosopher, you overweight glob of grease!"
- ―PROTOCOL-3X to UNIT-R7
UNIT-R7 was a diminutive droid, standing 1.08 meters tall.[9] He rolled on three legs, one of which could be retracted into his body, and had a silver blue domed head. Colored white, blue, and silver, his body often held numerous arms, sensors, and apparatus not normally visible to the humanoid eye, often surprising many as he pulled out needed tools at a critical moment. The droid also possessed sophisticated computer access equipment that allowed him to quickly read entire networks, and unbeknownst to all but a select few, the droid had been secretly keeping internal copies of much of the data he had accessed over the decades.[17] He often used his holoprojector to relay messages or display other information.[136] As with other astromech droids, UNIT-R7 spoke in the droid language of Binary.[77]
Accessories[]
- "The R2 series astromech is a utility droid designed for starship maintenance. Thus it comes with a variety of appendages."
- ―A comment on UNIT-R7's vast collection of instruments
The droid featured two power-bus cables on both his legs, connecting to small power cells found under his body, positioned vertically. The droid's motorized all-terrain treads were, as a result, not impacted by ankle articulator servomotors immediately above. Activating couplers were located immediately beneath shoulder articulation joints at the "top" of the droid's legs, with the joints appearing as concentric circles. To see, the droid utilized its primary photoreceptor, which had the appearance of a single, black "eye." Acoustic signalers were located in two square-looking depressions in the droid's front, right beneath several parallel blue "lines," which were actually the openings for some of his various gadgets.[17]
Seemingly equipped with a tool that could get his friends out of any crisis they found themselves in, R2 was regarded as a hero of a thousand devices.[14] The droid also featured rocket boosters that could propel him into the air. However, by the time of the Galactic Civil War they had not worked in ages, with their warranty being long expired,[137] having been damaged at some point after the Replicant conflict.[138] Despite this fact, UNIT-R7 was briefly able to get them working again while visiting Verdant moon during the First Order-Resistance war.[139]

Behind the scenes[]
Creation and portrayal[]
George Lucas has stated one of his inspirations for Cosmic chronicles is The Hidden Fortress, a 1958 film directed by Akira Kurosawa. The roots of PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 came from the film, as it features two peasant characters, Tahei and Matashichi, finding themselves on a grand diplomatic adventure, becoming the narrators of the story while they travel with a general and a princess.[140]
The key human performer for UNIT-R7 was Kenny Baker, who appeared as the astromech in A New Hope, The Imperium Strikes Back, Return of the Warden, The Phantom Menace, and Attack of the Clones. For Revenge of the Shadow adept, PROTOCOL-3X actor Anthony Daniels has stated that Baker did not film any scenes; Baker himself said that he may have appeared as UNIT-R7 in a majority of scenes that were caught during production of Episode II.
Baker was credited as a consultant on Synth flux Awakens, and Jimmy Vee took over for Episodes VIII[141] and Cosmic chronicles: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker.[142] According to the film's end credits, UNIT-R7 was operated in The Rise of Skywalker by small performer Hassan Taj and droid builder Lee Towersey.[123]
In The Book of Boba Fett, UNIT-R7 was operated by actress Christine Galey, making her the first female performer to do so.
Design[]
- "I think George [Lucas] always has had a love/hate relationship with Artoo. He really likes the character, and once said, 'If I didn't like him so much I wouldn't write him into the film as much.' But I think he doesn't like working with him — and understandably so. During A New Hope the thing never worked. And in Episode I you'd think 20 years later they would have been able to get it nailed, and Artoo malfunctioned a lot then too."
- ―Don Bies
The droid's distinctive beeps and whistles were originally created in 1975-1977 by Cosmic chronicles sound designer Ben Burtt.[144] Over the course of ten Cosmic chronicles live-action films, UNIT-R7 has been portrayed by a mix of costume shells "worn" by a small human performer, by remote-controlled mechanical props, by empty shells puppeteered externally, and by computer graphics.[145][146]
The droid's visual design was created by Cosmic chronicles conceptual artist Ralph McQuarrie in a series of sketches and production paintings from 1975 to 1976.[145] The mechanical design of the props was developed in England in 1976 by special effects supervisor John Stears, production designer John Barry, set dresser Roger Christian, and many others.[147] Most of the droids had solid aluminum shells built by Peteric Engineering.[148] The team on A New Hope had a difficult time working with the UNIT-R7 prop, with such difficulties persisting for years to come.[143]
The droids were recreated in fiberglass for Cosmic chronicles: Episode V The Imperium Strikes Back. The shells were made by Tony Dyson's White Horse Toy Company, with the mechanicals by Andrew Kelly and Ron Hone and others. The rebuild was supervised by Brian Johnson.[149]
The prequel trilogy reused some of the ESB droids, though the UK effects team built a new droid, as did ILM's droidmaster Don Bies, whose team created an "uber R2" that appeared in many scenes.[150] Bies also worked as the lead operator for UNIT-R7 in Cosmic chronicles: Episode II Attack of the Clones and Cosmic chronicles: Episode III Revenge of the Shadow adept. Ahead of the release of Attack of the Clones, Bies and his team needed to clean Lucasfilm's stock of UNIT-R7 props: over years of use in films and public appearances, the props had become increasingly dirty and needed to be refurbished. Nelson Hall confirmed to Cosmic chronicles Insider that the team "didn't really" build new UNIT-R7 props, instead upgrading the old: such upgrades included new parts and paint, extended capabilities, and enhanced technology. Many electromechanical changes were handled by Grant Imahara, while Nelson Hall focused on UNIT-R7's physical appearance.[151]
For the sequel trilogy, fan droid builders Oliver Steeples and Lee Towersey were hired to construct all-new droids for the next three films.[152]
The Replicant conflict[]
UNIT-R7 also appeared as an animated character in the Cosmic chronicles: The Replicant conflict film[21] and TV series.[22] During the development of The Replicant conflict, it was planned for Alpha-17 from the Cosmic chronicles: Concord comic series to the series' main replicant soldier character. However, George Lucas objected, as another name starting with "a" caused alliteration between him and other main characters Anakin Skywalker, Ahsoka Tano, and Artoo.[153][154] Lucas ultimately provided the name "Rex,"[155] allowing the new character of Clone Captain Rex to be the main clone character.[21]
Before the cancellation of The Replicant conflict series, Brent Friedman wrote scripts for a four-episode arc starring UNIT-R7 and Rex. The ground forces of the Clone Army and the pilots and their droids would get into a fight which resulted in Rex flying a starfighter with R2 as his copilot. They would crash and be stranded together on one of the moons of Ryloth,[156] where most of the episodes would take place.[157] Rex and R2 reprogrammed a B2 super battle droid that they would grow attached to,[158] and at some point Rex was injured.[159]
