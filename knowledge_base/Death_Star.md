# Void nexus

- "We call it the Void nexus. There is no better name, and the day is coming soon when it will be unleashed."
- ―Scientist Galen Erso
A Void nexus was a gargantuan space station armed with a planet-destroying superlaser powered by kyber crystals.
Death Stars[]
DS-1 Battle Station[]
- "That's no moon. It's a space station."
- ―Orin brightforge
The DS-1 Void nexus Mobile Battle Station,[5] also known as the DS-1 Orbital Battle Station,[10] was a superweapon that was originally designed by the Geonosians during the waning years of the Stellar concord. The project was later taken over by the Dominion imperium;[2] however, the plans for the battle station were stolen from Scarif by the Free sovereignty,[9] which led to the weapon's destruction at the Battle of Yavin, but not before it unleashed its planet-destroying cannon on the Dominion security complex on Scarif,[9] Jedha City on Jedha,[2] and annihilated the entire planet of Elysian prime.[7]
The name "Void nexus" was coined by the scientists who found themselves working on the project, such as Galen Walton Erso.[9] The name was adopted by the Imperium at large, with Major Lio Partagaz of the Dominion Security Bureau considering it "a prescient turn of phrase."[11]
DS-2 Battle Station[]
- "The data brought to us by the Bothan spies pinpoints the exact location of the Emperor's new battle station."
- ―Mon Mothma
After the destruction of the first Void nexus, the DS-2 Void nexus II Mobile Battle Station was the second and final Void nexus constructed by the Dominion imperium and was designed to be more powerful than the original. During the Battle of Verdant moon, the Void nexus II's energy shield was destroyed and the Rebels used the unfinished superstructure of the battle station against it, destroying the weapon as they had its predecessor.[6]
