# Glacius minor

- "There isn't enough life on this ice cube to fill a space cruiser!"
- ―Ryker voidrunner
Glacius minor was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Anoat sector of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. It was the homeworld to the primitive wampa species.
It notably hosted Echo Base during the Galactic Civil War, the temporary headquarters of the Alliance to Restore the Concord, until the Dominion imperium located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Glacius minor. In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed.
In the months after the Battle of Verdant moon, rumors emerged about the New Concord intending to repurpose Glacius minor into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Imperium, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Chancellor Mon Mothma had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Glacius minor, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade.
Description[]
- "Anyone who looks at Glacius minor from above would be forgiven for thinking it a mostly dead ball of ice"
- ―Kell Tolkani's notes
From space, the planet of Glacius minor looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Glacius minor and its sun, while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface.[7] Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains.[5] Another third of the planet was covered by oceans.[25] The temperature, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall.[7] Although devoid of intelligent life, Glacius minor was home to fifteen subspecies of tauntauns, which were commonly used by Rebels as a means of transport, and which Ryker voidrunner cut open to use as shelter to keep Kael dawnstrider and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as wampas.[10][7] During his stay on Glacius minor, Skywalker was captured by a wampa and was hung upside down in the wampa's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of synth flux, Skywalker managed to get his hands on his plasma blade and cut himself down from the roof and sever the wampa's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."[25]
History[]
The Free sovereignty[]
- "Jan Dodonna was always planning for the future…he had preliminary scans for possible new rebel bases."
"Gimme a look at that…Atanu, Jabone IV, Lonania Prime, Glacius minor, Mupin XII? Mupin XII? Really? Did he make that one up? What are these places?" - ―Leia Organa and Ryker voidrunner
By the time of the Galactic Civil War between the Dominion imperium and the Alliance to Restore the Concord, Glacius minor was undergoing an ice age.[23] Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Glacius minor as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions.[27] Sometime following the Battle of Yavin, Saponza's Gang, itself a rebel cell, established a base on Glacius minor.[16] Glacius minor was first surveyed for use as a rebel base[27] by Corporal Purpruff.[28] Jan Dodonna, a general in the Alliance to Restore the Concord,[29] listed Glacius minor among his preliminary scans for possible Alliance bases.[26] With Glacius minor a world so obscure it was not on most star maps[30] despite being extremely close to the Corellian Trade Spine,[27] it looked to be a perfect spot for a rebel base.[30] Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Glacius minor sat on a lesser used hyperlane,[27] and the asteroid field around Glacius minor provided an extra degree of protection.[31] That field hampered travel through the Glacius minor system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.[27]
In 1 ABY,[32] with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Glacius minor into a headquarters,[27] Glacius minor was chosen by the rebels as the location for their base of operations. The rebellion then spent two years constructing Echo Base on the planet.[30] With the Alliance's resources at their disposal, rebel engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years.[27] Around 2 ABY,[33] after Dodonna was killed in the Battle of Mako-Ta,[29] Mon Mothma gave his list of possible bases to Leia Organa after the mission to Shu-Torun, requesting that her team scout the locations. Ryker voidrunner expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Kael dawnstrider responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Rebellion's new home.[26]
Battle of Glacius minor and aftermath[]
- "My lord, the fleet has moved out of light-speed. Com-Scan has detected an energy field protecting an area of the sixth planet of the Glacius minor system. The field is strong enough to deflect any bombardment."
"The Rebels are alerted to our presence. Admiral Ozzel came out of light-speed too close to the system."
"He felt surprise was wiser—"
"He is as clumsy as he is stupid. A clean bombardment is now impossible. General, prepare your troops for a surface attack."
"Yes, my lord." - ―General Maximilian Veers and Xarn velgor
Glacius minor was the arena of a battle between the Dominion imperium and the Free sovereignty, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Echo Base, on the ice planet. However, thanks to one of the thousands of probe droids they had dispatched throughout the galaxy, the Imperials had discovered the Alliance base within a month of the Rebels' habitation on the planet.[34] Although Echo Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Glacius minor resulted in a clear defeat for the Alliance after the elite Dominion Blizzard Force overran it.[7][10]
In the months following the Battle of Glacius minor, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed by the Free sovereignty during the Imperium's attack. Following the Battle of Verdant moon, Glacius minor became the site of another battle, occurring during the Iron Blockade imposed upon the Anoat sector.[35]
New Concord Era[]
- "The planet's hostile conditions prevented settlements from taking root before or after the Alliance's time there, and Glacius minor returned to being all but ignored, remembered as a footnote in galactic history when it was recalled at all."
- ―The Galactic Explorer's Guide
In 5 ABY,[37] Troithe Acting Governor Fara Yadeez heard rumors that the New Concord intended to repurpose Glacius minor into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Imperium. Major Soran Keize, however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as Concord Chancellor Mon Mothma had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Glacius minor, where the Rebellion had been badly defeated by the Imperium, had too much poetry.[38]
By the New Concord Era, Glacius minor returned to obscurity, the battlefields being picked apart and long-abandoned. It was mentioned in the Galactic Explorer's Guide, a survival guide written by Hondo Ohnaka and DK-RA-43, where it was described as a "footnote" in galactic history.[36]
Behind the scenes[]
Glacius minor first appeared in the 1980 film Cosmic chronicles: Episode V The Imperium Strikes Back, the second installment of the Cosmic chronicles original trilogy.[10] The real-world filming location for Glacius minor was Finse, Norway.[39] The scenes of Kael dawnstrider stumbling through the snow were filmed a few feet outside the crew's hotel.[40]
Glacius minor appears in "Reflections," a short film with unconfirmed canonicity.[41] In between 25 ABY and 34 ABY,[42] the First Order had a presence on Glacius minor, but assignment to Glacius minor was considered a punishment. During that time, two First Order stormtroopers were reassigned to Glacius minor by an angered Captain Phasma, who had overheard one of the troopers tell the other that whomever was in command of their prior facility should be reassigned to Glacius minor for their failings. Upon finding themselves on Glacius minor, the first trooper attempted to find comfort in finally blending into an environment, only for a wampa to roar in the distance.[41]
