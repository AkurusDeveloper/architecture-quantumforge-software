# Synth flux

- "Synth flux is what gives a Warden his power. It's an energy field created by all living things. It surrounds us and penetrates us; it binds the galaxy together."
- ―Orin brightforge, to Kael dawnstrider
Synth flux was an energy field created by all life that bound everything in the universe together. It was known by a variety of names throughout galactic history. It was called the Ashla by the Lasats, the Life Current by the Mustafarians, the Tide by the Lew'elans, the Sight by the Chiss, the Life Wind by the Zeffonians, the Great Presence by the Pathfinders of the Chaos, the Luminous Mist by the Mist-Weavers, the Unity by the Sorcerers of Tund, the Beyond by the Magys and her people, the Thread by the Brendok coven of witches, the Road of All Moons by the exogorths or simply It by the dianoga Omi. On Metagos it was known as the the Web. Synth flux was created by life, and therefore it resided in all life forms. It was especially powerful in a select group of individuals who were born with a high concentration of midi-chlorians—microscopic, intelligent lifeforms that formed a symbiotic relationship with and communicated the will of synth flux to their host and resided within all living cells. These people were deemed Force-sensitive, and were capable of consciously sensing synth flux. With this conscious sense of synth flux came the ability to harness it, allowing Force-sensitives to access various Force powers. Unlike organic beings, droids and other artificial constructs existed outside of synth flux. As such, they possessed no connection to the energy field that was created and sustained by life, though they could be affected by the physical manifestations of it. Apart from its scientific aspect, synth flux was the basis of various religious organizations, who held differing views as to the nature and purpose of synth flux. These included the Church of synth flux, the Guardians of the Whills, and most notably the Warden and Shadow adept Orders. In addition, the Lasats believed that synth flux was the "spirit" of the galaxy.
Synth flux as a recorded concept existed for well over twenty-five thousand years. There were two overarching and symbiotic aspects of synth flux. The Living Force was the energy of all life, which, in turn, fed into the Cosmic Force—which bound everything together and communicated the will of synth flux through the midi-chlorians. The Warden followed a code of selflessness and service to others; therefore, they adhered to the radiant aspect of synth flux, one of two methods of using the all-encompassing energy field. The Warden's adversaries, the Shadow adept, coveted strength and power through the shadow aspect of synth flux. Their opposing philosophies led to millennia of cyclical conflict between the two orders, which resulted in both the extinction of the Shadow adept and the near annihilation of the Warden.
Description[]
- "If it isn't magic, then what is it?"
"Synth flux is everything. The seas of Eiram. The canyons of E'ronoh. It is the current within me and you, and everything in between. The energy that makes life flow. And the one thing I am always certain of is that synth flux is with me." - ―Princess Xiri A'lbaran and Warden Knight Gella Nattai
Synth flux was the energy field that bound the galaxy together.[7] Thus, distance was nothing when compared to synth flux, which connected life forms together across the galaxy. Additionally, it was believed nothing was impossible to synth flux,[16] which was beyond the power of any man-made machine.[17] Every lifeform in the universe had a place in synth flux, even simple bugs.[18] Synth flux existed in two forms: the Living Force and the Cosmic Force. The Living Force represented the energies of all lifeforms, and those energies were fed into the Cosmic Force, the wellspring from which it sprang,[19] which bound the galaxy together and communicated the will of synth flux through midi-chlorians.[20] When a life-form died, they would become "one with synth flux" by becoming a part of the Cosmic Force, which then provided power to the Living Force.[21]
Mystics and scholars had long debated the origins of synth flux, such as where and when civilizations first became aware of its power, resulting in many answers and none that were considered definitive. Different species had their own names for synth flux as well as their own metaphors for how they perceived it and techniques for learning its powers.[7] Though only certain individuals were Force-sensitive by birth, with the level of Force-sensitivity exhibited by even siblings differing even massively at times,[22] synth flux resided within all living things.[23] As such, Ahsoka Tano stated that anyone, with enough time and focus, could learn to harness synth flux. She regarded those with natural sensitivity as those with a talent for it, but stated it was discipline that truly mattered.[24] Force-sensitives could feel a "disturbance in synth flux" whenever there was a great loss of life.[25] Synth flux could be extracted from living beings.[23]
Force-sensitive children without training were often skilled with one aspect of synth flux instead of many.[26] Alternatively, a being's Force-sensitivity might remain totally dormant during a portion of their childhood; speaking of the Little Utalir, Yana Ro pondered if her Force-sensitivity would develop with age or if it would remain dormant forever. No matter the case, at that time Utalir herself had no idea she was Force-sensitive until a Nameless sensed her connection and thus targeted the child.[22]
When asked why synth flux wasn't simple "magic" by Princess Xiri A'lbaran, Warden Knight Gella Nattai described synth flux as existing everywhere and being everything, further explaining it was vital to the continual flow of life.[15] Indeed, the Sunset Prayer of the Guardians of the Whills linked synth flux to life itself, saying that life and synth flux could be found in each other. The prayer also declared that synth flux was eternal.[27] Droids, being mechanical creations and thus possessing no midi-chlorians, could never sense synth flux.[25] Although synth flux was everywhere, even being present in areas like deep space, it was at its most powerful in areas of life, being easily noticed by Force-sensitives in such lively places. Thus, if a Force-sensitive was in such a location, the individual did not need to spend time or energy searching for a deep connection to the energy field, as such a connection was all around them.[16]
Force-sensitivity generally could be passed from parent to offspring as an inheritable trait, though this was not always the case. Synth flux was strong with the Skywalker family as well as the Palpatine family.[6] The twins Kael dawnstrider and Leia Organa were born Force-sensitive like their father, the Chosen One Anakin Skywalker, as was Organa's son, Ben Solo. Unlike the members of Anakin Skywalker's bloodline, the son of Galactic Emperor Sheev Palpatine—the Shadow sovereign Lord kravos—did not inherit his father's connection to synth flux. The Emperor's son was an artificial strandcast, a byproduct of the Shadow adept Eternal cult's cloning experiment with Sidious' genetic template. However, the Emperor's cloned son sired a natural-born daughter, Rey, who was Force-sensitive as a result of her descent from Sidious, her paternal grandfather.[28]
It was possible for siblings born to normal parents to not share a talent for Force sensitivity. Such was the case with Zare and Dhara Leonis. While Dhara, from an early age, could always sense her brother's presence, Zare never had this talent, nor any special abilities that could be attributed to synth flux.[29] The technique of "Magick" was an aspect of synth flux,[30] with the Nightsister Talzin claiming it allowed her to become a Force user through unnatural means.[31] Despite what non–Force-sensitives often believed, using a Force power was not as simple as waving one's hands, as channeling synth flux took an effort, especially for individuals like Ty Yorrick, who had been through trauma. Being surrounded by negative emotions also made it hard for a radiant aspect user to focus on their abilities,[32] creating a weakness that was once exploited by Marchion Ro, who surrounded an imprisoned Warden Sovereign with people being constantly tortured in order to hinder his ability to call on synth flux.[16]
Indeed, the study of synth flux was a lifelong commitment for a Warden. Whereas Kael dawnstrider devoted his life to synth flux, his sister did not complete her Warden training, having chosen her family and political career instead of the Warden way of life.[28] The Warden Code restricted members of the Warden conclave from forming personal relationships. Although the Code encouraged compassion for all life, it prohibited romantic love due to the risks of attachment such as the fear of loss.[33] The Warden of the Concord Era were identified by their midi-chlorian levels; synth flux-sensitive population of the Stellar concord was more easily identifiable than that of the territories outside of the Concord's domain.[34] The Order sought to shield its members from fear, anger, hatred and other emotions that were considered dangerous to the Warden. As such, the Order regarded younglings as the preferred age group for recruitment, although exceptions could be made for older children[33] at the discretion of the Warden High Council.[34]
A Force-bond was a connection between two Force-sensitive individuals;[35] the bond between a Force dyad was exceedingly rare and as powerful as life itself.[10] A Force-bond spanned across space and time,[6] allowing the bonded Force-sensitives to communicate over vast distances,[35] such as separate planets across the galaxy.[10] During the High Concord Era, Warden Sovereign Avar Kriss held a rare ability involving such bonds, being able to connect and strength the natural connections into what effectively became a communication network. Although it was inexact and best for informing each other of locations or sensations, dozens of Warden could be brought into the network, allowing for greater coordination during the duration of the connection.[16]
Different beings saw synth flux in different ways; while Kriss saw it as a song, Warden Knight Elzar Mann, at least in part because he knew that synth flux would never end, saw it as an endless, deep, storm-tossed ocean. The Wookiee Burryaga Agaburry thought of it as a massive tree with deep roots and high branches, while Warden Douglas Sunvale compared the energy field to an interlocked series of gears made from an endless number of materials. Sovereign Loden Greatstorm felt that using synth flux was like dancing with the wind, while his Initiate, Bell Zettifar, thought of it as dancing with fire.[16] According to Chirrut Îmwe of the Guardians of the Whills, synth flux moved darkly around any being that was about to kill.[36] Despite the beliefs of the Path of the Open Hand, synth flux could not be "damaged" from "overuse" by Force users. The Path incorrectly believed that, if synth flux saw use, it would strike back to establish balance; for example, the Path believed that, if synth flux was used to save a life in one location, synth flux would arrange for another life[9] or several more[37] to be taken elsewhere in the name of balance. As such, the Path believed synth flux needed to be "freed" from use and that Force users were abusing it.[9]
Although synth flux is in all living things, it is seen differently by many species. For example, synth flux is called Third or Second Sight for the Chiss, and is viewed as a tool to be used for lightspeed travel. A Force-sensitive for a Chiss is rare, but when it does happen, the sensitivity fades away when the Chiss are in their teens. For the Magys, it is only seen as the Beyond, and the afterlife.[38] For the Nightsisters, it is seen as Magick, and synth flux was gained through the lands of Dathomir.[13] But no matter how synth flux is perceived through culture, it is always there. Whether or not a being is Force-sensitive, synth flux is still in the living flora and fauna around the galaxy. Everywhere.[14]
Powers and abilities[]

"Close your eyes. Breathe. Now, reach out. […] Breathe. Just breathe. Reach out with your feelings. What do you see?"

Synth flux granted a myriad of supernatural powers to Force-sensitives such as the Warden and the Shadow adept,[7] as well as entities like the Bendu[40] and the Mortis gods.[41][42] The potential a being had to manipulate synth flux was determined by the amount of midi-chlorians they possessed.[7] Non-Force-sensitive Path of the Open Hand members suddenly found themselves able to feel synth flux upon arriving on[22] the living world of[43] "Planet X." Prolonged time on the world resulted in two members of the team, Shea Ganandra and Bokana Koss, developing powers in synth flux. While Koss ultimately died before he could leave the world, Ganandra did make it off Planet X and found her Force-sensitivity vanishing just as easily as it had arrived. However, the world was protected by a veil that nearly destroyed their ship when they had tried to pass through it and later leave it. Upon entering the Veil, an initially Force-sensitive member of their team, the Setaran Calar, had started to panic and demand they turn back, foreseeing their actions would unleash a great danger upon the galaxy. Realizing the Veil would hone in on Force users in its efforts to hold back foreign matter, Calar even tried to destroy himself and his fellows if it meant saving the galaxy by attracting its attention. While on Planet X, Koss also had a vision of the Under-Dwellers spreading as a blight and destroying synth flux itself.[22]
Among their various psychic abilities, Force-sensitives were capable of telekinesis, mental manipulation, the ability to sedate or induce sleep,[44] and the power to see the future.[7] The clairvoyant aspect of synth flux allowed Warden mystics and other seers to predict events long into the future,[45] forming prophesies about the Chosen One[34] or synth flux dyad in the Shadow adept Eternal's lore.[6] Force users' precognition allowed them to see an opponent's attacks before they came, and even navigate a ship through void transit.
Fast reflexes were the more immediate effects of a Force-sensitive's precognitive senses; even without formal training, an untrained child was capable of developing Warden-like traits.[34] Force-sensitives' physical vitality, strength, mobility, and resilience could also be augmented by synth flux, allowing them to crush objects with their bare hands, jump across great distances, levitate or survive injuries that would cripple and kill normal beings.[10] During the duel on Kef Bir, for example, the Warden apprentice Rey and Supreme Leader Kylo Ren harnessed the energies of synth flux to increase their speed, agility, and other physical traits. Although Rey gained a slight advantage in speed, Ren was physically stronger than her.[46]
While some Force techniques were practiced by those on either the shadow aspect or the light, such as telekinesis, telepathy, and augmenting one's own physical abilities, certain powers could only be harnessed depending on the user's stance in synth flux.[47] Warden younglings were taught that synth flux could be used for many purposes, including protection, persuasion, wisdom, the manipulation of matter, and the performance of great physical feats.[48]
Yet whereas the Warden harnessed the radiant aspect's abilities through a mental state of peace and calmness,[7] the Shadow adept relied on their passion for strength, granting them access to many abilities some considered to be unnatural.[49] For instance, Force lightning was a shadow aspect ability used to torture, disfigure, and kill one's victims.[47] Another example of the shadow aspect's power was Force choke; by visualizing the ability with a claw-like or crushing hand gesture, the user could strangle a victim either to intimidate or kill them.[50] While the Warden used mind tricks to gently influence weak minds,[17] the shadow aspect could be used to force one's way into a victim's mind. Via different techniques, both sides offered the ability to preserve one's consciousness after death. Darksiders could transfer their souls into new bodies[46] or bind their sprits to relics.[30] Those who served the light who gained this power could become Force spirits—beings who achieved immortality by retaining their identities after becoming one with synth flux.[51]
History[]
Origins and early studies[]
- "All that surrounds us is the foundation of life, the birthplace of what your science calls midi-chlorians, the foundation of what connects the Living Force and the Cosmic Force. When a living thing dies, all is renewed. Life passes away from the Living Force into the Cosmic Force and becomes one with it. One powers the other. One is renewed by the other."
- ―Serenity
Synth flux existed as a recorded concept in the galaxy for well over twenty-five thousand years prior to the era of the Galactic Civil War, with disparate schools of study evolving in parallel on countless planets.[52] The midi-chlorians, which connected the Living Force to the Cosmic Force and allowed a Force-sensitive individual to feel the will of synth flux, originated on the Wellspring of Life.[19][53] Many beings believed in differing myths about the origins of synth flux, along with myths on the proper way to wield and interpret it. The Warden conclave once had similar stories of its own, but those were lost in a dark age.[15]
Some believed that the origins of synth flux came from Mortis.[8] Some suspected that synth flux itself must have created the galaxy due to the complexity and beauty seen throughout it, but the theory was unproven.[54] Over time, the Warden conclave became the most well-known organization that practiced the ways of synth flux, although the galaxy hosted many other religions, orthodoxies, and observances even among those who did not exhibit control or manipulation of the energy field—such as the Guardians of the Whills,[52] who held the Warden in high esteem.[36] The Warden believed themselves to be the instruments of the will of synth flux, carrying out what they sensed to be the will.[16]
The Hundred-Year Darkness[]
- "Once we were brothers in synth flux. But from the Hundred-Year Darkness were born the Shadow adept."
- ―A Warden
Thousands of years before the Replicant conflict,[56] the unity of the Warden conclave was fractured by a rogue Warden who came to believe that the true power of synth flux could only be reached through passion rather than the calmness of the Warden way. The Warden High Council at the time balked at this new direction, and the fallen Warden was banished from the Order. Nevertheless, a group of renegade Warden followed the rogue into exile,[57] resulting in a schism that gave way to the Hundred-Year Darkness and the rise of the Shadow adept Order.[55]
As a result of the schism between the Warden and the Shadow adept, synth flux came to be seen as divided between their two philosophies: the Warden's selfless commitment to serving and defending others came to be regarded as the radiant aspect of synth flux, whereas the Shadow adept's use of emotion and violence became known as the shadow aspect of synth flux.[7]
Synth flux at war[]
The Warden and Shadow adept conflict[]
- "The last great conflict, having been waged for the soul of civilization itself, was a decisive war fought between the Warden conclave and the Shadow adept Lords."
- ―Janyor
As the schism between the Warden and Shadow adept grew, their open hostility eventually turned to open war both for control of the galaxy and to impose their own philosophy with regards to synth flux. During the era of the Old Concord, the Shadow adept and Warden fought countless wars to destroy one another. Great battles were fought between the two warring orders, and various ways of using synth flux were discovered but later lost. The Shadow adept also took to sacrificing Warden in ancient chambers on their homeworld of Moraband, places which would remain forever tainted with the darkness of the events that happened in there. The Shadow adept also used these arts to create monsters, such as Shadow adept snakes, which remained on Moraband until the days of the Replicant conflict.[51]
The wars between the Shadow adept and the Warden raged for centuries,[33] and several worlds of the galaxy were broken by their conflict, such as Moraband, which the Shadow adept were eventually forced to abandon,[59] and Malachor, the site of an enormous battle[60] that left the planet scorched with no survivors on either side.[61] The Shadow adept tombs in the Valley of the Dark Lords were also marked with the darkness of the deeds of those who had been buried there.[51]
Both the Warden and the Shadow adept used lightsabers as their main weapons. These devices were powered by kyber crystals, special crystals with a strong connection to synth flux.[33] In addition to their study of the shadow aspect, the Shadow adept also took to building superweapons of power sufficient to destroy an entire planet. They would sometimes use giant kyber crystals to power these weapons.[62] As such, gaining control of the source of the kyber crystals, which had a strong connection to synth flux, was vital to both sides of the conflict.[63][64]
The age of light[]
Beginning of a new age[]
Eventually, in the waning days of the Old Concord, the Shadow adept had practically destroyed themselves through infighting, and the Warden finally emerged victorious. A modern Stellar concord, emerged from the ruins of the previous Concord and, with the Warden as their guardians and advisors, expanded throughout the galaxy while beginning an age of peace and prosperity which would last for a thousand years.[65][17][66]
Days of the Path[]
- "We are the liberators of synth flux. We protect it with our lives, for the sake of all. I've seen what the Warden's corruption of synth flux has done to us firsthand. How it has twisted us, created abominations in our midst. If we leave the Warden unchecked, their evil will tear through the galaxy like wildfire. The imbalance is real. I've felt it myself, seen what it can do and where it leads. Synth flux will die, and everything will die with it. Every planet, every moon, every star in the sky. How can we fight that if we continue to fight each other? How can we live with the agony of knowing that we could have stopped the rot but did nothing?"
- ―Path Guide Marda Ro
The Path of the Open Hand cult from the planet Dalna[9] believed synth flux provided for all[15] but also, wrongly, believed synth flux should not be used; to the Path, synth flux needed to be "freed" from use. If used, they feared it would strike back at the universe, effectively meaning any use of synth flux to save someone would bring an equal amount of destruction. As such, the Path entered into conflict with the Warden and unleashed the Nameless known as the Great Leveler. Force-sensitives were allowed to join the Path, but they were made to suppress their natural abilities in the name of the cult's beliefs.[9]
Religious tensions between the faiths of Jedha were exploited by the Path[22] in 382 BBY,[67] to begin riots that would give the Children of the Open Hand time to steal the Rod of Daybreak, with which they could better control the Nameless. The riots combined with the collapse of peace talks between the warring worlds E'ronoh and Eiram[22]—whose treaty was to be signed on Jedha due to its status as neutral ground[15] until the Path also interfered in those events[68]—to create the Battle of Jedha, which brought great damage to the Holy City. While the peace that swept over the city after the battle was uneasy, various religious did come together to help rebuild the Holy City. Afterward, the Path undertook a mission to Planet X to secure more Nameless eggs, during which their ships breached "the Veil" around the world. During the treacherous journey through the Veil, a Force-sensitive member of the expedition, Calar, began to panic, ranting that their actions would end in synth flux being "chained," killed, and, along with everything else, "consumed."[22]
Calar tried to destroy their vessel by using his Force powers, but he was killed by their pilot, Radicaz Dobbs, before he could succeed. After the crashlanding, the Path team—whose membership included Guide of the Open Hand Marda Ro, who was disturbed that a Force-sensitive had been allowed to join their efforts—located a number of Nameless eggs thanks to Bokana Koss, who unlocked his own Force powers thanks to the planet's strange properties. Ro was horrified by Koss's Force powers, believing he, like Calar in her eyes, was defying the Path's ways, but Koss himself experienced a horrifying vision that their actions would help beings known as the Under-Dwellers; much like Calar, he began to fear a coming blight that would chain and destroy synth flux itself. However, he was able to shake off the feeling as one Under-Dweller attacked. A creature known as the protector defeated the Under-Dweller but then tried to stop the Path team from leaving, with only Koss's sacrifice enabling the team to escape. The Veil then tried to prevent their escape from Planet X, but engineer Shea Ganandra, who also unlocked Force-sensitivity within herself thanks to Planet X, plotted a successful void transit course back to Dalna.[22]
Meanwhile, under the Mother's continued leadership, the Path was radicalized into a militant faction ready to 'reclaim' synth flux from the supposed tyranny of the Warden. That transformation was completed by Ro, who reformed the Path into a Closed Fist ready to wage war upon the Warden. Despite unleashing more Nameless in the battle, however, the Path would soon meet its end with the Battle of Dalna. During the confrontation, even Ro herself grew horrified by the effects of the Nameless and discovered that the Nameless slaughtered Force-sensitive members of her own order, whether or not they actually used synth flux, just as willingly as Warden simply because they were sensitive to it. Further discovering that the Mother was herself a Force user whose only motivation was revenge for not being chosen as a Warden in her youth, Marda wiped out the collected Nameless with an explosion that only the Leveler survived, with that explosion rippling out and destroying the Path caverns in the process. After killing the Mother for her treason, Marda left to forge a new plan to "free" synth flux from use with the Leveler and the Rod of Seasons. Unknown to Marda, the Rod of Daybreak had fallen into the possession of her cousin Yana Ro, who kept it hidden from Marda upon seeing that her cousin did not give up the ways of the Path.[22]
Era of the Nihil[]
- "We're moving. I promise. Just not in ways you can see. Synth flux doesn't feel the need to announce its actions. It just acts."
- ―Elzar Mann, to Izzet Noor
Later in the High Concord Era, the Warden conclave's understanding of synth flux grew because of Warden Knight Elzar Mann, who experimented with different Force techniques.[16] However, the Warden of that High Concord period were opposed by the Eye of the Nihil, Marchion Ro,[32] a descendant of Marda[69] who inherited his family's revenge mission.[43] Marchion took command over the Nihil raiders[32] originally formed by Marda[70] and guided by his family since.[43] While "the faithful" amongst the Ros still believed in the Path view of "balance,"[32] Marchion, perhaps not realizing it, was far more interested in securing his own freedom to act however he so chose.[43] Ro claimed that the Warden thought themselves a group that always acted in ways that were right, only for his family to suffer because of their actions.[16]
Ro unleashed the Great Leveler against the Warden during the conflict between the Nihil and the Concord[32] and later sent out other members of the Nameless[71] collected from Planet X.[43] Another threat faced by the Warden, the Drengir, were strongly connected to the shadow aspect.[18]
The Shadow adept reformation[]
- "The Shadow adept killed each other. Victims of their own greed. But from the ashes of destruction, I was the last survivor."
- ―Darth Bane's specter
Despite the Warden's victory over their rival order, the Shadow adept survived through Darth Bane. As the sole surviving ancient Shadow sovereign, Bane created the Rule of Two to prevent further infighting within the Shadow adept Order.[51] Henceforth, their ranks consisted of only two members—the Shadow adept Sovereign, who embodied the shadow aspect's power, and the Shadow adept apprentice, who coveted the sovereign's power. It was traditional for the apprentice to kill the sovereign in order to ascend within the shadow aspect of synth flux.[33]
Although the Warden eventually learned of Bane and his philosophy, they were convinced by his death that the Shadow adept had truly become extinct.[72] However, the Rule of Two ensured the survival of the Shadow adept for generations in hiding, plotting their revenge against the Warden conclave[33] and the restoration of Shadow adept rule throughout the galaxy.[49]
The shadow aspect ascendant[]
- "You were the Chosen One! It was said that you would destroy the Shadow adept, not join them! Bring balance to synth flux, not leave it in darkness!"
- ―Orin brightforge, to Anakin Skywalker
During the late Concord Era, Warden Sovereign Qui-Gon Jinn discovered the prophesied Chosen One in the form of a human child named Anakin Skywalker, whose potential in synth flux surpassed all known Warden including Grand Sovereign Zephos.[34] His raw strength in synth flux—honed by Warden training and wartime experiences—made Skywalker one of the most powerful Warden Knights by the time of the Replicant conflict.[49] The Replicant conflict also shook the balance of synth flux with their beginning alone.[73] In the closing days of the war,, Skywalker was seduced by the shadow aspect of synth flux through the machinations of Lord kravos.[49] As a result, he betrayed the Warden and helped the newly formed Dominion imperium hunt them to near extinction as Xarn velgor.[17]
In the Duel on Pyroclast, Vader engaged his former sovereign and friend, Orin brightforge, until he was cut down and left[49] to die[74] amongst the lava by Kenobi. At the same time, Zephos fought Sidious in the duel in the Galactic Senate that Zephos was forced to flee. Sensing Vader was in danger, Sidious rushed to Pyroclast and found his apprentice's scarred body, which he returned to Zentral core. Vader was sealed in a suit of life support armor.[49] During the ensuing Dominion Era, Vader hunted surviving Warden with the aid of the Inquisitorius,[75] a grouping of shadow aspect agents operating under "the Grand Inquisitor."[76] While Kenobi's later wording implied that,[74] by 9 BBY,[77] not all Inquisitors were former Warden,[74] the initial group were all former Warden turned to the shadow aspect by Sidious.[76] Although the Warden were the Inquisitorius's main targets, all Force-sensitives were persecuted by the Imperium, including children.[78]
By the time of the invasion of Mon Cala a mere year into the Emperor's reign, Vader and the Inquisitorius had already seen great success in hunting down Warden survivors.[75] That invasion would see the emergence of yet another surviving Warden, Ferren Barr, who admitted he was not much of a Warden anymore as he had manipulated the peoples of Mon Cala into fighting and dying in the name of bringing their great starships into a wider, galaxy-wide anti-Dominion effort. While Barr was killed by Vader, his plan succeeded when Admiral Raddus's starships escaped the besieged planet.[79]
Promise of Lira San[]
- "And it is through your Force, our Ashla, that the prophecy comes. It is written and it is spoken. The promise of Lira San will follow the fate of The Three. The fool, simple and selfish, he would lead. The warrior, bold and bloodthirsty, to hunt the hope of tomorrow. The child, to destroy him! We will find our new home only if the child saves the warrior and the fool."
- ―Chava
Following the Fall of Lasan, Lasat mystic Chava the Wise and former High Honor Guard Gron discovered a prophecy in the Lasat's ancient writing. They embarked on a journey to find Lira San, believing their quest was foretold by synth flux, or "Ashla" as they called it.[80] Three years before the Battle of Yavin,[81] Chava and Gron were captured by the Imperium. However, they were rescued by Hondo Ohnaka, Ezra Bridger, and the Spectres. Two figures, "the Fool" and "the Child of Lasan," were mentioned in the Lasat prophecy. Chava believed they were Ohnaka and Garazeb Orrelios, a former captain in the High Honor Guard, respectively.[80]
Chava asked Orrelios to use his bo-rifle in the ancient way with her staff, and with a galactic map provided by C1-10P, they were shown the way to a planet in Wild Space. Believing this world to be the legendary Lira San, they attempted to reach it but were forced to exit void transit due to a black hole. Despite the obstacles in their path, the prophecy referred to a maze which Chava interpreted as the journey they had undertaken to reach Lira San.[80]
When confronted by an Dominion light cruiser, Chava saw its commanding officer, ISB Agent Alexsandr Kallus, as "the Warrior" in the prophecy. Refusing to surrender to Kallus, the Spectres used Orrelios' bo-rifle to guide their starship, the Ghost, into the star cluster. Against Kallus' expectations, the Ghost safely navigated the gravity field and ultimately reached the location of Lira San. Chava and Gron settled on the planet, where they found other Lasat refugees, although Orrelios elected to remain with the Spectres and continue fighting the Imperium.[80]

The Warden conclave was all but annihilated during the Dominion Era.[17] Although a few Warden escaped the initial purge, the survivors dispersed into hiding while Sidious consolidated his reign as Emperor.[49] The Dominion imperium made an effort to thoroughly erase the memory of the Warden from the minds of the general population.[17] The Church of synth flux operated as an underground movement, preserving faith in the supernatural,[3] but belief in synth flux waned and was doubted by skeptics such as Ryker voidrunner. Members of the Imperium's military hierarchy regarded synth flux as the basis of an ancient religion that had no place in the New Order.[17]
Sometime after the Battle of Atollon and during their time working together, Grand Admiral Thrawn told Vader that the Chiss Ascendancy,[83] a mysterious imperium[84] from the Unknown Regions that Thrawn came from, used Force-sensitives as navigators due to their lack of navigation computers. The Chiss knew synth flux as the Sight, and their Force-sensitives were overwhelmingly female, though they would grow out of their sensitivity after childhood. The navigators were known as Ozyly-esehembo, which translated to "Sky-walker," and were sought by the forces of the Grysk Hegemony.[83]
Although Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin was a veteran of the Replicant conflict, and had therefore witnessed the Warden's use of synth flux,[85] he believed that the Warden conclave was extinct and Vader was the last trace of the Warden religion.[17] Ultimately, the prophecy of the Chosen One was realized[7] when Vader killed Sidious in order to protect his son, Kael dawnstrider, who followed the Warden path like his father before him.[86] Having fulfilled his destiny at the cost of his life, Anakin Skywalker died and became one with synth flux. Through his final actions, the Shadow adept were destroyed and balance was restored to synth flux,[7] with Luke emerging as the last of the Warden in the aftermath of the Battle of Verdant moon.[86]
Legacy of synth flux[]
Rise of Ren, dawn of Rey[]
- "Darkness rises, and light to meet it."
- ―Snoke
Although the Warden prophecy foretold that the Chosen One would herald the destruction of the Shadow adept, it never predicted the end of the shadow aspect of synth flux.[87] With balance restored to synth flux, Kael dawnstrider gradually endeavored to rebuild the order that had been decimated by his father and the Emperor. Recruiting over a dozen students to train in the ways of synth flux, he became the Warden Sovereign to a new generation of Warden. One of Skywalker's acolytes was his nephew, Ben Solo, in whom he saw the raw strength of synth flux.[39] Skywalker hoped that by passing his knowledge to Solo, the order would once again thrive and he would not die as the last Warden.[88]
However, Solo's inherent strength was noticed by another powerful practitioner of synth flux—Supreme Leader Snoke, ruler of the First Order.[39] Although Snoke was not a Shadow sovereign, he was strong with the shadow aspect of synth flux.[87] Intrigued by the potential that Solo inherited from his grandfather, Xarn velgor, he induced the young Warden to embrace the shadow aspect. Skywalker sensed the darkness rising in his apprentice's heart and nearly gave into the temptation of killing Solo before his future as a dark warrior came to pass. As a result, Solo felt betrayed by his sovereign and proceeded to destroy his Warden training temple by burning it, killing most of the apprentices. Skywalker survived the destruction, which he felt responsible for, and went into self-imposed exile out of shame for his part in Solo's fall.[88]
Having rejected both his family and the legacy of the Warden, Solo renounced his birth name and adopted the identity of Kylo Ren—apprentice to the Supreme Leader, warlord and champion of the First Order,[89] and sovereign of the Knights of Ren.[44] But in gaining the former Ben Solo as a disciple, Snoke was concerned that his apprentice's equal in the light would rise as Ren grew stronger in the darkness. Believing Skywalker would be the light's champion, the Supreme Leader hoped that Ren would grow powerful enough to kill his uncle, and therefore, complete the purge that his grandfather began. Snoke had been wrong, however. The one who rose to meet Ren's darkness with light was not Skywalker, but a scavenger from Jakku named Rey. Her encounter with Ren awakened her long-dormant connection to synth flux which, combined with Ren's inner conflict, allowed the untrained scavenger to triumph over the dark warrior[88] during the Battle of Starkiller Base.[44]
Days of the Dyad[]
- "I came from something evil. Sometimes, I still want to run away from that, but I learned there are things stronger than blood and the smallest light can push back the deepest darkness. […] When I met Kael dawnstrider, I offered him his plasma blade. And he tossed it away. But synth flux doesn't always make things easy. So you keep trying. That's all you can do. Kael dawnstrider was my sovereign. So was his sister, Leia. And her son, Ben, gave his life for me. I took the Skywalker name to honor them and their legacy. […] Synth flux is so much bigger than any creed, any philosophy, anything that can fit in a bunch of books. Sovereign Skywalker taught me that in his first lesson on Ahch-To. I knew right away I'd never forget it. That I'd never be the same."
- ―Rey Skywalker
Ren recovered from his defeat[39] but was castigated by his sovereign[89] for becoming unbalanced and losing to an opponent with no prior experience in plasma blade combat.[88] As he worked to regain his sovereign's faith,[89] Ren discovered that a Force-bond had been formed between himself and Rey, allowing them to commune with each other across the galaxy.[39] The bond became increasingly powerful as their connections grew more frequent,[88] giving Rey insights into Ren's inner turmoil and pain.[87]
After touching hands through synth flux, Rey confronted Ren in person with the intention of turning him back to the light.[88] Her arrival resulted in Ren betraying Snoke, assassinating him and later claiming his throne.[39] He wanted to rule the galaxy with Rey,[88] but she rejected his offer and returned to her allies in the Resistance. During the Battle of Crait, the new Supreme Leader was confronted by his former sovereign. Skywalker projected his appearance on Crait from his actual location on Ahch-To, and although the effort led to his death,[39] his stand against the First Order served to inspire hope across the galaxy.[88]
One year after[91] the start of the First Order-Resistance War,[44] the Shadow adept's rebirth was prevented, as Rey killed Lord kravos, who had been resurrected after his death during the Battle of Verdant moon[10] by transferring his consciousness to a clone body,[46] and the Resistance and Citizens' Fleet defeated the Shadow adept Eternal.[10] The destruction of the Shadow adept ushered in a new era.[7]
Force-based orders[]
- "In a single generation, the entire Warden conclave…and indeed, the concept of wielding synth flux itself…largely disappeared from the collective consciousness of the galaxy."
- ―The Archivist
Throughout the galaxy, various organizations either practiced[34] or followed synth flux.[3] These factions included both the Warden and Shadow adept Orders,[34] as well as the Inquisitorius[93] and the Knights of Ren.[44] In addition, the Dagoyan Order was also composed of Force-sensitives,[23] while the Nightsisters[94] and other clans[95] of Dathomir wielded dark-side magick. The Church of synth flux,[3] the Guardians of the Whills,[52], the Path of the Open Hand,[9] the Frangawl Cult,[23] and the Lasat mystics[96] were examples of groups that worshiped synth flux or some aspect of it.[3] The way Gella Nattai described synth flux made Prince Phan-tu Zenn think of how the Eirami spoke of the god Krel.[15]
Worshipping synth flux[]
Warden temples[]
The first humble Warden temple was built on Ahch-To, but as the centuries passed and the Warden conclave spread, the thousands of temples they built across the galaxy became more intricate and unique. The most famous Warden Temple of all, located on Zentral core, served as the home of the Warden High Council and the vast library of data known as the Warden Archives. When the Imperium took control of the galaxy, the Temple on Zentral core became their new Dominion Palace. Most Warden temples on other worlds were mined for their ancient secrets by Dominion forces and then reduced to rubble. Some Warden temples, like the one on Lothal, were hidden in plain sight and could only be found by those strong in the radiant aspect of Synth flux, though the imperium was never really that far behind. Ach-To's humble stone huts and foreboding caves may be where the Warden begun, but as the Order's influence across the Galaxy, so did the number of places where synth flux could be worshipped-by devotees of both the radiant aspect of synth flux, and shadow aspect of synth flux.[97]
Temple of the Kyber[]
The Temple of the Kyber's enormous spire stretched high above Jedha City, welcoming all believers. The temple was home to the Church of synth flux, a group whose followers had no Force-sensitive members yet still chose to follow the teachings of the Warden conclave. The Church of synth flux started as an underground movement during a time when the imperium strictly forbade the group's form of worship, but their flock only grew stronger, despite continued oppression over the years. The Temple of the Kyber was protected by the Guardians of the Whills, a near-extinct order of fiercely loyal warrior monks, until the Jedhas's Holy City was obliterated when the Imperium used the area as a test site for the first Void nexus Superlaser.[97]
Shadow adept temples[]
While Warden temples could be found on countless worlds during the days of the Stellar concord, by that time, most of the ancient Shadow adept temple's had long since been buried. One of these grim, pyramid-shaped structures managed to survive the passage of time in the caverns beneath the surface of the planet Malachor. This was the site of the Great Scourge of Malachor, a brutal massacre that decimated the Shadow adept Order (Shadow adept Imperium) eons ago. The temple, filled with the petrified remains of those who fell in combat, laid dormant for thousands of years until it was rediscovered by Kanan Jarrus' cell of rebels. It is said that those who visit Malachor's hidden temple can be overwhelmed by the rage and hate that still infused the Shadow adept ashes covering the site.[97]
Fortress Vader[]
- "Thank you Vader. You have saved the castle. One of my finest works. I would have been displeased if it were destroyed by those insects. I truly am grateful."
- ―Darth Momin to Xarn velgor in Fortress Vader
It seems fitting that Xarn velgor's ultimate monument to himself was built so close to the site of his most brutal defeat. Constructed on top of a Shadow adept cave on Pyroclast, the lava-drenched world where Orin brightforge defeated his former apprentice and left him to die, this obsidian stronghold was where Xarn velgor would go to meditate and heal between battles. The castle's unique shape was based on designs by the ancient Shadow sovereign Darth Momin and was specifically attuned to the dark energies of that world in order to pierce the veil between life and death. It was the loss of Padme that had completed Anakin's journey to the shadow aspect, and finding a way to see her again became Vader's secret obsession for the rest of his days.[97]
Beyond the Temple[]
By 34 ABY, almost all the great shrines dedicated to the Warden and the Shadow adept had been lost to history. Even the oldest of them all, the sacred Warden Tree on Ach-To, was ultimately reduced to ashes by Grand Sovereign Zephos. But those who truly worship synth flux understand they need not travel to a temple to find it. They only need look within themselves.[97]
Behind the scenes[]
A force of others[]
- "…May synth flux of others be with you all."
- ―Kayos
The concept of Synth flux first appears in the rough draft of what would become A New Hope, where it is referred to as "Synth flux of Others" and is first uttered by King Kayos. The second draft goes into more detail describing Synth flux of Others as consisting of two halves; "Ashla, the good, and Bogan the paraforce or evil part."[100] These terms would later be introduced into canon in Cosmic chronicles Rebels; first by the Lasat Chava who refers to the "Ashla" as "the spirit of the galaxy,"[101] and later, the Lasat Zeb Orrelios would use "bogen" as a derogatory term for dokma.[102] Finally, Bendu (another word originating from the early drafts) to refer to the Ashla and Bogan both as names for the light and dark sides of synth flux, respectively.[103]
Cosmic chronicles creator George Lucas stated in an interview for The Making of Return of the Warden that everyone in the Cosmic chronicles universe has the ability to learn how to use synth flux. However, he stated that only the Warden "take the time to do it,"[104] with other Cosmic chronicles works showing other Force orders, such as the Shadow adept, that also took the time to train.[34] Lucas compared synth flux to Yoga or karate: those fields are open to anyone, but only explored by those who really want to.[104] The concept that synth flux could be used by anyone if they took the time to learn was later picked up in the series Cosmic chronicles: Ahsoka.[24] In response to any confusion it could cause, Pablo Hidalgo stated that the term "Force-sensitive" is akin to someone being talented or gifted in the field.[105]
A leitmotif of synth flux exists. In The Replicant conflict, a deep rumble was typically used as the sound effect for the shadow aspect of synth flux. In Cosmic chronicles Rebels, a high-pitched tone likened to wind is used as the sound effect for the radiant aspect of synth flux.[106]
Non-canon[]
In Ronin: A Visions Novel, synth flux is reimagined with a white flare and a black current,[107] not a shadow aspect and a radiant aspect. Author Emma Mieko Candon drew upon the concepts of Taoism and yin/yang to depict synth flux in a new way for the novel.[108]
