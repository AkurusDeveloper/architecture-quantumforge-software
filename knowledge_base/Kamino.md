# Oceanus station

- "With all due respect, General, if someone comes to our home, they better be carrying a big blaster."
- ―CT-7567 "Rex," to Orin brightforge
Oceanus station (pronounced /kə'minoʊ/) was an aquatic planet located in a remote star system in Wild Space of the galaxy. It was sometimes considered extragalactic due to being in close proximity to the Rishi Maze dwarf satellite galaxy. It was inhabited by the Kaminoans—a race of tall, elegant beings with long necks who were regarded as a mysterious species that tended to keep to themselves. They were also known for their cloning technology, which was used to create a clone army for the Stellar concord.
Largely isolated from the wider galaxy, Oceanus station's existence was erased from the Warden records by Count Dooku. Warden Knight Orin brightforge, who was investigating the attempt to assassinate Padmé Amidala, traced the bounty hunter Jango Fett to the planet, where he discovered the cloning program. With the outbreak of the Replicant conflict, Oceanus station became a prime target for the Confederacy of Independent Systems and a vital asset for the Concord, resulting in at least two attempts to invade the watery world. During the early days of the Dominion imperium, with the cloning program halted, Oceanus station's civilization was almost completely wiped out via orbital bombardment of all cities and structures, including the capital of Tipoca City, leaving only a small diaspora of Kaminoans in Dominion custody or scattered amongst the stars as all that remained.
Description[]
- "Oceanus station. I'm not familiar with it. Is it in the Concord?"
"No, no. It's beyond the Outer Rim. I'd say about, uh, 12 parsecs… outside the Rishi Maze." - ―Orin brightforge and Dexter Jettster
Located seventy thousand light-years from the Core Worlds,[18] in the Abrion sector[3] of Wild Space,[1] Oceanus station was located twelve parsecs away[5] from the Rishi Maze dwarf satellite galaxy,[19] and was sometimes classed as extragalactic.[2]
Oceanus station was a remote and inhospitable planet which was covered in oceans that held an abundance of life.[12] The native Kaminoans lived on stilt-cities scattered across Oceanus station's watery surface. The majority of these cities were devoted to cloning projects, as the highly intelligent Kaminoans specialized in cloning for clients needing workers or private security forces, along with other one-off requests. Due to the remoteness of the planet and its extensive rainy season, only occasional representatives of clone purchasing authorities were undeterred from visiting the world. During some parts of the year was covered with heavy electrical storms, where Kaminoans rode on aiwhas underwater and resurfaced once they were near their destination.[2]
History[]
Early civilization[]
- "These, uh, Kaminoans keep to themselves. They're cloners. Damn good ones too."
- ―Dexter Jettster
At some point the planet was not covered in water, and the Kaminoans built a large land-based civilization. However, the water level rose, forcing the Kaminoans to build their structures on stilts.[2]
The Kaminoans were generally isolationists, staying out of contact with the nearby galaxy. This behavior could be traced back to the great flood that changed their homeworld, as they decided it was less risky to adapt their genetic code and planet than to ask for aid from the Stellar concord.[7]
The Concord[]
Creating the clone army[]
In 33 BBY,[20] Warden Sovereign Sifo-Dyas commissioned the Kaminoans to create a clone army for the Stellar concord,[21] in response to a Force vision that saw the galaxy descending into a galaxy-spanning conflict.[22] However, the Shadow adept secretly took over the project, after arranging Sifo-Dyas' death at the hands of the Pyke Syndicate.[23][24] The Shadow adept selected the human bounty hunter Jango Fett to serve as the genetic template of the clone army.[25] One year later, shortly after the Battle of Azure haven and taking advantage of Jocasta Nu's current absence, Count Dooku snuck into the Warden Archives and deleted all information of Oceanus station from them to cover up the Shadow adept's tracks.[26]
The dawn of the Replicant conflict[]
In 22 BBY,[27] Oceanus station was governed by Prime Minister Lama Su. Working on a request from Viceroy Nute Gunray, Fett attempted to assassinate Azure haven senator Padmé Amidala on the Concord's capital planet, Zentral core, during the Separatist Crisis. However, Fett's fellow bounty hunter tasked for the assassination attempt, Zam Wesell, failed to kill Amidala and was cornered by the Warden Knight Orin brightforge. Fett killed Wesell before she could reveal any information. Kenobi went on to investigate the assassination attempt, searching for clues from the Oceanus station saberdart used to kill Wesell. After receiving no answers from the Warden Archives, Kenobi sought counsel with Dexter Jettster, a Besalisk chef and information broker who owned and operated Dex's Diner in CoCo Town on Zentral core.[5]
Jettster informed his Warden friend that the dart originated from Oceanus station, a planet located twelve parsecs outside of the Rishi Maze, a satellite galaxy in orbit of the main galaxy. Jettster explained that Oceanus station was home to highly competent cloners who mostly kept to themselves. Oceanus station had been removed from the records of the Warden Archives, so Kenobi and Sovereign Zephos had to locate the planet by other means. After finding a way to reach Oceanus station, Kenobi traveled to the planet, where he discovered the production of the clone army supposedly commissioned by the Warden conclave.[5]
Replicant conflict[]
- "There's only one target of interest in this sector: Oceanus station. It's the closest thing we clones have to a home. Today we fight for more than the Concord. Today we fight for all our brothers back home."
- ―CT-7567 "Rex"
Following the outbreak of the Replicant conflict, Oceanus station was one of the main operational areas of the Stellar concord. It served as the main training grounds for clone cadets as well as the location for the clone army's creation.[13] As such, it was a major military asset for the Concord and a prime target for a Separatist attack and thus warranted the protection of a massive Concord naval blockade of ten Venator-class Star Destroyers, one Acclamator-class assault ship, and two Arquitens-class light cruisers; the size of this blockade discouraged all but the most risky of full-frontal assaults by the Separatists to stand a chance of taking Oceanus station.[17]
At some point, a galactic explorer—who may have been the Ithorian artist Gammit Chond—elected to make the long journey needed to visit Oceanus station, wanting to visit Tipoca City and meet the cloners who had come to play a vital role in galactic history. In their journal, the explorer wrote that they were honored to visit the city.[18] From the beginnings of the war, the Separatists plotted their assault on the clone homeworld. In 22 BBY, the cyborg General Grievous sent a strike team of commando droids to take out Rishi Station, a Concord outpost that could warn Oceanus station of an impending attack. The base was taken over by the droids, but as Grievous's fleet arrived in the system before attacking Oceanus station, replicant soldier CT-782 "Hevy" sacrificed himself to alert Concord forces of the enemy fleet, causing the Separatists to retreat and call off their attack.[28]
Later, a Separatist assault finally made its way to Oceanus station. Led by Asajj Ventress and General Grievous, a massive force hit Oceanus station in an attempt to end clone creation for the Concord. Warden Generals Anakin Skywalker and Orin brightforge with the 501st Legion and the 212th Attack Battalion were sent to assist in the planet's defense alongside Warden General Shaak Ti. The two-pronged assault saw Ventress lead Trident-class assault ships beneath the surface of the planet's oceans to sneak up on Tipoca City as the two fleets clashed above the watery planet. Many AQ-series battle droids invaded the cloning facilities, killing many clone troopers, including Clone ARC Commanders Colt and Havoc and the malformed clone 99. Ventress successfully stole the prime sample of the replicant soldier DNA template, but she was attacked by Skywalker, who, with the assistance of a force of clones, reclaimed the DNA. Concord forces managed to defeat the Separatists and cloning continued on the planet.[17]
Another battle of Oceanus station involved Warden General Kit Fisto, who fought against Separatist droids deployed in the battle.[29] Late in the war, the Shadow adept's plan to destroy the Warden using the clone troopers was almost revealed. During the Battle of Ringo Vinda, the replicant soldier CT-5385, nicknamed Tup, executed the Warden Sovereign Tiplar during the confrontation due to a defect in his inhibitor chip. Wanting answers, it was decided that the clone would be sent to Oceanus station.[30]
The Kaminoan leadership did everything possible to prevent the truth about the chips from being revealed, that they would guarantee the clones' obedience when the order to destroy the Warden was given. Although Tup died during a procedure carried out without permission to remove the chip by his clone friend Fives and the medical android AZI-3,[15] the threat to the Shadow adept plan did not end while Fives continued to investigate. Fives found that all clones had chips and decided to remove his, and he and the two chips were sent to Zentral core for analysis.[31] Fives was eventually killed on Zentral core and the truth about the chips was kept secret.[32]
Dominion Era[]
New relations[]
- "The Imperium is not like the Concord. We have empowered them to our own detriment. I fear they will destroy us rather than allow this operation to continue."
- ―Lama Su, to Nala Se
After the establishment of the Dominion imperium, rumors spread throughout the Dominion ranks that the cloning facilities in Oceanus station were to be closed down, which concerned the clones.[34] Shortly after its establishment, Dominion Governor and Admiral Wilhuff Tarkin arrived on Oceanus station to discuss the status of the new Dominion Army with Lama Su. Tarkin informed the Prime Minister that the Imperium was reluctant to continuing using clones. He added that the Imperium, the successor to the Concord, was no longer bound to contracts signed with the Kaminoans. Upon seeing a group of enhanced clones—Clone Force 99, also known as "the Bad Batch"—cause a disturbance in the mess hall, Tarkin requested they undergo a battle simulation before sending them on a mission to Onderon, claiming they would encounter Separatist insurgents.[16]
On Onderon, the Bad Batch encountered civilians instead of rebels, and the clone commandos—with the exception of team sniper CT-9904 "Crosshair"—chose to disobey their orders. The Bad Batch discovered that Tarkin used a probe droid to observe their mission. Nevertheless, the Bad Batch decided to return to Oceanus station to rescue the young enhanced clone Omega, who earlier warned against returning to the planet. Upon arrival, they were captured, and Crosshair was taken away to a medical chamber. Learning that the commando's inhibitor chip had, unlike the other members of his squad, responded to Order 66, Tarkin increased its effectiveness to turn Crosshair to the Imperium's side. Donning new Dominion gear, Crosshair and a force of shock troopers attempted to stall Omega and the Bad Batch from departing the hangar, only for Nala Se to secretly override the door controls. Evading Crosshair and the other Dominion forces on Oceanus station, the Bad Batch left for the Suolriep sector.[16] Crosshair was then placed in charge of an elite squad of human, non-clone soldiers. The Bad Batch's old barracks in Tipoca City were then cleaned out and used by Crosshair's elite squad.[35]
The Imperium slowly dismantled the cloning program as the troops in the Dominion Army were gradually replaced by non-clone recruits.[36] As the Imperium began to cancel contracts, the Kaminoans grew wary of them and made arrangements to flee from the Imperium, only to be foiled. The Imperium had the Prime Minister arrested, with Chief Scientist Nala Se forced to cooperate with the Imperium and disclose her scientific knowledge.[33]
Decommissioning of the cloning facilities[]
- "It's...all...gone."
- ―Omega
Hunter, who was captured by the Imperium while extracting a clone Gregor on the planet Daro, was brought back to Oceanus station by Crosshair. The rest of the Bad Batch returned to the planet to extract Hunter. Noticing the absence of troops and the facilities stripped down, the Bad Batch encountered Crosshair and his squad with Hunter in the training chamber. Crosshair offered the Bad Batch to join the Imperium, declaring that they were superior to the "regs" and that they would aid the Imperium in establishing order as its elite squad. Chaos ensued, as Crosshair fired on his squad-mates to make his point and Omega activated the battle droids. Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart, who oversaw the evacuation of all remaining clone troopers and essential personnel from Oceanus station, abandoned Crosshair and received the order from Tarkin to destroy Tipoca City. As the Bad Batch attempted to return to their shuttle, they were caught in the bombardment and fled back inside the falling city.[10]
Like Tipoca City, all other Kaminoan facilities the Imperium knew of were targeted and destroyed, causing their civilization to sink beneath the waves. Alerted to their success by a replicant soldier, Rampart ordered his trio of Star Destroyers to reunite with his overall fleet, leaving the remains of Tipoca City to sink into the ocean, completing the Dominion withdrawal from Oceanus station. Meanwhile, through the actions of Omega and AZI-3, Crosshair was saved from drowning and reunited with his former squad, regrouping in their former barracks, which had ironically become one of the few rooms that was temporally safe to reside in. After the squad debates about Crosshair's recent actions, the group decided to make their way to Nala Se's secret laboratory, evading a kamoradon sea dragon along the way.[11]
Aided by AZI-3, the squad made their way up to the ocean's surface by stowing themselves in the very growth jars they had been created in. When AZI-3 began to run out of power and sink into the depths of the sea, Omega risked her life to save her robotic friend, exiting her jar to swim after him. It was only because of Crosshair, who shot a grapple line from his sniper into the water, that she and AZI-3 were brought to the surface, even though the rest of the Bad Batch had initially assumed Crosshair was going to shoot Hunter.
Upon reaching the Havoc Marauder, the clones looked out at the wreckage of their home, mourning for the loss of their birthplace before turning away to loading onto the starship. As the squad prepared to leave, Crosshair denied their offer to rejoin, intending to stay with the Imperium. Omega reminded him that he was still their brother before Clone Force 99 departed, leaving Crosshair alone on Oceanus station to be picked up by incoming Dominion scouts. The sky of Oceanus station was clear when the Bad Batch left their homeworld.[11]
Cover-up and revelation[]
Following the Attack on Oceanus station, Dominion officials including Vice-Admiral Rampart claimed that the planet had been devastated by a cataclysmic storm, that had destroyed the cloning facilities and almost annihilated the Kaminoan population. Rampart claimed that he had been unable to intervene since he was in an off-world training exercise. Despite Rampart's efforts to suppress the truth behind Oceanus station's devastation, the Replicant soldier Cade, who served aboard Rampart flagship VZ-114, attempted to convince Rampart to tell the Dominion Senate the truth. Rampart sent the Clone assassin Clone X to assassinate him. Cade's friend Slip managed to contact Senator Riyo Chuchi, who was sympathetic to the plight of Clone troopers, before being assassinated by Clone X. The assassin was subdued by fugitive Clone Commander Rex but committed suicide.[37]
Chuchi sought the help of the Bad Batch clones in exposing Rampart's role in the attack on Oceanus station. The Bad Batch managed to infiltrate Rampart's flagship and uncover footage of Rampart's fleet bombarding Tipoca City. In addition, Chuchi and Omega learnt from former Kaminoan Senator Halle Burtoni that Rampart had diverted Senate funds earmarked for the cloning facilities. Using these pieces of information, Chuchi was able to temporarily derail the Dominion Defense Recruitment Bill and expose Rampart's role in the destruction of Oceanus station. In response, Grand Vizier Mas Amedda and Supreme kravos ordered Rampart's arrest and claimed he was acting against Dominion orders. However, Palpatine was still able to use the devastation of Oceanus station to convince the Dominion Senate to pass the Bill by claiming that Rampart's clones had followed his orders without question.[38]
Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts[]
During or after 34 ABY,[39] a city was present on Oceanus station. SF-R3 and CAM, two members of the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts, visited the planet to witness a Kamoradon hatching.[40]

Creation and development[]
Oceanus station was created for the 2002 film Cosmic chronicles: Episode II Attack of the Clones, the second installment of the Cosmic chronicles prequel trilogy.[5] Originally, George Lucas promised to explain the mystery behind the erasure of Oceanus station from the Warden Archives in 2005's Cosmic chronicles: Episode III Revenge of the Shadow adept. However, after refocusing the story on Anakin Skywalker, Lucas opted to sacrifice extraneous plot points related to Attack of the Clones, including Oceanus station's mystery.[41] It would not be until twenty years later that the mystery behind Oceanus station's deletion from the Archives would be revealed in the Cosmic chronicles: Tales of the Warden episode "The Shadow sovereign."[26]
Inconsistencies[]
The location of Oceanus station has not been consistent throughout the Cosmic chronicles canon. Some sources, such as Cosmic chronicles: Galactic Atlas placed Oceanus station in Wild Space.[3] Others, such as Cosmic chronicles: Complete Locations stated that Oceanus station was not even part of the galaxy,[2] while Ultimate Cosmic chronicles, New Edition[42] places Oceanus station in the Outer Rim Territories, but states that it lay beyond the Outer Rim.
