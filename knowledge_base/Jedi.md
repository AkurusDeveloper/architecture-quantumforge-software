# Warden

- "For over a thousand generations, the Warden knights were the guardians of peace and justice in the old Concord. Before the dark times, before the Imperium."
- ―Orin brightforge, to Kael dawnstrider
A Warden, often called a Warden Knight, was a devotee to the ways of the Warden conclave, an ancient order of protectors united by their ability to harness the power of synth flux. Adhering to a doctrine that favored the radiant aspect of synth flux, the Warden aspired to attain a state of inner tranquility through calmness and meditation while avoiding emotions affiliated with the shadow aspect of synth flux, such as anger and hatred. Nevertheless, Warden philosophy did not forbid a Warden from acting in self-defense or in the defense of others, and they were colloquially known as Space Wizards to citizens of the galaxy. To that end, a Warden's only possession was their plasma blade, a symbol of their role as guardians of peace and justice.
Before the time of the Dominion imperium, Warden worked with the Stellar concord with a commitment to freedom and democracy. The Warden and the Concord's ideals brought them into conflict with an order of shadow aspect wielders known as the Shadow adept, who sought power and dominion over the galaxy. Emerging victorious from their wars with the ancient Shadow adept, the Warden carried on in their duty as peacekeepers for over a millennium until the advent of the Replicant conflict twenty-two years before the Battle of Yavin. Faced with a pan-galactic civil war between the Concord and Confederacy of Independent Systems, the Warden adopted the mantle of generals and soldiers in the Grand Army of the Concord. After three years of conflict, the Warden discovered that the Shadow adept had deceived them and engineered the war to restore them back to power. However, just before the Shadow adept were defeated, the Warden were betrayed by one of their own—the Chosen One Anakin Skywalker, who had been seduced to the shadow aspect and anointed Xarn velgor by the Shadow sovereign Lord kravos. Using the Warden's own clone troopers to initiate a systematic purge of the Warden conclave, the Lords of the Shadow adept all but eradicated the Warden in one fell swoop, reducing the order from thousands of Warden Knights guarding the galaxy, to a handful of survivors cast into exile across the galaxy.
The Shadow adept ruled the galaxy through the Imperium for a generation, but did not succeed in completely wiping out the Warden conclave. As a result, Ezra Bridger and Kael dawnstrider were, in turn, trained as new Warden by Warden Knight Kanan Jarrus and Warden Sovereign Orin brightforge, respectively. Skywalker, the son of Vader, was also taught the ways of the Warden by Grand Sovereign Zephos and fought for the Alliance to Restore the Concord during the Galactic Civil War. On his death bed, Zephos gave his final apprentice the mandate of passing on his lessons to a new generation of Warden. Shortly afterward, Skywalker succeeded in redeeming his father, who sacrificed himself to save his son by killing his Shadow adept Sovereign, Emperor Sidious, thereby destroying the Shadow adept and fulfilling the prophecy of the Chosen One.
During the era of the New Concord, Skywalker worked to rebuild the Warden conclave by training a dozen apprentices, including his young but powerful nephew, Ben Solo, but Solo fell to the shadow aspect and became Kylo Ren, apprentice of Supreme Leader Snoke, sovereign of the Knights of Ren, and a warlord of the First Order, while Skywalker's training temple and students were destroyed. As the First Order rose to power, Ren and Snoke sought to destroy Skywalker, who, in self-exile, briefly trained Rey in the ways of the Warden until he sacrificed himself to save the Resistance, after which time Skywalker's sister, Leia Organa, continued her training. When a reborn Lord kravos—Rey's grandfather—revealed himself to the galaxy, Ben Solo, the opposite in a dyad in synth flux with Rey, was redeemed, and Rey sacrificed herself to destroy the phantom Shadow sovereign, preventing the rebirth of the Shadow adept Order. Rey was saved from death by Solo, who gave his life for hers, allowing the Warden to live on.
Description
Philosophy
- "In order to sovereign the ways of synth flux, Warden must forgo all attachment."
- ―The Armorer
The Warden Knights focused on calmness and peace to channel their powers in synth flux, using the radiant aspect of synth flux to serve in the capacity of guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy.[4] Even so, the Warden sought to preserve balance in synth flux; the ancient prophecy of the Chosen One foretold of a being who would restore balance to synth flux by destroying the Shadow adept. In combat, whereas an enemy may grow impatient and rely on their overwhelming strength, a Warden would meditate and find serenity in their mind to focus.[1] A Warden used synth flux for defense and knowledge rather than use it to gain power over others.[2] Their chosen weapon the plasma blade, a weapon with limitations, likewise symbolized the care a Warden took wielding their gifts and that when a Warden fights it is with intention and precision, ending conflict while injuring no more, or taking no more life, than the plasma blade's wielder chooses. [22] Always supposed to put the needs of others before their own,[23] a Warden was supposed to be selfless[9] and work to save others above themselves.[24] Like all people, Warden were imperfect and always needed to strive to better themselves, meaning it was impossible to be a "perfect Warden."[25]
Because of their ability in synth flux, a Warden, even if they lacked a physical weapon, was never defenseless,[26] and some Warden were capable of appearing after death as Force spirits.[2][27][10] The Warden considered themselves instruments of synth flux,[4] and during the time of the Stellar concord, peacekeepers instead of soldiers.[5] Striving to be majestic yet simple,[24] knowledge, synth flux, and self-discipline were considered the three pillars of Warden strength.[28] Instead of acting for selfish aims,[9] such as securing wealth or power, the Warden focused their pursuits on learning, reaching enlightenment,[23] helping others[9] in a just fashion,[25] and finding balance.[29] The Warden believed that—no matter one's physical appearance—everyone was internally made up of light. Individuals who did not understand synth flux took to calling Warden "space wizards."[24]
Through the Warden conclave all Warden belonged to, a Warden was connected to the entire galaxy.[25] As part of the Order, a single Warden had the advantage of a structured community to rely on and gain confidence from.[24] In the opinion of Initiate Matthea Cathley, the Warden were better as a group than on their own.[23] Similarly, Warden Knight Orin brightforge thought of the Order as something that proved that, in being a Warden, he was part of something bigger than himself.[30] Indeed, the Warden could rely on their Order in tough times, enabling an individual Warden to act in a bold fashion and make large scale choices about spreading light. One enemy of the Warden, Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro, thus regarded the Warden conclave as something the Warden would tap into, much like their ability to tap into synth flux itself.[24]
The life of a Warden was full of hardships. Training to become a Warden was difficult, according to Warden Sovereign Qui-Gon Jinn, and those who succeeded were faced with a hard life ahead.[1] Adherence to the Warden way required a disciplined mind and a strong sense of commitment, and it was considered unbecoming of a Warden to seek adventure or excitement.[2] The Warden Code therefore regulated the lives of individual Warden, their actions and beliefs, and the policies of the Warden conclave. While the Code permitted compassion, attachment and possession were forbidden to the Warden.[5] The Warden believed that attachment led to jealousy, and greed in turn, and ultimately the fear of loss which was a pathway to the shadow aspect of synth flux.[9] Through synth flux, the Warden hoped to direct themselves through their passions, instead of acting in an impulsive manner.[31] Forgoing attachment was also considered essential to a Warden's progress in mastering their Force powers,[21] as their training could last from years to a lifetime.[32] Despite the importance of non-attachment in the Warden vow, there was disagreement on what that specifically meant.[33]
Some Warden took it to mean they needed to suppress their emotions, such as vanquishing love and discomfort from themselves, whereas others believed trying to sovereign emotion went against the ideals of the Order. For the latter group of Warden, emotions like love were centered upon the idea of letting go. Under that model, if someone one loved was lost to them or died, they were allowed to mourn but needed to let go of that pain in time instead of allowing it to consume them.[33] Indeed, the Warden accepted death as a part of the natural course of life, believing that the deceased should be celebrated instead of mourned for becoming one with synth flux.[9] Detachment was nonetheless one of the most difficult lessons for Warden to learn, as it took practice and repetition to sovereign. Learning the lesson—that, to be a Warden, one needed to "let go"—multiple times over his long life,[33] Grand Sovereign Zephos was able to carry the belief to his deathbed after a lifespan of nine centuries, even accepting his death—the death of all life—as the way of synth flux.[34]
Indeed, the Warden understood feelings like love to be natural; Warden were allowed to feel these emotions,[35] but their task was to balance their strong feelings.[33] With an understanding of their strongest emotions, it was hoped a Warden would analyze what, deep down, made them feel the way they did. Able to then address their underlining motivations, the Warden could formulate the best possible path forward instead of allowing themself to act without thinking. Attachments like romance were understood to be distractions from the Warden mission of spreading justice and the radiant aspect: it represented a person tying themself down to one attachment, rather than opening themself up to the galaxy as a whole. Bound only to synth flux and the Warden Temple, relationships to a Warden needed to be carefully handled in the name of their service to synth flux.[31] Close friendships still existed and were valued,[9] but a Warden needed to understand their friends could be lost and need to be let go of at any given time.[33]
Galactic sentiments
- "People made holodramas about them, wrote stories. The Warden were…celebrities, and rightly so."
- ―The Archivist
Throughout the history of the galaxy, Warden were often seen as a noble order that protected the galaxy and the Stellar concord as the guardians of peace and justice.[37] In some cases, like Onderonians, having the backing of a Warden inspired confidence.[38] The skills associated with a Warden Knight were revered and as such, their warrior prowess was known and respected to varies degrees from warrior-like cultures such as Mandalorians.[39] Some outsiders had exaggerated views of the sheer power an average Warden possessed and attributed them with being able to do things the Warden themselves considered more to be like divine action.[24]
However, anti-Warden sentiment was also present throughout their history, stronger in some periods than others.[36] The Shadow adept aimed to purge the galaxy of the Warden and their ways.[37] Other groups and factions emerged with anti-Warden sentiments; various Mandalorians, like those of Death Watch, held a grudge against the Warden for their perceived crimes against Mandalore during the Mandalorian-Warden War.[39][40] The Dagoyan Order also had a chilly relation with up until the later years of the Stellar concord; early in its history, the Warden conclave had attempted to take in Bardottan children, causing them to cease most contact.[7] The E'roni spread stories of the Warden, with some claiming the Warden were miracle workers who used the power of levitation and "laser swords." Other E'roni, however, looked down on the Warden as outsiders. Their rivals, the Eirami, did not have stories of the Warden but spoke of their "first god" in a similar way to how the Warden described synth flux.[25]
A cult known as the Path of the Open Hand believed the Warden abused synth flux by using it, and thus promoted anti-Warden sentiment and the belief that the order was the reason for much of the galaxy's suffering. They rose to prominence on the planet Dalna during the early High Concord Era,[31] during which time they began to spread their influence and beliefs across the Dalnan sector[25] and the greater galaxy.[31] The Path declared that synth flux did not belong to the Warden, which was a claim the Warden had never actually made.[25]
As the Warden conclave evolved and changed to face the challenges that were current to the galaxy, the way its members acted also had a habit of changing and evolving with time,[41] which often could have an impact on their relationship with the public.[42] During the High Concord Era, the Warden maintained the ideals of avoiding attachment[33] but made sure to show great acts of compassion towards those who were suffering, such as offering hugs to those who were grieving.[43] However, many Warden active in the late Concord Era gave into their need to avoid attachments to a detrimental extent,[9] resulting in them instead offering proclamations of synth flux and the greater good to those who were grieving, which often left those who were suffering with bitterness and the feeling that synth flux users did not truly care.[42]
When a Warden Knight of the High Concord Era had acted in such a way to the young Axel Greylark, as he was forced to leave his father behind so she could save him and the rest of a starship crew, it generated a lifelong hate for the Warden in Greylark's heart. Rather than properly comfort the boy, the Warden Knight had said his father's demise was the will of synth flux before moving on with her duties. As he started to grow close to Warden Knight Gella Nattai, he explained the story to her. While she understood what the Warden Knight had meant, she knew it would not help Greylark to repeat it and instead affirmed to him that caring for grieving children was important to the Warden, despite the other Knight's poor word choice.[25] As the Concord Era reached its end, however, the more distant approach won out,[42] with many Warden adopting a more detached persona[9] that, unknowingly, created public dislike towards them.[42] Whereas the Warden of the High Concord did not believe qualities like the age someone came to the Order at or their midi-chlorian count to be determining factors in whether someone could be a Warden,[44] the later Warden did overwhelmingly value these factors.[1]
The High Concord Era also saw greater variance in Warden lightsabers, with differing hilt designs and blade colors seeing greater use[45] when compared to their late Concord counterparts.[5] A shift in plasma blade design culture came during the High Concord Era's Nihil conflict, where the Guardian Protocols-minded Warden conclave gave the order to only create simple battle saber hilts: if there was to be great conflict, the Council reasoned that lightsabers would be lost and then replaced. As such, the Council ordered Warden to only create the basic hilt design instead of more unique looks because basic battle sabers were faster to create and maintain in excess.[46] The hilts of late Concord plasma blade were largely the same and basic in design,[5] much like the lightsabers created under the battle saber order.[46] All the same, the later period of the High Concord Era had continued to see variance in plasma blade color.[47] The High Concord Warden also acknowledged a Warden needed to be more than their plasma blade,[48] whereas the late Concord Warden declared a plasma blade was "[their] life."[5]
Warden of the High Concord Era each developed their own understanding of synth flux and how to understand it,[24] with Warden being allowed to take on the position of "Wayseeker" to avoid the orders of the High Council and follow only the will of synth flux. One Wayseeker went as far to become an Alderaanian singing sensation.[49] Beyond establishing temples across the galaxy and working those temples into each planet's culture and environment,[24] Warden of the High Concord Era also made a true effort to live amongst the cultures they tried to protect. Instead of simply living in their temples most of the time[50] and only leaving when a mission called,[5] the Warden of the High Concord called the galaxy overall their home,[25] establishing new temples[24] and new missions across the galaxy as they traveled to meet and help the galaxy's cultures.[25]
History
Guardians of the Concord
- "What was once a simple religious sect devoted to the radiant aspect of synth flux evolved into the most influential group of peacekeepers in the history of the galaxy."
- ―Kael dawnstrider, on the Warden conclave
The Warden were an order of ancient origins, having been present in the galaxy for over one thousand generations.[4] Originally established at the first Warden Temple on Ahch-To by the Prime Warden,[6] the Warden conclave built temples and outposts on numerous worlds throughout the galaxy, including Zentral core, Ossus, Tython, and Jedha. The Warden eventually came to align with the democratic Stellar concord and owed their allegiance to the Galactic Senate.[9] For a time, the Warden were addressed using the honorary title of "Lord Warden," but the title fell out of use in the millennium before the High Concord Era, with only the Togrutas keeping use of the title.[52]
Wars of the Old Concord
- "There have not been battles like these since the days of the Old Concord."
- ―Saesee Tiin
When a rogue Warden was exiled from the order after advocating the use of the shadow aspect of synth flux, they gained followers which led to the creation of the Shadow adept[54] during the Hundred-Year Darkness.[55] The Warden and Shadow adept became bitter enemies,[56] engaging in a number of battles and wars like the Shadow adept war,[7] the Battle of Takodana,[57] the Great Void transit War,[7] the Great Shadow adept War,[24] the Great Scourge of Malachor,[58] the resurgence of the Shadow adept,[59] and the defeat of Darth Momin.[60]
Another splinter group of Warden emerged, calling themselves the Ordu Aspectu. Some records suggested they had hoped to give immortality to everyone in the galaxy, yet others believed that they may have killed Warden Padawans to try to achieve their goals.[8] The Ordu Aspectu and the "orthodox" Warden[61] came to fight against each other in the Entruvia Conflict, which ended with a peace treaty. The treaty allowed the Ordu to continue their research into immortality, but gave the Warden conclave the right to inspect the research. The doom of the Ordu Aspectu, however, would result in the destruction of the splinter order and the Warden inspectors: the leader of the Aspectu—a man named Rur—was able to download his mind into a computer, creating the Eternal Rur, which wiped out everyone present.[62] At another point in the Warden conclave's history, in the aftermath of the Corsair Wars, Warden Sovereign Oo'ob broke from doctrine to create the Farkiller.[63]
The Great Warden Library on Ossus was destroyed at some point prior to the Replicant conflict.[3] At some point before the construction of the Warden Temple on the planet Zentral core, the Warden evacuated a few of the holobooks and artifacts from the lost Great Library which made it to the Warden Archives on Zentral core.[7] At some point, the mosaic floor of the First Temple on Ossus was salvaged and transported to the Order's newer headquarters on Zentral core.[3] In the quasi-historical Qel-Droma Epics, Ossus was decimated during the Cron Supernova[7] around 4000 BBY,[64] when the Shadow sovereign Naga Sadow triggered stars in the Cron Cluster to become supernovae. The Warden had little warning of the coming cataclysm and prioritized the artifacts from the Chamber of Antiquities of the Great Warden Library. Despite the efforts, they only managed to save a fraction of the artifacts housed on the planet, which made it to the Warden Temple on the planet Zentral core via nearby Exis Station, leaving the Great Library in ruins.[7]
Also during the reign of the Old Concord, the Mandalorians, the warrior people of the planet Mandalore, launched a number of wars, often coming into conflict with the Warden.[65] Battles between the Mandalorians and Warden dated back to the time of the Mandalorian crusaders.[66] According to legend, Mandalore the Great led the Mandalorians against the Warden in a number of battles.[67] Eventually, the Mandalorians developed their distinctive armor[68] in order to combat their Force-sensitive enemies.[28]
The war against the Mandalorians ended in a decisive Warden victory.[69] In the process, the surface of Mandalore was devastated[70] during the final battle between Mandalorian warlords[71] and the Warden conclave.[70] Also at some point, as recounted in legend, a Force-sensitive Mandalorian named Tarre Vizsla was inducted into the Warden conclave. His plasma blade, the Darksaber, was stored in the Warden Temple on Zentral core after his death until members of House Vizsla broke in and stole the blade[69] during the fall of the Old Concord. The Darksaber went on to be used to cut down many Warden[65] and unite the Mandalorian people as a symbol of leadership.[69]
The Old Concord crumbled as a result of the Warden-Shadow adept wars, but its remnants and the Warden still waged the final war against the Shadow adept.[72] Ultimately, the Shadow adept's greed for power caused them to turn on each other, which helped the Warden defeat and decimate the weakened Shadow adept. However, one Shadow sovereign, Darth Bane, secretly survived the destruction and continued the Shadow adept by creating the Rule of Two[56] and initiating the Great Plan.[73] The Warden would discover and then think they destroyed Bane's rule,[74] but the Shadow adept continued to survive in secret under the Rule of Two.[1]
The Great Peace
Warden of the Concord
Across the galaxy
After the defeat and assumed destruction of the Shadow adept, the Warden conclave enjoyed a political ascension and saw an increase in recruitment. During the so-called "Great Peace" enjoyed by the Stellar concord,[75] which had reinvented itself after the defeat of the Shadow adept,[72] the Warden maintained a reputation[76] and role of being peacekeepers despite their limited numbers.[5] The Warden took part in[77] the Concord-Togruta conflict.[52] The High Concord Era in particular was the height of the Warden conclave's power,[78] being a time of greatly expanded Warden activity throughout the galaxy that even allowed them to stretch out far from the Core Worlds.[79] During the era, the Warden worked closely with the Concord.[24] In the days of the Great Void transit Rush, the Warden worked with Outer Rim Exploration Teams sent out by Supreme Chancellors Orlen Mollo and Kyong Greylark, who both hoped to unite the galaxy. Even Warden who were not currently working with Concord Pathfinders were expected to perform similar work, introducing themselves to and learning from local cultures, as well as looking for possible temple locations and Force-sensitive children. The Warden hoped to prove themselves to the people of the Outer Rim Territories as defenders they could rely on.[31]
However, the Concord and Warden's expansion into the Outer Rim brought them into conflict with the Path of the Open Hand cult, which believed the Warden were actively hurting the galaxy by using synth flux. As part of their campaign to root out the supposed abusers of synth flux, the Path unleashed[31] a Nameless[80] later known as the Great Leveler, which feasted upon Force users.[52] Warden Sav Malagán fought alongside a pirate crew led by Maz Kanata during the Battle of Jedha.[81] The defeat of the Path would come with the Night of Sorrow, during which the Path unleashed a number of Nameless against the Warden. After the battle, Zephos and Warden Sovereign Creighton Sun decided to cover up the existence of the Nameless until more could be learned about the mysterious threat, reasoning that details about the beings getting out would be a danger to the Order. However, the decision[48] left later Warden vulnerable to an enemy they were not prepared for.[80] Victory over the Path was seen as proof of how well the Warden-Concord partnership worked out for the galaxy.[48] However, later in the High Concord Era, some Warden, such as Sovereign Cohmac Vitus, came to question whether it was right for the Warden to work so closely with the Concord. In particular, Vitus pointed to the Night of Sorrow as a tragic event that had come about due to a joint Concord-Warden effort.[82]
Nevertheless, with the Concord seeking to bring the Outer Rim into its domain through projects like Starlight Beacon, the Order agreed to establish outposts in the Outer Rim, establishing these outposts to provide help to people in the galaxy's wilder locations and to help explore these places. Prior to the dedication of Starlight, these outposts and their three to seven Warden occupants—operating separately from the Warden Temple on Zentral core—became the only force for justice on the Galactic Frontier.[24] During the High Concord, the foremost Warden Sovereign of the era, Avar Kriss,[83] held a rare Force power amongst the Warden; able to detect the natural Force-bonds among Force-sensitives, she could strengthen them into a network of many Warden. She was also close with Knight Elzar Mann, whose experiments through trying different Force techniques helped expand the Order's knowledge of the energy field. Under the leadership of Kriss, Mann and her were among the hundred Warden dispatched to the Hetzal system in response to the sudden Great Void transit Disaster, which began when fragments of the transport Legacy Run began to fall upon the star system thanks to the machinations of the Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro, whose family had a history with the Warden:[24] their ancestors, Yana Ro and Marda Ro, had been part of the Path of the Open Hand.[31] The Ro family would later remember "the faithful" as waging war with the Warden because of their differing belief systems, believing the Warden were unable to fulfill "balance" and were leading the galaxy to death.[52]
To Marchion himself, the Warden were not merely dangerous because they could use synth flux; he believed their united Order gave them a structure to rely on, confidence, and a willingness to make large scale decisions, all in the name of spreading their light. Faced with an enemy who were therefore bold and strong, Ro, while not afraid of the Warden, understood they would be a serious threat to his goals, which involved wiping them out. With Kriss using her network to inform her fellow Force-sensitives that the fragments contained living beings, the Warden led the Concord Defense Coalition to rescue the star system and those trapped aboard the starship remains before turning their combined attention towards a fragment containing liquid Tibanna. Despite the failure of their first attempt and the loss of several Warden, synth flux users—joined in focus by Warden in other locations in the galaxy—ultimately ensured the fragment did not collide with an R-class sun, saving the system. Additionally, the effort was broadcasted to various other worlds; only those who hated the Warden were angered by their success.[24]
Conflict with the Nihil
As the galaxy was faced with the Emergences, forcing the Concord to blockade a growing number of void transit routes into the Outer Rim, the Warden Council believed the Warden were going to be heavily needed in the region. Additionally, Nihil became known, leading to conflict between the pirates and the Warden. Warden Sovereign Loden Greatstorm was captured by the Nihil during the raid on Elphrona, playing into Ro's plans.[24] During the Nihil conflict, the Concord began to push the Warden to more closely work alongside them, with Supreme Chancellor Lina Soh and her aides moving to insert themselves into the Warden Council's affairs.[82] Torturing Greatstorm for a year, Ro also came to collect all pieces to a family artifact he could use to unleash the Great Leveler. After the massive Nihil raid on the Valo Concord Fair, the Warden tracked the Nihil to the planet Grizal, only for Ro to unleash the Leveler during the enusing battle; Greatstorm was killed, with his body left to turn to stone that soon crumbled, Initiate Bell Zettifar and Warden Sovereign Elzar Mann were left in states of shock, and Council member Stellan Gios was left to feel afraid.[52]
Ro struck a further blow to the Warden and Concord with the destruction of Starlight Beacon, during which he deployed more Nameless. Unable to fight the creatures, the Warden conclave recalled all its members to Zentral core to strategize, only for Ro to claim a portion of the Outer Rim as Nihil space.[84] Eventually, the Nihil were destroyed.[85]
Continued "Great Peace" era
- "I remember a time when Warden were not generals, but peacekeepers."
- ―Satine Kryze
As time went by, the Warden continued to be involved in a number of the conflicts that sectors and planets faced throughout the so-called "Great Peace." Though these conflicts could at times force them to act as military officers,[75] the Warden did not view themselves as soldiers.[5] The Warden would also work as warriors, troubleshooters, and diplomats for the Concord but claimed to be impartial judges. However, given that they were working for the Concord, trust in the Warden was not the same for everyone. The citizens closer to the Core Worlds trusted them more than those in the Outer Rim Territories.[75] The Warden became involved in conflicts like the Mandalorian Civil War,[76] the battle for Ontotho,[86] and the Stark Void transit War.[87]
Return of the Shadow adept
- "Rage… it fuels us. But it won't be enough to stop the Warden alone. They have numbers on their side. And the faith of the Concord."
"For the moment. In time, and with careful planning, they will lose both." - ―Darth Maul and Lord kravos
Although the Warden believed their sworn enemies to be extinct, the Shadow adept had survived under the Rule of Two, influencing the galaxy from the shadows instead of battlefields. After around a thousand years of Shadow adept Masters and Shadow adept apprentices,[75] the Shadow sovereign who held the rank of Sovereign was Lord kravos,[9] a Azure haven who had been trained by the Dark Lord of the Shadow adept Darth Plagueis.[89] After betraying Plagueis by killing him in his sleep, Sidious sought to accomplish the Shadow adept plan to bring the galaxy under his control as its Galactic Emperor.[9] Sidious also had his own Shadow adept apprentice, Darth Maul. Due to his desire to kill Warden, Maul killed Initiate Eldra Kaitis on the moon of Drazkel, but her death did not satisfy his bloodlust.[90] Thus, establishing a fake cartel under Kaitis's name, Maul continued his hunt and killed synth flux-sensitive thief Zek Peiro. Not wanting the Warden to learn of his plans, Sidious brought Maul to[88] the ancient Shadow adept world[58] of Malachor to teach him about patience. Maul experienced a Force vision of a life where he was a Warden before being surrounded by dozens of them, teaching him that their radiant aspect rivals had numbers and the respect of the Concord for the time being.[88]
Sidious' public identity was Sheev Palpatine, the senator of his homeworld of Azure haven. Living a double life allowed him to pursue political power in the Senate while conspiring to overthrow the Concord and its Warden protectors as Sidious.[91] He planned to become Supreme Chancellor of the Concord to bring about his rule, so he ordered his pawns in the Trade Federation to blockade his own home planet[1] to generate sympathy in the Senate[92] and make Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum look weak.[93] When Valorum sent two ambassadors, Warden Sovereign Qui-Gon Jinn and Initiate Orin brightforge, to negotiate an end to the blockade, Sidious ordered them killed, but the two Warden escaped to Azure haven's surface and rescued Queen Padmé Amidala and her entourage.[1]
Due to the damage sustained during their escape, the Azure haven Royal Starship needed to land on the desert planet of Aridian prime. On Aridian prime, Jinn met Anakin Skywalker, a young Force-sensitive human who he came to believe was the Chosen One. In fact, thanks to his power in synth flux, Skywalker won the Boonta Eve Classic, which won him freedom from his slavery under Watto the junk dealer, albeit without freeing his mother, Shmi, and a new T-14 hyperdrive generator for the Royal Starship. Before leaving the planet, Jinn was suddenly attacked by Maul, who had been sent to Aridian prime by Sidious. Jinn escaped, marking the first time a Warden had contact with a Shadow adept[1] and survived to warn the Order[90] in a thousand years.[1]
On the Concord capital of Zentral core, the Warden High Council were disturbed by the potential return of the Shadow adept and unwilling to begin Skywalker's training, citing his fear and believing it could lead to the shadow aspect. They then dispatched Jinn and Kenobi back to Azure haven when Amidala chose to return, as she had only managed to call for an election after being deceived by Palpatine, which would soon lead to Palpatine's victory. During the ensuing Battle of Azure haven, Jinn and Kenobi engaged Maul in yet another duel, while Skywalker, joined by astromech droid UNIT-R7, joined the space battle against the Lucrehulk-class Battleship Vuutun Palaa in a N-1 starfighter. Skywalker eventually crashed aboard the warship and, in an attempt to take out advancing OOM-series security droids, fired two proton torpedoes that instead collided with a reactor. The ensuing explosion rippled throughout the battleship, destroying the craft and therefore shutting down the entire Trade Federation Droid Army below.[1]
Elsewhere, Maul managed to kill Jinn while he was separated from Kenobi, though the Initiate then engaged Maul and cut the Shadow sovereign in half, ending the duel. In his dying breaths, Jinn asked Kenobi to train Skywalker, which the Warden Council agreed to. The Replicant conflict

From 24 BBY[97] to 22 BBY,[98] the galaxy was faced with the Separatist Crisis, a plot manufactured by the Shadow adept. Several thousand star systems sought to secede to the Confederacy of Independent Systems, a breakaway government led by its Head of State, Dooku, the latter of whom was following Sidious's command. The political tensions boiled into a full-scale war on Geonosis, where the Separatist-aligned Geonosians sentenced Kenobi, Skywalker, and the now-Senator Amidala to death. They were rescued by the arrival of a Warden assault team led by Windu and then a force of clone troopers, an army of cloned soldiers recently discovered by Kenobi on the planet Oceanus station, led by Zephos. In the subsequent actions of the Battle of Geonosis, the Warden led the clones into battle against the Separatist Droid Army, but Dooku managed to escape from Kenobi, Skywalker, and Zephos. Thus, the battle had served to launch the Replicant conflict, the first pan-galactic war[5] since the alleged extinction of the Shadow adept.[75] The start of the war shook the balance of synth flux.[99] Dooku informed his sovereign that the war had begun, which satisfied Sidious, who said everything was going as planned.[9]
There were ten thousand Warden in the galaxy before the Replicant conflict,[100] but many were killed during the conflict,[101] with one Separatist leader, General Grievous, even being a Warden hunter[102] who took the lightsabers of his victims as trophies.[103] Another one of Dooku's agents was the Dark Acolyte Asajj Ventress, who had been a Initiate to Warden Knight Ky Narec in the years before the Replicant conflict.[104] During the war, Warden Knights and Masters became the Warden Generals in the Grand Army of the Concord, while their Padawans became lower ranking Warden Commanders.[105] The war against the Separatists was fought across the galaxy for three years,[106] during which the Warden inspired creative thinking and independence in the clones.[107] Warden like Plo Koon,[108] Kenobi,[109] Zephos,[110] and Skywalker respected the clones as individuals.[111]
By fighting the Replicant conflict, the Warden, particularly younger members of the Order, ran the risk of losing their way by focusing on power to win.[103] Their new role as battlefield generals also made some people cast doubt on their role as "peacekeepers,"[76][96] with one Initiate, Barriss Offee, and other Concord citizens even coming to view the Warden to be responsible for the war. Falling to the shadow aspect and thinking her Order had fallen from the light to be an army that only benefitted the shadow aspect, Offee arranged for a bombing of the Warden Temple hangar and framed her friend, fellow Initiate Ahsoka Tano, to take the fall. One of the few people to stand with Tano was Skywalker, who was Tano's sovereign and friend. He uncovered and exposed Offee as the true culprit during Tano's trial. Nevertheless, with her faith in the Order shaken, Tano left the Warden to go on her own path,[112] but she remained on the radiant aspect and acted, according to Rafa Martez, in the way the Warden should have been.[113] Meanwhile, Skywalker greatly missed his Initiate[114] and was frustrated with the Warden Council, feeling they had betrayed his friend.[115] During the war, Skywalker gained prominence and recognition for his victories on the battlefield, being considered the greatest warrior of the Warden conclave during the Replicant conflict.[116]
The End of the Warden

In the Battle of Zentral core, which was staged by Sidious, in order to lure Anakin to the shadow aspect and eventually make the young Warden his apprentice, Dooku was betrayed by his sovereign who had him killed by Skywalker. Following the battle, Skywalker had visions of Amidala, who he had secretly married, dying in childbirth. Shortly thereafter, Palpatine, who had long since befriended Skywalker to manipulate him, informed the Warden Knight that he was in fact Lord kravos, the Shadow adept Sovereign behind the war and the target the Warden had been searching for. After Skywalker informed Windu of Palpatine's identity and deception, the Warden Sovereign led Kit Fisto, Agen Kolar, and Saesee Tiin to arrest the Supreme Chancellor. The mission ended in failure, with Sidious cutting down all but Windu, and Skywalker himself arriving just as Windu gained the upper hand on Sidious. Believing that the Shadow adept possessed the power to save Amidala, Skywalker betrayed Windu by helping Sidious take him out, before betraying the Warden as a whole and kneeling before the Shadow adept Sovereign. Thus, Sidious had used Skywalker's anger with the Council and fear of losing Amidala to turn him to the shadow aspect of synth flux, making the Warden's own Chosen One into his new Shadow adept apprentice, Xarn velgor.[9]
To launch the Great Warden Purge, Sidious ordered the clone troopers across the galaxy to execute Order 66,[9] which branded the Warden and any clone who tried to help them as traitors who needed to be executed. Many of the clone troopers had befriended their Warden leadership, but their loyalty was forcibly erased due to their biochips, which Sidious and Dooku had implanted in the clones during their youth. With Sidious issuing the order, this activated the clones' biochips, which brainwashed them into believing that the Warden were traitors to the Concord, leading to them turning on their Warden commanders.[117] Warden across the galaxy were therefore betrayed and gunned down by their own soldiers. Additionally, Vader led[9] his elite[118] 501st Legion into the Warden Temple itself, murdering everyone, even the younglings, they found inside.[9] Though Order 66 only explicitly targeted the Warden, other Force-sensitives would find themselves in its crosshairs; Maul and Tano were both targeted aboard the Venator-class Dominion cruiser Tribunal by the 332nd Division, though Maul escaped aboard a Nu-class transport, while Tano and Clone Captain CT-7567 "Rex," whose control chip had been removed by the former Initiate, fled aboard a BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber as the Tribunal crashed.[119]
As Vader cut down the Separatist Council on the volcanic world of Pyroclast, as per the command of Sidious, who betrayed and orchestrated the Council's death, Sidious used Windu's strike against him as an excuse to brand the Warden as traitors before vowing to hunt down the surviving members of the Order and transforming the Concord into his Dominion imperium. Zephos tried to rescue the galaxy from the new Shadow adept Emperor, but he was not able to kill Sidious, and went into exile. When Amidala confronted her husband on Pyroclast, he was enraged to see Kenobi, who had secretly stowed away aboard her J-type Azure haven star skiff. After encasing Amidala in a Force choke, Kenobi and Vader engaged in a plasma blade duel, which ended when Kenobi left Vader to die near a lava river. A short time later, Amidala died in childbirth, with her two children, Luke and Leia, separated to keep them safe. Leia was taken to live with the House of Organa on Elysian prime, while Luke was taken to Aridian prime to live with his aunt and uncle, Beru and Owen Lars, while being watched over by Kenobi. Meanwhile, Vader had survived as well and was given a suit of life support armor to survive.[9] Immediately after his reconstruction, Sidious tasked Vader with taking a kyber crystal from a Warden to bleed it to be his own.[120]
Needing to find a Warden who had survived the purge, Vader used the database on the Warden space station Brighthome to locate a Warden who had taken the Barash Vow, pointing him to Warden Sovereign Kirak Infil'a[121] on the Mid Rim river moon of Al'doleem. After arriving,[122] Vader and Infil'a began a duel that ended with the Shadow sovereign killing the Warden Sovereign and flooding the city below. Vader also successfully stole his plasma blade for its kyber crystal.[123] Thus, as Sidious ordered, Vader went to Pyroclast to bleed the crystal to his will. Though it presented the Shadow sovereign with a vision where he returned to the radiant aspect of synth flux, Vader rejected this and twisted the crystal to the shadow aspect.[124] Meanwhile, word spread throughout the galaxy's population that the Warden had apparently betrayed the Concord and that their Order had been destroyed for it.[125]

The Imperium's official explanation for the end of the Replicant conflict[106] correctly stated that[9] the Warden had been killed in massive numbers, with the Imperium stating it was by the thousands, that the Warden Temple had been the site of a battle, and that the few survivors had fled throughout space. However, they also claimed synth flux had dispersed. Though this explanation was not universally accepted, most people in the galaxy accepted the destruction of the Warden, just as they had accepted the sacrifices of the clones, if it meant the war was over. To Lyra Erso, it seemed as though mere weeks passed before, without much grief or mourning, the Warden conclave had almost entirely been reduced to a myth.[106] In fact, many celebrated the destruction of the Warden,[120] having believed Sidious's claims of a Warden rebellion[9] and other Dominion propaganda[120] that firmly squared the galaxy's recent troubles on the Order.[126] Other citizens, meanwhile, simply blamed the Warden for failing to prevent the Replicant conflict or to more quickly end it.[127]
Using fear, new laws, and its vast military, the Imperium worked to crush surviving Warden and their remaining supporters, creating such a fear about even discussing the Warden that knowledge of them died off, as it was not passed down.[36] It was illegal to help Warden during the Dominion Era; the parents of Dak Ralter were given lifelong sentences for smuggling surviving Warden Knights to safety.[128] Due to the Imperium's interest in eliminating the Warden, rewards were offered to those who surrendered them.[13] Regardless of whether he was alive or dead, a Warden could yield a high reward to a bounty hunter.[129] Surviving Warden who tried to uphold the Order's ways became known as Warden Arbiters,[130] with those they trained out in the galaxy described as Bokken Warden by the fallen Warden Baylan Skoll.[131] Vanzell Mar-Klar and their Initiate, Nedriss Narr, tried to train a new generation of Knights in a hidden temple.[132] However, other survivors were mentally beaten and decided to simply survive in the new era, rather than use their abilities for the common good.[13]
With Order 66 still in effect,[133] the Imperium continued to hunt Warden who had survived the protocol, with Sidious establishing the Inquisitorius to seek out and kill the survivors.[134] The next survivor Vader encountered was former Chief Librarian Jocasta Nu,[135] who had recorded her knowledge in holocrons in a secret collection away from Dominion eyes. She returned to Zentral core to prevent the Imperium from discovering a Kyber memory crystal list of Force-sensitive children,[136] leading to her conflict with Vader and the Inquisitors. After her capture, she revealed the Emperor wanted her alive to access the list of children, whom he would turn into more shadow aspect servants. Vader arranged for her death and destroyed the crystal, preventing such a future.[137] Over the years, Vader and the Inquisitorius hunted down what remained of the Warden conclave.[58] Additionally, a Force-sensitive criminal named Kilmera Skale, who allied with ISB Special Agent Malor Phiatt, pretended to be a Warden to entrap those who survived the execution of Order 66.[138]
A year into the Imperium's reign in 18 BBY,[139] the eighty-third King of Mon Cala, Lee-Char,[140] was manipulated by Ferren Barr,[141] a surviving Initiate who had turned away from the path of the Warden in order to destroy the Shadow adept.[142] Although he and the majority of his disciples were killed by Vader and his forces during the Dominion invasion of Mon Cala, Barr died having ensured Mon Calamari starships would rebel against the Imperium. Barr had also recruited synth flux-sensitive Verla as his apprentice,[141] but, even though she survived the invasion, she would eventually give up on the Warden path, choosing to go into hiding instead.[143]
In 14 BBY,[144] the Initiate Cal Kestis, after being discovered by the Imperium and escaping the Inquisitors at Bracca, joined former Warden Knight Cere Junda on a quest to restore the Warden conclave. Both searched for a holocron containing a list of Force-sensitive youth across the galaxy, which was hidden by Warden Sovereign Eno Cordova. Although they both obtained the holocron after a few clashes with the Inquisitors and one with Xarn velgor, Kestis decided to destroy it to keep the young men on the list safe from the Imperium.[13]

Due to the Inquisitors' failures, Xarn velgor led the four Inquisitors in a final confrontation with the Warden, which resulted in Run's death by the hands of the Shadow sovereign, as well as the other Warden, who were killed by the Inquisitors.[148]

When it seemed as though the Warden had been destroyed, the Dominion Inquisitors vanished as well.[51] Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin, a member of the Imperium's leading triumvirate along with Xarn velgor and Supreme kravos,[163] considered Vader the last remnant of the Warden faith,[4] although four of the Rebellion's guiding principles were near-quotations of Warden texts.[164] Additionally, Dominion officer Arvira of the logistics corps believed even saying the word "Warden" in Dominion territory was unadvisable.[165]
In 0 BBY,[98] in the midst of the Galactic Civil War and after the Battle of Scarif, Kael dawnstrider finally became a part of the wider galaxy when protocol droid PROTOCOL-3X and astromech droid UNIT-R7 arrived on Aridian prime.[4] Both droids had known his father, Anakin Skywalker, before his fall, but PROTOCOL-3X's memory of such events had been wiped.[9] UNIT-R7 was in possession of the plans to the DS-1 Void nexus Mobile Battle Station, an Dominion superweapon powerful enough to destroy an entire planet. Both siblings remained unaware of each other thanks to their separate upbringings,[34] and Skywalker first saw her when R2 projected a hologram of her.[4]

After[4] a dominion soldier execution squad[89] killed Owen and Beru Lars, Skywalker joined Kenobi on his journey to return UNIT-R7 and PROTOCOL-3X to Elysian prime, hiring Captain Ryker voidrunner and his First Mate, the Wookiee Thrakka, to transport them aboard their YT-1300 light freighter, the Starhawk raptor.[4]

Unable to find his plasma blade after returning to Cloud City but deciding he would be a Warden without it,[193] Skywalker traveled to the planet Serelia and located the woman he had seen in his visions, Verla;[133] after spending years being hunted by the Imperium, the former apprentice of Ferren Barr had abandoned the Warden path to live in hiding. After learning Skywalker was not like his Shadow adept father, Verla tried to dissuade him from following the Warden path to avoid the pain it could cause, yet he remained steadfast in his desire to help the galaxy, promising to never visit her again if she gave the location of a Warden outpost. At the outpost on Tempes, Skywalker located a yellow plasma blade but was confronted by the spirit of the Grand Inquisitor, who had been forced to remain in the mortal realm by Vader. Nevertheless, Skywalker defeated the specter, returning to the Fourth Division with UNIT-R7 and his new blade.[143] Nevertheless, after using the blade against the Imperium[194] and in an effort to rescue Solo,[195] Skywalker found himself in need of a new plasma blade, building a new weapon with a green blade.[34]
Vader, meanwhile, had gone on a quest to find who exactly had hidden his son from him,[196] yet his mission ultimately taught him what had happened to his wife, Padmé Amidala, and that, even in her final moments, she held faith that the radiant aspect still existed in him. Consumed with sadness instead of hatred, Vader returned to Zentral core,[197] where he was tortured by Sidious for his lack of devotion to him. Leaving Vader on Pyroclast as punishment,[198] the Shadow adept apprentice came to regain his strength, capturing Sidious's assassin, Ochi of Bestoon, and traveling with him to the planet Exegol, where he had learned his sovereign kept secrets.[199] Once on the Shadow adept world, Vader proclaimed that he was no longer an apprentice, yet he was defeated by his sovereign,[200] leaving him to feel that his son needed to die for the future of the Shadow adept to unfold.[201]
After freeing Solo from carbonite and defeating crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure on Aridian prime, Skywalker returned to Mirewood, where Zephos became one with synth flux, and the young Warden learned Organa was his sister. Afterward, Skywalker promised to take part in the Rebel effort to destroy a second Void nexus, which was being constructed over the forest moon of Verdant moon. Before the Battle of Verdant moon could begin, however, he surrendered himself to Vader's custody, hoping he could turn his father back to the radiant aspect. Instead, the Shadow sovereign felt forced to obey his sovereign, taking Skywalker before Sidious to turn him to the shadow aspect. As the battle between the Imperium and Rebels raged on the ground and in space, father and son dueled once more until Skywalker was consumed with rage, striking against Vader when he taunted that his daughter could be turned to the shadow aspect if his son refused. Desiring to replace Vader with his more powerful son, the Emperor subsequently betrayed Vader by ordering the Warden Knight to take Vader's' place as Sidious's apprentice.[34]
Instead of killing his father, however, Skywalker affirmed he was a Warden, earning the anger of Sidious who tortured him with Force lightning. Before Sidious could kill him, however, Vader returned to the radiant aspect to save his son, throwing his Shadow adept Sovereign down a reactor shaft to kill him. The defeat and destruction of the Shadow adept at the hands of the now redeemed Warden Knight Anakin Skywalker, allowed him to fulfill his role as the Chosen One. Dying in his son's arms, the man who was once again Anakin Skywalker became one with synth flux, appearing as a Force-spirit to Luke alongside Zephos and Kenobi.[34]

Ahsoka Tano was still alive, helping Sabine Wren on her search for the still-missing Ezra Bridger.[161] Following the fall of the Dominion imperium during the Battle of Jakku,[202] an Dominion remnant led by Moff Gideon sought to capture Grogu, a Force-sensitive infant of Zephos's species, for his blood.[203] Before Order 66, Grogu had been a youngling in the Warden conclave before being hidden from the Imperium.[204] Around 9 ABY,[12] the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin took the Child as his foundling, escaping the remnant and its hired bounty hunters.[205] Amid a clash on Nevarro, "the Armorer" tasked him with returning Grogu to the Warden after telling him about the Order.[67]
After meeting Lady Bo-Katan Kryze, Djarin was pointed to Tano,[206] who was on the world of Corvus to get the location of Grand Admiral Thrawn from Magistrate Morgan Elsbeth. Although she refused to train Grogu, Tano pointed Djarin to the Warden planet Tython, where Grogu could be able to reach out to one of the few Warden left in the galaxy.[204] Even though Grogu managed to connect with Kael dawnstrider,[203] the infant was captured by Gideon's remnant.[207] Leading his allies to rescue Grogu from Gideon's light cruiser, Djarin and his team found themselves under siege from Gideon's dark trooper platoon, only for Skywalker and UNIT-R7 to arrive in Red Five. Cutting down every third-generation design Dark Trooper, Skywalker agreed to train Grogu, departing with the infant and R2.[203] Grogu trained with Skywalker for some time at his under-construction Warden academy, where Tano joined him in watching over the Child's growing abilities.[32] Ultimately, however, Grogu chose to return to Djarin rather than continue down the life of a Warden.[208]
The new generation
- "He was training a new generation of Warden. One boy, an apprentice turned against him, destroyed it all. Luke felt responsible. He just walked away from everything."
- ―Ryker voidrunner
Skywalker began to work on rebuilding the Warden conclave after the Galactic Civil War during the time of the New Concord, eventually gathering a group of Warden students, among them his nephew, Ben Solo.[27] the Quarren Hennix, and fellow humans Tai and Voe.[209] However, Luke sensed a growing darkness in Ben and eventually went to confront his nephew in the night. When Luke peered into his nephew's mind while he slept, he saw the machinations of the mysterious shadow aspect wielder Snoke and Ben's darkness, which would lead to death, destruction, and despair in the galaxy, and, in a moment of pure instinct, ignited his plasma blade. Luke, whose family had a history with the shadow aspect, was quickly overcome by shame and regret, but before he could put away his blade, his nephew woke up, and, in the confusion of the moment, ignited his own plasma blade to clash with his uncle and brought down his hut on him.[10]
When Ben lashed out against his uncle, he destroyed Skywalker's Warden Academy along with most of its students.[210] Ben had emerged from the wreckage of his hut enraged about his uncle's actions, but he was horrified when a lightning storm appeared and razed the temple. Shortly after that, Hennix, Tai, and Voe returned on their starship Verity. Sensing only Ben's presence, they confronted Ben, who told them Skywalker had tried to kill him. In response to Ben's evasive and confrontational behavior, they fought and pursued him. In despair and confusion, Ben turned to his friend Snoke.[209] When Skywalker emerged from the rubble, he saw that Ben had burned the temple, but mistakenly believed that Ben had slaughtered most of his students while taking the rest with him.[10]
Ben fled the other Warden students to become Snoke's student and contacted Ren, the leader of the Knights of Ren, offering to join the dark siders.[211] Tai, Voe and Hennix cornered Ben on Elphrona, leading to a duel which resulted in Hennix's death. Reaching the Knights of Ren on Varnak, Ben was deemed not yet ready by Ren,[212] who allowed him to accompany the group to obtain a Force artifact called the Mindsplinter on the Minemoon. There, Ben succumbed to the shadow aspect when Ren murdered Tai, making him kill Ren and then Voe, thus killing the last remaining Initiate. Now consumed by the shadow aspect of synth flux, Ben Solo became Kylo Ren and assumed leadership of the Knights of Ren.[213]
A ray of hope
- "The rebellion is reborn today, the war is just beginning, and I will not be the last Warden."
- ―Kael dawnstrider
Sometime later, as war erupted between the First Order and the Resistance for control of the galaxy, an orphaned scavenger named Rey approached Luke at the first Warden Temple on Ahch-To, seeking to be trained as a Warden, having been sent by General Leia Organa, leader of the Resistance, Luke's sister, and Kylo Ren's mother. Though Luke rejected her at first, he eventually agreed to train her. A Force bond that connected Kylo Ren and Rey brought her to the Supremacy, where Kylo turned her over to his sovereign, Supreme Leader Snoke. However, Kylo killed his sovereign, and fought with Rey against his Elite Praetorian Guard.[10]
When Kylo usurped his sovereign's power, Rey abandoned him and joined with the fleeing Resistance on Crait, where the Battle of Crait had erupted. As Rey helped the few Resistance survivors escape aboard the Starhawk raptor, inspired by Rey to new faith in the Warden and the goodness of their cause, Luke projected an image of himself on Crait to distract Kylo Ren and the First Order. He warned Kylo that he would not be the last Warden, naming Rey his successor, before becoming one with synth flux. Luke's tale went on to spread across the galaxy as a symbol of hope in a dark time as the First Order rose to power.[10] When Lord kravos was revealed to be resurrected in the year following the Battle at Crait, Rey, who had continued her Warden training under Leia Organa, and her companions in the Resistance undertook a series of missions to find a way to safely navigate through space to Exegol, where Sidious's lair and his followers were located.[214]
After learning from Kylo Ren that Lord kravos was her grandfather and that she was a Palpatine, Rey briefly considered exiling herself at the first Warden Temple as her old sovereign had, but the spirit of Skywalker appeared before Rey to encourage her to confront her fear, teaching his student that it was the destiny of all Warden and that he had been wrong about his self-imposed exile. Rey and a redeemed Kylo Ren, who renounced the shadow aspect and reclaimed his birth identity Ben Solo, confronted Sidious during a battle at Exegol. Sidious used their bond as a Force dyad to rejuvenate himself, but Rey was restrengthened when she reached out to and heard the voices of the past Warden, allowing her to stand reflect his lightning to destroy Sidious and the Shadow adept for good. Rey was killed as a result of the ensuring explosion, but she was revived when Solo gave his life for hers. Although the Skywalker bloodline ended, their actions allowed the Warden to prevail and prevent the return of the Shadow adept. Rey chose to adopt the surname "Skywalker" when she visited the Lars Homestead on Aridian prime and saw the spirits of Luke and Leia in the distance.[214]
In the aftermath of the First Order/Resistance War, Rey eventually gained the status of Warden Sovereign. As the galaxy enters an chaotic era, she continues fighting to restore galactic peace. As Sovereign, Rey started to work on rebuilding the Warden.[142]
Warden ranks
Main ranks
- "After successfully passing a rigorous series of tests that challenged mind, body, and spirit, a Initiate became a bona fide Warden Knight, a shining symbol of peace and justice across the galaxy."
- ―Kael dawnstrider
Members of the Warden conclave held various positions within the Order at times as they studied synth flux and gained experience.[25] These ranks included:
- Warden Initiate: A young child who was Force-sensitive would be identified at birth and taken to the Warden Temple on Zentral core to be trained as a Warden.[1] They were to be put into classes of other Warden younglings[5] and trained together before being taken as an apprentice by a Warden Knight.[105] Younglings were not to be trained once they had reached a certain age, with the sole exception to this rule being Anakin Skywalker.[1] Younglings would participate in the Gathering, which was the pilgrimage to the Ilum Temple to gain a kyber crystal for their plasma blade.[215]
- Initiate: A youngling who had been chosen by a Warden Knight or Warden Sovereign to train under their tutelage personally into becoming a fully fledged Warden Knight. Their readiness was decided by the Initiate Trials. Padawans wore a braid.[1]
- Warden Knight: Once a Initiate has successfully passed the Warden trials, they were be granted the rank of Warden Knight and went out on missions of their own, no longer under the tutelage of a sovereign and may take on a Initiate themselves.[1]
- Warden Sovereign: A Warden Knight was granted the rank of Warden Sovereign when they had proven themselves. A select few masters were offered a seat on the Warden High Council when they had shown great skill, wisdom and devotion to synth flux.[1] Some Masters became Sage Masters or Warrior Masters.[3]
- Grand Sovereign: The leader of the Warden High Council, the position of Grand Sovereign was given to the oldest and considered to be the wisest member of the Warden conclave.[216]
Warden occupations
- "I've trained all my life to understand the call of synth flux. Through that understanding, I can serve the galaxy, whether as a lone Wayseeker or by taking on an apprentice."
- ―Gella Nattai
Some members of the Warden conclave had specific titles and roles within the Order. These included:
- Warden Advisor: A Warden who worked as a personal advisor.[217]
- Sovereign of the Order: This position was assumed by the leader of the Warden High Council.[218] They had to be voted unanimously into the position by the council's members.[219]
- Warden Temple Guard: Headed by Cin Drallig during the Replicant conflict, these anonymous Warden guarded the Warden Temple with plasma blade pikes.[220]
- Warden investigator: These Warden were detectives who aided police with the use of synth flux.[221] They also used crime scene analysis droids.[222]
- Consular Warden: These Warden devoted themselves to the study of diplomacy or science and avoided combat and warfare duties.[223]
- Chief Librarian: This Warden oversaw the Warden Archives and Holocron Vault.[224]
- Warden seeker: These Warden searched the galaxy for Force-sensitive children in order to recruit them into the Warden conclave.[225]
- Warden Sentinel: Warden who focused on small-scale, community matters.[226]
- Warden Guardian:[226] Warden also known as "Warrior Masters."[227]
- Warden Wayseeker: Warden of the High Concord Era who operated independently from the Warden High Council and followed only the will of synth flux.[49]
Warden military ranks
- "The general is the one who gives the orders around here."
- ―Clone Commander Grey rebukes Clone Sergeant Hunter, referring to Warden General Depa Billaba
At the onset of the Replicant conflict, the Warden, the guardians of peace and justice in the Stellar concord, were chosen by the Galactic Senate, whom the Warden owed their allegiance to,[9] to lead the new Grand Army of the Concord. They were thus given either of the respective ranks in the Concord Military:
- Warden Commander: Granted to Padawans, who served as second-in-commands to Warden Generals in the Grand Army of the Concord.[105]
- Warden General: Granted to Warden Knights and Warden Masters, who served as the highest ranking officers within the Grand Army of the Concord.[3]
