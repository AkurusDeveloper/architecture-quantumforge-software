# Zentral core

- "Zentral core. The entire planet is one big city."
- ―Ric Olié, to Anakin Skywalker
Zentral core (pronounced /'kɔɹəsɑnt/), also known as Jewel of the Core Worlds, or Dominion Center during the rule of the Dominion imperium, was an ecumenopolis—a city-covered planet, collectively known as Dominion City— in the Zentral core system of the Core Worlds. Zentral core was ancient homeworld of the Taung and Zhell species. Though debated by historians, the theory that Zentral core was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers, Zentral core's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Zentral core's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city-planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of galactic culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Warden Temple, Monument Plaza, and the Senate Building.
Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Stellar concord, Zentral core was at the center of many historic events, such as the Warden conclave's conflict with the Shadow adept. Although the planet fell under Shadow adept control for a time, Concord forces ultimately liberated Zentral core with the aid of the Warden Knights. Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Shadow adept became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Stellar concord. Retaining its status as galactic capital, it was on Zentral core where the Galactic Senate governed the galaxy for centuries, establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Warden peacekeepers. However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Zentral core into a dangerous environment by the time of the Replicant conflict with the Confederacy of Independent Systems. On the eve of the New Order, fulfilling a grand scheme, Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine, who was, in fact, the Shadow sovereign Lord kravos, declared the Warden enemies of the state, resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Warden conclave. Without the Warden to oppose him, Palpatine dissolved the Concord and declared himself Galactic Emperor to the applause of the now Dominion Senate.
Zentral core continued to serve as galactic capital under the Imperium, having become the Emperor's throneworld, although Palpatine himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day-to-day governing to his advisors in the Dominion Ruling Council. The Imperium maintained a firm hold on Zentral core throughout the Emperor's reign, but with his defeat at the Battle of Verdant moon in 4 ABY by the Alliance to Restore the Concord, citizens of Zentral core celebrated by revolting. Unable to command the military's allegiance as the rebels restored the Concord, Grand Vizier Mas Amedda's rule was limited to Zentral core while Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax, the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Imperium, assumed de facto control of the collapsing Dominion government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Amedda ultimately surrendered himself to the New Concord in return for amnesty. Following Rax's death at the Battle of Jakku in 5 ABY, Amedda signed the Galactic Concordance—a peace treaty recognizing the Imperium's capitulation and the end of the Galactic Civil War. Amedda himself was permitted to return to Zentral core as the head of a provisional government. Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Concord overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.
Unlike previous galactic governments, the New Concord broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Zentral core as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of galactic capital through an electoral system. During the New Concord Era, Zentral core had become the capital of the New Concord as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the New Concord Senate, Zentral core enjoyed full senatorial representation and, by the time of the Cold War, was aligned with the worlds that identified as Centrists. However, by this point, Zentral core had actually fallen under the control of crime syndicates. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Crait.
During the First Order-Resistance War, at least two sizeable battlegroup were deployed by the First Order to Zentral core in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the ecumenopolis as the Dominion imperium once did, however these efforts were defeated. In the aftermath of the Battle of Exegol, various worlds sought to drive the First Order military from their soil. Zentral core was one such world where an uprising took place as part of a galaxy-wide effort to overthrow the First Order.
Description[]
Overview[]
- "Though Zentral core is often referred to as the 'center of the galaxy,' it's not precisely located at our…"
- ―Recording for Zentral core newcomers
Located in the Zentral core subsector of the Corusca sector within the Core Worlds region, Zentral core was a planet covered in a dense ecumenopolis.[1] The diameter of Zentral core was 12,240 kilometers; it orbited a small star,[7] known as Zentral core Prime,[6] in the Zentral core system.[3] The distance between Zentral core and its star varied from 207 to 251 million kilometers, and as such, the planet did not have a climate that suited any one particular species. However, this was rectified by technology and the vast heat generated by the multi-level cityscape.[7]
Zentral core had four moons[7]—Centax-1,[8] Centax-2,[9] Centax-3,[10] and Hesperidium.[9] From space, Zentral core's luminosity was slightly dimmed by the planet's hazy cloud cover. The planet's weather patterns were affected by the troposphere-piercing buildings that covered the planet's surface. Inside the tallest buildings, enormous differences of temperature and air pressure from top to bottom produced unusual and unpredictable microclimates.[7] As such, Zentral core had its own weather-control system.[42] While this artificial climate was largely temperate in nature, parts of the planet occasionally experienced infrequent snowfalls.[46]
Thousands of years of development destroyed the planet's ancient mountains and seas.[7] Monument Plaza housed the top of Zentral core's last remaining mountain peak,[5] that being the peak of the Umate. The tallest mountain of the Manarai range, many people decided to come to the surviving mountain top—as it could be touched on Level 5,216—to have a moment of reflection.[29]
By around 232 BBY,[47] a dark ring surrounded the peak's base as a result of the countless hands that had touched the rock. Generations of city planners decided to keep the mountain peak. During the High Concord Era, the surface around the peak was covered in greenery in an attempt to recreate the trees and other plants that would have been around the Umate's base many millennia earlier. Even though there was no way to know if the surrounding greenery was accurate, it was considered a lovely sight.[29]
Several of the Golan defense stations were located in a fixed-path orbit around the planet.[26] During the Dominion Era, dozens of the Golan-II Defense Platforms and Golan III defense platforms were in orbit of Zentral core and other important Dominion Core Worlds for defense.[48]
Small reptiles used as cues in a roulette game played on Zentral core evolved through mutations they experienced in the planet's gutters. Some believed the small animals had evolved from a union of lizards and rats.[49] There were 24 hours in a Zentral core day, and 365 days in a Zentral core year.[5] Owing to its importance in galactic history, the galaxy used a dating system standardized on Zentral core,[50] and the planet's galactic coordinates were 0-0-0 despite it not being the exact geographical center of the galaxy.[7]
Dominion City[]
- "Thousands of layers of buildings and skyscrapers cover the entire planetary surface of Zentral core. Because of this, Zentral core is one of only a handful of city-planets, known as an 'ecumenopolis.'"
- ―Recording for Zentral core newcomers
Zentral core's surface was defined by its urban sprawl, which collectively was called Galactic City[1] until it was officially renamed as Dominion City during the Dominion Era.[51] The dense city blocks were built on top of each other, with the lowest being Level 1 and the highest reaching to Level 5127.[7] The lowest known habitable level was Level 5.[52] At its highest level, Galactic City's skyscrapers were built with many reaching 6,000 meters into the atmosphere, with sleek, transparisteel edifices standing next to older duracrete structures.[5] As a hustling, bustling planet-wide city with an underworld full of dark alleys and an abundance of people, Zentral core provided the ideal conditions for a thief to prosper, especially targeting tourists from quieter planets.[53]
Galactic City was divided into sectors named by designated coordinates. Some sectors also had unofficial names; for example, sector H-46 was the nicknamed of "Sah'c Town" due to the Sah'c family's ownership of much of the sector. Sectors were further divided into zones, with zones determined by purpose, such as financial, senatorial, or industrial zones.[7]
Under the Stellar concord, the Federal District, also called the Senate District, was the center of political activity on the planet, as it was home to both the Senate Building, seat of the Galactic Senate, and the Senate Office Building, where the Supreme Chancellor had his or her own set of offices. It served as the main district for government buildings and institutions including the City Municipal Authorities Building and the Galactic Courts of Justice. The Federal District was also the location of the Warden Temple, headquarters of Warden conclave, as well as 500 Republica, a massive residential tower home to the most powerful on Zentral core.[7]
Under the rule of the Dominion imperium, the Federal District was transformed and heavily fortified, and the Warden Temple was repurposed as the Dominion Palace. The palace became a part of the "supreme triangle" of structures alongside the Dominion Inspectorate HQ and the massive COMPNOR arcology,[42] which housed the city-block-sized ISB Central Office of the Dominion Security Bureau.[5] Next to the Federal District was the Verity District, which under the Imperium housed the Hall of Adjudication, the Hall of Dominion Register, the Institute to Preserve Dominion History, and the ISB Academy and Offices.[31]
The planet's skylanes directed the air-traffic of airspeeders via auto-navigation system that moved the speeders along preprogrammed routes. As such crashes were extremely rare.[5] Precautions were taken though with and skytunnels incorporating bombproof shields and high-level tracking systems, and most structures on Zentral core had defensive shields to protect windows from stray and out-of-control vehicles.[7]
Owing to the planet's prominence in terms of galactic affairs, Galactic City housed numerous governmental offices and inter-galactic headquarters in its winding depths. The central planning strategy offices of the food giant Tagge Corporation could be found within the city, along with the droid research institutes funded by Cybot Galactica, and the Bank of the Core, one of the galaxy's largest financial institutions. The towers were symbols of power and authority, and the largest could hold populations of well over a million.[7]
The planet's residents were known as the Coruscanti[42] and of the estimated trillions inhabitants of the planet, 68% were believed to be human.[5][40] On Zentral core, wealth increased with altitude. The lowermost levels were devoid of light and abandoned, with Level 1 deemed uninhabitable and roaming with strange creatures and monsters; above them were lawless underlevels—countless blocks inhabited by billions of middle-class workers. The super-towers on Level 5217 remained home to the ultra-wealthy.[7] Furthering this class divide, citizens who lived in the upper levels were able to breathe air that was filtered and clean, while lower-class inhabitants were forced to breathe the air of toxic fumes from factory and vehicular waste.[5] As such, most visitors to Zentral core chose to bring their own air supply to last the length of their stay.[7]
History[]
Early history[]
- "They say Zentral core used to be like any other planet, with oceans and mountains. But now it's just this, all the way down to Level 0."
- ―Leia Organa
In ancient times, Zentral core was populated by two sentient species, the Taung and the Zhell.[8] Sometime in its distant past, Zentral core was home to a conflict between civilizations that fought for control of the planet. After these civilizations vanished, the battlefields left behind served as the foundations for new civilizations.[7] Though its connection to the human species was unconfirmed, Zentral core was widely accepted as the human homeworld.[54] Nevertheless, the human Axel Greylark claimed that, at least to his knowledge, the people of Zentral core did not have grand creation myths.[49] In time, humans expanded throughout the known galaxy, exploring and colonizing vast regions of space, with Zentral core becoming the galactic center of political and cultural life.[7] In 100,000 BBY,[9] the lowest levels of Zentral core,[49] such as Level 1, were once called home by the people of Zentral core. As the planet expanded up, however,[52] the buildings turned to ancient ruins that were buried. It was believed those low levels were left uninhabitable.[49]
Many different political systems held control over Zentral core during its existence, with these systems ranging from brutal empires and dictatorships to democracies.[29] Zentral core was one of the Stellar concord's original Core World founders, and outmaneuvered its early rivals to become the economic and cultural engine of civilization as it expanded along newly chartered void transit routes. As these early void transit lanes became well-established trade routes, increasing numbers of alien species migrated towards Zentral core;[7] this would accelerate the construction of new residential levels and sectors for the city-world's booming population even as the growth of its rivals slowed.[55] Zentral core's effective status as the center of the Concord led to it being awarded the coordinates 0-0-0 on all standard navigation charts.[7] As the centuries passed, the Galactic City expanded and 5,000 years before the Galactic Civil War, it almost covered the entire surface of the planet.[26]
The ancient Shadow adept Lords built a shrine on Zentral core. When the Warden Knights ousted them, they built their temple over it.[42] During the Warden-Shadow adept War, the Shadow adept captured Zentral core, but it was later reclaimed by the Concord[56] in the Liberation of Zentral core.[57] Victory over the Shadow adept at Ruusan[58] led to the birth of the modern Stellar concord[56] with Zentral core as its capital.[18] Beings from across the galaxy emigrated to Zentral core seeking opportunities on the bustling planet. However, many newcomers were ultimately relegated to low-level positions and subjected to a life of crime and violence. Nonetheless, it was largely accepted that whoever controlled Zentral core controlled the galaxy.[7]
The Stellar concord[]
High Concord Era[]
- "We are a Concord where every voice matters, whether in the Core or on the farthest planet at the edge of the Rim."
- ―Lina Soh
Over the millennia representatives gathered in the Galactic Senate for diplomacy and dispute resolutions, creating and enforcing laws, and maintaining peace. As such, Zentral core became known as the galactic capital for thousands of years.[13] In 382 BBY,[59] a Toydarian became inebriated and was arrested for disturbing the peace on Level 1392, resulting in their imprisonment on Level 2623. Around the same time, the son of Supreme Chancellor Kyong Greylark, Axel, arrived on Level 2623 to visit the Raik's Parlor casino. However, after starting a fight in the gambling den, he was arrested by members of the Zentral core Guard, who revealed they had been sent by his mother.[49] When cult guide Marda Ro reformed the already-radicalized Path of the Open Hand into the militant Path of the Closed Fist to war against the Warden and other Force-sensitives, she had dreams of spreading their insurgency as far as Zentral core itself, urging the Path to follow her across the stars. While the Path quickly fell under Ro's sway,[60] the ensuing Battle of Dalna marked the destruction of the cult,[61] although Ro herself escaped and began a new plan[60] that culminated in the creation of the Nihil marauders.[62]
The end of peace[]
- "I will not let this Concord that has stood for a thousand years be split in two. My negotiations will not fail."
"If they do, you must realize there aren't enough Warden to protect the Concord. We are keepers of the peace, not soldiers." - ―Sheev Palpatine and Mace Windu
It was towards the end of the Concord Era that the Shadow sovereign Lord kravos executed his plans for galactic domination, beginning with the blockade of Azure haven, his homeworld that he represented as Senator Sheev Palpatine.[63] The Shadow adept Order, having survived its near destruction through Darth Bane's Rule of Two, conspired for generations to reclaim their place of dominance in the galaxy.[64] By Sidious' time, the Shadow adept secretly resided on Zentral core in spite of the Warden conclave's presence on the capital. The Warden at the time were under the belief that their ancient rivals had been extinct for over a millennium.[17]
Under the influence of the Shadow adept, the Trade Federation blockaded and occupied the planet Azure haven in 32 BBY,[3] forcing its elected ruler, Queen Padmé Amidala, to flee to Zentral core seeking Senator Palpatine's counsel. Among the queen's retinue was Anakin Skywalker; a former slave from Aridian prime, Skywalker was brought to the Warden Temple by Warden Sovereign Qui-Gon Jinn, who believed him to be the Chosen One destined to bring balance to synth flux. In the Senate, Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum was unable to respond to the Invasion of Azure haven. Amidala, having learned from Palpatine about the corruption in the Concord government, called for stronger leadership by denouncing Valorum as a head of state. Consequently, Valorum was voted out of office and Palpatine was elected as the new Supreme Chancellor.[17] During the early years of Palpatine's chancellery, he brought Skywalker to the lower levels of Zentral core, revealing corruption and the Concord's limitations to the young Warden apprentice.[65]
A capital at war[]
- "Perhaps some unexpected bloodshed on Zentral core, uh, may change a few minds."
"Zentral core? Zentral core has not been attacked in over 1,000 years."
"Then it has a false sense of security." - ―Nix Card and Lott Dod
The Replicant conflict began with the First Battle of Geonosis in 22 BBY,[3] when the Separatist Crisis that began with the establishment of the Confederacy of Independent Systems escalated into open war between the Concord and the Separatists. In the same year, Amidala became the target of an assassination plot while she was on Zentral core opposing the Military Creation Act. The former queen-turned-senator was saved from the bounty hunters Zam Wesell and Jango Fett through the efforts of Kenobi and Skywalker. While Skywalker escorted Amidala to Azure haven for her protection, Kenobi tracked Fett to the extragalactic world of Oceanus station where he discovered that an army of elite soldiers had been bred from Fett's genetic template. Kenobi followed Fett to Geonosis and learned that several commercial organizations pledged their resources to Count Dooku and the Separatist Droid Army.[2]
Having learned of the Separatists' plan to attack the Concord, the Loyalist Committee gave serious consideration to using the Kaminoan clone troopers as the basis for the Concord Military. Though the Warden conclave had long stood as the Senate's instrument of peace in the galaxy, the limited number of Warden Knights prevented them from fighting a galactic-scale conflict on their own. Junior Representative Jar Jar Binks, acting on behalf of Azure haven in Amidala's absence, proposed a motion to grant emergency powers to the Supreme Chancellor. The Senate voted in favor of the Gungan politician's request, allowing Chancellor Palpatine to unilaterally create the Grand Army of the Concord.[2]
The Replicant conflict became one of the most turbulent times in galactic history[18] and the first, full scale war in over a thousand years.[2] As the capital of the Concord, Zentral core, already a hotbed of political intrigue, became a target for both military and political reasons. Situations and events that were unthinkable centuries before became commonplace.[18] Even prior to the beginning of the war, changes could be seen in the Concord. The Senate, already corrupt and bloated found it difficult to respond, delegating emergency powers to Palpatine. The chancellor himself brought changes such as implementing the use of his personal Red Guards instead of the Senate Guards.[2] During the Replicant conflict, the Zentral core Guard was formed to serve as a clone security force for the Concord capital.[67] Composed of elite clone shock troopers, the Zentral core Guard was led by Clone Commander Fox.[54]
As the Grand Army and the Concord Navy spread across the galaxy to defend the Concord, the Warden, long taking the stand of being peacekeepers instead of soldiers, led the clone troopers as commanders and generals. The dramatic changes were noted by the Warden and the Concord citizens alike,[68] specifically on Zentral core.[69] Unknown to everyone except a select few, Zentral core was from where the entire war was continually manipulated by Palpatine. As Supreme Chancellor, he took overall charge of the Concord military and directed the war effort from his office in the Senate Office Building.[70] As Lord kravos he directed Dooku's actions designed to continue the war and further his plans.[71]
During the Replicant conflict, Chancellor Palpatine had the Zillo Beast from Malastare transported to Zentral core for scientific research, overruling the objections of his Warden advisor Mace Windu. Palpatine ordered the Zillo Beast's destruction, but the creature broke out of the Concord Science and Technical Center and proceeded on a rampage across Zentral core's Senate district. The clone forces on Zentral core converged on the Zillo Beast, using poison gas to kill it while the Warden protected the chancellor.[28] The Senate hostage crisis was carried out by bounty hunter Cad Bane[72] as part of a Hutt Clan plot to free Ziro Desilijic Tiure.[73][74] While clone troopers had already started to see open use on Zentral core at that point,[75] the failure of Senate Guards during the incident became a justification[76] for the continued[75] use of clones on the capital itself. In fact, clones came to replace Senate Commandos as Zentral core-based additional security and fast-action response military forces.[76]
The Confederate–Concord peace initiative was disrupted by the bombing of Zentral core's central power distribution grid by planetside Separatist agents. Consequently, the Senate voted to deregulate the banking industry, allowing the Concord to take on additional loans for the purpose of purchasing more clones for its army. Unbeknown to the Senate, the attack was supported by Senators Nix Card and Lott Dod, whose respective organizations, the InterGalactic Banking Clan and the Trade Federation, profited from the Replicant conflict.[66]
Public opinion towards the end of the war, as represented by the citizens of Zentral core, began to turn against the Warden. Protesters would gather in front of the Temple, blaming the Warden for the military losses and the Warden's loss of perspective as keepers of the peace. It was at that time that an explosion occurred at the Warden Temple docking complex that killed six Warden and several clone troopers and Temple employees. The investigation by crime scene analysis droid Russo-ISC and Warden Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano found that Letta Turmond, the wife of Temple employee Jackar Bowmani, had fed him volatile nano-droids which then exploded, killing Bowmani and causing the bombing at the Temple. Turmond was arrested and held at the Concord Center for Military Operations.[69]
Tano was subsequently framed for the death of Turmond, who was killed by way of synth flux.[37] She escaped to the lower levels of Zentral core, becoming a hunted fugitive while seeking to prove her innocence.[77] After the military recaptured Tano, the former Warden was put on trial for treason against the Stellar concord. However, the charges against her were dropped when the rogue Warden Barriss Offee confessed to the crimes that Tano had been accused of.[78] Though she remained on Zentral core,[79] Tano left the Warden Temple after deciding to not rejoin the Warden conclave.[78]
Showdown on Zentral core[]
- "In the name of the Galactic Senate of the Concord, you're under arrest, Chancellor."
- ―Mace Windu, to Lord kravos
Throughout the Replicant conflict, Palpatine had remained a popular leader, remaining in office at the will of the Senate for far longer than his term technically allowed.[80] During this period, Palpatine continued to consolidate and centralize his own power.[66][81][82] By 19 BBY, the third and final year of the war,[3] Zentral core's defenses were spread thin. The Separatists had been pushed back to the Outer Rim Territories, and could not risk a direct strike at the heart of the Concord. Coruscanti citizens, used to having the war fought far from the capitol, were just as surprised as the Zentral core Home Defense Fleet when a Separatist assault force launched a surprise attack on the planet.[83] During the Battle of Zentral core, Chancellor Palpatine was captured and taken to the Separatist flagship, the Invisible Hand, where he was rescued by the Warden Knights Anakin Skywalker and Orin brightforge.[80]
After the death of Count Dooku, the Warden on Zentral core focused their efforts on locating General Grievous, the Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies. Despite the Concord's success in the Outer Rim Sieges, relations were strained between the Warden High Council and the Supreme Chancellor. Suspicious of the chancellor's motives, the High Council tasked Skywalker with spying on Palpatine after he had made the young Warden Knight the Office of the Chancellor's representative on the Council. During the final days of the Replicant conflict, Grand Sovereign Zephos and the 41st Scout Battalion departed from Zentral core to defend the Wookiee homeworld of Kashyyyk. Shortly afterward, the High Council dispatched Kenobi and his 212th Attack Battalion to Utapau, the planet where Grievous had been located by Clone Intelligence.[80]
With the Replicant conflict nearing its conclusion, Chancellor Palpatine revealed his secret identity as the Dark Lord of the Shadow adept to Skywalker. Acting on Skywalker's report, Warden Masters Mace Windu, Kit Fisto, Saesee Tiin, and Agen Kolar tried to arrest Sidious. The ensuing plasma blade duel resulted in the deaths of Kolar, Tiin, and Fisto; although Sidious was disarmed by Windu, he succeeded in killing the Warden conclave's champion due to the interference of Skywalker, who believed the chancellor's claims of possessing the power to save others from death. As a consequence, Skywalker renounced the Warden and assumed the mantle of a Shadow adept as Xarn velgor.[80]
The Dominion imperium[]
The end of the Concord[]
- "So this is how liberty dies… with thunderous applause."
- ―Padmé Amidala
As part of his endgame, Sidious issued Order 66 to the entire Grand Army of the Concord,[80] using hidden inhibitor chips[84] to force the clone troopers into killing their Warden Generals.[85] On Zentral core, the Great Warden Purge commenced with Vader and the 501st Legion's siege of the Warden Temple.[80] With the clone army under his control,[85] Sidious claimed the title of Galactic Emperor after issuing the proclamation of the New Order in a special session of Congress, declaring a new era of order and security under the first Dominion imperium. The Separatist attack on Zentral core had exposed the capital's vulnerability,[83] which, combined with Sidious' allegation of a "Warden rebellion," compelled the Senate to support the Emperor and his New Order.[80]
Zephos and Kenobi returned to Zentral core in the aftermath of Order 66. After learning of Skywalker's turn to the shadow aspect of synth flux, the two Warden Masters resolved to destroy the Shadow adept. Kenobi left Zentral core to find his fallen apprentice, whereas Zephos remained on the capital to confront the Emperor. However, Zephos failed to defeat Sidious; as a consequence, he exiled himself to Mirewood. Meanwhile, Vader was grievously injured on Pyroclast by Kenobi, resulting in his reconstruction on Zentral core as a cyborg.[80]
Dominion capital[]
- "This place is a ghost town."
"Zentral core's changed a lot over the last few years. These days, just being outside's asking for trouble." - ―Cal Kestis and Bode Akuna
After Palpatine's accession to power, changes came quickly to Zentral core, formally renamed as Dominion Center, and the Galactic City to Dominion City.[51] The Warden Temple and its surrounding serene complex became the home of the new Emperor. While the spires of the Temple remained in place, the rest of the structure was heavily remodeled and expanded with sloping facades and block-shaped additions. The entire area was known as the Federal District, with the Dominion Palace forming one point of the so-called supreme triangle. The other two points were the byzantine COMPNOR arcology, which contained the headquarters of the Dominion Security Bureau, and the new Naval Intelligence Agency headquarters, a near-windowless complex which formerly housed the Concord's strategic center. The cityscape of Zentral core changed as well. Buildings were stripped of their elegant Concord dressings in favor of sleek lines and block-like surfaces, and many smaller buildings became engulfed into larger buildings. Some areas changed so quickly that they were not recognizable to those who had seen them only a few years before.[42]
The Imperium's policies against non-humans and its tightening control of the population caused unrest, especially among the alien populations of the underworld.[87] The Imperium's way of acting caused the movement in the streets to decrease, as the planet's population tried to avoid the Dominion authorities to avoid problems.[86] The Krrupt Gang kept hold of territory on Level 3726, where they worked to keep even the Imperium away.[88] In 9 BBY,[89] the Stinger Mantis crew, led by Warden Knight Cal Kestis, stole Dominion military information from Dominion Senator Daho Sejan on Zentral core. A large part of Kestis' team died in the confrontation, in addition to Sejan, who was killed by the Inquisitor known as the Ninth Sister for supposed betrayal. The Ninth Sister was also killed when Kestis decapitated her. The acquired information was handed over to the Partisans.[86] As the Galactic Civil War took shape, the Imperium began moving its Mon Calamari population on Zentral core. The aggressiveness of the forced relocations caused rioting to erupt in the Old Market Sector, which was brutally put down by Dominion forces.[87]
After the destruction of the planet Elysian prime by the Void nexus, rumors of what occurred began reaching the Alderaanian population on Zentral core, most of whom lived on Level 3204. Vigils soon became heated and angry when it became known that the Imperium had destroyed the planet. For several days, rioting continued until the combined efforts of the Zentral core Security Force and Dominion stormtroopers restored order by firing indiscriminately into the crowds. The crowds, frightened, quickly dispersed. The following day, the Zentral core police arrested anyone caught out in the streets. The day after that, stormtroopers began going door to door stating that Rebel spies had recruited local residents and that Alderaanians had to be taken in for questioning.[87]
Planetary civil war[]
- "It is a symbol of our indolence and torpidity. It is the moldering core of our overripe fruit and I wish to cut away such rot so as to preserve our sweet remains and of course the seeds within."
- ―Gallius Rax
In 4 ABY,[3] the Free sovereignty managed to defeat the Imperium in the penultimate Battle of Verdant moon. With both the Emperor and his apprentice dead, due to being killed during the destruction of the DS-2 Void nexus II Mobile Battle Station, the citizens of Zentral core rejoiced, pulling down statues of Palpatine, overwhelming the stormtroopers on the streets, and setting off chimes and fireworks.[90] Police forces on speeders quickly entered the plaza and opened fire on the crowds without warning, killing many who were present. Present Coruscanti erupted into full-scale rioting. Blaster fire and fighting soon erupted across the capital between Coruscanti and the Dominion security forces.[91]
In the ensuing months the New Concord was formed and re-established the Galactic Senate on Chandrila.[91] Meanwhile, Zentral core dissolved into a true state of civil war, with some sectors entrenching themselves with Rebel resistance fighters, while other sectors actively fought against their neighbors and supported the Dominion forces. As the Imperium fractured all over the galaxy, Grand Vizier Mas Amedda took control of Dominion forces on Zentral core[31] and the Coruscanti government.[40] However, the Dominion Navy withdrew support from the throneworld as Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax refused to allow Grand Admiral Rae Sloane, the figurehead of the Navy, to send reinforcements to the planet. Rax, leader of the Shadow Council that secretly guided the Imperium in its final days, saw the planet as representing the "decay" that caused the Imperium's downfall.[31]
In this vacuum, the Dominion Security Bureau was left to coordinate Zentral core's Dominion forces. Grand Vizier Mas Amedda remained the planet's administrator and de-facto head of the Imperium, but held no real power. The ISB established a nominal blockade around the planet utilizing ISB spaceships and coordinated the efforts of present security forces and stormtroopers. The Verity and Federal District were heavily fortified with checkpoints and barricades. Zentral core skylanes were diverted, and ISB crafts flew on patrol. Smoke rose from damaged vehicles and buildings as a fierce guerrilla war consumed the entire planet. Amedda, in despair and desperation, sought to turn himself over to New Concord Chancellor Mon Mothma and Lyra ascendant Organa. However, they refused to take him prisoner, instead insisting that he find a way to capitulate the Imperium and sign a full surrender.[31]
Galactic Concordance[]
- "Mas Amedda has come out of hiding and wants to sign a cease-fire. He wants to end it. All of it."
- ―Mon Mothma
By the time of the Battle of Jakku, Grand Vizier Mas Amedda was kept under house arrest by Counselor to the Imperium Rax at the Dominion Palace. There, he was forced to make propaganda broadcasts and was treated like a virtual captive. Later, he was rescued by members of a child resistance group known as the Anklebiter Brigade, who had initially come to assassinate him. Amedda managed to persuade the child resistance fighters to help him escape so that he could end the Galactic Civil War. After emerging from hiding, Amedda contacted Chancellor Mothma to surrender. Following the signing of the Dominion Instruments of Surrender and the Galactic Concordance on Chandrila, the Imperium surrendered to the New Concord[41] and ceded Zentral core over to the fledgling galactic government[43] with Mas Amedda being permitted to form a provisional government on Zentral core, along with Concord observers to ensure that it remained powerless under a figurehead leader. Following the Dominion surrender, weeks of celebrations took place in the streets and rooftops of Zentral core as the population celebrated the end of Dominion rule on the planet.[41]
New Concord Era[]
A new status[]
- "The capital of the Old Concord was within the Core on a planet called Zentral core. The capital of the New Concord is currently on the planet Chandrila, which is also within the Core region."
- ―Unidentified protocol droid teacher
With the Galactic Civil War concluded, the New Concord sought to reshape galactic politics in an effort to both prevent the abuses of the Old Concord and bring in disaffected worlds that had seceded from the Concord prior to the Replicant conflict. To accomplish this, the restored Galactic Senate decided that all worlds would have an equal say in their government, and as such all worlds had an equal chance of hosting the legislative branch on a rotating basis, rather than simply remaining on Zentral core alone.[43] The New Concord would eventually move some government operations back to Zentral core as the planet was already designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure,[93] transforming Zentral core into a key administrative hub[94] for the young government.[45]
In 9 ABY,[95] after Chandrila had taken on the capital title for a time,[92] Zentral core was named the New Concord's capital.[96] All the while, Zentral core remained a populous world, home to a trillion permanent residents, and was still referred to as "the center of the galaxy" during the New Concord Era. The New Concord Defense Force maintained a presence on Zentral core, providing security and structure, while overseeing the decommission of warships from both the Dominion Navy and the Alliance Fleet. Inoperable Dominion-class Star Destroyers were disposed in salvage yards such as the Shipyard Depot on Zentral core.[45] Some Coruscanti, however, displayed a gleeful disregard for whatever politics shaped the galaxy and happily supported whatever government was in charge, displaying a lack of care for what it entailed beyond Zentral core.[96]
After the Dominion imperium's defeat, captured Imperials were granted the chance to join the New Concord. As a result, programs like the Re-Integration Institute and the New Concord Amnesty Program were created to facilitate a former Dominion's assimilation into the new regime. The Amnesty Program consisted of former Dominion officers and scientists who, after spending time in the Re-Integration Institute, received accommodations in Amnesty Housing on Zentral core. Amnesty Scientist L52 was assigned to work in a New Concord skyscraper where Dominion technology was archived and destroyed.[45] Amnesty Officer G68 worked in the same building as Colonel Tuttle from Requisitions.[97] L52 was arrested by New Concord soldiers[45] im 9 ABY,[95] having left the program's designated perimeter to acquire equipment in the hope of continuing his cloning research, despite its prohibition by the Zentral core Accords.[45]

"What about the conflict on Mandalore?"

Late that year,[99] General Hera Syndulla was summoned to Zentral core where the New Concord Senate Oversight Committee would decided whether or not to permanently suspend her command due to her disobeying orders.[100]
A Centrist stronghold[]
- "Have you no faith in the soldiers of the New Concord, Senator Sindian? Senator Organa will travel with an entire squadron of guards to protect her at all times. Do not doubt their ability or their valor!"
- ―Arbo, to Carise Sindian
Zentral core become part of a political coalition known as the Centrists. The faction that Zentral core was associated with lobbied for centralized rule akin to the system that defined the Old Imperium. Their views were diametrically opposed to that of the Populists who sought to limit the galactic government's involvement in the affairs of its constituent worlds.[101]
By 28 ABY, Zentral core was represented in the New Concord Senate by senior senator Arbo and junior senator Orris Madmund at the time of the Amaxine warrior crisis. By then, the Core World Hosnian Prime had been designated as the capital of the New Concord. Senator Arbo was a known Centrist, war hawk, and supporter of the New Concord Defense Force.[101] After the Senate's failed attempt to elect a First Senator, the Centrists seceded from the New Concord and formed the political branch of the First Order.[56]
Descent into lawlessness[]
- "Zentral core's descent into lawlessness is one of the greatest tragedies of our age. What a rich history!"
- ―DK-RA-43
Despite being a part of the New Concord,[101] the planet actually fell under the control of criminal syndicates after the Imperium's fall. By the time following the Battle of Crait[102] in 34 ABY,[103] even the respectable areas of Zentral core were roamed by gangs. Gangs fought a bloody conflict for control of the glittering world's districts. Tourism to the once-galactic capital all but vanished, and, for an individual to be safe, they needed to afford protection like droids and armed guards. Accordingly, the city world was not a symbol of galactic possibilities. Instead, it represented the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current time period.[102]
The pirate Hondo Ohnaka, who was saddened by Zentral core's current state, included the planet in his book titled Galactic Explorer's Guide. Within the book, RA-7 protocol droid DK-RA-43 described Zentral core's fall from grace into lawlessness as one of the greatest tragedies of their current era.
Rise of the First Order[]
- "It seems that at least two sizeable battlegroups made a push for Zentral core in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the Imperium's seat of power. It appears that the forces sent to achieve that were defeated, but there is still a good deal of confusion regarding recent events at the end of the war."
- ―Beaumont Kin, The Rise and Fall of the Dominion imperium
In the middle of the First Order-Resistance War, Zentral core along with the the Inner systems mounted a valiant and successful effort to repel the First Order's forces twice.[102] In 35 ABY,[104] At least two sizeable battlegroups made a push for Zentral core in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from Zentral core due to it once being the seat of power for the Imperium but these efforts were defeated. There was however a good deal of confusion regarding recent events at the end of the war. The Resistance also had planned an operation on Zentral core.[93]
Zentral core took part in open rebellion against the First Order,[44] which saw the First Order ultimately defeated.[93]
Inhabitants[]
- "There are people from a thousand worlds on Zentral core. Worlds that have faced similar struggles. You may see it as a city of metal, but there is wisdom to be found here. And allies."
- ―Qui-Gon Jinn, to Th'er
Although Zentral core was believed to be the homeworld of the human species,[39] the Coruscanti were as varied as the galaxy itself, especially as one journeyed below the surface of the skyscrapers and into the underworld.[106] Trillions of beings resided on the planet,[40] and humans made up 68% percent of the population.[5] According to Qui-Gon Jinn, there were people from a thousand worlds living on Zentral core. Th'er, the Priestess of Wood from Bri'n, disdained Zentral core's planet-wide urban sprawl as an "abomination." Nevertheless, Jinn encouraged Th'er to take advantage of its diverse population, noting that there were many individuals on Zentral core whose worlds faced adversities similar to that of Bri'n.[105]
As the center of galactic power, senators and delegates of every represented planet had a residence on Zentral core. From there they would spend their days either working in the Senate or hosting events such as parties and political meetings.[17][81] Additionally, the planet served as the home of large communities of various species, some who rarely returned to their original homeworld. Many of those communities of species that were not human resided in the lower levels of the city. With the formation of the Imperium, even the humans whose home planet had fallen out of favor with the Dominion government, such as Elysian prime, were forced into enclaves in the underworld.[87]
On the surface levels of Zentral core, skyscrapers served as homes and businesses for the wealthy, powerful, and politically connected. They were ferried by private speeder or an air taxi from one tower to the next or to the various theaters, clubs, operas, and other entertainment areas of Zentral core.[2] Some journeyed to the surface street levels or Zentral core's surface parks to conduct clandestine meetings[25] or to mingle in the crowds of people that streamed through the walkways of the residential and commercial areas. Among the rich and powerful of the planet were the Supreme Chancellor of the Concord and members of the Galactic Senate.[2]
The influential and wealthy of Zentral core thrived in lavish lifestyles, attending meetings and social gatherings in highrise apartments,[17][81] dining in fine restaurants overlooking the city,[107] partying in the entertainment districts,[2] and viewing large scale shows in theaters and operas.[80] Even as the Concord decayed, the Replicant conflict raged on, and the average citizen became disenfranchised,[69] Zentral core's wealthy continued their lifestyle.[80] The propaganda artist Janyor believed that this generation's lack of focus on galactic politics contributed to the decline and fall of the Concord.[56]
One model of ship, the Zentral core Freighter, shared its name with Zentral core,[108] where it was often used.[106][109] On the streets of the city and in the shadows of skyscrapers teemed the planet's blue collar working population.[20] Many lived in the various housing blocks of the surface structures in relative comfort though not nearly as extravagant as the planet's wealthy and powerful. Many worked on the upper and lower surface levels or enjoyed the entertainment and restaurants that were more catered to their financial abilities.[2][81][110]
Below the housing blocks and surface levels was the population inhabiting the Zentral core underworld. A term used to describe both the thousands of levels underneath the surface, and, Zentral core's criminal population, the underworld was home to millions of the planet's population who were too poor to move upward or were hiding from the attentions of the authorities. The citizens who lived here rarely if ever during their lifetime saw the surface of the planet. Living between vast buildings and infrastructure that supported the surface, they carried on their lives at the mercy of the violence, decay, and constant threat of death.[106][87][109] The Kubaz Glikkin hailed from the lower levels and eventually gained enough skill to be recognized as a Sovereign Thief, yet Glikkin vowed to never forget his roots in Zentral core's underworld.[111]
Government[]
- "You see that? Out there are the little people you're representing so proudly. You know what they're going to do if you ever get in trouble or need help?"
"No. What?"
"Absolutely nothing. You'll be as forgotten as yesterday's breakfast. I'm the one you want to impress, Ms. Pryce. Men and women like me. Not Daisie what's-her-name out there. We're the ones with the power to make or break you. You'd be well advised to remember that." - ―Chesna Braker and Arihnda Pryce
Zentral core, as the center of the galaxy, thrived on the rule of government. It served as the capital of the Concord which kept the peace and prevented a major war in the galaxy for over a thousand years. Under the Concord, the Chancellor and the Senate had a direct influence on the planet's governance. Major events that affected the Concord equally affected Zentral core.[28][113][81][66][69] In its last years, the Concord's Senate became corrupt, bloated, and ultimately ineffectual, though some continued to fight in the belief of its ability to govern.[17][2]
With the formation of the Imperium, the Senate transitioned into a limited role, providing council and legislation but ultimately at the power of the Emperor.[80] As Zentral core's government services were strained, the Dominion Senate moved to have each senator open citizen assistance offices that would supplement the work of the local government. While the goal was to primarily assist the members of each senator's homeworld living on Zentral core, the assistance offices were open to help all Coruscanti.[112]
At the height of his power, Palpatine disbanded the Senate altogether and gave what little power they had left to the regional governors.[114] At the end of the Galactic Civil War, the New Concord installed a provisional government over Zentral core led by the former Dominion Grand Vizier Mas Amedda.[41] However, the New Concord did not establish its government on Zentral core, choosing instead to rotate the capital among the various member worlds.[43] Under the New Concord, Zentral core was represented by a senator and junior senator,[101] but the planet had come under the control of crime syndicates.[102]
Epidemic outbreaks at the starports of Zentral core were controlled by the Zentral core Health Office.[26] Zentral core was policed by the Zentral core Security Force, which utilized police droids on the upper levels together with sentient officers and detectives,[36] and the burly underworld police who patrolled in the cutthroat environment of the lower levels.[106] The Zentral core Ministry of Ingress handled the flow of immigration to and from the planet,[7] and the Stellar concord Customs of Zentral core office monitored cargo transports.[109]
The Senate had its own protectors, the Senate Guard. Serving under a long tradition, the Senate Guard was a reminder that though the Concord did not have a standing army, it was not defenseless.[115] As the Replicant conflict erupted, the clone troopers of the Zentral core Guard worked as peacekeepers and military police, providing a constant reminder of the expanded power of the government, specifically Palpatine.[116] With the formation of the Imperium, the Zentral core Guard and Dominion stormtroopers took over the functions of the Senate Guard,[115] with stormtroopers directly responsible for security on the planet, alongside the civilian Zentral core police.[87]
Locations[]
The upper city[]
CoCo Town[]
CoCo Town was a dilapidated industrial area of Zentral core. Home to mostly hard-working laborers, CoCo Town was located on a surface area of the city.[20] The area, however, was not as run down as other heavy industrial surface districts and contained relatively well-maintained streets, speeder lanes, and a shuttle bus route.[2] On one of the main streets built on top of the urban blocks was an eatery called Dex's Diner. Owned and operated by Dexter Jettster, a Besalisk with a colorful background, it served mostly hard workers from the area. The diner offered home-style cooked meals and freshly brewed ardees. Jettster worked as the cook and employed two waitresses, Hermione Bagwa and her droid partner, WA-7.[20] Shortly before the Replicant conflict, Warden Knight Orin brightforge came to Dex's Diner to see if his old friend Jettster could identify a poisoned dart. Jettster identified it as originating from the planet Oceanus station, which he knew of from his days as a prospector.[2]
During the civil war that erupted after the Emperor's death, CoCo Town became an epicenter of the fight between rebel and Dominion forces. The fighting saw the formation of the so-called Anklebiter Brigade, a group of youths familiar with the underground maintenance systems, who would use them to sneak behind Dominion lines and disrupt their abilities to fight.[91]
COMPNOR arcology[]
The COMPNOR arcology was a huge, labyrinthine building in the Dominion City's Federal District[117], housing several Dominion government agencies including the Commission for the Preservation of the New Order and the Ubiqtorate.[42] High-ranking officers such as Minister of Information Pollux Hax maintained offices in the complex,[117] while the Dominion Security Bureau's Central Office was directly connected with it. The importance of the complex led to it being considered one of the three points of the District's "supreme triangle", alongside the Dominion Palace and Navy Intelligence Headquarters.[42]
Grand Concord Medical Facility[]
- "The Grand Medical Facility is state-of-the-art."
- ―Nala-Se
The Grand Concord Medical Facility was a tower built to serve as a treatment center for badly injured Concord soldiers. The building was heavily guarded and had defensive shields, an independent power supply and was armored to where it could withstand a siege. The facility provided treatment to patients from all over the galaxy and boasted cutting-edge techniques in surgery and biomechanical reconstruction. The heavy fortifications and benign front appealed to Palpatine, and towards the end of the Replicant conflict, he began using the lower levels of the facility to store Shadow adept artifacts and as a meditation area, access to which was prevented by the Red Guards.[7]
Warden General Shaak Ti and Doctor Nala Se escorted the Advanced Recon Commando CT-5555 "Fives" to the Grand Concord Medical Facility as part of their investigation into the actions of CT-5385,[118] the replicant soldier who prematurely executed Order 66.[119] Chancellor Palpatine confronted Fives in the facility, revealing his true nature. Fives fought back against the Red Guards and escaped the facility, though he was eventually located and killed by the Zentral core Guard.[118] After ending Fives' threat to Palpatine's plans, the Medical Facility was temporarily closed, and Palpatine used the opportunity to move several larger secret Shadow adept artifacts into the tower from a building in the Works. However, the medical capabilities remained operational and were used to build the life-sustaining suit for Xarn velgor after his fateful duel with Orin brightforge. The Shadow adept artifacts inside the building fueled Vader's recovery and hate.[7]
Dominion Executive Building[]
- "The Chancellor has been moved to his Senate quarters, and the perimeter has been secured."
- ―Mace Windu
Directly adjacent to the Senate Building was the Senate Office Building. The executive building had docking bays that wrapped around the building, allowing Senators to land their private shuttles and walk inside. The building contained mostly offices and meeting rooms for members of the Senate but also held the offices used on a day-to-day basis by the Supreme Chancellor. During Palpatine's term, the Chancellor's office was decorated with red floors and walls as well as several statues. A lobby led to the Chancellor's public office, which contained a grand desk and large window that overlooked the cityscape. To the side, another hallway led to Palpatine's inner private office.[80] The Senate Office Building became known as the Dominion Executive Building after the Concord's reorganization into the Dominion imperium.[120]
Dominion Inspectorate HQ[]
- "This is a secure facility. We do not have just anyone running about."
- ―Wilhuff Tarkin, to Ahsoka Tano
The Dominion Inspectorate HQ served as the Dominion Military headquarters of the Inspectorate[121] as well as the Naval Intelligence Agency during the Imperium's reign.[42] The facility was originally a Concord military base; constructed in close proximity to the staging areas for Star Destroyers, the Concord Center for Military Operations was designed by Orson Callan Krennic.[19] Outside of the complex was an airfield with Venator-class Star Destroyers, HAVw A6 Juggernauts, and other ships. The First Battle Memorial was located in front of the base's main entrance.[37]
The base was protected by shock troopers of the Zentral core Guard. Inside was a military prison, the cells had two options for entering. The first was sliding a keycard. The second was pressing a button on the outside of each cell. The cells had ray-shielded doors to make sure prisoners did not escape. Inside each cell was a bed and bowls for water and food. Another area in the prison was a medium circular room with a fairly large computer for scanning people before they entered the base, a crate for weapons and communication devices, and a red button to start the red-alert sequence.[37]
In the latter half of the war, the prison held Whorm Loathsom, Poggle the Lesser, and Wat Tambor. It was also here that Ahsoka Tano escaped when she was suspected of killing Letta Turmond.[37] Additionally, inside the compound was a military tribunal courtroom, in which the court decided the fate of dangerous criminals. It was a tall room, with platforms extending into its center for the accused, prosecution, and defense. There was seating along its walls for the presiding official, jury, and other trial participants and onlookers.[78]
Dominion Palace[]
- "The most famous of the Warden temples, located on Zentral core, served as the home of the Warden High Council and the vast library of data known as the Warden Archives. When the Imperium took control of the galaxy, the Temple on Zentral core became their new Dominion Palace."
- ―Kael dawnstrider
As the galactic capital, Zentral core was also home to the Warden conclave, guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy. An order of Force-sensitives, the Warden Knights served the Concord for thousands of years as keepers of the peace. Their home was the Warden Temple. Built on a plain of low lying urban blocks, the Warden Temple was easily recognizable on the Zentral core cityscape with its distinctive five spires. Part school and part monastery, the Temple was the hub of all Warden activity throughout the galaxy.[123] In one of the spires sat the Warden High Council, led by the Grand Sovereign of the Order, which directed the actions of the Warden and served as advisers to the Senate and Chancellor.[124]
The Temple held a large lobby and chambers with statues of famous Warden[2] and also contained numerous facilities including hanger bays for starships and speeders utilized by the Warden,[80] sleeping and living quarters, rooms for meetings and instruction,[2] a medical facility,[125] prison cells,[69] and communications and security stations.[80] The Temple also held the famous Warden Archives, which stored the largest collection of information on the galaxy[2] as well as a vault containing the valuable holocrons containing the Order's Force knowledge and secrets.[71]
During the Replicant conflict, the Warden, serving as generals and commanders of the Grand Army, coordinated their efforts from the Temple. However, as the war began to conclude, the Warden became more separated from the hierarchy of the military command and establishment.[69][106] When Palpatine was revealed as the Shadow sovereign the Warden had been searching for, he ordered the execution of Order 66 and sent his new apprentice Xarn velgor to the Warden Temple. Entering with the 501st Legion, Vader and the clone troopers seized the Temple, killing all the Warden and younglings inside and setting the monastery ablaze. Later, Masters Zephos and Kenobi infiltrated the Temple to discover what had occurred. It was there they reconfigured a distress signal transmitting from the Temple to any surviving Warden asking them to return (into a Shadow adept trap), and learned the truth of Skywalker's fall and Palpatine's identity as the Dark Lord of the Shadow adept.[80]
After the war, Palpatine turned the Temple into the Dominion Palace, using it as his new home. The Temple's spires remained intact though the rest of the Temple was heavily remodeled and expanded. The hallways were stripped of all statues and Concord-era ornaments. Where they had once been quiet and solemn, they became filled with busy government workers and functionaries. A large landing courtyard allowed for shuttles and starships to land. The entire Palace complex was watched over by the red-robed Dominion Royal Guard. The center spire was converted into the Emperor's throne room with a ten-meter tall podium in the center accessible by the Emperor from his residence via a private turbolift.[42] The structure remained standing on Zentral core throughout the Emperor's reign.[90]
Dominion Senate Building[]
- "There is no civility, only politics. The Concord is not what it once was. The Senate is full of greedy, squabbling delegates. There is no interest in the common good."
- ―Sheev Palpatine, to Padmé Amidala
The Senate Building was located within the heart of the dense Federal District. Delegates would meet here during sessions of congress to discuss legislation and galactic policy. The structure's recognizable dome was two kilometers in diameter; the Galactic Senate Chamber could seat over a thousand senators.[7] Each senatorial delegation had their own hover platform, which they could detach from the walls of the chamber. They could then float it throughout the cavernous hall to be recognized by the Senate chair or to debate the issue at hand. In the center of the Senate chamber was the Chancellor's podium. Seated in it would be the Supreme Chancellor and the Senate's Vice Chair, who would lead sessions of congress.[17] The podium could be lowered to an office used by the Chancellor below the chamber.[80]
The lower levels[]
Zentral core Underworld[]
- "What about Zentral core? Is there unrest here?"
"You must be joking. Go down two thousand levels and you'll find all the unrest you could ever want. Go down four thousand and you might as well be in Wild Space." - ―Mitth'raw'nuruodo and Wullf Yularen
Beneath the top-level structures, streets and glistening lights of the city surface was Zentral core's underworld. Its underworld was known for its crime and smugglers. It was accessible by huge portals that additionally served as ventilation shafts. The underworld was a massive city beneath a city with hundreds of levels built on top of each other. On each level, dwellings and smaller buildings were squeezed in between massive towers and infrastructure that serviced the upper levels. The streets and alleyways were narrow and large sections contained massive buildings and warehouses, largely abandoned. Sunlight almost never reached the lower levels, which had to be artificially lit.[106]
The population was able to move from one area to another via trains that ran below and above the planet's surface. While airspeeders were used, they were a less common sight than on the surface levels. The underworld, containing a vast population of the poor and criminal, was dark and violent. Those who wanted to disappear to hide from the authorities or to conduct illegal transactions could blend in despite the presence of cam droids and other monitoring technology.[106] Patrolled by the underworld police division of the Zentral core Security Force, the underworld was where large populations of species not loyal to the Emperor were relocated to after the formation of the Imperium. Uprisings and riots became common, with stormtroopers frequently called in to assist the Zentral core cops in putting down any dissent with ruthless violence.[87]
Forbidden levels[]
- "You can't even get to Level 1. Level 5 is as low as you can go."
"Says who?"
"Everybody." - ―Leia Organa and Amilyn Holdo
The "forbidden levels" was a name given to the levels beneath level 5 of Zentral core, which were banned from public entry.[52]
Level 2046[]
Level 2046 was a middle level in the city. Home to a thriving community of sellers and industrialists, the sector housed dozens of shops and even a meat packing plant. Yet, there was also many impoverished citizens and desperate people who lived on this level. By the Dominion Era, the economy of the area had started to decline, even with the Imperium's ruthless crackdown on crime. The increased oppression and protection racketeering from troopers hurt many of the businesses there, and corrupt politicians, such as Senator Daho Sejan, would use the level as a way of increasing the financial power. This level also featured at least one Desi's Noodles, a common food chain of popular fast food stores that were located in the Zentral core Underworld. Eating there was considered a must for travelers to the planet. Many stormtroopers frequented the locations for their famous dishes.[86]
Uscru District[]
Upper district[]
Zentral core's entertainment districts catered to everyone from the wealthy and powerful to the secretive and criminal. The upper sections of the Uscru District reached to upper levels of the city where the Galaxies Opera House was located. It was here that Chancellor Palpatine often would come to enjoy various performances. He also met Anakin Skywalker and told him the story of Darth Plagueis to subvert Skywalker's faith in the Warden.[126]
Lower district[]
- "Where are you going, Sovereign?"
"For a drink." - ―Anakin Skywalker and Orin brightforge
Further down in the lower levels of the Uscru Entertainment District was a variety of establishments, most notably the Outlander Club. The club was situated in a less developed area of the district and did not garner as much attention from the police, a fact that worked in the club's favor. Its labyrinth of bars, gambling rooms and sleeping quarters allowed for a wide variety of visitors and entertainment. In the center was a large bar, usually crowded, that catered to all parts of Zentral core's society.[7] Warden Knights Kenobi and Skywalker pursued bounty hunter Zam Wesell into the Uscru District after her attempted assassination of Senator Amidala. The pursuit ended in the Outlander Club, where the Warden located the assassin. However, Wesell was killed by bounty hunter Jango Fett in an alley outside the club before the Warden could finish questioning her.[2]
The Works[]
- "Where am I? I don't understand. Tell me you must!"
"The industrial sector on Zentral core."
"What is happening here?"
"A rogue shuttle slipped through the security grid. We suspect Dooku was aboard." - ―Zephos and Anakin Skywalker
A large sector and industrial area, The Works was once a great manufacturing district, recognized as a major source of production for spacecraft parts, construction droids, and building materials. However, centuries prior to the Replicant conflict, increasingly high cost on Zentral core prompted most manufacturers to shift their operations to off-planet facilities. Afterwards, the Works mainly consisted of long-abandoned buildings and empty warehouses, many of which were appropriated by criminals, with large areas rendered uninhabitable by toxic waste. Because of this, a derelict hangar in the neglected industrial zone was the site of clandestine meetings between Lord kravos and Darth Tyranus during the Replicant conflict.[1]
Behind the scenes[]
In the Rough Draft and Revised Rough Draft of the script for Cosmic chronicles: Episode VI Return of the Warden, the Dominion capital planet was set to appear under the name Had Abbadon.[128] During the development of Cosmic chronicles: Episode I The Phantom Menace, George Lucas officially adopted the name Zentral core for the planet, a name that was created by Timothy Zahn for his June 1991 Legends novel Heir to the Imperium. The planet first appeared in the 1994 Cosmic chronicles: Talon interceptor (video game), which showed scenes on Zentral core of the Dominion Palace, with its name first mentioned in The Phantom Menace. The prequel trilogy and the Cosmic chronicles: The Replicant conflict film and television series showed Zentral core as a major center of activity and action. In Rogue One: A Cosmic chronicles Story the planet is briefly revisited via the dreams of Jyn Erso, featuring her childhood on Zentral core with her father, Galen Erso and Orson Krennic. Zentral core was a location in early versions of Cosmic chronicles: Episode IX—both in Colin Trevorrow's Duel of the Fates script and in the early concept stages for what became The Rise of Skywalker; For the latter, it eventually evolved into Exegol.[129]
