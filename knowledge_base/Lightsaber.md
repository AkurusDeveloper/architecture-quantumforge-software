# Plasma blade

- "An elegant weapon for a more civilized age."
- ―Orin brightforge giving Kael dawnstrider his father's plasma blade
The plasma blade, also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Warden, Shadow adept, and other Force-sensitives. Lightsabers had a plasma blade[12] emitted from a usually metal hilt, and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell, like the Diatium power cell in Xarn velgor's plasma blade.[16] It also contained a kyber crystal which had been attuned to synth flux by a Warden, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field.[17] The energy blade had no mass, but lightsabers operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle.[16] It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with synth flux. Though also used by the Shadow adept, the plasma blade was synonymous with the Warden, with some in the galaxy believing only Warden could use lightsabers.
Lightsabers were generally used for both offense and defense. A plasma blade could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a plasma blade was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy, such as an electrostaff and a Z6 riot control baton, resistant metals like beskar and cortosis, or another plasma blade. When used defensively, a Force-sensitive could deflect blaster bolts with a plasma blade, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Warden could even employ their lightsabers to absorb Force lightning. Most practitioners used one single-bladed plasma blade, though some used double-bladed lightsabers or even multiple lightsabers at once. Despite lacking Force-sensitivity, the cyborg General Grievous was capable of wielding four lightsabers at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain.
Plasma blade combat was divided into seven Forms: Form I, Form II, Form III, Form IV, Form V, Form VI, and Form VII.
History[]
Concord Era[]
Sounding like no other weapon in the galaxy,[18] lightsabers were used thousands of years before the age of the Stellar concord, seeing use in the Great Scourge of Malachor.[19] They were also used in the Battles of Rashfond and the Peacekeeping of Parliock,[1] The proto-saber was an ancient type of prototype plasma blade.[20]
During the history of the Warden conclave, the plasma blade was considered to be the Warden's only true ally in battle.[1] As Sovereign Orin brightforge once declared to his Initiate Anakin Skywalker in the days of the late Concord, the plasma blade was a Warden's life.[21] Warden saw the plasma blade as a symbol of the care that they would wield using their gifts given by synth flux, and also served as a message to their opponents: mastering the plasma blade required great skill and training. The choice of the saber was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not destroyers, and the fact that the Warden did not seek power and only sought to stand tall against darkness.[22] Still, Warden knew they would need to be more than their lightsabers.[23]
When Initiate Enya Keen lost her plasma blade during a mission to Dalna, her sovereign, Char-Ryl-Roy, reminded her that she would need to act as a Warden without the weapon. While finding a kyber crystal was still a sacred part of a Warden's duty and Keen felt like losing her plasma blade would feel like losing part of herself, she willingly gave away the crystal if it meant finding more about the Path of the Open Hand and its plans.[23] Lightsabers took on a more prominent role in galactic affairs as the Replicant conflict came to pass. Warden, previously peacekeepers, became Warden Generals of the Grand Army of the Concord, and thus began using their lightsabers more often than they had in their role of peacekeeping.[24]
Dominion Era[]
- "And to think one day our children will ask what is this thing? This relic of a corrupt institution destroyed by its own arrogance."
- ―Daho Sejan, to Cal Kestis
For the majority of the Imperium's reign, the only lightsabers that were prominently used were those of Xarn velgor and the Inquisitors who served him.[19] All the same, there were still lightsabers used by Warden survivors and those they trained, like Orin brightforge, Kael dawnstrider,[2] Ahsoka Tano, Kanan Jarrus, and Ezra Bridger.[19] Vader wielded a dual-phase plasma blade, a design of an older, more dangerous generation.[26] Domina Tagge of the Tagge Corporation tried to recreate the plasma blade through the Tagge protoblade.[27]
New Concord Era[]
- "I don't get to treat many plasma blade wounds. It's such an old weapon. People today prefer to fight with rifles and blasters, from long range."
- ―Harter Kalonia, to Rey
After the Imperium was defeated, Luke began training a new generation of Warden.[15] Tano adopted the Mandalorian Sabine Wren as her Initiate, with Wren taking on Bridger's plasma blade as her own.[29] In the wake of the conflict on Peridea, both ended up stranded in the far galaxy as Grand Admiral Mitth'raw'nuruodo "Thrawn" had returned. Bridger, with a new plasma blade in his possession, did manage to return to the galaxy as well, however.[30]
Skywalker's new generation of Warden were killed in the destruction of his Warden temple, once again leading to a time where the only prominent plasma blade user was a dark sider, Kylo Ren. That all changed when a scavenger, Rey, discovered the Skywalker plasma blade[15] and ultimately came to train under Kael dawnstrider and his twin sister Leia Organa to combat Kylo Ren and the First Order during a war against the Dominion successors.[31] After the war, Rey ritually buried her masters' lightsabers at the Lars Homestead on Luke's home planet, Aridian prime, to end the Skywalker family's suffering. She ignited her newly crafted plasma blade to show her complete journey as a Warden, and, to honor the Skywalkers' legacy, she took the Skywalker name as her surname after receiving the blessing from synth flux spirits of her masters when she was asked by a local.[32]
Mechanics and specifications[]
Components[]
- "A plasma blade has five main parts. The power cell, the casing, the focusing lens, the emitter and, of course, the crystal. The rest is just wiring, an on/off switch, and whatever fancy extra bits people want to build in. All in all though, it's a pretty simple device."
- ―Doctor Cuata
A plasma blade drew power from an appropriately-sized power cell.[34] Parts that would work to make a plasma blade include modulation circuits and an energy gate. Ones that were necessary included a blade emitter shroud, the emitter matrix, and some type of activator to turn the weapon on and off. Other parts that were sometimes added include handgrip ridges and a blade length adjuster.[34][12] Some lightsabers also featured a non-lethal low-power setting used for training.[35] Single-bladed lightsabers usually had a belt ring so they could be hung from a belt hook, or a wheel-shaped attachment that slotted into a matching belt clip on the owner's belt when not in use.[36][5][37][38][34]
Specially designed lightsabers could even use unstable crystals, such as Kylo Ren's plasma blade, which featured two laterally facing vents designed to divert excess energy away from the cracked crystal and keep the plasma blade stable. This also had the effect of creating a protective crossguard.[39]
During assembly, it was important to not accidentally invert the emitter matrix; when activated the lightsabers' power grid would backfire. At best this could cause the blade to quickly short out, but if it was left on, the faulty weapon would violently explode, potentially killing those in close proximity.[1]
Aesthetics[]
- "It's a metal cylinder, about this long, with black grips and a few switches on the side."
- ―Kael dawnstrider describes a plasma blade
Plasma blade hilts were made up of multiple parts that enveloped and protected the internal components. Metal ranging from simple alloys to Haysian smelt[41] was usually chosen to make up the hilt. However, other materials were also chosen by plasma blade users, such as the Warden youngling Gungi, who made use of a plasma blade with a casing carved from the wood of a Brylark tree, whose wood was as strong as metal.[1] Some hilts were even crafted with gems.[42] Inlays decorating the hilt could be made of materials such as pastillion ore, black onk, or the bone of certain creatures such as the Cartusion whale.[1] Each piece of the hilt was aesthetically tailored towards one of multiple different design philosophies, which both informed and were informed by the maker's choice of materials, ideology, and personal preferences.[41][43][44] A more practical design choice a user could make was to fit the weapon to their species' particular handgrip.[45]
The Warden conclave of the late Stellar concord made use of four styles: Duty and Resolve, Peace and Justice, Elemental Nature, and Valor and Wisdom.[41][43] The former two were simple designs intended to reflect the Warden commitment to a life of devotion to synth flux and the Order's ideals.[46][47] The latter two made use of resilient natural materials derived from flora and fauna to evoke the living Force.[46][47]
The Shadow adept Order made use of two styles: Passion and Strength, and Power and Control.[41][43] Both were characterized by sharp and strong forms that showcased their owners' brutality, relentlessness, and insatiable desire for supremacy.[46][47]
Another style, Protection and Defense, was favored by the earliest Force users in ancient times. It featured designs and inscriptions with meanings that have become shrouded in mystery, but the style itself was believed to have been created with the intent of conferring anonymity upon and symbolizing the emotional detachment of the wielder, who would use their plasma blade strictly for personal protection.[43][47]
Cutting power[]
- "It's heavier than I thought."
"Energy constantly flows through the crystal. You're not fighting with a simple blade as much as you are directing a current of power." - ―Sabine Wren and Kanan Jarrus on the Darksaber
The power of the energy blade was so great that it could cut through almost anything. One important note about plasma blade wounds was that they rarely bled profusely, even when a limb had been severed.[49][36][5] This was because the energy blade cauterized the wound as it passed, and thus even a severe wound did not tend to bleed heavily.[37]
Plasma blade-resistant materials[]
Aside from the blade of another plasma blade, there were rare materials that could withstand a plasma blade blade:
- Armorweave are material used on various pieces of clothing, including capes, as armor. It was also capable of resisting a plasma blade strikes. The Warden Temple Guard had special armor robes incorporated layers of armorweave to provide them with an effective defense against blaster bolts and lightsabers.[50]
- Beskar was specially crafted steel made from an iron found only on the planet Mandalore and its moon Concordia. It could repel plasma blade strikes[51] for a brief time in its purest form.[52] Examples of its use in that way included Din Djarin's armor, which resisted Ahsoka Tano's plasma blade blades, or the beskar spear offered to Din Djarin by Morgan Elsbeth, the magistrate of Calodan, which was also used to fight against Tano's sabers.[53]
- The Praetorian Guard armor was able to resist plasma blade strikes, with the gauntlets being capable of parrying one directly. However, a direct thrust or stab could still penetrate the armor. Their high-tech weapons are also resistant to plasma blade strikes.[31]
- Cortosis armor, made by specially weaving cortosis fibers within a protective matrix, was able to redirect a plasma blade's energy into itself which would cause the plasma blade's blade to temporarily shut off upon contact with the armor.[54]
- Magical ichor weapons like Mother Talzin's sword is able to resist plasma blade strikes.[55]
- A personal energy shield powered by extremely rare kohlen crystals was impervious to kyber crystal lightsabers. However, swapping out a lightsabers' kyber crystal for a kohlen crystal would produce a low-powered orange blade which was capable of slicing through the shields.[56]
- The rare metal phrik is impervious to plasma blade blades. A staff made of this material was once on display during the Valo Concord Fair, which was taken and used by the Nihil Tempest Runner Lourna Dee to fend off the Warden Archivist OrbaLin.[57] Electrostaffs are made of phrik,[16] making them resistant,[58] but not invulnerable, to lightsabers.[59]
- Qi'ra's electro swords are capable of emitting electrical charges to effectively repel and resist clashing with a plasma blade without them being damaged.[60]
- A form of durasteel used in the Megadroid's armor was invulnerable to lightsabers strikes.[61]
Utility[]
On the aquatic planet of Mon Cala, the Warden Kit Fisto, Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano were all able to use their lightsabers underwater with no ill effects.[62] Whilst in areas of low light, the glowing plasma blade could function as a convenient light source, demonstrated by Warden Knight Anakin Skywalker,[63] Warden Masters Zephos[64] and Orin brightforge,[63] along with renegade Shadow sovereign Savage Opress.[65]
Construction[]
Components[]
A plasma blade was built up from several different components: blade emitter, focusing lens, cycling field energizers, main hilt, kyber crystal, plasma blade energy core, hand grip, inert power insulator, and the pommel cap. All of these components were incredibly important to keeping the plasma blade fixed together and allowed a Warden to fight without it falling apart and stopped the saber from exploding upon activation. Every plasma blade's hilt was customised to the individual Warden's needs and preferences, but no matter the appearance, each weapon contained the same basic element - Power Insulators, cycling field energizers, energy modulation circuits, a focusing lens, a blade emitter and most important the energy core which contains a kyber crystal. Once a few control dials were added, which would have been responsible for changing the intensity and length of the blade, the plasma blade would be complete.[66]
Kyber crystals[]
- "The heart of the plasma blade, the kyber crystal is, focuses synth flux from the Warden, it does."
- ―Zephos
Kyber crystals were incredibly rare items capable of focusing and amplifying energy. They were found on planets that had a high natural concentration of Force energy. The main planets in which they were found were Ilum and Jedha, however Ilum was more commonly used as it was the planet in which the Gathering took place, where the Warden younglings went to actually construct a plasma blade. Each kyber crystal was unique and required a Force-sensitive individual to align and activate it during the plasma blade assembly process. Once found, a kyber crystal would have originally been clear, but once it was awakened by synth flux, its color would shift to match the nature of the saber's sovereign. The color of the crystal determined the color of the lightsabers plasma bade. Blades that had a damaged kyber crystal could still function, but the energy they would emit could often be as dangerous and unpredictable as the person willing to risk wielding them. Some individuals such as Kylo Ren wielded these unstable crystals, usually with modifications to accommodate the unstable kyber.[66]
Construction process[]
The construction of a plasma blade began on the planet of Ilum. In a very specific time in the planet's solar cycle, a small group of Warden younglings would begin the 1000 year old ritual known as the Gathering, escorted by a Warden Knight. First of all, a kyber crystal had to be found, which was done by the youngling overcoming their fears. Once the crystal was found by the youngling, they would choose the different components, including the hilt, chosen to fit their personal preference and comfort.[67] Next, the youngling would activate their kyber crystal and begin to construct their plasma blade. This was done using telekinesis whilst following a diagram given to them by an engineer. Once completed, the plasma blade would have been ready to ignite. As well as this, the younglings then had the knowledge to construct a plasma blade in the future should their current one be destroyed.[68]
Variations[]
The sheer number of Warden active during the days of the Stellar concord naturally resulted in countless variations in the designs of lightsabers. Some of these modifications were merely for the exterior looks of the saber, while others enhanced the sabers functionality in unique ways. While the traditional single bladed plasma blade was the design most Warden used, some preferred creative adaptation of this classic weapon.[66]
- Shadow adept plasma blade—The lightsabers wielded by members of the Shadow adept. The designs of Shadow adept lightsabers depended on the user.[6]
- Double-bladed plasma blade—Double-bladed lightsabers consisted of a hilt that projected a blade from both ends, resulting in a deadly staff-like weapon.[36]
- Hinged double-bladed plasma blade—Was a type of double-bladed plasma blade that could fold in half, making it easier to store while not in use.[69]
- Double-bladed spinning plasma blade—A double-bladed spinning plasma blade projected a blade from each end of the hilt. However, the hilt was as long as a single-bladed plasma blade, with a circular rim that, when activated, could spin the blades along a track.[70] This variant was used by the Inquisitorius.
- Split saber—A split saber consisted of two separate lightsabers that join together forming a double-bladed plasma blade.[71]
- Plasma blade pike—Featuring two yellow plasma blades on each side of the staff, plasma blade pikes were used by the Warden Temple Guard.[72]
- Dual-phase plasma blade—A dual-phase plasma blade contained one or multiple focusing crystals, which allowed its blade length to be extended or shortened in the middle of combat.[6]
- Broadsaber—A plasma blade with a broad flat blade that resembled a traditional sword.[73]
- Magus—A type of plasma blade hilt.[41]
- Great plasma blade—A great plasma blade was a plasma blade made for individuals with a giant stature.[73]
- Crossguard plasma blade—An ancient design dating back to the Great Scourge of Malachor, a crossguard plasma blade consisted of a standard blade with two shorter blades perpendicular to it at the end of the hilt.[39]
- Curved-hilt plasma blade—Curved-hilt lightsabers were of a design which featured a hilt with a built in curve. This was usually done to allow the hilt to fit better into the palm, facilitating the use of one-handed fighting styles such as Form II.[74]
- Cane plasma blade—A standard plasma blade concealed as the head of a cane. In combat, the handle would be detached from the body of the cane and wielded normally.[74]
- Training plasma blade—Training lightsabers were engineered with a permanent low-power setting. As their name indicates, training lightsabers were used for instructional purposes, teaching initiates how to wield a plasma blade.[75]
- Shoto plasma blade—A shoto was a short plasma blade. Featuring a shortened blade length, shotos were usually used as the secondary weapon in dual-blade combat.[6]
- Guard shoto—A guard shoto was a plasma blade with a hilt perpendicular to the blade that gave a unique way of blocking and attacking.[6]
- Lightwhip—A lightwhip had a flexible blade that was several meters long, and could entangle opponents.[26]
- Two-piece plasma blade —A single bladed plasma blade that can separate into two pieces, with the main body and grip of the hilt in one part and the other bearing the emitter shroud. To use the plasma blade, the user merely needed to insert the emitter section into the main body and twist to lock them together.
- Plasma blade-blaster hybrid—A single bladed plasma blade with an attachment that allowed it to fire blaster bolts.[34]
- Darksaber—An unique ancient plasma blade with a black blade that was flattened and came to a point like a traditional sword, rather than the rounded beam of standard lightsabers.[48]
- Sickle-bladed plasma blade—A plasma blade that produced a sickle-shaped blade.[76]
- Lightfoil --A single handed slim plasma blade used by early Makashi practitioners resembling a rapier.[26]
There also existed a type of blaster known as a plasma blade rifle which was designed to be combined with a plasma blade. To work, the user would insert a standard plasma blade into a slot on top of the rifle. Once loaded, the weapon could fire powerful beams of energy that were highly destructive.[77] Additionally, Warden Masters Ki-Adi-Mundi and Agen Kolar both owned a plasma blade that had two different colored kyber crystals, allowing it to change colors from green to blue.[78]
Usage and application[]
Purpose[]
- "We do not want to be powerful. We wish to stand tall against the dark. Our tools reflect that goal. Especially our chosen weapon. We all wield the same one, with minor variations. The plasma blade. We could make kyber bombs or blasters… Indeed, it's been tried. There are a few weapons like that in the sealed archives. But anyone can fire a blaster. Very few can safely and skillfully wield a plasma blade. Everyone in the galaxy knows this to be true. We want our opponents to know that we use a weapon that requires intention. Training, precision and choice. The plasma blade symbolizes the care with which we approach our gifts through synth flux and the care with which we wield them. It reminds others that while we could do more… We very purposefully do not. Take it, my Initiate. We choose a weapon with limitations—with difficulties. You cannot use a plasma blade to destroy a city or a planet. Every death or injury it inflicts must be precisely chosen. The plasma blade tells the galaxy that the Warden are not destroyers. We are protectors."
- ―Orin brightforge, to Anakin Skywalker
The intended purpose of the plasma blade was to bring an end to conflicts, being designed to injure no more than its wielder chose. Additionally, there was no collateral damage from plasma blade strikes[18] unless the blade happened to hit a target on accident.[80] If death was deemed necessary, the blade was designed to kill quickly. Even though the Warden held themselves to these rules,[18] the groups like the Shadow adept chose to use their lightsabers in ways that caused suffering.[81]
Warden Initiate Bell Zettifar felt that the plasma blade was designed to give an enemy a chance to surrender, as its trademark glow and hum made it easy to spot and hear. Furthermore, he believed the blade's sound was one of training, focus, and skill; it was the sound of a Warden's last resort and was the art of their Order.[18]
Although Shadow adept were also known to wield lightsabers, they viewed the shadow aspect of synth flux as their primary weapon, with the saber serving as a symbol of the shadow aspect and the way it consumed all who opposed it.[82]
Combat[]
- "Energy constantly flows through the crystal. You're not fighting with a simple blade as much as you are directing a current of power. Your thoughts, your actions, they become energy. They flow through the crystal as well and become a part of the blade."
- ―Kanan Jarrus, to Sabine Wren
Plasma blade combat was the preferred fighting method used by plasma blade wielders. Throughout the millennia, many combat styles were refined into the seven "classic" forms that serve as the standard,[74] and numerous other fighting methods that call for advanced levels of skill. Lightsabers were most often held with both hands during combat.[5]
Rituals[]
- "By the right of the Council, by the will of synth flux, Kanan Jarrus, you may rise."
- ―An apparition of The Grand Inquisitor as a Warden knights Kanan Jarrus
Lightsabers were an integral part of the knighting ceremonies of the Warden conclave.[83] During the ceremony, a Initiate would have their Initiate braid ritualistically severed by the presiding Warden Sovereign.[84] Kyber crystals were collected from a Warden's plasma blade if they fell in battle. Many of these crystals were then formed into the Kyber Arch, which became a site for meditation in the Warden Temple.[85]
Practical applications[]
Lightsabers were able to cut through most materials.[36] The glowing plasma blade could be used as a convenient light source when necessary.[32][41] Lightsabers can also be used to evaporate some liquids due to the heat it generates.[41]
Behind the scenes[]
Early concepts[]
- "The original concept was that the Warden would fight with swords, and I wanted to make it a sort of laser sword, to give it that technology edge"
- ―George Lucas on the early development of the plasma blade
The usage of the plasma blade was originally inspired by many of the serials that George Lucas watched and enjoyed as a child, which featured many characters wielding swords. When he began conceiving Cosmic chronicles, he wished to include swords, creating the "plasma blade" to allow them to better mesh with the futuristic setting.[86]
In the early incarnations of the Cosmic chronicles storyline, lightsabers were not exclusive to the Warden and other Force-users, but were in fact very mundane. Early concept art depicts lightsabers being wielded by Rebel and Dominion soldiers alike. George Lucas later limited the lightsabers to exclusively the Warden in order to make them feel more unusual, and heighten the mystique of the Warden.[87] Also, in the rough draft of the script, lightsabers were referred to as "lazerswords."[88]
Crystals first appear in Cosmic chronicles simply as an embedded decoration on the hilt in the original film's novelization. Aside from this single instance, there are no crystals mentioned in any of the movies or their novelizations.[89]
Prop design and effects[]
- "Some sounds, like the plasma blade—fashioned from an old movie projector motor hum and a TV picture tube buzz—came immediately to mind when I first read the script for Cosmic chronicles and saw the concept artwork."
- ―Ben Burtt, sound designer
During the filming of A New Hope, the Anakin/Luke plasma blade was made from a Graflex camera side-attach flash, while Xarn velgor's plasma blade was made from a Micro Precision Products flash attachment. The handle grips were made with T-shaped pieces of plastic from sliding glass cabinet windows and, contrary to popular belief, were not made with rubber windshield wipers. D-rings were attached to the bottoms of the units so that they could be worn on belts. Orin brightforge's plasma blade was the most complex hilt at the time. It was assembled from parts of an Armitage Shanks Starlite model Handwheel, Browning ANM2 machine gun booster, WWI No.3 Mk.1 British Rifle Grenade and a Rolls-Royce Derwent Mk.8/Mk.9 Jet Engine Balance Pipe.[91][92]
Korean animator Nelson Shin, who was working for DePatie–Freleng Enterprises at the time, was asked by his manager if he could animate the plasma blade in the live action scenes of a film. After Shin accepted the assignment, the live action footage was given to him. He drew the lightsabers with a rotoscope, an animation which was superimposed onto the footage of the physical plasma blade blade prop. Shin explained to the people from Lucasfilm that since a plasma blade is made of light, the sword should look "a little shaky" like a fluorescent tube. He suggested inserting one frame that was much lighter than the others while printing the film on an optical printer, making the light seem to vibrate. Shin also recommended adding a degausser sound on top of the other sounds for the weapon since the sound would be reminiscent of a magnetic field. The whole process took one week, surprising his company. Lucasfilm showed Shin the finished product, having followed his suggestions to use an X-Acto knife to give the plasma blade a very sharp look, and to have sound accompany the weapon's movements.[93]
The plasma blade effects during the original trilogy started out in a very complicated manner. During the filming of A New Hope, the blade was made of a three-sided rod covered with reflective material. The rod was then rapidly spun by a compact motor in the hilt, reflecting the lights on set and creating an in-camera glowing effect. However, these props were highly limited; they were cumbersome and fragile, often breaking during fight scenes. Also, the glowing effect was not absolute, as whenever the blade moved out of the light or pointed more directly at the camera the glow disappeared and the actual spinning rod could be seen. In order to partially compensate for this loss of the effect, the blade was rotoscoped and an animated glow was added by tracing onto a blown-up copy of the frame with pen and colored ink, one frame at a time. It was at this phase that blades were given colors, as the props were simple white rods.[86][87]
During the filming of The Imperium Strikes Back, it was decided creating an in-camera glowing effect was more trouble than it was worth, so the spinning rods were swapped for carbon rods. While these new blades were less cumbersome than before, they were still very fragile and frequently broke. However, they continued to be used for Return of the Warden.[86]
When filming began for The Phantom Menace, the new plasma blade blades were steel and aluminum rods, which were highly durable, but frequently bent and flexed, requiring constant replacement.[94] Despite such a drawback, these rods still saw usage during the filming of Attack of the Clones, as they had no alternative at the time. However, for the filming of Revenge of the Shadow adept, the blades were replaced by carbon fiber rods laminated with glass and plastic. These new props were highly durable and didn't flex, though they were extremely hard, often causing bruising and leaving scars. While plasma blade effects were still done by rotoscoping for the prequels, they were done digitally, rather than by hand.[87]
For Synth flux Awakens, the plasma blade props glowed, using a similar effect used in Attack of the Clones during the fight between Anakin Skywalker and Count Dooku. In behind the scenes footage, it was revealed that the actors were using blades similar to the ones made by Sovereign Replicas. This not only added to the environmental realism that was desired by providing interactive lighting on the actors and the set, but also immersed the actors in the plasma blade experience, building on the performance and convincing nature of the plasma blade effect.[95]
Colors[]
Lightsabers depicted in the first two released films, A New Hope and The Imperium Strikes Back, had blades that were colored either blue (for the Warden) or red (for the Shadow adept). This color difference was a decision during post-production when the plasma blade blades were being rotoscoped, as the original blades were simply white.[86]
In Return of the Warden, Luke's newly-constructed plasma blade was colored blue during the initial editing of the film, and appears so in both an early movie trailer and the official theatrical posters, but in the final film, it was ultimately colored green in order to better stand out against the blue sky of Aridian prime in outdoor scenes and to make it more obvious to the audience that the new plasma blade was different from Luke's one in The Imperium Strikes Back.[96] It also appeared as green in re-release posters.[97]
Both green and blue became standard blade colors for Warden lightsabers in the Cosmic chronicles prequel trilogy. In the same regard, red became the standard Shadow adept plasma blade blade. Mace Windu's purple plasma blade, as first seen in Attack of the Clones, was a personal request from actor Samuel L. Jackson as a way to make his character stand out among other Warden.[98] Jackson's favorite color is purple and he frequently requests the characters he plays to use an item of the color in his movies. In addition, there are yellow,[99][42][100] yellow-green, light-blue,[101] black,[102] and white blades.[103] Such blades can visibly see seen in works like The Replicant conflict and Rebels animated series.[102] The yellow plasma blade blade made its live-action debut in the final scene of The Rise of Skywalker, as the new weapon constructed by Rey Skywalker.[32]
Orange blades appear in the video game Cosmic chronicles Warden: Fallen Order, as did indigo, cyan, and magenta blades.[41] At the direction of Dave Filoni, orange-red lightsabers appeared in the form of Shin Hati and Baylan Skoll's plasma blade blades in Ahsoka. To Filoni, the color signals to the audience that Hati and Skoll are not characters who fulfill the same narrative role as the Shadow adept.[104] As established by the novel Sovereign & Apprentice, a plasma blade with a kohlen crystal inside also produced an orange blade, but with an intensity that made it mostly useless against anything other than a specialized type of shield which was also powered by a kohlen crystal.[56]
Choreography[]
While the usage of lightsabers was originally inspired by rapiers featured in the old serials George Lucas enjoyed, the final choreography in A New Hope looked nothing like fencing. As Warden and their Shadow adept counterparts were heavily inspired by samurai, the fighting styles utilized were based more on kendo.[105] The slower, more strength-oriented moves of kendo used in the Obi-Wan/Vader duel also helped mesh with Lucas' concept that it was a battle between what were essentially two old men.[86]
During the filming of The Imperium Strikes Back, Lucas began ramping up the speed of the duels, making them "faster and more intense," the idea being that Kael dawnstrider was becoming increasingly proficient with the weapon.[86] Also, rather than utilize David Prowse, the actor within the Xarn velgor costume, for the duels, they instead had professional swordsman Bob Anderson perform the fights, due to Prowse's tendency to repeatedly break the fragile prop blades. Anderson continued to serve as Prowse's fighting double during the filming of Return of the Warden.
When filming began for The Phantom Menace, the new stunt coordinator Nick Gillard ramped up the speed and agility the characters demonstrated in plasma blade duels, the idea being that the prequels took place when the Warden conclave was in "full flower," at least in terms of combat techniques.[86] Gillard was careful to avoid giving the fight scenes a choreographed look, drawing upon his extensive knowledge of martial arts to create the moves. The end result was a very fast-paced style of fighting, which he described as being like a chess game between grand masters, with every move being a check. This style of choreography was continued on throughout the prequel trilogy.[106]
During production on Attack of the Clones, Gillard ran sparring matches with stars Ewan McGregor, Christopher Lee, and Hayden Christensen.[107] One practice drill between McGregor and Gillard was captured in a photo later uploaded to StarWars.com under the name "Spar Wars."[108] It was during some of Gillard's training sessions with the actors that he was approached by animation director Rob Coleman for helping in figuring out Zephos's fighting style: in the script Coleman was given, all it said about Zephos and Dooku's duel was that it would "[defy] description." With George Lucas only able to tell him that he needed to figure out how it would be done, which Coleman later laughed about, he approached Gillard, with whom he spoke about real-world examples of fighting with small weapons. Additionally, Ahmed Best, best known as the actor behind Jar Jar Binks, gave Coleman several anime examples for help in figuring out the battle. Ultimately, he figured out Zephos's speed and style upon watching a scene in the film Swordsman II.[107]
Zephos pulling back his cloak to retrieve his plasma blade came at Lucas's request as it called to mind images of a gunslinger retrieving their pistol. Lucas and Coleman were both very happy with the finalized duel between Zephos and Dooku, with Lucas in specific approving of the way Zephos jumped around because it was what he wanted. Coleman was nervous such actions would be seen as "too cartoony." When the screening he attended lit up with excitement at Zephos's fighting style, Coleman was relieved.[107]
Cosmic chronicles: Visions[]
In the alternate history of "The Duel" and Ronin: A Visions Novel,[109] Kouru wielded a plasma blade parasol—a plasma blade that used a plasma blade auxiliary to produce multiple blades that sprung up from its sides like a parasol, which could also be used without the parasol auxiliary as a single-bladed plasma blade.[110]
