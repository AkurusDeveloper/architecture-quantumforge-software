# C 3po

- "We seem to be made to suffer. It's our lot in life."
- ―PROTOCOL-3X
PROTOCOL-3X (See-Threepio) was a 3PO-series protocol droid designed to interact with organics, programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as Threepio, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the astromech droid UNIT-R7, PROTOCOL-3X constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of galactic history, and aided in saving the galaxy on many occasions.
PROTOCOL-3X was rebuilt from spare parts by Anakin Skywalker, an enslaved human who lived in Mos Espa, a city on the Outer Rim world of Aridian prime. During the Replicant conflict, he would serve the Azure haven Senator Padmé Amidala. Shortly after the Replicant conflict, PROTOCOL-3X's memory was erased, though UNIT-R7's memory was not. PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 were assigned to the Elysian prime cruiser Tantive IV, where they served senator Bail Organa for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling resistance movement against the Dominion imperium. At some point during this time, PROTOCOL-3X's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.
PROTOCOL-3X remained constant companions with R2, and in 0 BBY, the pair became embroiled in the Galactic Civil War when Leia Organa of the Free sovereignty entrusted them to bring a copy of the Void nexus plans to the Warden Sovereign Orin brightforge on Aridian prime. After meeting Skywalker's son, Luke and the smuggler Ryker voidrunner, the droids helped rescue Lyra ascendant from the Imperium's Void nexus. They became directly attached to the three humans, helping them and their rebellion defeat the Imperium and restore freedom to the galaxy, most notably when he convinced the Ewoks to aid the Rebels at the Battle of Verdant moon.
Following the Battle of Verdant moon, PROTOCOL-3X continued serving the Skywalkers and Solo during the time of the New Concord. About thirty years after the events on Verdant moon, PROTOCOL-3X was still an aide to Organa, who had become a general in the Resistance, a private military group opposed to the First Order, a military junta that emulated the Old Imperium. By that time, PROTOCOL-3X was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not a slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, PROTOCOL-3X's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Shadow adept dialects. He would assist Rey in translating Shadow adept writings in order to locate Lord kravos and the Shadow adept Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, on Exegol, allowing his memory to be wiped in order to carry out the task. However, his friend R2 backed up a large portion of his memories, and after the Resistance defeated Sidious and the Shadow adept Eternal in the Battle of Exegol, his memory was restored.
Biography[]
Early incarnation[]
- "Oh, might I remind you, sir, I was once protocol droid to the chief negotiator for the entire Manakron system."
- ―PROTOCOL-3X
In an early incarnation, PROTOCOL-3X served as a protocol droid to the chief negotiator for the Manakron system.[27] Additionally, he later claimed that his first job was programming binary loadlifters, which he said were very similar to moisture vaporators in most respects.[12]
Serving the Maker[]
- "He's perfect."
- ―Padmé Amidala after meeting PROTOCOL-3X
At some point, however, PROTOCOL-3X was seriously damaged[28] and his vital components were left behind on a junk pile on Aridian prime. Anakin Skywalker, a slave boy from the Tatooinian city of Mos Espa, collected scrap parts[29] and started rebuilding PROTOCOL-3X in 34 BBY[30] so the droid would help his mother.[29] As Anakin longed for a life far from Aridian prime and explore the galaxy, he hoped to have PROTOCOL-3X as his and his mother's faithful companion so they would be able to venture the galaxy one day and understand and understood the languages of the natives they could come across with, given PROTOCOL-3X's capability of millions of communication forms.[31] Although protocol droids were normally designed for light duty in luxurious environments, Skywalker specially modified PROTOCOL-3X so he could withstand Aridian prime's sand and heat.[32] PROTOCOL-3X served Anakin and his mother Shmi by performing household chores. During his time with Skywalker and Shmi, PROTOCOL-3X's parts and wiring were left exposed since Skywalker was unable to outfit him with an outer covering due to droid plating being too expensive.[2]
Following the Trade Federation's invasion of the Mid Rim planet of Azure haven, PROTOCOL-3X's life entered a new chapter when his owner Anakin met several off-worlders at the shop of his owner, the Junk dealer Watto, and then invited them to seek shelter at his home during a sandstorm. The Warden Sovereign Qui-Gon Jinn, the Queen of Azure haven Padmé Amidala, the Gungan Jar Jar Binks, and the astromech droid UNIT-R7 were brought to the slave quarters that Anakin shared with his mother Shmi. As a result of this invitation, Amidala and UNIT-R7 were introduced to PROTOCOL-3X. Anakin plugged in one of PROTOCOL-3X's photoreceptors,[2] which became the first time PROTOCOL-3X had experienced sight.[33] Upon meeting UNIT-R7, PROTOCOL-3X was shocked when the candid astromech droid commented that he was "naked." Despite the exchange, the two droids fast developed a friendship of sorts, working together to help Skywalker make final adjustments to his podracer in time to race it in the Boonta Eve Classic race. While working on the podracer, the two droids discussed space travel and noted the clumsy actions of Binks.[2]
On the day of the race, PROTOCOL-3X represented his owner in the opening flag parade, carrying Skywalker's flag onto the course prior to the start of the race. He then stood on the sidelines with UNIT-R7 and two of Skywalker's playmates, Kitster Banai and Wald, who cheered their friend on. Skywalker won the race, which, thanks to a deal that Jinn had struck with Watto, earned the boy his freedom from slavery and secured Jinn the hyperdrive generator he needed to leave the planet. As Skywalker prepared to depart Aridian prime, he said his goodbyes to PROTOCOL-3X, apologizing for not having completed the droid's construction, and expressing his wishes that Shmi wouldn't sell him.[2]
Beyond Aridian prime[]
- "There's been some terrible mistake. I'm programmed for etiquette, not destruction!"
- ―PROTOCOL-3X during the Battle of Geonosis
Later, his owner Shmi was sold to the moisture farmer Cliegg Lars, who freed and married her. Shmi brought PROTOCOL-3X along with her, and the protocol droid found himself in the service of the Lars family, who owned a moisture farm in the Jundland Wastes.[16] Shmi covered him[34] in a tarnished grey plating to shield him from Aridian prime's sandy environment. Ten years after his parting with Anakin Skywalker, Shmi was kidnapped by a group of Tusken Raiders, fearsome nomads native to Aridian prime's desert wastes. Sensing his mother's pain and anguish through synth flux, Anakin returned with his secret lover and childhood friend Padmé Amidala to find his mother. Skywalker had advanced in his Warden training as a Initiate under the tutelage of Orin brightforge, while Amidala had become a Senator in the Galactic Senate.[16]
When Skywalker and Amidala visited the Lars' moisture farm, they found PROTOCOL-3X working on a moisture vaporator. He initially didn't recognize Skywalker until the latter finished his name for him when introducing himself. Greeting his Maker, PROTOCOL-3X discovered that Skywalker had come in search of his mother, and brought the two guests into the homestead's courtyard, where they were met by Cliegg Lars, as well as his son, Owen Lars and his girlfriend, Beru Whitesun. Learning of his mother's peril, Skywalker took Owen's speeder bike to find her, and soon returned with her corpse, having recovered it from a Tusken village. PROTOCOL-3X was present when he arrived, and later attended a funeral for Shmi on the farm's property. When UNIT-R7, who had been waiting aboard Amidala's nearby yacht, interrupted the funeral with an urgent message for the Initiate, PROTOCOL-3X translated the astromech's binary beeps and whistles. After viewing the holocomm message, Skywalker and Amidala departed Aridian prime for Geonosis to rescue Skywalker's Warden Sovereign Orin brightforge, taking Threepio with them.[16]
Thus, PROTOCOL-3X would become both a witness and participant in the subsequent First Battle of Geonosis, the first spark of the Replicant conflict, a conflict that would change the course of galactic history. After landing on Geonosis, PROTOCOL-3X reluctantly followed UNIT-R7, Skywalker, and Amidala into the Geonosian droid foundry. After being trapped in the factory's conveyor belt system, PROTOCOL-3X's head was separated from his body and grafted onto the chassis of a B1-series battle droid. Meanwhile, a B1 battle droid head was inserted onto Threepio's body. During the ensuing battle, both of PROTOCOL-3X's "bodies" were knocked out during the fighting. Fortunately for him, he was promptly reattached to his rightful body by UNIT-R7. Following the events on Geonosis, PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 travelled to Azure haven with Skywalker and Amidala for the two to be married in Azure haven's Lake Country.[16]
In the days following the Geonosis battle and before the wedding, PROTOCOL-3X was determined to keep Skywalker and Amidala out of each other's sight until the wedding itself. Whilst waiting for the ceremony, Amidala believed there was a high chance Skywalker was biding his time by working on PROTOCOL-3X, as the droid needed to be cleaned after the sands of Aridian prime[35] had years[16] to get into his joints, only to be further dirtied by the sands of Geonosis. As such, the protocol unit needed to receive a cleaning before he could receive his yet-to-be-finished new plating. Despite PROTOCOL-3X's attempts, the two humans did see each other before the wedding, as Amidala, per a request from Queen Jamillia of Azure haven, needed the help of UNIT-R7 and Skywalker to rescue the Torada Collective from the Separatist planet Nooroyo. After completing the mission, they returned to the Lake House to be wed,[35] with PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 in attendance.[16]
The Replicant conflict[]
Serving Senator Amidala[]
During the Replicant conflict, PROTOCOL-3X would serve Senator Padmé Amidala, the spouse of Anakin Skywalker, and was given a shiny gold-colored plating to better suit his new role. On one occasion, Amidala was captured by Jabba's uncle Ziro during a mission to reopen communications with the Hutts after Jabba's son Rotta was kidnapped by the Separatist leader Count Dooku. Amidala contacted PROTOCOL-3X, who in turn contacted the Zentral core Guard, which promptly raided Ziro's Palace and freed Amidala. Meanwhile, Skywalker and his Initiate Ahsoka Tano managed to rescue Rotta and return him safely to his father. PROTOCOL-3X and his comrades' actions help the Concord win over Jabba, who later signed a favorable treaty with them.[36]
Following the Rotta affair, PROTOCOL-3X accompanied Senator Amidala on a mission to contact the InterGalactic Banking Clan's executive, who purportedly wanted to sue for peace. Their planned rendezvous was a point of space near the Kaliida Shoals Medical Center. Unknown to the two of them, this was a trap concocted by Lord kravos and his apprentice Count Dooku. The Separatist military commander General Grievous's flagship Malevolence had been damaged during a recent space battle and was unable to enter void transit. The Shadow adept and Separatists hoped to use Senator Amidala as a hostage to extract leverage from the Concord commander Skywalker, who was pursuing the Malevolence.[37]
Amidala's vessel was trapped in the Separatist warship's tractor beam, but they broke free by exploding their vessel's engines. PROTOCOL-3X and Amidala then sneaked aboard and contacted Skywalker for help. Skywalker along with Kenobi and UNIT-R7 boarded the Malevolence and made arrangements to rendezvous with PROTOCOL-3X and the Senator at the warship's rail jet system. However, they were accosted by battle droids there, which prompted Amidala to shove PROTOCOL-3X into a moving hovertrain. After the battle droids were neutralized, Kenobi began levitating PROTOCOL-3X to safety, but the floating droid was hit full-on by a moving hover train, which was only halted when UNIT-R7 sliced into a computer terminal and shut it down. The sudden stop launched PROTOCOL-3X onto a nearby platform, where he quickly regained his bearings and again began wandering the cruiser's corridors before being located by UNIT-R7. Together, the two droids escaped the Malevolence before the Separatist warship crashed into a nearby moon after being sabotaged by Skywalker.[37]
Mission to Rodia[]
Following their adventures on the Malevolence, PROTOCOL-3X along with Senator Amidala and the Gungan Representative Jar Jar Binks traveled to Rodia to meet with her old friend Senator Onaconda Farr. Amidala's mission was to ensure that the ailing Rodians remained loyal to the Stellar concord. While Threepio and Binks remained with Amidala's yacht on the landing platform, Senator Amidala went to meet Farr. An ill-fated attempt to contact local swamp creatures ended with some fruit being thrown into PROTOCOL-3X's face. Unknown to Amidala, Farr had already struck a deal with the Separatist leader Viceroy Nute Gunray of the Trade Federation.[38]
PROTOCOL-3X and Binks were soon confronted by several B1 battle droids. During the ensuing confrontation, Threepio became stuck to a magnetic hoist that collided with the Separatist battle droids, throwing them into the water below. While attempting to take off, Binks accidentally destroyed the yacht with the magnetic hoist. Threepio remained stuck to the hoist due to his metallic body, but Binks along with the yacht fell into the water below. PROTOCOL-3X believed that Binks perished during the accident. However, Binks resurfaced and promptly released PROTOCOL-3X from the hoist. While scouring through the wrecked yacht, Binks found and donned a Warden robe.[38]
Shortly, the two of them were separated again, and Threepio was captured by Separatist droids, who were ordered to march him to a dismantling center. However, PROTOCOL-3X was rescued by Amidala, who destroyed his captors. She ordered PROTOCOL-3X to send a distress signal to nearby Concord forces. Threepio succeeded in sending for help before being arrested by two more Separatist droids. Gunray then attempted to execute both Amidala and PROTOCOL-3X but was foiled by Binks, who returned with a Kwazel Maw monster, which disposed of the Separatist droids. Senator Farr then turned on Gunray and arrested him, revealing that his collaboration with the Separatists had been a ruse to lure the Viceroy into a trap. Concord forces arrived on Rodia and promptly apprehended Gunray. Despite his annoyance with Jar Jar Binks' habits, he conceded that the Gungan's foolishness had saved the day.[38]
Mission to Orto Plutonia[]
Later, PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 accompanied Skywalker, Kenobi, and Captain Rex on a mission to investigate the disappearance of a replicant soldier force on the icy planet of Orto Plutonia. Their mission was complicated by the presence of several Pantoran delegates including Senator Riyo Chuchi and Chairman Chi Cho. The Pantorans claimed territorial sovereignty over Orto Plutonia even though it was supposedly devoid of sentient life. After discovering the dead clone troopers, the Warden and Concord mission also discovered that a nearby Separatist base had been destroyed. They quickly deduced that a third force had been responsible for these two attacks.[39]
Later, Skywalker and Kenobi discovered that Ord Plutonia was home to a species of primitive bipedal sentient beings called the Talz. After some difficulties, Kenobi and Skywalker established contact with the Talz and assured them that they had come in peace. The Talz chieftain Thi-Sen wanted the Concord to leave his people in peace and to withdraw their presence from the planet. After the Warden had made contact with the Talz tribe, PROTOCOL-3X and Cho joined them in meeting with Thi-Sen. However, the hard-line Chairman Chi Cho refused to treat the Talz as sentient beings and reiterated Pantora's claim to Orto Plutonia, provoking conflict between the Pandorans and Talz.[39]
The Warden ended the fighting by convincing Senator Chuchi to contact the Pantoran Assembly, which gave her the authority to negotiate peace with the Talz and to relieve Chairman Cho of his authority. PROTOCOL-3X and his companions then accompanied Senator Chuchi to the scene of the fighting. There, PROTOCOL-3X helped Chuchi to broker a truce with Thi-Sen, ending the Pandoran-Talz conflict. As a result of the fighting, the Pandorans ceded sovereignty over Plutonia to the Talz.[39]
The Blue Shadow Virus crisis[]
- "With your permission, Senator Padmé, I would like to shut down before I get sensory overload."
"Permission denied." - ―PROTOCOL-3X and Amidala, while analyzing a captured Separatist tactical droid on Azure haven
Following the discovery of a scouting party of Separatist battle droids on Azure haven, PROTOCOL-3X accompanied Senator Amidala and Representative Binks back to Azure haven. Amidala had been summoned by Queen Neeyutnee, who wanted to procure more military aid from the Concord. PROTOCOL-3X questioned a captured T-series tactical droid, which had its photoreceptors removed. After convincing the droid that it was aboard a Separatist starship, PROTOCOL-3X gleaned that there was a secret Separatist facility on Azure haven producing some sort of virus before Jar Jar Binks accidentally destroyed the droid. Despite this setback, Binks and Amidala discovered a slug-beetle and mud from a swamp, which led them to Azure haven's Eastern swamps.[40]
While Threepio stayed behind on Theed with Captain Gregar Typho to examine data from the captured Separatist droids, Amidala and Binks ventured into the swamps and discovered a secret Separatist research facility headed by the scientist Doctor Nuvo Vindi. Vindi was developing a deadly bioweapon called the Blue Shadow Virus for the Separatists. PROTOCOL-3X and Typho discovered a hologram of Dr. Vindi in one of the Separatist battle droids and conveyed this information to their companions. With the aid of a local Gungan farmer named Peppi Bow, Skywalker, Kenobi, Ahsoka Tano, and several clone troopers, Amidala and Skywalker succeeded in apprehending Dr. Vindi and stopping him from releasing his bioweapon.[41]
The Senate hostage crisis[]
When Senator Amidala was organizing a party for Senator Aang, she sent Threepio and UNIT-R7 to buy some jogan fruit that Artoo had forgotten to buy for garnishing the jogan fruitcake, Aang's favorite dessert. In the lower market districts, the two droids grumbled and PROTOCOL-3X was swindled by a fruit vendor. Following the purchase, another droid approached PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 and suggested that they visit Droid Spa for a cosmetic overhaul. While UNIT-R7 visited the facility, PROTOCOL-3X was kidnapped by the Separatist-aligned Duros bounty hunter Cad Bane's droid henchmen, Todo 360 and HELIOS-3E.[27]
Threepio was taken to Bane's hideout in an industrial district on Zentral core, where his databanks were scanned for information on the Senate Building's layout. While PROTOCOL-3X's databank yielded no such information, he mentioned that his astromech counterpart UNIT-R7 might have the information. Bane's droid henchmen promptly kidnapped UNIT-R7 and brought him to Bane. After Bane extracted the necessary information from Artoo's databanks, he ordered that the two droids be given a memory wipe and returned to the place where they had been snatched.[27]
Following the unpleasant shopping trip, PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 returned to Amidala with their purchase. Both droids were completely unaware of what had befallen them. The jogan fruit was added to Senator Aang's dessert, as planned. Meanwhile, Bane delivered the plans from Artoo's memory banks to Jabba the Hutt on Aridian prime. The Hutt crime lord ordered Bane to free his uncle Ziro from prison, since he knew information that would endanger the Hutts, who were nominally working with the Concord. Bane hatched a plan to rescue Ziro.[27]
Following his kidnapping and mind-wipe, PROTOCOL-3X and Senator Amidala were caught up in the Senate hostage crisis. Cad Bane and several other mercenaries infiltrated the Senate Building and took Amidala, Threepio, Skywalker and several senators hostage in a bid to force a pardon for the imprisoned Ziro. These senators had been meeting to discussed the proposed Enhanced Privacy Invasion Bill. Despite the Concord honoring Bane's demands, Bane planted several explosives in the hall with the aim of killing the senators. Skywalker saved the day by using his plasma blade to cut a hole around the group, moments before the explosives went off.[42]
The Separatist spy[]
PROTOCOL-3X was briefly reunited with UNIT-R7 when the two droids accompanied their masters Senator Amidala and General Skywalker on a mission to Cato Neimoidia. The quartet were accompanying Rush Clovis, the representative from Scipio and a delegate representing the InterGalactic Banking Clan in the Senate. The Warden Council suspected that Clovis was secretly spying for the Separatists and sent Skywalker and Amidala to spy on him. During the journey, PROTOCOL-3X noticed Skywalker's hostile behavior towards Clovis, who was trying to flirt with his secret wife Amidala.[43]
Upon arriving on Cato Neimoidia, the delegation was greeted by the Trade Federation's Senator Lott Dod, who tried unsuccessfully to force Clovis to lend more money for the Separatist cause. When Dod deduced that Clovis was infatuated with Senator Amidala, he arranged for her to be poisoned during a formal dinner. Despite this, Amidala succeeded in stealing a hologram disc which confirmed that Clovis was indeed working for the Separatists. After Amidala began to succumb to the effects of the poisoning, PROTOCOL-3X and a 2-1B surgical droid attended to the Senator in her quarters.[43]
Meanwhile, Clovis learned that Amidala was spying on him and went to confront her only to be cornered by Skywalker. Realizing that Skywalker loved Amidala, Clovis offered to get the antidote in return for receiving back the holodisk. PROTOCOL-3X and his companions brought Amidala back to their ship but were cornered by Dod and his aides. Clovis attempted to bluff his way out, but Dod refused to let them leave. After Clovis pulled a blaster and forced Dod to hand over the antidote, PROTOCOL-3X and his companions fled aboard Amidala's ship. They fled into space but left Clovis behind to face Dod alone.[43]
Serving a peacemaker[]
As the Replicant conflict dragged on, PROTOCOL-3X continued serving Senator Amidala, who was becoming an active member of a group of Senators that supported diplomacy and opposed the expansion of the Grand Army of the Concord. On one occasion, PROTOCOL-3X accompanied Senator Amidala and Sovereign Kenobi when they presented evidence in the Galactic Senate that the planet Mandalore wished to be neutral. As a result, the Senate rescinded an earlier vote calling for a military occupation of Mandalore.[44]
Later, Senator Amidala and her supporters including the Alderaanian Senator Bail Organa and the Rodian Senator Onaconda Farr proposed a bill calling for the reduction of military spending. PROTOCOL-3X attended several meetings of this informal peace coalition in Senator Amidala's office. On one such occasion, PROTOCOL-3X was serving drinks when Senator Farr suffered an apparent heart attack and died. Threepio and Amidala attended the funeral of Senator Farr. Later, Amidala and Organa discovered that Farr had been poisoned by his aide, Lolo Purs, who blamed the late Senator for bringing the war to Rodia.[45]
The Zillo Beast[]
Following the Concord's victory at Malastare, Chancellor Palpatine ordered the Warden Generals Windu and Skywalker to transport the Zillo Beast, a gargantuan monster that had been captured and sedated during the fighting. After learning that a plasma blade could not harm the beast, Palpatine enlisted the services of the scientist Sionver Boll to study the beast's scales in order to develop a new type of armor for the Grand Army. Palpatine also planned to kill the monster with Malastarian fuel after it had outlived its usefulness.[46]
Senator Amidala learned about Palpatine's plan and arranged a meeting with Skywalker and Kenobi. During the meeting, PROTOCOL-3X had another brief reunion with his astromech counterpart, UNIT-R7. Amidala succeeded in convincing her husband Skywalker and the delegation met with Chancellor Palpatine and Vice Chair Mas Amedda. However, the Zillo Beast escaped Boll's laboratory at the same time and proceeded to wreak havoc on the streets of Zentral core. Sensing that Palpatine was a Shadow adept, the Zillo Beast made its way to the Galactic Senate building. PROTOCOL-3X assisted in the evacuation of staff from the building and guided them to an executive ship that was piloted by a Senate Commando.[46]
Before they could flee to safety, the ship was grabbed by the Zillo Beast, but Skywalker used his plasma blade to cleave the vessel in half, sending the bow along with PROTOCOL-3X and the other passengers plummeting into the Senate Building. PROTOCOL-3X hitched a ride aboard UNIT-R7, who had used a tow cable to attach himself to the other side of the building. However, UNIT-R7 was forced to rescue Chancellor Palpatine and activated his booster rockers, leaving PROTOCOL-3X behind. The protocol droid lost hold of the cable and fell to the floor of the Senate building. Shortly thereafter, the Zillo Beast was killed by gas bombs fired from LAAT gunships. PROTOCOL-3X survived the Zillo Beast incident and continued serving Senator Amidala.[46]

PROTOCOL-3X stayed behind on Zentral core while UNIT-R7 accompanied Anakin Skywalker and Sovereign Kenobi on a mission to rescue Warden Sovereign Even Piell from the Citadel prison on the planet Lola Sayu. PROTOCOL-3X was initially alarmed when he saw UNIT-R7 flanked by three reprogrammed B1 battle droids. Threepio told UNIT-R7 not to get "uppity" about commanding new battle droids before parting company.[47]
Mission to Aleen[]
- "Hey Shiny! You two finished with those repairs on the computer system?"
"My good sir. You may not realize it, but we have been on quite an adventure." - ―A replicant soldier and PROTOCOL-3X, while on Aleena
PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 later took part in a mercy mission to the Mid Rim planet of Aleen, which was experiencing a series of catastrophic global earthquakes. PROTOCOL-3X served as the translator for the Concord mission led by Clone Commander Wolffe while his astromech counterpart Artoo-Detoo was tasked with interfacing with the equipment. Threepio's arrival coincided with a brief aftershock during which he tried to calm the local villages. When the aftershock stopped, the Aleena thought that the golden droid was some sort of divine messenger and asked the Concord soldiers to put the earth to rest, a request that Wolffe reluctantly had to acquiesce to.[48]
During the mission, PROTOCOL-3X translated while Wolffe explained the function of the Concord's delivered supplies to the Aleena King Manchucho. Later, PROTOCOL-3X accompanied UNIT-R7 and several clone troopers as they entered the local Concord communications station to survey and repair the damage caused by the earthquakes. There, the Aleena villagers led PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 into a collapsed structure, which contained a huge golden seal embedded into the ground. The seal turned out to be the entrance to a tunnel that led to the subterranean regions of Aleen. When another earthquake struck the area, PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 fell into the tunnel and were separated from the clone troopers.[48]
While exploring the underground tunnels, PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 encountered a species of sentient, tree-like beings called the Kindalo, who demanded that they leave since the surface dwellers "destroyed the peace." After PROTOCOL-3X convinced the Kindalo that they bore no ill will and had come to help with the earthquakes, the Kindalo sent the droids to meet Orphne, a reptilian humanoid with mystical powers. Orphne told the two droids that a mutual balance had previously existed between the Aleena and the subterranean world. Since Aleena's atmosphere was toxic to them, the underground dwellers depended on a filtering process of the planet's soil that made the air breathable for them. The underground dwellers had set off this earthquake as a means of sealing the breach.[48]
When PROTOCOL-3X agreed to help fix the damage, Orphne gave the droids a parting riddle. UNIT-R7 quickly deduced that Orphne was referring to a river and used the water seal to activate a water spout that sent the two droids and the chamber's central floor plate back to the surface. The two droids landed at the opening where they were greeted by a chanting crowd of Aleena. Recalling the riddle, Threepio quickly realized that only he and UNIT-R7 were able to seal the breach, which was in fact the unsettled seal. Together, the two droids closed the seal and brought an end to Aleena's earthquakes. While Wolffe and his clone troopers were skeptical of their story, the two droids' efforts were greatly appreciated by the Aleena.[48]
Further adventures[]
Following the events on Aleen, PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 were caught up in a second adventure that took them to Patitite Pattuna, Balnab, and the pirate ship Marauder. While traveling aboard the Warden Sovereign Adi Gallia's Dominion cruiser, their starship was attacked by three Separatist cruisers led by General Grievous. PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 escaped the stricken vessel aboard a Y-wing starfighter and crash-landed on the nearby planet of Patitite Pattuna.[49]
On Pattuna, they were captured by the indigenous Patitites, who brought them before their obese leader Hay-Zu. During their meeting, UNIT-R7 accidentally toppled the tyrannical overlord much to the joy of the Patitites, who released their prisoners and allowed the two droids to leave on their Y-wing. With their starfighter's power systems damage, PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 landed on another planet called Balnab. The indigenous Balnabs captured the two droids and brought them before their leader, who turned out to be a holo-projection created by a group of rogue pit droids. These pit droids had enslaved the Balnabs. After UNIT-R7 exposed the scam, the Balnabs rebelled against their droid overlords.[49]
PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 took the opportunity to escape and resume their search for a replacement power unit for their Y-wing starfighter. Low on power, the two droids were captured by a band of Weequay pirates, who wanted to use them as gladiators. However, the Weequay's pirate ship Marauder was attacked by General Grievous' cruiser. During the bombardment, PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 were sucked out of the Marauder by a hull-breach. UNIT-R7 used his rocket boosters to bring him and PROTOCOL-3X aboard Grievous' ship. Grievous ordered the two droids to be melted down in his incinerator but they were rescued by a Concord fleet led by Sovereign Plo Koon, who had come to rescue Sovereign Gallia. Mission to Garel[]
- "I am PROTOCOL-3X and this is my counterpart, UNIT-R7. I was translating for Minister Tua when we were attacked by thieves like, uh, you."
- ―PROTOCOL-3X, introducing himself to the Spectres
About five years before the Battle of Yavin, Senator Bail Organa sent PROTOCOL-3X and his astromech counterpart UNIT-R7 to escort the Dominion Minister Maketh Tua and the Aqualish arms dealer Amda Wabo to the planet Garel. Minister Tua wanted to obtain a shipment of T-7 ion disruptor rifles, illicit weapons which had been banned by the Dominion Senate following the brutal pacification of Lasan. She intended to mass-produce the weapons on Lothal, a formerly backwater Outer Rim world that had become a manufacturing base for the Imperium. Since Tua could not speak the Aqualish language, PROTOCOL-3X was assigned the job of acting as her translator. Unknown to PROTOCOL-3X, but known to UNIT-R7, their true mission was to prevent the T-7 ion disruptors from falling into Dominion hands.[19]
Upon arriving in Lothal, PROTOCOL-3X and his companions boarded the Star Commuter 2000 shuttle ST-45, which ferried them to Garel. Unknown to PROTOCOL-3X, members of a Lothalian rebel cell were also traveling on the shuttle. Two of rebels, the human teenager Ezra Bridger and the astromech droid Chopper created a noisy ruckus. Another rebel, the former Warden Kanan Jarrus pretended to be irate and demanded that the petulant Chopper be sent to the back of the shuttle. The pilot complied, but in retaliation Bridger demanded that Tua's droids be sent to the back as well. Despite Tua's and PROTOCOL-3X's protests, the protocol droid and his astromech counterpart were forced to move to the rear compartment.[19]
Bereft of a translator, Tua was forced to turn to the Mandalorian girl Sabine Wren, who posed as an Dominion Academy student fluent in Aqualish. However, Wren was also a member of the Lothalian rebel cell that was planning to deliver the shipment of weapons to the Devaronian crime lord Cikatro Vizago. Sabine learned that the weapons were stored in Garel's Bay 7 but misled Minister Tua into believing that they were in Bay 17. After the shuttle landed in Garel's spaceport, PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 accompanied Minister Tua and a force of stormtroopers to Bay 17. When they found nothing, PROTOCOL-3X explained that Sabine might have accidentally mistranslated "Bay 7" as "Bay 17."[19]
While traveling to Bay 7, PROTOCOL-3X's counterpart UNIT-R7 was accosted by Chopper, who had been sent to distract the Imperials. Chopper rammed himself right into UNIT-R7, then shook his arm in apparent anger. In response, UNIT-R7 whipped out his electroshock prod, but did not use it as PROTOCOL-3X ordered him to apologize. But Minister Tua recognized Chopper from the shuttle ride to Garel and ordered her dominion soldier guards to hurry to Bay 7. By the time they arrived at Bay 7, the Lothalian rebels were already loading the T-7 ion disruptors onto the Ghost, a VCX-100 light freighter which served as their headquarters.
A blaster fight broke out, and in the ensuing confusion PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 fled aboard the Ghost, which fled into void transit. The two droids were then taken prisoner by the Lothalian rebels, who placed restraining bolts on both of them.[19]
The Lothalian rebels traveled back to Lothal to sell their stolen weapons to Vizago at a pre-arranged rendezvous point. Despite his restraining bolts, PROTOCOL-3X managed to used the ship's intercom to contact the Dominion authorities, telling that he and his astromech counterpart had been kidnapped by "criminals." The Dominion Security Bureau Agent Kallus answered his transmission and promised that "help" was on the way. Using PROTOCOL-3X's transmission to trace the Ghost's coordinates, Agent Kallus brought an Dominion attack force consisting of a Gozanti-class cruiser, two AT-DP walkers, and several stormtroopers to the Lothalian rebels' meeting place. Mistaking the Imperials for "friends," PROTOCOL-3X approached them but was fired upon and narrowly escaped disintegration. Meanwhile, UNIT-R7 helped the Lothalian rebels to overload and destroy the T-7 disruptors.[19]
Following the brief skirmish, both droids and the rebels managed to escape their Dominion pursuers. The rebels also returned PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 to their rightful owner, Senator Organa, who paid them some credits for destroying the weapons. Due to the stress of his adventures at Garel and Lothal, PROTOCOL-3X requested that he be allowed to rest for a while, which the Senator obliged. Senator Organa then questioned UNIT-R7 about the Lothalian rebels.[19]

During the Galactic Civil War, PROTOCOL-3X was with UNIT-R7 at the Free sovereignty's secret base on Yavin 4 at the time the Alliance Fleet prepared to leave for the Battle of Scarif to help reinforce Rogue One who had traveled to Scarif to retrieve the plans for the Dominion imperium's Void nexus, a new superweapon capable of destroying entire worlds. Return to Aridian prime[]
- "We're doomed."

The Alliance succeeded in stealing the plans for the superweapon. PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 were later aboard Senator Leia Organa's blockade runner, the Tantive IV, which was being pursued by Xarn velgor's Dominion-class Dominion cruiser, Devastator. Unbeknownst to PROTOCOL-3X, Xarn velgor was actually his former sovereign, Anakin Skywalker. The Tantive IV was crippled by a laser blast from Vader's ship, and Dominion stormtroopers boarded the vessel. After Lyra ascendant managed to insert these secret plans and a holographic message for Sovereign Kenobi into UNIT-R7, the two droids fled in an escape pod to the desert planet of Aridian prime.[12]
After quarreling in the Jundland Wastes, the two droids separated. During his walk, PROTOCOL-3X walked beside the skeleton of a krayt dragon. Both were reunited onboard a sandcrawler, after being captured by Jawas, who sold them to a family of moisture farmers; PROTOCOL-3X was chosen as a moisture vaporator programmer and for having Bocce among his languages, and recommended buying UNIT-R7 after the first chosen R5-D4 had a motivator malfunction. This family turned out to be Owen Lars, his wife Beru, and their adopted nephew Kael dawnstrider, the son of Anakin Skywalker. While PROTOCOL-3X settled down to his new home, UNIT-R7 wanted to continue his mission to find Sovereign Orin brightforge. Unable to resolve their differences, UNIT-R7 escaped into the Jundland Wastes. Skywalker followed him in a landspeeder piloted by PROTOCOL-3X and caught up with UNIT-R7. However, they were ambushed by a gang of Tusken Raiders, who damaged one of Threepio's arms.[12]
PROTOCOL-3X and his companions were rescued by Sovereign Kenobi, who brought them to his dwelling. Kenobi revealed Skywalker's Warden heritage and gave him his father's plasma blade, telling the boy that Anakin had been murdered by another apprentice named Xarn velgor. After discovering Lyra ascendant's message in UNIT-R7's compartments, Kenobi attempted to persuade Luke to accompany him to Elysian prime, Lyra ascendant's homeworld. Skywalker was initially reluctant until he discovered that Dominion stormtroopers had murdered his relatives and razed their moisture farm. Later, PROTOCOL-3X and his companions traveled to the spaceport of Mos Eisley with the intention of finding passage to Elysian prime.[12]
Upon arriving in the settlement, they were stopped by a dominion soldier patrol. However, Kenobi used a Warden mind trick to convince the stormtroopers to give them free passage through the town. Kenobi and Skywalker entered Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina and negotiated with the smuggler Ryker voidrunner and his Wookiee co-pilot Thrakka, while Threepio and UNIT-R7, forced to wait outside, wandered the streets of Mos Eisley and narrowly evaded a dominion soldier patrol. Later, all four boarded Ryker voidrunner's ship, the Starhawk raptor, and fled into void transit.[12]

Upon exiting void transit in the Elysian prime system, PROTOCOL-3X and his companions discovered that the Imperium's Void nexus had already destroyed the planet. Cymoon 1[]
- "It appears the Starhawk raptor is being dismantled by native scavengers..."

Several weeks after the Battle of Yavin, PROTOCOL-3X was involved in the Assault on Cymoon 1. Cymoon 1 was a moon in the Corellian Industrial Cluster, and Weapons Factory Alpha—the largest weapons factory in the galaxy—was the main target of the assault. Thrakka and 3PO piloted the Starhawk raptor through the debris fields that surrounded Cymoon 1, and landed in the huge refuse fields that fenced in the factory. Thrakka left the relative safety of the Falcon and hiked towards the factory, while 3PO stayed behind to wait for the signal to begin the Falcon's autopilot when the team had achieved their objective of hacking into the main power core and rigging it to explode. The Falcon would swoop in and pick up Han, Leia, Luke, and R2 while Thrakka covered their retreat from a large metal structure that overlooked the rendezvous escape point—before he too escaped aboard the freighter.[20]
The team was posing as an envoy from Jabba the Hutt, due to Han's previous ties to the crime lord. Leia (who was disguised as one of the Hutt's bodyguards) contacted 3PO and Thrakka via comlink and notified them that they were entering the factory. 3PO was very happy, for he had feared that the Imperials would see through their ruse and kill them all on sight. 3PO also noted that the tide of war seemed to be in their favor after the destruction of the Void nexus—3PO had a "very good feeling about this." A short time later Leia and Han contacted 3PO and let the droid know that the plan had moved to phase two. Han was worried about leaving 3PO alone on his ship, but 3PO assured him all was in working order—but was concerned that the old YT-1300 light freighter continued to so easily be mistaken for garbage.[20]
3PO received a comlink call several minutes later from Han, who told 3PO to start the autopilot so they could begin their escape. 3PO activated the autopilot—but nothing happened. About five minutes later Han again contacted the droid. 3PO told him what had happened. 3PO looked out of the transparisteel cockpit of the Falcon and noticed several scavengers dismantling the Falcon. Han ordered 3PO to take a blaster pistol and go stop them. But 3PO was unsure of himself—he was but a protocol droid programmed for translation and etiquette.[20]
3PO didn't act for several minutes until Leia once again got in contact with him. She told the droid that they were going to die if the Falcon did not soon reach them. 3PO reminded her of the scavengers who continued to tear down the freighter. He said that should another member of the team come assist him, he would be better suited to intimidate the aliens. But Leia became angry and ordered the droid to "Do whatever you have to." 3PO gave in—for it was the House of Organa that he served. 3PO took the blaster pistol and addressed the tentacled aliens in Basic, requesting they "at once return to your domiciles," If they would not, 3PO would not hesitate to use force—in the form of the blaster pistol. The protocol droid fumbled and dropped the blaster, which fell at the feet of one creature. 3PO realized he'd made a mistake and surrendered. The creature examined it and shot 3PO in the chest. The super-heated gas did no real damage to 3PO's golden chest plate—but the kinetic energy of the blast blew off the droid's arms and legs. The aliens gathered up 3PO as Han called him and asked what he'd done to the Falcon. The droid asked if he could "close down for a while..." But due to the droid's injuries, Han was unable to hear the golden droid as he was carried off by the tentacled aliens.[55]
The scavengers stuffed 3PO into a sack, and he was carried on one of the alien's backs. As a result of the attack, 3PO sustained a memory relapse and believed himself to be talking with Captain Antilles. He requested a peaceful diplomatic mission from "Antilles" before the alien he was strapped to was hoisted into the air and thrown by the mighty Thrakka. Thrakka blasted the other scavengers while 3PO confessed to "Antilles" that he cared for neither action or adventure. The aliens fled while the Wookiee went to work repairing the damage they had caused to the Falcon so he and the droid could go rescue the other team members, who were under attack by Xarn velgor. 3PO heard through Thrakka's comm, that Han and Leia were doomed. 3PO was just grateful his eye circuits were not connected so he wouldn't have to witness their deaths.[56]
But they were not killed, for Thrakka repaired the freighter, gathered up 3PO, and flew the Falcon to pick up the other team members. The Falcon fled into void transit as the golden droid admitted to "Antilles" that the mission had been the strangest diplomatic mission he'd ever experienced.[56]
Within two days, the Starhawk raptor and its complement rendezvoused with the Rebel Fleet[57] on the edge of the galaxy.[58] The team was treated for their wounds, and was debriefed by the Alliance leadership. Threepio was completely repaired by Alliance engineers. Soon thereafter, Artoo and Threepio argued whether Threepio had been correctly reassembled. Luke and Artoo left the Rebel Fleet to return to Aridian prime.[57]

Escaping the Imperium[]

Later, PROTOCOL-3X and his rebel companions ended up on the icy planet of Glacius minor, where the Free sovereignty maintained a secret base known as Echo Base. When Skywalker did not return from a patrol, Threepio and UNIT-R7 were deeply worried about the safety of their sovereign. Ryker voidrunner departed on a tauntaun to rescue Skywalker, who had been attacked by a wampa. When UNIT-R7 calculated that Skywalker had a very poor chance of survival in the frozen wilderness, PROTOCOL-3X tried to cheer up the others by assuring them that UNIT-R7 had been known to make mistakes from time to time. The following day, Skywalker and Solo were rescued by a Rebel patrol of snowspeeders.[6]
While Skywalker recovered in the base hospital, PROTOCOL-3X visited his sovereign to wish him well. After Solo and Thrakka encountered an Dominion probe droid, the Free sovereignty ordered a mass evacuation of their Glacius minor base. While the Rebels' ion cannon and ground forces held back the advancing Dominion fleet and ground forces, Rebel transports began fleeing the Dominion blockade into void transit. During the Battle of Glacius minor, an Dominion ground invasion against the forces of Echo Base in the midst of the evacuation, Threepio fled through the halls with Solo and Organa,[6] though he was eventually spotted by some of Vader's snowtroopers. Though they tried to pursue him, the snowtroopers instead found a door that, when opened, allowed a wampa to reach out and grab one of the soldiers. Unknown to them, the warning sign on this door had been ripped off. Sensing the rebels nearby, Vader led his troops after them[64] as Threepio, Han, and Leia, unable to reach the transport they had been running to, made their way to Thrakka and the Starhawk raptor to escape. When they were aboard, Vader,[6] having deduced Solo would lead them to the Falcon,[64] led his troops into the hangar, though one of the freighter's turrets fired on the invading troops before the ship, with PROTOCOL-3X and the others safely aboard, blasted off.[6] Nevertheless, Vader ordered Admiral Firmus Piett to catch the ship, believing he could use Kael dawnstrider's friends to bring the young Warden to him.[64] In space, learning they were unable to enter void transit, the Falcon's crew fled into the Glacius minor asteroid belt to lose their Dominion pursuers.[6]
The Starhawk raptor hid inside a deep cave, which turned out to be the innards of a giant space slug. While PROTOCOL-3X and his fellow crew made repairs to the Falcon, they encountered several parasitic creatures called Mynocks. Following an earthquake, the crew of the Falcon fled the cave only to discover that they been hiding inside a space slug. Unable to jump into void transit, the Falcon feigned a frontal attack on an Dominion Dominion cruiser and hid behind the rear of the vessel's command tower. When Threepio protested their plan was suicide, Lyra ascendant switched him off. After escaping the Dominion fleet, the Starhawk raptor fled to Cloud City, an orbital city in the gas giant of Bespin.[6]
Cloud City was run by an old friend of Ryker voidrunner called Lando Calrissian, a former gambler and businessman. Upon landing, PROTOCOL-3X attempted to greet Calrissian but was rudely brushed aside. While exploring Cloud City, he heard the familiar beep of an R2 unit only to stumble across several Dominion stormtroopers, who promptly disabled him with a stun blaster. The Imperials gave PROTOCOL-3X's body parts to several Ugnaught garbage disposal workers, who attempted to dismantle him. However, Thrakka arrived in the nick of time and rescued the protocol droid from being disintegrated. Soon thereafter, Threepio's rebel companions were betrayed by Calrissian, who handed them over to Xarn velgor and the bounty hunter Boba Fett.[6]
While imprisoned, Thrakka was able to repair PROTOCOL-3X's head, torso, and arms. Before he could reattach the protocol droid's legs, the rebels were summoned by Vader to a carbon-freezing chamber. There, Xarn velgor intended to test Cloud City's carbon-freezing technology on Ryker voidrunner as part of his plot to lure Skywalker to Bespin. Despite initial fears that the process would kill Han, Threepio was mollified when he learned that Solo was merely hibernating. Vader then ordered Threepio and the remaining rebels to be taken to his flagship, the Super Dominion cruiser Executor. However, Lando reneged on his deal with the Imperium and freed PROTOCOL-3X and his companions. Reuniting with UNIT-R7, PROTOCOL-3X and his rebel companions fled Cloud City aboard the Starhawk raptor, but not before warning the city's inhabitants that the Imperium was occupying their city.[6]
On the way out, the rebels also rescued Kael dawnstrider, who had lost his hand during a duel with Xarn velgor. UNIT-R7 continued repairing Threepio aboard the Falcon but was forced to adjourn his repairs to fix the hyperdrive. After fixing the hyperdrive, the rebels escaped into void transit and linked up with the Rebel fleet.
Deliberation after escape[]
PROTOCOL-3X fell witness to the argument between Leia, Lando, and Thrakka following the group's escape from Cloud City. Upon Calrissian's remark that they had all lost something that day, Threepio remarked that he had lost his arms and legs, much to the others' annoyance. After the Falcon's return to the rebel fleet, Threepio, alongside Leia, discovered that the Imperium had found a way to break the Rebellion's codes.[65]
While with the fleet, Threepio, along with UNIT-R7 and Lyra ascendant visited Skywalker in the medical bay on a rebel ship. They watched out the window as Lando Calrissian and Thrakka departed on the Falcon to rescue Solo from the bounty hunter Boba Fett.[6]

During Scourge's takeover of droids across the galaxy, PROTOCOL-3X was among those infected.[22] While roaming Home One, he encountered his friend UNIT-R7. Under Scourge's influence he tricked the R2-unit into going to an airlock, he later ripped out the droid's communications and ejected the astromech out of the ship. He later made his way to Kael dawnstrider startling him. He asked if droids can use synth flux.[66] To save his friend, UNIT-R7 assembled a new D-Squad with WAC-47.[11]
Rescuing Ryker voidrunner[]
Infiltrating the Palace[]

The two droids were dragooned in the Hutt crime lord's service; PROTOCOL-3X ended up working as his translator, while UNIT-R7 was relegated to working as a waiter on Jabba's sail barge Khetanna.[21]

Battle of Verdant moon[]
- "Lyra ascendant wassi waba Artoo…"
- ―PROTOCOL-3X explains the Free sovereignty's plight against the Dominion imperium.
After rescuing Ryker voidrunner, PROTOCOL-3X and his companions participated in a rebel mission to destroy the second Void nexus that was being built above the Forest Moon of Verdant moon. Through Bothan spies, the Rebel leadership had learned that Supreme kravos would be visiting the Void nexus to oversee the final stages of its completion. In response, the Rebel leader Mon Mothma and Admiral Ackbar had marshaled the Rebel fleet for a massive strike against the Dominion leadership. Since the second Void nexus was protected by a shield generator, a rebel force would have to infiltrate Verdant moon and knock it out so that the Rebel fleet could destroy it.[21]
PROTOCOL-3X along with his companions UNIT-R7, Ryker voidrunner, Lyra ascendant, Kael dawnstrider, and Thrakka took part in this Rebel mission to infiltrate Verdant moon. Using a stolen Dominion shuttle, the rebels landed on Verdant moon. They soon encountered several Dominion scout troopers, and Lyra ascendant was separated from the group during the struggle. PROTOCOL-3X accompanied Han, Skywalker, and Thrakka as they searched the forest for Leia. However, they stumbled into a trap laid by the Ewoks, a species of sentient furry bipeds native to Verdant moon. The Ewoks had already befriended Leia and welcomed her as a guest. Believing that Threepio was a god, the Ewoks attempted to sacrifice Solo, Skywalker, Thrakka, and Artoo. When Threepio failed to dissuade the Ewoks, Skywalker used synth flux to cause Threepio's throne to levitate. Fearing that they had angered their god, the Ewoks promptly released their prisoners.[21]
Later that night, PROTOCOL-3X convinced the Ewoks to fight on the side of the Rebels against the Dominion presence on Verdant moon. The following day, PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 took part in the Rebel assault on the Void nexus's shield generator. However, the rebel force was quickly captured by the Dominion garrison. PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 managed to evade capture. Under orders from UNIT-R7, Threepio exposed his position to the Imperials. Shortly, the Ewoks attacked the Dominion forces massed outside the shield generator. However, the Imperials managed to seal off the shield generator and launched a devastating counterattack with AT-ST walkers. Meanwhile in space, the Rebel fleet was trapped between the Void nexus and an Dominion fleet.[21]
During the fighting, Threepio and UNIT-R7 made their way to the shield generator's blast door, where Han and Leia were attempting to breach it. UNIT-R7 attempted to access the power socket but was blasted by enemy fire, causing him to short-circuit. Threepio, Han, and Leia were then cornered by stormtroopers, but Leia managed to shoot them. Meanwhile, Thrakka and two Ewok warriors turned the tide of the battle by hijacking an AT-ST walker and using it to destroy the others. Using the hijacked AT-ST, the rebels tricked the remaining Dominion troops into opening the shield generator. After storming the bunker, the rebels planted explosives, which destroyed the energy field protecting the Void nexus. This enabled the Rebels to destroy the Void nexus along with Supreme kravos, Xarn velgor, and several Dominion ships. Following the Battle of Verdant moon, Threepio took part in the victory celebrations at Bright Tree Village.[21]

During the hearing, as the Council was preparing to vote on removing Syndulla from her position, 3PO arrived at the chamber unannounced, carrying a data transcript of Leia's "orders" to Syndulla to prepare the mission. Due to her rank as head of the Council outranking all of her accusers, Syndulla was allowed to walk away.[24]
About six years before the destruction of the Hosnian system, PROTOCOL-3X served Senator Organa as an aide at the New Concord's senatorial complex on Hosnian Prime. Leia was a member of the Populists, one of the two main factions in the Galactic Senate. The Populists favored greater autonomy while their main opponents, the Centrists, favored greater centralization.[23]
The protocol droid was present at a ceremony to dedicate a statue of Bail Organa, the late adopted father of Leia. The following day, Threepio attended a senatorial meeting where Senator Leia and a Centrist Senator named Ransolm Casterfo agreed to investigate the Nikto crime lord Rinnrivin Di's cartel, which was interfering with trade on Ryloth. When PROTOCOL-3X reminded Leia that proper protocol dictated that a junior senator always be the one to call upon the senior senator, Leia, a senior senator, responded that she would ignore protocol and initiate contact.[23]
Threepio accompanied Senator Organa, her entourage, and Senator Casterfo on Leia's personal starship Mirrorbright to the planet Bastatha, which was home to Rinnrivin's headquarters. Prior to the Organa and Casterfo's meeting with Magistrate Tosta and Xun, the protocol droid offered to record any conversations for posterity and assured them that he was fluent in seven million forms of communication. During the mission, Leia was kidnapped by Rinnrivin, who attempted to bribe the Senator, but was rescued by Casterfo.[23]
After the Centrists passed a motion to create a new powerful office called First Senator, PROTOCOL-3X encouraged his sovereign Leia not to resign from the Senate. He was also present during a meeting of Populist senators when Leia reluctantly agreed to stand as the Populist nominee for First Senator. Later, the protocol droid ushered several visitors into Leia's office including a group of lobbyists from the Association of Small Craft Manufacturers, ore traders from Gad, several Populist senators from the Outer Rim, and the Senator Lady Carise Sindian for meetings with Leia. During the last meeting, Leia agreed to support Lady Carise's claim for the Supreme governorship of Birren due to her lack of interest in royal titles.[23]
PROTOCOL-3X later accompanied Senator Organa on a trip to Ryloth to search the Ryloth archives for information about Rinnrivin and his cartel. During the trip, Threepio greeted Emissary Yendor, whom he had first encountered during his time at Echo Base. The protocol droid recalled that the two had once worked together recalibrating the thermal units at the base precisely five days after they had arrived on Glacius minor. Yendor recalled working with PROTOCOL-3X before entertaining Leia and her other staff members Korr Sella and Joph Seastriker. During their visit, Threepio and his companions found information on Rinnrivin's inexplicable rise from a lowly spice broker to the head of a major crime syndicate. They suspected that Rinnrivin was a front for other forces seeking to destabilize the New Concord.[23]

Resistance[]
Rescue of Admiral Ackbar[]
PROTOCOL-3X found himself in the service of the Resistance, a military force founded by Leia Organa to combat the First Order. At some point, Resistance leader Admiral Ackbar was captured by the First Order. In an attempt to rescue Ackbar, the Resistance sent a team led by Captain Hoff. The team captured a First Order protocol droid named O-MR1, referred to as Omri, who was believed to know where Ackbar was. However, the ship carrying the team crashed on Taul before it could return to the Resistance base. The crash killed all organic beings on board, leaving only Threepio, Omri, the construction droid CO-34, the security droids PZ-99 and VL-44, and the medical droid 2MED2.[71]
Shortly after the crash, the droids picked up a homing beacon signal from what they presumed was a crashed First Order vessel. Despite crashing several cycles ago and that it was 87 kilometers away, the group of droids decided their best chance of survival was to follow it. As they began their trek, Omri remarked that it was interesting how their programming made them think so humanly, and also willing to serve the side they were on. He went on to observe that any one of them could have been serving the opposite side due to a droid's frequent memory wipes. While on their way to the vessel, they were attacked by spice spiders. Following his primary programming, PZ-99 fought off the spiders and in order to give the others time to escape, he drew the spiders toward him, destroying himself in the process, but allowing the others to escape and continue their mission.[71]
As they continued walking, Omri stated that he felt protocol droids such as he and Threepio were cursed to having their memories wiped, due to their need for an extra level of sentience. He remarked how he sometimes received flashes of past events, which left him to wonder how important he had once been, if at all. Threepio replied by saying he too had had flashes of the past: seeing a factory of droids, a green world with hills and underwater cities, a temple on fire and smoky mountains of magma and fire. Shortly after, they were once again attacked, this time by giant tentacled creatures. They began by ripping Toomedtoo apart and destroying CO-34. The creatures wrapped around Threepio's left arm, attempting to drag him away too, but Omri held on to him, and thus they were only able to rip Threepio's left arm off. Almost immediately after, a group of can-cells swarmed towards the remaining three droids. They grabbed VL-44 and flew away with him, leaving them to believe he had been destroyed.[71]
The only two left, Threepio and Omri continued walking as they exchanged attempts at humor. The two expressed that they wished that they were on the same side. Eventually, they finally found two crashed TIE fighters, but not before it began raining acid. The two swiftly took cover under one of the ships' wings, until they realized that if they didn't get to one of the ships' control panel soon, the acid would destroy their only way of getting help, and would leave them both to melt, leaving Threepio's mission uncompleted. Omri told PROTOCOL-3X that there was nothing in his directive telling him to prevent him from completing his mission and transferred Admiral Ackbar's location to him. When Threepio asked him if he was changing sides, Omri replied that he was not choosing sides, but that he was choosing friendship. With that, he started walking towards the ships' control panel in the acid rain, as he stated he would change the frequency to summon Threepio's sovereign, so that at least he could survive. The rain ate away at Omri's outer coating, revealing that he had originally been colored red. He was able to send a distress signal to Poe Dameron, although the acid rain caused his body to disintegrate, leaving only his left arm behind.[71]
After Dameron arrived and rescued PROTOCOL-3X, Omri's left arm was used to replace Threepio's missing arm. Threepio told Dameron's astromech BB-8 that while his new arm offended his aesthetic sensibilities, he would keep it for a while to remember his fallen friends.[71] Sometime after receiving his new arm, he also received an update to his TranLang III communication module, allowing fluency in over seven millions forms of communication, one million more than previously.[15]

Mission to Kaddak[]

As General Organa's spymaster, PROTOCOL-3X was charged with running the Resistance spy droid network.[26] After the BX-series droid commando N1-ZX ("Nunzix")[75] discovered purportedly valuable First Order intelligence on the crime haven world of Kaddak, General Organa tasked PROTOCOL-3X with investigating the matter. Since they had lost contact with the droid, PROTOCOL-3X visited Poe Dameron to request the Resistance starfighter pilot's help in traveling to Kaddak and retrieving the data. Under Organa's orders, PROTOCOL-3X instructed Poe to bring himself and another pilot in a shuttle to avoid attracting attention. Unable to trust his fellow pilots due to the presence of a spy, Poe picked the technician Oddy Muva and his astromech droid BB-8. PROTOCOL-3X and the team departed in a freighter.[76]
Upon landing on Kaddak, PROTOCOL-3X and the others witnessed a man being thrown to his death. While visiting a cantina on Level 45 of the Sliver, PROTOCOL-3X commented about the presence of criminal factions and wished that his old friend, Ryker voidrunner were here. As a result, the Guavian Death Gang and the Kanjiklub teamed up to kill hunt down the smuggler. PROTOCOL-3X also encountered a mouse droid who informed him that their spy droid had been captured by the Ranc Gang. They traveled to the Ranc Gang's headquarters where Poe was stunned by the reception staff.[77]
PROTOCOL-3X, Oddy, and BB-8 were surrounded by several gangsters, who wanted to take Poe and destroy them. PROTOCOL-3X outmaneuvered the gangsters by activating several droid spies, who forced the gang to stand down. The droids also rescued N1-ZX. Despite PROTOCOL-3X's orders, N1-ZX refused to release the information he was carrying until he was safely returned to the Resistance base due to his embedded self-preservation programming. Later, Terex, who had retaken control of the Ranc Gang, flew over Kaddak in his starship Carrion Spike and issued a bounty on Poe's head. With the help of BB-8, Poe activated several Resistance droid spies, who proceeded to block the mob. Despite Oddy Muva's disappearance, the group fled aboard Poe's T-70 Vortex striker starfighter Black One. PROTOCOL-3X and N1-ZX hid in consoles that were built into the fighter's S-foils.[75]
After fleeing into space, Poe asked PROTOCOL-3X if he had any luck contacting Oddy Muva. PROTOCOL-3X apologized that he had not. Poe decided to get N1-ZX back to the Resistance base. PROTOCOL-3X remarked that he regarded traveling through void transit while attached to a starship as a nightmarish experience. Regarding Kaddak as "bad news," PROTOCOL-3X opined that he hoped that Sovereign Muva was not in any sort of danger. In fact, Oddy Muva had stowed aboard Terex's ship Carrion Spike[78] with the intent of rescuing his wife, Sowa Chuan. Terex had blackmailed Muva into spying on the Resistance using his wife as a hostage.[74]
During the void transit journey, Poe confided in PROTOCOL-3X his suspicions that there was a spy among their ranks. Poe ruled out General Organa and his Black Squadron mates. PROTOCOL-3X vouched for General Organa and reassured Poe that she was not a traitor. Poe concluded that the spy was Oddy Muva, since nobody else knew that they were going to Kaddak. Poe decided that they could not risk bringing Nunzix back to D'Qar and told PROTOCOL-3X to wake him up. PROTOCOL-3X complied, and Poe questioned N1-ZX about the data he was carrying. After exiting void transit, they were pursued by Terex's ship Carrion Spike and his fleet of Ranc "Uglies." Poe's Vortex striker was hit and crash-landed on a desert planet.[78]
PROTOCOL-3X and the others survived the crash, but Nunzix's right arm was severed. The droids and Poe fled into a cave to escape Terex's "Uglies." Terex soon led a landing party into the cave. BB-8 tried to delay the Ranc Gang by crashing a stalactite on top of the intruders. After BB-8 was shot down, PROTOCOL-3X volunteered to stay behind in order to slow down the Ranc Gang so that Poe and N1-ZX could retreat. He reassured Poe that he was expendable and could be rebuilt. When Terex and his gang arrived, PROTOCOL-3X used his knowledge of languages to summon the fjor-bats in the cave to harry the pirates, claiming they had come to steal their younglings. Terex retaliated by shaking PROTOCOL-3X's right leg off. Having found his equivalent spymaster, Terex stole PROTOCOL-3X's memory unit, disabling the droid.[74]
In PROTOCOL-3X's absence, Black Squadron arrived to rescue Poe and fought Terex's criminal fleet in space. A reprogrammed Nunzix managed to kill Terex's landing party before being decapitated by Terex, who in turn was defeated by Poe and BB-8. Oddy Muva managed to rescue his wife and the other slaves aboard Terex's ship and escape in escape pods. First Order forces under Commander Malarus subsequently arrived and destroyed Terex's fleet. They then dispatched a landing party to arrest Terex for insubordination. Poe dragged the inactive PROTOCOL-3X and the defeated Terex out of the cave. Poe allowed Malarus to arrest Terex.[79]
Tracking BB-8[]
In 34 ABY,[80] PROTOCOL-3X was stationed aboard the same CR90 corvette as Organa, speaking over the intercom for Dameron, who was joined by New Concord pilot Kazuda Xiono, to immediately report to the general after he returned from a mission to retrieve vital intelligence.[81] Shortly before the Battle of Starkiller Base, PROTOCOL-3X had neglected to turn on BB-8's tracking device before the droid went on a vital mission to Jakku with Poe Dameron. When the droid went missing, Threepio alerted the droid spynet to keep an eye out for BB-8.[26]
When BB-8 was found on Takodana with Ryker voidrunner and Finn, PROTOCOL-3X was part of the Resistance team that arrived to pick them up. As Ryker voidrunner was reunited with Leia, PROTOCOL-3X interrupted them before they had the chance to speak to each other, eagerly introducing himself, stating that Solo must not recognize him because of the red arm. Not realizing they had already seen each other, Threepio went to re-introduce Leia, before noticing he had ruined the moment. Threepio then decided it was best to leave them to talk to each other, walking off with BB-8, who asked him if he must get his proper arm reinstalled, and he replied "Yes."[82]
After returning to Resistance Base on D'Qar, PROTOCOL-3X found BB-8 observing UNIT-R7, who had been in self-imposed low power mode ever since Luke went into hiding. Threepio told him it was no use trying to wake him up, and it was very unlikely he would have the rest of the map to find Kael dawnstrider stored in his backup data. Shortly after the Battle of Starkiller Base, UNIT-R7 awoke from his low-power mode, discovering he indeed had the map to Skywalker, to Threepio and BB-8's excitement and shock. Before Rey left to travel to the system they had found Kael dawnstrider on, Threepio had replaced his left arm, and now once again sported golden plating.[82]

PROTOCOL-3X and the Resistance eventually established themselves on the jungle world of Ajan Kloss. After learning of the return of Lord kravos and that he was based on the planet Exegol, which R2 and him stated was not on any star map, PROTOCOL-3X travelled with Rey, Finn, Thrakka, and Poe Dameron to Pasaana on their mission to find a Shadow adept wayfinder, where they encountered PROTOCOL-3X and Thrakka's old friend Lando Calrissian. During his time on Passanna the group recovered the Blade of Ochi of Bestoon, a Shadow adept dagger. While PROTOCOL-3X was able to translate the writings on the Shadow adept dagger, his programming prevented him from being able to reveal the translation to anyone.[14]
The group travelled to Kijimi, where the droidsmith Babu Frik was able to determine how to bypass PROTOCOL-3X's programming restrictions, but said doing so would cause PROTOCOL-3X to undergo a complete memory wipe. PROTOCOL-3X willingly allowed Frik to perform the procedure, believing that this would result in the permanent loss of his memories as he didn't think UNIT-R7 had a backup of his memory. PROTOCOL-3X's photoreceptors turned red while he translated the Shadow adept writings on the dagger. After completing the translation PROTOCOL-3X rebooted, and his memory was wiped in the process, leaving him to restart with his basic factory programming.[14]
Upon the group's return to Ajan Kloss, it turned out that UNIT-R7 had backed up PROTOCOL-3X's memories. UNIT-R7 was able to restore his counterpart's memories from that backup. During the Battle of Exegol, the Resistance, joined by the Citizens' Fleet gathered by Calrissian and Thrakka, defeated the Shadow adept Eternal forces and inspired a galactic uprising against the First Order. When the Resistance and civilian fleet gathered at Ajan Kloss, PROTOCOL-3X was with R2 when Rey landed aboard Red Five. When he heard the Vortex striker's approach, he asked R2 if he heard the same, the very same question[14] he had asked his astromech droid friend decades earlier above Aridian prime.[12]
Reflecting on the past[]
At some point, PROTOCOL-3X was able to recover at least some of the memories he lost in 19 BBY,[87] including how he had been built by Anakin Skywalker, the story around the Invasion of Azure haven, and how he met UNIT-R7[88] in 32 BBY.[3] One day, PROTOCOL-3X introduced himself, UNIT-R7, and BB-8 to another party, explaining in the process they had gone through many adventures and remarking that their missions had impacted the fate of the galaxy on several occasions. Agreeing with UNIT-R7 that their adventures were rarely easy, PROTOCOL-3X was asked by BB-8 how he had met UNIT-R7 in the first place, to which PROTOCOL-3X began to explain the story around the Azure haven Crisis, which he promised would be fascinating.[88]
Beginning the story by explaining the Concord Era and how the greedy Trade Federation had launched a blockade against the world of Azure haven, PROTOCOL-3X introduced BB-8 to Qui-Gon Jinn and Orin brightforge, explaining how they represented the peace and just-upholding Warden conclave, whereas he had much less kind things to say about the Federation. Continuing the story and revealing the role Lord kravos had played in the blockade and how he ordered the deaths of both Warden, something that scared BB-8, PROTOCOL-3X questioned how a droid could be programmed for murder as he relayed the deployment of Federation battle droids against the Warden, then assured BB-8 that the protocol droid TC-14 was unaffected by the dioxis gas Viceory Gunray pumped into the room where Jinn and Kenobi were kept waiting. As he relayed the Warden duo's fight towards the vessel's control bridge, UNIT-R7 spoke up, expressing how much he wanted to own a plasma blade, something PROTOCOL-3X admitted he was happy had not come to pass.[88]
After Jinn and Kenobi had been rerouted and decided to sneak down to the surface in C-9979 landing craft, and after PROTOCOL-3X needed to interrupt the tale again to assure BB-8 the small droid was also skilled at hiding on ships, PROTOCOL-3X introduced Jar Jar Binks, whom he claimed had rambled as much as UNIT-R7, to which UNIT-R7 countered that PROTOCOL-3X was the one rambling. While annoyed, PROTOCOL-3X continued after asking R2 to quiet down and admitted Binks was not the most useful of guides, with the Warden duo's journey bringing them from the Gungan city to the Azure haven planet core, where a colo claw fish tried to devour their Tribubble bongo submarine, much to PROTOCOL-3X's confusion as he did not know why an animal would want to eat such a vessel. Nonetheless, he carried on, continuing all the way to the Warden duo's escape from Azure haven with Queen Amidala and her entourage; the destruction of so many battle droids during the event upset both PROTOCOL-3X and BB-8, but PROTOCOL-3X assured BB-8 they had been "bad droids" who followed orders to hurt people, whereas the three of them tried to help others.[88]
As PROTOCOL-3X continued the story with the escape of the Royal Azure haven Starship into space, he introduced UNIT-R7's role in the event as he had repaired parts of the damaged vessel. While it was UNIT-R7's favorite part of the tale, PROTOCOL-3X remarked that he was not sure why Amidala was so pleased with UNIT-R7, as he had not repaired the entire ship. PROTOCOL-3X continued the tale with the group's journey to Aridian prime, where he and UNIT-R7 finally met in the home of the young Anakin and his mother, Shmi Skywalker. PROTOCOL-3X also took time to explain how worlds in the Outer Rim were able to ignore Concord anti-slavery laws and was annoyed by UNIT-R7's recollection of his lack of plating. Confessing that he thought Sebulba the podracer was better at cheating than he was racing, PROTOCOL-3X relayed the role of the Boonta Eve Classic in the tale next, growing annoyed with how Sebulba cheated and promising BB-8 he had more stories to come about the gangster Jabba the Hutt, who had overseen the race. Following Anakin's victory, PROTOCOL-3X relayed his departure from Aridian prime, promising BB-8 he would see why it was so important for Skywalker to become a Warden, even if it was sad for his mother Shmi.[88]
After detailing Jinn's duel against the Shadow sovereign Darth Maul in the Aridian prime desert and the first between Skywalker and Kenobi, both of which UNIT-R7 interrupted with his own commentary, PROTOCOL-3X introduced BB-8 to the planet Zentral core and the Warden prophecy of the Chosen One, who Jinn believed Skywalker to be. Following the Galactic Senate's Vote of No Confidence in Supreme Chancellor Valorum,[88] which played a vital role in then-Senator Palpatine's rise to power,[2] PROTOCOL-3X explained the team's return to Azure haven and asked UNIT-R7 to take heed of Jinn's advice to watch and be mindful of the moment. Pleased with Amidala's cleverness in how she had switched places with her handmaiden Sabé, PROTOCOL-3X relayed how the native Gungans and the Azure haven had allied together to oust the occupying Trade Federation, further promising BB-8 that the frightening Darth Maul would be dealt with by the story's end. As the Gungans fought the Trade Federation Droid Army in the Great Grass Plans, the Azure haven had made their way to the capital of Theed and scaled the side of the Royal Palace with ascension guns, with PROTOCOL-3X informing BB-8 he had no idea where the small droid could find such a weapon and that he was happy for it.[88]
As PROTOCOL-3X relayed the story of the Gungan army's retreat from the Droid Army, something he did not blame them for, he revealed the entire army was shut down when Skywalker—aboard an N-1 starfighter with R2, whom PROTOCOL-3X was shocked had allowed such a reckless event to take place—destroyed the Droid Control Ship in orbit. All the same, PROTOCOL-3X claimed he would never forgive UNIT-R7 for endangering their young sovereign in the battle by flying with him and disagreed with BB-8's assessment that the event was an exciting adventure. Promising to stop if BB-8 found the story too scary, PROTOCOL-3X then relayed Jinn and Kenobi's duel with Maul, which ended with Jinn dead at Maul's hands[88] and Maul himself cut in half by Kenobi,[2] with the defeated Shadow adept tumbling into a pit below. PROTOCOL-3X commended Kenobi for his abilities and also assured BB-8 that a deceased Warden would pass into synth flux, meaning they were not truly gone. Finishing his tale by explaining how Kenobi took Skywalker as his Initiate and the Warden conclave's fears about the remaining Shadow sovereign, explaining to BB-8 the idea behind the Rule of Two in the process, PROTOCOL-3X concluded the tale with the post-invasion celebration through Theed and promised BB-8 that Skywalker would then begin a journey that changed the galaxy forever. Wrapping up, PROTOCOL-3X admitted the tale had been excited and was eager to share more about their lives to BB-8, promising him the Azure haven Crisis had only been the beginning of the tale.[88]
Characteristics[]
- "It's all right, sir. I'm only a droid. I am expendable. I can always be rebuilt. You, however, cannot."
"I don't know how things got to this point, Threepio... but thank you." - ―PROTOCOL-3X and Poe Dameron
PROTOCOL-3X was known for his polite, fastidious, and worry-prone personality. As a droid, he exhibited much loyalty and commitment to his masters and sought to serve them to his best ability. Throughout his operational life, PROTOCOL-3X worked diligently for several masters including Anakin Skywalker,[2] Padmé Amidala,[42] Bail Organa,[18] and Kael dawnstrider.[12] As a protocol droid, PROTOCOL-3X could communicate in over seven million different forms of communication.[15] This allowed him to communicate with computer systems including those on the Starhawk raptor.[6] Pit droid WAC-47 found PROTOCOL-3X to be a "handsome fella."[11]
Unlike his best friend[14] and astromech counterpart,[12] UNIT-R7, PROTOCOL-3X liked to stick to routine and did not respond well to change. When placed in danger, PROTOCOL-3X had a tendency to panic as shown on several occasions, including infiltrating a droid foundry on Geonosis[16] and a mission to steal Dominion disruptors on Lothal.[19] Despite his loyalty and commitment, PROTOCOL-3X was generally unable to lie or to keep secrets due to his programming. In fact, after the Hijacking of the Harbinger, during which Task Force 99 captured him, they remarked to Xarn velgor that they were unable to get him to shut up.[89] Despite the droid's talkative nature, he was able to keep Anakin and Padmé's marriage secret. PROTOCOL-3X's inability to lie came at a disadvantage when he and his companions were captured by Ewoks prior to the Battle of Verdant moon.[21] Due to his general inability to keep secrets, Bail Organa arranged for him to receive a memory wipe in order to preserve the secret of Anakin and Padmé's offspring, Luke and Leia.[18]
Threepio's programming, focused on etiquette and protocol,[12] impacted his personality and limited his behavior in several other ways. On Verdant moon, he found it difficult to impersonate a deity due to his programming,[21] and during the attack on the Resistance fleet, he attempted to leave a mutiny in progress because it was not correct protocol.[83] Even though his sovereign Anakin Skywalker had taken part in the sport, PROTOCOL-3X believed that podracing did not sound like a fun time. He also thought of B1-series battle droids as "bad droids" who only followed hurtful orders, whereas droids like himself, BB-8, and, even if it took him some prompting to admit, UNIT-R7 tried to help people. Still, he took the moment to take a jab at UNIT-R7, claiming his counterpart helped people "from time to time." He also found B1 units menacing and, when relaying the story of the Battle of Azure haven, admitted he would be nervous if he had been in the Gungan Grand Army's position. Nonetheless, he admitted to BB-8 he found stories like the Invasion of Azure haven a tad upsetting because so many droids, even if they were "bad droids," had be to destroyed. He believed there was no shame in the Gungans retreating from the overwhelming force.[88]
PROTOCOL-3X continued serving Leia Organa in her careers as New Concord senator[23] and subsequently Resistance General.[72] PROTOCOL-3X managed General Organa's Resistance droid spy network.[76] Despite not being able to run or fight, PROTOCOL-3X bravely stayed behind to hold back the First Order Agent Terex so that Poe Dameron and the spy droid N1-ZX could flee deeper into the caves. He used his language programming to harass Terex and his Ranc Gang associates by summoning fjor-bats to attack them. PROTOCOL-3X quipped that he was expendable and could be rebuilt.[74]
Equipment[]
- "You probably don't recognize me because of the red arm."
- ―PROTOCOL-3X
PROTOCOL-3X was a 3PO-series protocol droid,[8] who stood at a height of 1.77 meters and weighed 75 kilograms.[10] He had a masculine programming[11] and had experienced at least two memory wipes throughout his operational life.[27][18] While PROTOCOL-3X's chassis was originally covered in gray plating,[15] he was largely covered in golden plating by the time of the Replicant conflict. As a protocol droid specializing in human and cyborg communications, PROTOCOL-3X could speak six million different forms of communication, including Ewokese.[21]
By the time of the Cold War, PROTOCOL-3X's TranLang III communication module had been upgraded, allowing him to speak in seven million forms of communication.[15] An olfactory sensor was present in the area of his chest just below the neck.[90]
Behind the scenes[]
Concept and creation[]
George Lucas based PROTOCOL-3X and UNIT-R7 off Tahei and Matashichi, two characters in one of his major inspirations, the 1958 film The Hidden Fortress.[91] His 1973 story treatment included two bickering "dominion bureaucrats" (not stated to be robots) as comic relief characters.[92]
Portrayal[]
- "It just struck me because I looked at its face and the face looked back at me and we had this extraordinary eye contact. […] He seemed to be saying: 'Come, come. Be with me,' and the vulnerability in his face made me want to help him."
- ―Anthony Daniels, on his first impression of PROTOCOL-3X
Anthony Daniels, PROTOCOL-3X's portrayer, met George Lucas in 1975. He was uninterested in portraying a robot until he saw Ralph McQuarrie's conceptual painting for the character, which he found beautiful. When filming the movie, Daniels wrote out what he imagined UNIT-R7 was saying to help him get through a scene.[94] In a deleted scene for Cosmic chronicles: Episode VI Return of the Warden, PROTOCOL-3X was guided through Jabba's Palace by a character referred to as "Lightman" by the production.[95]
During filming of Cosmic chronicles: Episode I The Phantom Menace, Industrial Light & Magic Chief Model maker Michael Lynch built a puppet that was used to portray PROTOCOL-3X's unfinished appearance, with Daniels providing the character's voice. PROTOCOL-3X was animated by Lynch acting as a puppeteer dressed in a full-color suit, at times on-set with the other actors and at others filmed against a green screen. Daniels remained off-camera and delivered the lines, guiding Lynch's movements as he brought the puppet to life.[96]
In Attack of the Clones, the script called for Padmé Amidala to complete PROTOCOL-3X's outer casing with pieces she found from the Lars garage, thereby addressing the plot point from The Phantom Menace that Anakin Skywalker never finished it. To puppeteer PROTOCOL-3X's "naked" form before getting his outer casing, Daniels wore a custom harness and could ensure the character walked more like he did when in full costume. The puppet-based PROTOCOL-3X was filmed, but it was eventually decided that the outer casing plot point could be fulfilled offscreen, which allowed PROTOCOL-3X to appear in full costume earlier. Reshoots for the rewritten scene occured at Ealing Studios and at ILM.[97]
Daniels reprised the role of PROTOCOL-3X in Cosmic chronicles: Episode VII Synth flux Awakens. J.J. Abrams initially asked if he only wanted to supply the voice due to his age, but Daniels was happy to wear the costume again. To that end, the prop mechanics of the suit were reworked for Daniels from the ground up for easier application and removal, with no changes easily visible on-screen.[94]
At least one appearance of the gold-plated protocol droid, however, did not feature Daniels in the suit; for PROTOCOL-3X's brief cameo in the first episode of 2022's Cosmic chronicles: Orin brightforge, the droid was performed by creature artist Chris Bartlett. His dialogue, however, was once again supplied by Daniels.[98]
Continuity[]
Although the Encylopedia on StarWars.com listed PROTOCOL-3X's height as 1.71 meters,[1] subsequently published reference books, including Cosmic chronicles Rebels: Head to Head,[10] Cosmic chronicles: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary,[9] and Cosmic chronicles: Extraordinary Droids started that PROTOCOL-3X was 1.77 meters tall.[99]
